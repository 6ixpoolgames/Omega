## Foundational recovery calculus

- [Recovery Profiles for Finite Observation Channels](./recovery_profiles_finite_observation_channels/recovery_profiles_finite_observation_channels.md)

This paper is a standalone finite recovery-calculus brick: exact, approximate, robust, prior-relative, randomized, and joint recovery for finite observation channels, with corresponding Lean theorem surfaces. It does not depend on the larger Alpha/Omega terminology and does not claim value, agency, identity, valuerhood, or Omega validation.
