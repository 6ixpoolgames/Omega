# Omega v4: foundation review and drafting brief

2026-10-09. Review in preparation for a fresh v4, not the v4 manuscript or a
publication. Recommendations below synthesize the originator's current brief,
earlier cosmology, and retained research. New wording is proposed editorial
synthesis; it does not silently amend the originator's commitments.

**Originator's correction, 2026-10-09:** the bridge from physics to Gradient
Ethics is value requires valuers together with conditional normativity.
Alpha–Omega cosmology is an interpretive layer on top, not a premise of GE.
The dependency order and proposed manuscript structure below reflect this.

## 1. North star

> Characterize the physical structure of possibility: how physical configurations
> support situated continuations, how physical compositions reshape those
> continuations, and how processes within that reality can preserve and expand
> the conditions through which value becomes possible.

The first part is a scientific objective in its own right. The later parts
connect it to emergence, agency, Gradient Ethics and decision. A successful
description of physical possibility remains useful if its effective size has
no unique scalar, or if the proposed ethical connection needs revision.

The ethical connection already has its stated premises: value requires valuers,
and normative reasons are conditional on valuing. Physics characterizes the
conditions and consequences relevant to that argument. Lushness is the proposed
substrate proxy within it. Testing that proxy is not the same as searching for
an unspecified bridge from physical facts to unconditional obligation.

The organizing intuition is stronger than a request for a complexity score:
**physical processes change the conditions under which further processes occur.**
A construction changes later access; a memory changes later response; an ecology
changes which processes can coexist and construct successors. These changes
belong to the native law and state. No external award for organization is needed.

Lushness names the intended effective weighted breadth of continuation.
Generativity is a proposed physical cause of increases in that breadth.
Value concerns what is instantiated within the physical organization and its
perspectives. These are connected questions with different evidential burdens.

## 2. Overall assessment

V2's opening is still the clearest cosmological orientation: a completed reality,
internally differentiated and encountered from within. V3.1 and v3.2 added useful
precision about physical realization, composition, access, recovery and bounded
judgment. Recent work clarified the quantum carrier and eliminated several
premature identifications of an entropy with its extent.

V4 should combine those strengths. It should read as one argument about reality,
possibility and situated consequence, rather than a chronological tour of
candidate metrics. The latest candidate should not become the ontology merely
because it is the latest thing computed.

Explanatory order must follow logical dependence: physical possibility structure;
value requires valuers and conditional normativity; GE and ODT; then the wider
Alpha–Omega interpretation. The cosmological picture can orient the reader, but
accepting it is not an admission requirement for the physics or ethics.

The programme has a credible descriptive core, several useful finite results,
an unresolved comparison problem, and ambitious explanatory, ethical and
metaphysical hypotheses. Its current weakness is the connection between those
levels. More names for the full physical model do not establish that connection.
The next advances should be explicit constructions, mechanism tests, or precise
obstructions that narrow the remaining choices.

## 3. Physical foundation and its cosmological interpretation

### One reality, differentiated internally

As an interpretive layer, the preferred ontology treats the whole as fundamental and its identifiable
components as internal differentiations. This is stronger than saying that
everything consists of the same kind of little object. Preserve it as the
programme's metaphysical starting point, without presenting it as an experimental
consequence of the finite adapters.

The block-Everett picture expresses completion together with quantum relational
structure. Completion is not a final instant and does not make internal change
illusory. It contains delays, losses, recovery, flourishing and finite episodes.
It neither guarantees that cosmic history improves nor makes every verbal
possibility part of physical reality. Formal admissibility, physical realization
and phenomenal habitation remain distinct questions.

### The futuresfield is the physical development

For finite quantum models, the current sufficient description is the state,
native law, local structure and complete propagator family. The
[quantum formalism](../../quantum/FORMALISM.md) gives the conventions. A pure
universal state undergoing unitary evolution can have rich internal relational
development; purity does not establish one unit of continuation extent.

The futuresfield adds no independent force or substance. A history matrix,
multiway graph, process tensor or response atlas is a representation of some
aspect of the development, with its own preservation obligations.

QFT/local field dynamics is the preferred physical modeling direction. Wolframian
rewriting remains an intellectual source and possible representation route.
Neither a specific rewrite system nor a Planck update clock is established as
the physical basement. Conversely, adopting QFT as the next adapter does not
derive it from Alpha or settle quantum gravity.

### Frames belong inside that reality

A physical perspective is situated organization, coupling and access. A frame
describes the relevant restriction or conditioning of the encompassing process.
An analyst's question need not correspond to a newly instantiated physical
observer. Do not collapse these meanings into one generic use of “frame.”

The existing construction already supplies situated weights from the native
law. Conditional normalization makes a frame's probabilities sum to one; it
does not prove that two frames have the same physical continuation breadth.
Cross-frame comparison must retain the maps and conditions relating them.

A sufficient present determines its future under the law. A reduced local state
may be insufficient because of environmental correlations. Keeping those
correlations is compatible with present rooting; time-averaging past presents
is not a repair. A multitime future is generated from the present, rather than
requiring ancestral history as an additional hidden state.

The ultimate frame retains its intended role as the encompassing comparison
where regression of situated normalization closes. It is not an external ideal
chooser. Turning that intended comparison into a complete order remains an
open mathematical task; bounded incomparability need not be elevated into an
ontological claim that no encompassing comparison could exist.

## 4. The common spine and the main research hypothesis

The mathematical constructions examined so far ask different questions of one
spine:

**sufficient present → lawful joint continuation → physical composition →
changed residual conditions → further continuation.**

It carries weights, coherent relations, locality, resources and timing as the
physical theory requires. The central recursive fact is that the output of a
physical process becomes part of the conditions for subsequent physical
processes. No list of human goals or freely selectable actions is fundamental.

Access, persistence, entropy, causal dependence, record formation and recovery
are useful because each exposes some part of that relationship. None inherits
the meaning of the entire object by being useful. Equally, a scalar need not
distinguish every pair of physically different developments to be legitimate.

The substantial generativity hypothesis is this:

> Some organizations repeatedly produce enabling conditions for further
> organization, thereby increasing weighted continuation breadth in regimes
> where matched unorganized preparations do not.

“Repeatedly” is a research target for compounding, not an admission criterion:
transient enabling can also contribute. “Matched” must be specified physically.
The hypothesis permits losses, reversals, saturation and parameter dependence.
It predicts something beyond selecting examples called generative in advance.

For explanation, separate three comparisons: configurations may differ in
breadth; a specified mechanism may cause a difference; and its products may
retain or expand the ability to cause further differences. The existing work
supports parts of the first two. It has not established general compounding
construction of new capabilities or an open-ended ecology.

## 5. Lushness: preserve the ambition, state the calibration

Lushness is the “how much” question. It should not be redefined as organization,
a collection of unrelated diagnostics, record density, ethical goodness, or
whatever an available algorithm happens to return. A profile or partial
comparison may be the best intermediate answer, provided it says what is being
compared. A literal additive volume element is optional.

For a declared classical complete-development law from an exact root,

\[
L_x(n)=\exp H(X_1,\ldots,X_n\mid X_0=x).
\]

This is the current classical calibration: an effective weighted number of
complete futures. Normalized probability can support growing breadth. Distinct
prefixes remain distinct from the original root after reconvergence; rerooting
at the common later state asks a different residual question. Terminal outcomes
keep their probability rather than donating it to surviving alternatives.

A deterministic exact-root classical development has breadth one under this
calibration. That is not by itself a defective implementation, and does not
require an organization bonus. Whether this calibration captures the intended
substrate well enough is still a substantive test. Its susceptibility to
stochastic activity must be evaluated, rather than hidden by excluding noise.

There is already a simple explanatory equation worth making visible in v4.
For a finite homogeneous Markov law K, define g(x)=H(K(x,·)). Then

\[
\ell_n(x):=\log L_x(n)=\sum_{t=0}^{n-1}(K^t g)(x).
\]

This follows directly from the entropy chain rule. At this resolution,
organization affects breadth through the future distribution of physical states
and the branching available there. It does not receive another multiplicative
factor. For a specified stationary reference π,

\[
\ell_n(x)-\sum_y\pi(y)\ell_n(y)
=\sum_{t=0}^{n-1}(\delta_xK^t-\pi)g.
\]

The reference averages separately rooted scores; it does not add entropy of an
unknown initial root. This is an analytical identity, not a new empirical result
or a quantum extension. It exposes the actual mechanism a classical experiment
must explain. Initial advantages can saturate, persist or reverse depending on
the law and comparison. Choice of sampled time resolution remains explicit.

Better access to one outcome does not automatically increase breadth. Making
a binary outcome certain from a fair split lowers its immediate entropy.
A reliable constructor could nevertheless enable a richer later continuation.
That later gain must be calculated, not inferred from reliability or the word
“constructor.” This is a useful sharp test of the generativity hypothesis.

Quantumly, a general positive joint path distribution is unavailable for
arbitrary incompatible history descriptions. The native coherent object remains
well specified in the adapters, but an invariant quantum continuation breadth
is not. Lower-one/classical-upper limits are conditional requirements for the
same justified family, weights and calibration, not a solved interpolation.
Decoherence and global purity alone do not choose the family or its extent.

The [compatibility audit](../../quantum/COMPATIBILITY_AUDIT.md) is therefore the
next mathematical task. It should produce a construction, a precisely scoped
conflict, or a specific missing physical declaration. Another entropy sweep is
not an adequate substitute.

## 6. Gas, baseline and evidence

Thermal continuation is the reference substrate, not an excluded nuisance.
“Vacuum energy” remains an analogy; thermal gas is not the QFT vacuum and no
universal baseline constant has been established. A baseline profile depends
on the law, conserved sector, resources, boundaries, resolution and horizon.

Separate existence of a positive excess, its magnitude, its duration and its
regeneration. A finite positive episode is not erased by later loss. But a
cumulative original-root advantage can remain after a process has ceased to
generate any fresh advantage. Report both original-root and freshly rooted
comparisons when making a persistence claim.

The defensible headline is narrower than “generativity defeats gas”:

- The [FHP work and invariant audit](../../research_notes/omega_v2/fhp_invariant_audit_v0.md)
  retain breadth differences against the stated references, but identify an
  invariant-sector mismatch in the larger gas comparison. Those results are
  not a matched-sector proof of generative advantage.
- The [reactive probe](../../research_notes/omega_v2/reactive_breadth_report_v0.md)
  shows that co-location in an explicitly specified autocatalytic law can
  improve later weighted branching, including fresh projections. One reported
  coordinate exceeds the stationary reacting-mixture mean. That reference is
  not the FHP fluid gas, and increased activity has not been separated from
  retained constructive enabling.
- The [boundary scan](../../research_notes/omega_v2/reactive_boundary_and_compounding_2026-10-07.md)
  finds losing regimes and resolution-dependent crossings. It motivates a
  better compounding model rather than establishing a universal threshold.
- The [quantum ledger](../../quantum/CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE.md) records coherent
  reconstruction, record-extension and eraser checks, alongside the failure
  of candidate readouts as intrinsic quantum breadth.

The counts of passed software tests support implementation checks. They are not
counts of independent discoveries. Existing finite results do not establish
cosmology, general ethical protections, or a universal winner.

## 7. Identity, valuers, awareness and ethics

### Identity is an organization through development

Retain the trajectory interpretation: identical patterns or residual laws need
not be the same physical occurrence. Function, causal lineage, numerical
identity and phenomenal continuity answer different questions. A sufficient
present for future prediction does not answer every historical identity query.

Vortices motivate maintained organization through changing constituents. A
singularity or topological label is not thereby a universal identity criterion.
Restoring a function, copying a pattern and repairing an individual's trajectory
must not become interchangeable by convenient compression.

The originator retains a stronger open possibility: vortical organization in
the futuresfield may become sufficiently dense to admit a singularity-like
limit. Its connection to identity and consciousness is speculative, pending
proper characterization of the futuresfield/universal possibility structure.
“Density,” “vortex” and “singularity” need physical and mathematical meanings
before this becomes a dynamical claim. Do not infer a gravitational singularity,
an awareness threshold or a numerical identity criterion from the analogy.
Exploring such dynamics is a downstream ambition, not a prerequisite for v4.

### Valuerhood remains open without excluding unfamiliar processes

Value requires valuers is the motivating dependence claim. Every consequential
process remains eligible for investigation as a potential valuer. This supplies
neither equal moral weights nor a claim that all processes actually value.
Lushness-generating composition may illuminate valuerhood; it is not yet a
definition or theorem identifying the two.

Plurality is not an axiomatically superior shape. A genuinely encompassing
integrated organization may retain everything a plurality supplies. Conversely,
an efficient controller may destroy complementary access it cannot reproduce.
The comparison concerns actual continuation, not the number of outer boundaries.

### Value lives within perspectives; breadth is the substrate proxy

The bridge is **value requires valuers plus conditional normativity**. Given a
commitment to value, the conditions through which the relevant valuation can
exist and develop enter the reasons for action. Physics supplies the account
of those conditions and how actions alter them. This does not assert a
categorical obligation binding a process independently of valuing, or infer
equal concern for all valuers from an agent's particular commitment alone.

This bridge is part of GE's stated foundation. The open work is its scope and
application, including whether lushness faithfully characterizes the substrate
and supports the stronger proposed comparisons. Alpha–Omega, block-Everett,
cosmopsychism and theology offer interpretations; they do not supply a missing
normative premise.

The ethical wager is that preserving and expanding physical possibility is a
defensible early proxy when the forms of value are poorly known. As knowledge
improves, GE should become more discerning about which continuation instantiates
value. Better knowledge changes justified steering; it must not retroactively
alter the physical measure to make preferred outcomes win.

Structural obstruction, destruction, capture and recovery can be described
before attaching an ethical sign. Whether lushness faithfully tracks their
importance for value is a separate hypothesis. In particular, stochastic
branching caused by injury cannot be deleted merely because the example is
uncomfortable. Nor does measuring it establish that injury is good.

Rights, justice, cooperation and virtue remain proposed higher-level patterns
to explain through their effects on continuation and correction. Their presence
as explicit guards in a toy does not constitute derivation. The stronger
maximization thesis, practical protections under uncertainty, and the unresolved
standard for licensing irreversible sacrifice must remain distinguishable.

ODT remains an intended decision theory. Its license/compare/arbitrate structure
is a useful existing scaffold, not its final ambition. Future work must specify
physical interventions, counterfactual comparisons, policy selection and action
under incomplete knowledge. An ethical checklist alone does not complete ODT.

### Awareness and the metaphysical horizon stay visible

The functionalist extension should be stated positively: consciousness, in its
functional description, is proposed as a **temporoperceptual coordinator** that
integrates asynchronous signals, memory and anticipation into a usable present,
and organizes or reconfigures the disparate deformation structures composing
an individual. “Deformation structures” here refers to physically embodied
processes through which that organization affects continuation. Coordination
must be realized by those processes; the proposal adds no separate inner
controller that performs the integration from outside them.

This concerns both temporal coordination and the organization of an individual,
not merely a clock or a passive display. It should be connected to research on
temporal integration, distributed cognitive access, and embodied predictive
control. Evidence for particular functions, theories of conscious access, and
an account of phenomenal experience have different evidential status. The
functional proposal does not depend on identifying a futuresfield singularity.

Primary-source anchors checked for this framing:

- [Eagleman and Sejnowski (2000), temporal integration and postdiction](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10720334/):
  flash-lag experiments and a postdictive account support temporally extended
  perceptual integration. This is evidence about a specific visual phenomenon,
  not proof of a universal temporal coordinator or a complete consciousness theory.
- [Dehaene, Kerszberg and Changeux (1998), neuronal global workspace](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9826734/):
  an original computational model connects specialized processors through a
  distributed workspace for coordinated effortful cognition. It supplies a
  close theoretical counterpart to coordination, not proof that coordination
  alone constitutes phenomenal experience.
- [Seth, Suzuki and Critchley (2012), interoceptive predictive coding](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2011.00395/full):
  a theoretical account connects bodily prediction, agency and conscious
  presence. It is relevant to embodied self-organization, not validation of
  Omega's individual, κ or singularity hypotheses.

These are connections to established research traditions, with different levels
of empirical support. They do not jointly establish the proposed synthesis.
The programme's additional question is how these coordinating functions organize
the individual's physical influence on subsequent continuation.

Cosmopsychism has a specific proposed role: bridging across κ, the unresolved
relation between physical organization and experience, with the fewest additional
assumptions. That economy is the originator's rationale for the proposal, not an
established minimality theorem. A fully functionalist reading remains compatible.
Situated habitation and the constructed present belong in this discussion.
A correspondence without physical backreaction is different from a new physical
interaction; neither should be inserted covertly into the continuation weights.

Restore the theological scope of v2 in a clearly identified part of v4:
ultimate ground, manifest actuality and formal description; unity, Logos,
non-excisive individuation, communion, judgment, redemption and the apophatic
veil. Completion does not establish divine knowledge, agency, goodness, an
afterlife or necessity. The aim is a coherent conditional interpretation,
not a claim that the quantum probes proved theology.

The Aquinas, Spinoza and demiurge reinterpretations are proposed links to
theology and philosophy. Present their actual correspondences and departures
explicitly when drafting, rather than flattening them into either generic
metaphysical speculation or wholesale endorsement of the source traditions.
Their role differs from cosmopsychism's proposed bridge across κ, and from
the value-requires-valuers/conditional-normativity bridge grounding GE.
This review does not invent a detailed demiurge correspondence beyond the
originator's clarification.

Alpha supplies the proposed general grammar of distinction, relation and
orientation; Logos a lawful realization; Omega its completed manifestation.
This wider ambition belongs in v4, while derivation of physical law from Alpha
remains open. A theory of our universe and a claim about all possible laws
are different scopes.

## 9. Proposed manuscript architecture

Write the main account fresh. Let the reader meet the object before meeting its
failed proxies. Use six short, accessible parts, with the mathematics and claim
map available beside them:

1. **The physical structure of possibility.** Sufficient presents, coherent
   continuation, frames, locality, composition and QFT direction; the scientific
   objective and why it matters independently of ethics.
2. **How organization changes what follows.** Access, construction,
   maintenance, obstruction, repair and compounding; laws remain native.
3. **How much possibility?** Lushness, classical weighted breadth, thermal
   comparison, quantum requirements and the exact remaining mathematical gap.
4. **Value requires valuers.** Identity, agency, valuation and conditional
   normativity; the explicit bridge to ethics and the role of the lushness proxy.
5. **Gradient Ethics and decision.** Cooperation, harm, recovery, correction,
   protections and the intended ODT, with their conditional arguments stated.
6. **Alpha–Omega: the interpretive horizon.** Unity, block-Everett completion,
   internal time, Logos, awareness, theology and situated knowledge; an
   interpretation of the programme rather than a prerequisite for it.

Appendices: formal conventions, four or five decisive worked examples, evidence
and failure ledger, version migration, glossary and verified references.
Do not carry every historical candidate into the main argument.

A useful recurring example is a matched collection of matter: dispersed,
organized to enable a transformation, damaged, repaired, and capable of producing
further enabling organization. Use it to connect the questions. It is an
illustration until a specified model supplies an actual result; do not narrate
its preferred ranking as though it had already been demonstrated.

## 10. Research commitments beyond the manuscript

**First, make the comparison question mathematically decisive.** Complete the
existing quantum compatibility audit. Specify which changes are mere
redescription, which alter a frame, and which change physics. Try a construction
on that domain, or prove a scoped conflict. The deliverable is more than another
well-behaved diagnostic.

**Second, investigate generativity as a mechanism on the classical domain where
the breadth rule is already explicit.** The chain-rule identity above gives the
bridge to test. Separate transient activation, reversed turnover, retained
enabling and production of new enabling functions. Preserve losses and negative
regimes. This line need not wait for a universal quantum measure; it also cannot
be advertised as its solution.

**Third, extend the physical adapter when the mathematical question requires it.**
A small regulated local field model should supply a concrete missing capability:
for example spatial propagation and physical record formation under one native
Hamiltonian. Keep regulator, preparation and environment explicit. Building a
larger simulation without a discriminating question is not progress by itself.

**Fourth, test the proxy within the stated ethical bridge.** Value requires
valuers and conditional normativity supply that bridge; do not repeatedly
present it as absent. Keep model consequences and
the reasons for ethical judgments independently inspectable. Investigate when
familiar protections emerge and when the breadth proxy conflicts with them.
A failure may belong to representation, comparison or the ethical hypothesis;
do not automatically protect the last by adding a missing feature.

Success before a universal answer could be a restricted invariant comparison,
a useful reduction with stated error, a mechanism explaining breadth advantages,
or an impossibility result that genuinely eliminates a class of proposals.
All would sharpen the programme rather than merely rename it.

## 11. Editorial acceptance checks

The opening should let a new reader answer: what is Omega, what is being
investigated, what is lushness, why does generativity matter, and how does ethics
enter? The first page should state an affirmative programme rather than a list
of cautions. Put qualifications beside the claims they actually constrain.

Every major bridge should have a status: adopted interpretation, intended
definition, standard mathematics, finite evidence, or open hypothesis.
Use those labels to permit ambitious conjectures, not to make the text timid.

Keep one current meaning for each central term. Do not use “frame” for both a
real apparatus and an unperformed measurement without explanation, “Omega” for
both the actual universe and every conceivable law without a scope change, or
“lushness” for both an unknown target and an arbitrary diagnostic.

Do not publish raw numerical outputs. Link compact reports, source, tests and
declared limitations. The retained 58 quantum test passes belong to the prior
cleanup; this review runs no experiments and revalidates no historical suite.
Publication needs a reference check, especially for inherited empirical
consciousness claims. This local review is not a fresh literature survey.
