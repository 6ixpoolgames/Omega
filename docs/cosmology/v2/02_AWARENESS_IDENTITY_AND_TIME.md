# 02 — Awareness, Identity, and Experienced Time

## 1. Awareness before a theory of the subject

The epistemic starting point is that awareness occurs. This does not settle what awareness is made of, how subjects are individuated, or whether awareness belongs only to systems like us.

The project uses **awareness** for minimal phenomenal presence. **Consciousness** refers to its organized or elaborated manifestations, potentially involving integration, cognition, memory, or self-modeling. This is a working distinction. It leaves open whether awareness is fundamental, emergent, universal, graded, or dependent on a particular substrate.

In particular, broad awareness is not equivalent to sophisticated reflective cognition. A proposal that awareness is widely distributed need not attribute human consciousness to every physical system. Conversely, a structural description of a system does not by itself establish even minimal awareness.

Chalmers is an important neighbor for separating functional explanation from the question of phenomenal occurrence. Omega leaves the explanatory gap open while constructing the physical and perspectival organization to which an eventual answer would have to connect. [Chalmers, *Facing Up to the Problem of Consciousness*](https://consc.net/papers/facing.pdf)

## 2. The correspondence problem

Let $\mathcal P_{\mathrm{phys}}$ be a proposed class of physically situated perspectives, and $\mathcal E$ a space of possible phenomenal occurrences. Write

$$
\kappa\subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{phys}}\times\mathcal E
$$

for the unknown correspondence between them. A relation is used rather than a function because uniqueness, individuation, and multiplicity are unresolved. This notation is a place to state hypotheses, not a coupling mechanism.

An identity theory might identify the two sides under suitable descriptions. A dual-aspect account might treat both as aspects of one reality. Emergent, panpsychist, idealist, and psychophysical-law accounts would impose different conditions on $\kappa$. The packet does not need to choose among them to describe physical records and agency.

Awareness could also prove relevant to what counts as an adequate physical description. Such a possibility would require more than renaming an unexplained feature: a proposed extension must say which distinctions it introduces and what follows from them. If it changes observable statistics, those changes become empirical commitments.

## 3. Page as the closest awareness-measure neighbor

Don Page's Sensible Quantum Mechanics and *Mindless Sensationalism* provide a particularly close precedent. Sets of conscious experiences receive measures through expectation values of associated positive operators. Schematically,

$$
\mu(S)=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho A(S)).
$$

Omega should credit this structure directly when using it. Page's framework does not require a continuously traveling substantial mind to organize its basic experiential measure. His awareness operators are additional psychophysical structure, not operators already identified by ordinary quantum theory. [Page, *Mindless Sensationalism*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0108039)

Our proposed extension investigates how situated perspectives, future-relevant equivalence, agency, and valuerhood might organize the correspondence. It is not yet a mathematical extension theorem of Page's theory. We do not inherit his broader theological positions, a particular typicality principle, or an identification of awareness with the full class of conscious perceptions he considers.

An awareness measure need not be a normalized probability over all experiences. Nor does a measure over experiences supply a transition law between them. A trajectory account requires additional joint or sequential structure. A physical record projector is also more restrictive than a general awareness operator. Equating them would be a new hypothesis.

## 4. Universal habitation and the non-privileged present

The preferred conjecture is that every admissible situated perspective is inhabited, with no additional universal present deciding which locus is currently actual. The domain of “admissible” must remain explicit.

There are two versions. If admissibility means compatibility with a specified awareness correspondence, universal habitation extends that correspondence across all qualifying locations. If it means every structurally realized perspective independently of phenomenal criteria, the claim is stronger and panpsychist in reach. The latter is not established by the former.

This distinction also clarifies anthropic reasoning. Any consciously observed situation must permit the consciousness observing it. That conditional is coherent even if it is tautological. Its stronger explanatory uses depend on what independently specifies the class of perspectives and their measure.

The conjecture does not privilege human observers. It permits awareness wherever the relevant conditions occur, including at other scales or in unfamiliar substrates if those conditions permit them. It also leaves open whether several structural perspectives constitute distinct phenomenal subjects, overlapping subjects, or no subject in the ordinary sense.

## 5. Memory and experienced time

An inhabited present need not carry an extra, immaterial record of having visited earlier states. Human memory is physically organized information within the present situation. The structure can include records interpreted as a past, expectations directed toward a future, and processes maintaining continuity across successive local states.

On the block reading, all these differently situated occurrences belong to the completed reality. The experienced flow is accounted for structurally through the ordered contents and relations of those occurrences, rather than an awareness-token moving along a second timeline. Whether this fully explains the phenomenology of passage remains part of the correspondence problem.

Present records do not generally determine a unique microscopic history. Several histories can agree on a person's memories while differing elsewhere. They need not agree on all future consequences. A local psychological description is therefore not automatically a sufficient statistic for the future.

A globally specified state in a specified reversible model raises a different question from a coarse present record. The framework must not infer global ambiguity or uniqueness from what holds at only one descriptive level.

## 6. Coarse graining around a perspective

The motivating intuition is that awareness supplies, or is associated with, an invariant around which compatible histories can be grouped. Distinctions that make no difference to that situated occurrence should not multiply its description artificially.

The operational construction starts with a physically specified class of accessible future interactions. Two full situations are equivalent only when those interactions cannot distinguish them at the declared resolution. This captures a precise part of the intuition without assuming that awareness itself determines the equivalence relation.

The unresolved step is substantial: does sameness of awareness imply sameness of the relevant future structure? It need not if unnoticed environmental correlations can affect later experiences or actions. A successful awareness-centered quotient must preserve those correlations, or explain why they are genuinely inaccessible for the question under study.

Rovelli's relational quantum mechanics is a neighbor for indexed descriptions and relations between physical systems. It does not require consciousness to cause measurement. Omega can borrow relational bookkeeping without identifying a physical interaction with phenomenal habitation. [Rovelli, *Relational Quantum Mechanics*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9609002)

## 7. Identity, vortical continuity, and the soul invariant

An organism persists through material turnover. A civilization persists through changing members. These cases motivate looking for organizational continuity rather than an immutable token.

**Vorticality** names an exploratory pattern of recurrence: effects cross a system's boundary, influence its environment, and return through feedback that helps sustain or reshape the system. Literal circulation in a fluid is one possible realization. The general agency proposal concerns causal organization.

The **soul invariant** is a placeholder for whatever makes a sequence or bundle of embodiments count as one continuing organization from within. No specific scalar, conserved quantity, or identity relation has been discovered. Structural continuity also does not establish phenomenal continuity or survival after bodily destruction.

Fission exposes the problem: two successors can preserve different aspects of one predecessor. A theory may support continuity relations without selecting one uniquely identical survivor. Parfit's treatment of psychological continuity and Buddhist no-self analysis are useful neighbors because both make room for consequential continuity without an unquestioned substantial ego. They do not verify Omega's cosmology. [Parfit, *Reasons and Persons*, Part III](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/reasons-and-persons-9780198249085); [SN 22.59](https://suttacentral.net/sn22.59/en/sujato)

Valuerhood need not wait for this problem to be solved. A process's continuation can matter even where personal identity across its transformations remains disputed.

## 8. Quantum erasure and the limits of the image

The delayed-choice eraser offers a concrete illustration of record-relative descriptions within one joint quantum arrangement. Conditional subsets can show interference while the unsorted earlier record remains unchanged. The calculation in the appendices uses established quantum correlations and requires no awareness-driven change to the past.

This supports the consistency of the block-and-perspective description. It does not establish that awareness causes decoherence, erase every particle history through observation, or supply evidence for memories moving between universes. Those would be additional claims with additional burdens.

The black-hole and white-hole imagery can remain as intuition: a situated locus has a constrained relation to recorded history and an oriented spread of possible consequence. “Wormhole” imagery evokes effects leaving a system and returning through its world. None of these metaphors supplies a gravitational mechanism, a unique past, or a proof of quantum immortality.
