# Omega Cosmology — v2

## A completed reality, encountered from within

**Version:** 2.0 — first complete working edition

**Date:** 15 September 2026

**Provenance:** a human-originated research program developed through sustained dialogue and formal experimentation with AI systems.

Omega begins with an image: reality as a completed structure, internally differentiated into perspectives whose actions help constitute what follows. From within, there are histories, uncertainties, decisions, losses, and possibilities. At the level of the whole, these relations need no external moving present. Completion includes consequence.

The ethical question is situated within this picture: which forms of agency sustain and enrich the possibility of value? Value requires valuers. Valuers depend on conditions that their own activity, and the activity of others, can preserve, transform, or destroy. The framework studies this recursively changing field of continuation before attempting to settle what its best realization would be.

The motivating hypothesis is that persistent generativity has a special place in this structure. A process that can maintain itself, respond to contingency, and generate further value-capable organization can continue opening possibilities that a sterile repetition cannot. Whether this tendency supports a defensible measure of lushness, a stable ethical corridor, or a wider theory of search remains a research question. The hypothesis supplies a target; it does not supply its own proof.

## The recurring moves

1. **Begin with structure; recover process internally.** Time, action, and becoming are relations within a world, not necessarily changes to a world viewed from outside.
2. **Treat perspective as constitutive.** A situated organization determines what is accessible, consequential, internal, and continuous for it. Perspective helps individuate an inhabitant as well as describe its view.
3. **Start below familiar identity.** Valuerhood and agency are explored through consequence, feedback, and persistence. A settled theory of persons or souls is not an admission requirement.
4. **Separate possibility of value from particular valuations.** The futures field is a substrate for value. Its richness is not identical to pleasure, preference satisfaction, complexity, or population.
5. **Take the persistence filter seriously.** What remains present in later situations has passed conditions of continuation. This gives the framework a Darwinian engine without making every survivor virtuous.
6. **Follow the effects of action on the space of action.** Agents alter their environments, capabilities, successors, and the distinctions that future agents can use. The board is endogenous.
7. **Assess integration through what it sustains and generates.** A coordinated plurality and an internally rich singleton may implement comparable organization. Excision and sterile closure are the concern.
8. **Use limits to discipline positive claims.** Dissolution, foreclosure, failed recovery, and missing knowledge help locate the conditions of continuation. They do not automatically determine a unique optimum.

## Commitments and evidential status

The packet distinguishes established inputs, model-relative deductions, philosophical commitments, conjectures, and analogies. An equation may specify a conjecture without proving that reality realizes it. A conditional theorem may be useful without establishing its premises. A physical model may support several metaphysical readings.

The preferred cosmological reading is relational, completed, and perspectival. Universal habitation of admissible perspectives is a live conjecture. The relationship between awareness and physical structure remains open. Gradient Ethics can be assessed without accepting either that conjecture or the theological interpretation.

The theological limit is integral to the project. Formal and empirical inquiry can constrain what an adequate account of reality must accommodate. It cannot distinguish accounts that agree on every accessible consequence. The framework therefore seeks to narrow an apophatic veil, not declare it crossed. “Approaching a summa” names an ambition toward coherence and breadth, not a proven convergence to exhaustive knowledge or Thomistic doctrine.

## Edition policy

This is a fresh account of the current framework. Earlier packet files and addenda remain historical sources in the original Google Drive folder; this edition integrates their surviving content and the subsequent corrections. They are not additional premises a reader must reconcile with v2. In particular, v2 does not inherit a blanket rejection of scalar lushness, a blanket condemnation of singletons, or a privileged moving locus of awareness.

The intended conceptual object is governed by the originator's current clarifications. Claims about formal results remain governed by the actual assumptions and scope of their source artifacts. Neither kind of authority substitutes for the other.
