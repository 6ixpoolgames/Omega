# 01 — Reality, Completion, and Perspective

## 1. Reality as internally differentiated structure

The founding intuition is that one fundamental reality can constitute all creation through its internal relationships. Multiplicity need not require several independently existing substances. Spatial things, temporal sequences, organisms, and minds may be organizations within a common whole.

Wolfram's atoms-of-space model supplies a close intellectual neighbor: distinguishable elements acquire spatial and material significance through a changing hypergraph of relations. The elements are not particles located in pre-existing space. Omega borrows this relational starting point and the multiway representation of alternatives, while leaving open whether Wolfram's particular physics describes our universe. [Wolfram, *The Wolfram Physics Project: A One-Year Update*](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2021/04/the-wolfram-physics-project-a-one-year-update/)

Two readings deserve separation. On one, there is one kind of elementary entity, and a world is composed of many instances. On the stronger reading preferred here, the whole is fundamental and its identifiable components are internal differentiations. The second can support unity without assuming that independent ingredients must first be assembled. Neither reading alone establishes aseity, awareness, or goodness.

## 2. Completion and internal change

A completed world includes all of its events and their relations. Calling it completed does not place it at a final instant after a process of construction. It denies the need for an additional time in which the whole acquires its internal times.

Within that whole, an organism develops, remembers, acts, and dies. Those differences are real features of the structure. The statement that the whole is complete does not imply that its internal states are identical, reversible, equally accessible, or causally interchangeable.

Minkowski's spacetime formulation is a foundation for expressing temporal relations geometrically. A metaphysical block reading goes beyond the mathematical representation. Everett's relative-state formulation supplies a separate neighbor in which the universal quantum description contains correlated alternatives. Omega's block-Everett picture combines these orientations as working architecture; it is not a new consequence of either source alone. [Minkowski, *Space and Time*](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Space_and_Time_(Hermann_Minkowski)); [Everett, 1957](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.29.454)

Completion has a scope. A completed model contains its admissible histories. It does not thereby contain every verbally describable scenario, mutually inconsistent laws in one situation, or every possible model as a physical component. A larger space of models is a further construction.

## 3. Multiway graphs

A multiway graph represents situations and their permitted continuations. Branches distinguish alternatives; convergence records situations represented as the same after different routes. Different graphs can describe the same underlying process at different resolutions.

For an initial classical scaffold, write

$$
\mathcal B=(X,\leadsto),
$$

where $X$ is a declared space of situations and $\leadsto$ their admissible continuation relation. A path is a sequence consistent with that relation. This is deliberately sparse: probability, metric distance, consciousness, and moral relevance have not been inserted.

A physical quantum realization needs more structure. Alternatives carry complex amplitudes and can interfere. Its drawn paths cannot simply be counted as independent worlds. The formal appendices construct such a representation from a supplied Hilbert space, state, and unitary dynamics.

“Exhaustive” therefore means exhaustive under the specified model and its rules of admissibility. The distinction between formal availability and actual realization remains explicit.

## 4. Perspective as situated access and organization

A perspective is a way reality is organized and accessible at a locus. It includes which distinctions can affect that organization, which interactions it can participate in, and which continuations count as downstream relative to it.

An adapter is a formal representation of that perspective: a map from a richer description to the distinctions and operations relevant to an inquiry. An adapter can be chosen by a scientist without there being a conscious subject corresponding to it. A physically realized perspective must earn its interpretation through the system's actual organization and causal relations.

A minimal working description is

$$
P=(\mathcal O_P,\mathcal A_P,\sim_P),
$$

where $\mathcal O_P$ specifies accessible records or observations, $\mathcal A_P$ specifies admissible interactions, and $\sim_P$ specifies distinctions the representation treats as equivalent. Orientation and any proposed persistence relation must be supplied by the model. The notation does not force a final theory of identity into every perspective.

The crucial adequacy condition is consequence preservation. If two situations can lead to distinguishable outcomes under an interaction relevant to the inquiry, a compression cannot identify them and still claim to preserve that inquiry exactly. Approximate compression must state its tolerances and horizon.

This is the project's perspectival move in its most general form: begin with a larger structure, then determine which distinctions survive at a situated interface. Wolfram's observer theory is a close neighbor concerning bounded observers and effective descriptions. Omega extends the question toward awareness, valuerhood, and continuation; these extensions are not supplied by observer theory itself. [Wolfram, *Observer Theory*](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2023/12/observer-theory/)

## 5. Three questions about possibility

Formal admissibility asks what a model permits. Physical realization asks what belongs to actual physical reality. Phenomenal habitation asks where awareness occurs. The three may ultimately coincide under an adequate ontology, but their identity is not a premise needed for every chapter.

Anthropic compatibility restricts an account to conditions consistent with the perspective making the observation. It can exclude proposed conditions under which that observation is impossible. It does not, without further assumptions, establish that every compatible structure exists or that every such structure is inhabited.

The preferred habitation conjecture applies the perspectival move again: awareness is situated wherever an admissible awareness-bearing perspective occurs, without a universal present selecting one locus. Whether the admissible class includes all physical perspectives is left to the coupling account developed in Chapter 02.

## 6. Agency in a completed world

An agent's policy can be a determinant of downstream structure even when that structure is considered as a completed whole. A counterfactual model compares the consequences of different policies in a matched setting. It does not imagine editing an already completed universe from outside.

This gives structural steering a precise target: changes in the agent's internal organization correspond to changes in continuation. An appropriate causal model must determine which replacements are legitimate. Arbitrarily changing every surrounding condition along with the policy would make the comparison uninformative.

Everettian branching adds another distinction. Deterministic unitary evolution of a global quantum state is compatible with a branching description of relative records. Neither determinism nor branching implies that an action has no consequences. The downstream correlations partly constitute what the action is.

An additional claim that awareness selects, creates, or reweights branches would require a further theory. Structural steering does not need it.

## 7. Alpha as an optional generalization

Alpha names the ambition to study admissible configurations and oriented consequence across domains. Temporal trajectories are one adapter. Derivations, repairs, transformations, and other dependencies may supply different adapters in other spaces.

Distinction, relation, and orientation are useful descriptive ingredients. They do not prove that every structure has a gradient, that every gradient is physical, or that all spaces share the same topology. A claim of universality must state the class of structures and the maps preserving the relevant relationships.

Wolfram's all-rules argument offers a stronger metaphysical neighbor: treating possible rules and their consequences together removes the need for an external operator to select and execute one. Omega takes seriously the possibility that formal consequences belong timelessly to the whole. Whether formal existence constitutes physical and phenomenal actuality remains an additional commitment. [Wolfram, *Why Does the Universe Exist?*](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2021/04/why-does-the-universe-exist-some-perspectives-from-our-physics-project/)

Alpha is not required for the quantum realization, the persistence inquiry, or Gradient Ethics. It records how the same method might apply more widely if suitable structures can be identified.
