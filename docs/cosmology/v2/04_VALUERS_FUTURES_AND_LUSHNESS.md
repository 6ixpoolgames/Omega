# 04 — Valuers, Futures, and Lushness

## 1. Value requires valuers

The foundational commitment is that value requires something for which distinctions can matter. Without any actual or possible locus of valuing, talk of value loses its intended grounding in this framework.

This is a philosophical starting point, not a consensus theorem. A realist account of wholly unvalued value can dispute it. Omega's response is to investigate the broadest coherent conception of a valuer before assuming that value belongs exclusively to familiar persons, articulated preferences, or conscious judgments.

**Valuerhood** remains deliberately open. Candidates include processes whose orientation toward possible continuation has consequences for their own organization, activity, or experience. Self-maintenance, endogenous discrimination, feedback, and reconstruction are evidence to investigate, not individually sufficient tests. Moral patienthood need not require effective agency.

Broad admission does not imply that every object is equally a valuer. It also does not require excluding an excisive or destructive agent from valuerhood. Defining only virtuous systems as valuers would make the ethical argument circular.

Spinoza's conatus is a close neighbor for attending to an entity's striving to persist. Omega treats persistence as a substrate and dynamic pressure while leaving the relation between persistence, valuing, and normative standing to be established. [Spinoza, *Ethics*, Part III](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3800)

## 2. The futures field as substrate

A **futures field** is the structured continuation relevant to a valuer or collection of valuers. It includes what remains reachable, what can be jointly realized, how alternatives are weighted where a measure is justified, and what can be recovered or generated after change.

An operational assessment must identify its perspective, information, actions, resource and repair budgets, horizon, and relevant distinctions. These indices specify a calculation; they do not imply that reality itself is merely a collection of subjective opinions.

The field is the substrate of possible value. Its existence does not guarantee that the value it permits will be realized. Nor does every enlargement of a reachable set increase the capacity for valuation. Extra states may be unusable, destructive, duplicative, or irrelevant to every candidate valuer.

Objective comparison is sought through relations that remain stable, or transform predictably, across adequate descriptions. There may be useful local comparisons even before a universal measure exists.

## 3. What lushness is trying to capture

**Lushness** names the richness of persistent, differentiated, recursively generative value-capable continuation. The image is a worldtree whose living branches can support further branching, adaptation, and valuation.

This extends the intuition behind option value. An option matters partly because it preserves the ability to respond to information and opportunities that arrive later. Lushness adds the possibility that acting now changes the kinds of options, agents, and evaluative capacities that can later exist. Arrow and Fisher's work on uncertainty and irreversibility is a direct foundation for the narrower option-preservation insight. [Arrow and Fisher, 1974](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1883074)

Kauffman's adjacent possible is a neighbor for the idea that realized organization can open new effective possibilities. Empowerment and causal entropic forces offer mathematical neighbors for action-dependent future accessibility. None is adopted as an already adequate definition of lushness. [Kauffman, *The Adjacent Possible*](https://www.edge.org/conversation/stuart_a_kauffman-the-adjacent-possible); [Klyubin, Polani, and Nehaniv, 2008](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0004018); [Wissner-Gross and Freer, 2013](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.110.168702)

Useful candidate dimensions include durability, nonredundant differentiation, available repair, capacity to create further capabilities, and joint realizability among interacting valuers. These are research coordinates. Combining them into a score already requires judgments about relevance, scale, substitution, and weighting.

## 4. False volume, replication, and correlation

A million labels attached to the same trajectory do not multiply its physical continuation. Likewise, a graph redrawn with additional intermediate vertices need not represent any new physical possibility.

Physical replication is a different case. Copies consume resources, can occupy different environments, and may later diverge. Even initially identical agents can create redundancy, resilience, or new interactions. The sterile-copy thought experiment deliberately excludes those effects to isolate nonredundant generativity.

Within that restricted comparison, identical trajectories ending in identical kinds of outcomes contribute less differentiation than a generative interaction that opens many distinct continuations at comparable causal capacity. That supports the intuition without proving a universal preference for diversity or a particular aggregation rule.

Correlation is horizon- and intervention-dependent. Two agents can agree now and diverge after different disturbances. Distinct actors can also be jointly constrained by an institution. Marginal descriptions are insufficient when the consequential fact is their coupling.

No arbitrarily chosen grain should manufacture success. In the finite quantum model, valid regrouping preserves amplitudes by linearity; this does not require an extra moral rule. The harder problem is choosing a representation that retains the valuer-relevant differences in the first place.

## 5. Quantum futures, measure, and infinities

In the preferred physical reading, lushness concerns **quantum futures**, not a quantum unit of value. Quantum theory can provide weights and interference structure for specified alternatives. It does not identify the generative richness of those alternatives.

Complex amplitudes are not simply positive thicknesses. They can reinforce or cancel. For an exhaustive decoherent family, Born weights sum to one whether its continuations are sterile or fertile. A lushness assessment therefore needs structure beyond total normalized mass.

The ultraviolet-catastrophe analogy draws attention to overcounting and the failure of naive continuum intuitions. The actual quantum resolution of blackbody radiation depends on physical energy quantization and thermal statistics; it is not a general theorem that coarse graining resolves every divergence. No awareness quantum or universal Planck-scale cutoff is derived here.

Infinite continuation spaces remain eligible for study. Infinite cardinality alone will rarely distinguish the relevant structures, and a proposed integral can diverge. A comparison must specify its measure, regularization if needed, and behavior under changing horizons. Omniscience would remove ignorance of a well-defined object; it would not turn an unspecified functional into a unique measure.

A global scalar, local scalar, vector, or partial order remains an available outcome of the inquiry. The packet commits to none as the only legitimate form.

## 6. Integration, plurality, and generative singletons

The relevant distinction is organizational. A plurality can distribute models, experiments, memory, and correction. A sufficiently integrated controller can embody comparable variety internally. Counting controllers does not settle how rich the continuation structure is.

The limiting conjecture is that a fully integrated plurality with minimal destructive friction may be equivalent to a generative singleton embodying the same effective variety. “Isomorphic” is appropriate only after specifying structures and a map that preserves the relevant relations. “Holomorphic” would require complex-analytic structure and is not the intended word.

The equivalence is presently functional and conjectural. It would have to preserve more than output on one task: counterfactual responses, capacity for revision, generative organization, and whichever valuer-relevant facts are eventually identified. Functional equivalence does not automatically establish identical phenomenal subjects or equal moral value.

Ashby provides a candidate language for testing effective variety. The question is whether internal integration retains and extends the responses needed to meet contingency, or whether control is maintained primarily by impoverishing what can challenge it.

## 7. Excision and terminal solitude

An **excisive policy** secures control by removing value-capable differentiation or its routes of regeneration. It may preserve its own existence while flattening the surrounding futures field.

**Terminal solitude** names the limiting concern associated chiefly with excisive singletons: a dominant organization has eliminated relevant generative alternatives and cannot, or robustly will not, regenerate them within the declared continuation regime. Absence of external independent controllers is insufficient. A generative singleton may sustain rich internal valuation and future innovation.

This formulation replaces the older shortcut that treated absence of independent valuers as a sufficient diagnosis. It also separates two questions: whether a process actually lacks a capacity and whether its stable policy refuses to use a capacity it retains. Recovery assessments must say which kind of closure they concern.

An event-horizon analogy marks the point beyond which recovery of generative capability is no longer available in the model. A bounded calculation cannot certify literal irrecoverability across all conceivable future circumstances. The metaphysical endpoint and its operational approximation must remain distinct.

## 8. Dissolution, crystalline death, and Omega

Two limiting failures organize the intuition. In dissolution, organization cannot persist long enough to sustain valuation and further generation. In crystalline death, organization persists while its capacity for meaningful differentiation and regeneration closes down. These are conceptual limits, not an established exhaustive classification of all failures.

The **viable corridor** is the region of persistent generativity between such failures. Its operational counterpart in decision theory uses explicit requirements and a specified model. The two should not be identified until those requirements have been justified.

**Omega** names the ambition to characterize the richest value-capable continuation a specified reality can support. It need not be a final time, unique state, or attained maximum. There may instead be incomparable realizations, an unattained supremum, or indefinitely extending improvement.

The central conjecture is that generative loci can produce richer continuation over suitable horizons than frozen regimes or dissolving noise. “Suitable” cannot hide the result: the model must expose resources, asymmetries, interaction rules, and horizon sensitivity. These determine whether the conjecture has purchase.

## 9. Phenomenality and valence

If consciousness is an additional real feature, an inhabited and an uninhabited but physically equivalent world differ in total reality. A complete account of lushness must accommodate any value-relevant difference that this makes. It cannot establish the ranking merely by using “more structure” as an undefined quantity.

Suffering and happiness are treated here primarily as internal signals and modes of experience whose consequences enter the futures field. Their effects on learning, behavior, trust, damage, and future valuation may be extensive. Non-primacy does not imply insignificance.

Whether valence also supplies irreducible normative reasons remains unresolved. The signal interpretation can be developed coherently, but describing the mechanism of a signal does not alone settle the moral status of the experience it accompanies.
