# Omega Cosmology — v2

## A completed reality, encountered from within

**Version:** 2.0 — first complete working edition

**Date:** 15 September 2026

**Provenance:** a human-originated research program developed through sustained dialogue and formal experimentation with AI systems.

Omega begins with an image: reality as a completed structure, internally differentiated into perspectives whose actions help constitute what follows. From within, there are histories, uncertainties, decisions, losses, and possibilities. At the level of the whole, these relations need no external moving present. Completion includes consequence.

The ethical question is situated within this picture: which forms of agency sustain and enrich the possibility of value? Value requires valuers. Valuers depend on conditions that their own activity, and the activity of others, can preserve, transform, or destroy. The framework studies this recursively changing field of continuation before attempting to settle what its best realization would be.

The motivating hypothesis is that persistent generativity has a special place in this structure. A process that can maintain itself, respond to contingency, and generate further value-capable organization can continue opening possibilities that a sterile repetition cannot. Whether this tendency supports a defensible measure of lushness, a stable ethical corridor, or a wider theory of search remains a research question. The hypothesis supplies a target; it does not supply its own proof.

## The recurring moves

1. **Begin with structure; recover process internally.** Time, action, and becoming are relations within a world, not necessarily changes to a world viewed from outside.
2. **Treat perspective as constitutive.** A situated organization determines what is accessible, consequential, internal, and continuous for it. Perspective helps individuate an inhabitant as well as describe its view.
3. **Start below familiar identity.** Valuerhood and agency are explored through consequence, feedback, and persistence. A settled theory of persons or souls is not an admission requirement.
4. **Separate possibility of value from particular valuations.** The futures field is a substrate for value. Its richness is not identical to pleasure, preference satisfaction, complexity, or population.
5. **Take the persistence filter seriously.** What remains present in later situations has passed conditions of continuation. This gives the framework a Darwinian engine without making every survivor virtuous.
6. **Follow the effects of action on the space of action.** Agents alter their environments, capabilities, successors, and the distinctions that future agents can use. The board is endogenous.
7. **Assess integration through what it sustains and generates.** A coordinated plurality and an internally rich singleton may implement comparable organization. Excision and sterile closure are the concern.
8. **Use limits to discipline positive claims.** Dissolution, foreclosure, failed recovery, and missing knowledge help locate the conditions of continuation. They do not automatically determine a unique optimum.

## Commitments and evidential status

The packet distinguishes established inputs, model-relative deductions, philosophical commitments, conjectures, and analogies. An equation may specify a conjecture without proving that reality realizes it. A conditional theorem may be useful without establishing its premises. A physical model may support several metaphysical readings.

The preferred cosmological reading is relational, completed, and perspectival. Universal habitation of admissible perspectives is a live conjecture. The relationship between awareness and physical structure remains open. Gradient Ethics can be assessed without accepting either that conjecture or the theological interpretation.

The theological limit is integral to the project. Formal and empirical inquiry can constrain what an adequate account of reality must accommodate. It cannot distinguish accounts that agree on every accessible consequence. The framework therefore seeks to narrow an apophatic veil, not declare it crossed. “Approaching a summa” names an ambition toward coherence and breadth, not a proven convergence to exhaustive knowledge or Thomistic doctrine.

## Edition policy

This is a fresh account of the current framework. Earlier packet files and addenda remain historical sources in the original Google Drive folder; this edition integrates their surviving content and the subsequent corrections. They are not additional premises a reader must reconcile with v2. In particular, v2 does not inherit a blanket rejection of scalar lushness, a blanket condemnation of singletons, or a privileged moving locus of awareness.

The intended conceptual object is governed by the originator's current clarifications. Claims about formal results remain governed by the actual assumptions and scope of their source artifacts. Neither kind of authority substitutes for the other.

# 01 — Reality, Completion, and Perspective

## 1. Reality as internally differentiated structure

The founding intuition is that one fundamental reality can constitute all creation through its internal relationships. Multiplicity need not require several independently existing substances. Spatial things, temporal sequences, organisms, and minds may be organizations within a common whole.

Wolfram's atoms-of-space model supplies a close intellectual neighbor: distinguishable elements acquire spatial and material significance through a changing hypergraph of relations. The elements are not particles located in pre-existing space. Omega borrows this relational starting point and the multiway representation of alternatives, while leaving open whether Wolfram's particular physics describes our universe. [Wolfram, *The Wolfram Physics Project: A One-Year Update*](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2021/04/the-wolfram-physics-project-a-one-year-update/)

Two readings deserve separation. On one, there is one kind of elementary entity, and a world is composed of many instances. On the stronger reading preferred here, the whole is fundamental and its identifiable components are internal differentiations. The second can support unity without assuming that independent ingredients must first be assembled. Neither reading alone establishes aseity, awareness, or goodness.

## 2. Completion and internal change

A completed world includes all of its events and their relations. Calling it completed does not place it at a final instant after a process of construction. It denies the need for an additional time in which the whole acquires its internal times.

Within that whole, an organism develops, remembers, acts, and dies. Those differences are real features of the structure. The statement that the whole is complete does not imply that its internal states are identical, reversible, equally accessible, or causally interchangeable.

Minkowski's spacetime formulation is a foundation for expressing temporal relations geometrically. A metaphysical block reading goes beyond the mathematical representation. Everett's relative-state formulation supplies a separate neighbor in which the universal quantum description contains correlated alternatives. Omega's block-Everett picture combines these orientations as working architecture; it is not a new consequence of either source alone. [Minkowski, *Space and Time*](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Space_and_Time_(Hermann_Minkowski)); [Everett, 1957](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.29.454)

Completion has a scope. A completed model contains its admissible histories. It does not thereby contain every verbally describable scenario, mutually inconsistent laws in one situation, or every possible model as a physical component. A larger space of models is a further construction.

## 3. Multiway graphs

A multiway graph represents situations and their permitted continuations. Branches distinguish alternatives; convergence records situations represented as the same after different routes. Different graphs can describe the same underlying process at different resolutions.

For an initial classical scaffold, write

$$
\mathcal B=(X,\leadsto),
$$

where $X$ is a declared space of situations and $\leadsto$ their admissible continuation relation. A path is a sequence consistent with that relation. This is deliberately sparse: probability, metric distance, consciousness, and moral relevance have not been inserted.

A physical quantum realization needs more structure. Alternatives carry complex amplitudes and can interfere. Its drawn paths cannot simply be counted as independent worlds. The formal appendices construct such a representation from a supplied Hilbert space, state, and unitary dynamics.

“Exhaustive” therefore means exhaustive under the specified model and its rules of admissibility. The distinction between formal availability and actual realization remains explicit.

## 4. Perspective as situated access and organization

A perspective is a way reality is organized and accessible at a locus. It includes which distinctions can affect that organization, which interactions it can participate in, and which continuations count as downstream relative to it.

An adapter is a formal representation of that perspective: a map from a richer description to the distinctions and operations relevant to an inquiry. An adapter can be chosen by a scientist without there being a conscious subject corresponding to it. A physically realized perspective must earn its interpretation through the system's actual organization and causal relations.

A minimal working description is

$$
P=(\mathcal O_P,\mathcal A_P,\sim_P),
$$

where $\mathcal O_P$ specifies accessible records or observations, $\mathcal A_P$ specifies admissible interactions, and $\sim_P$ specifies distinctions the representation treats as equivalent. Orientation and any proposed persistence relation must be supplied by the model. The notation does not force a final theory of identity into every perspective.

The crucial adequacy condition is consequence preservation. If two situations can lead to distinguishable outcomes under an interaction relevant to the inquiry, a compression cannot identify them and still claim to preserve that inquiry exactly. Approximate compression must state its tolerances and horizon.

This is the project's perspectival move in its most general form: begin with a larger structure, then determine which distinctions survive at a situated interface. Wolfram's observer theory is a close neighbor concerning bounded observers and effective descriptions. Omega extends the question toward awareness, valuerhood, and continuation; these extensions are not supplied by observer theory itself. [Wolfram, *Observer Theory*](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2023/12/observer-theory/)

## 5. Three questions about possibility

Formal admissibility asks what a model permits. Physical realization asks what belongs to actual physical reality. Phenomenal habitation asks where awareness occurs. The three may ultimately coincide under an adequate ontology, but their identity is not a premise needed for every chapter.

Anthropic compatibility restricts an account to conditions consistent with the perspective making the observation. It can exclude proposed conditions under which that observation is impossible. It does not, without further assumptions, establish that every compatible structure exists or that every such structure is inhabited.

The preferred habitation conjecture applies the perspectival move again: awareness is situated wherever an admissible awareness-bearing perspective occurs, without a universal present selecting one locus. Whether the admissible class includes all physical perspectives is left to the coupling account developed in Chapter 02.

## 6. Agency in a completed world

An agent's policy can be a determinant of downstream structure even when that structure is considered as a completed whole. A counterfactual model compares the consequences of different policies in a matched setting. It does not imagine editing an already completed universe from outside.

This gives structural steering a precise target: changes in the agent's internal organization correspond to changes in continuation. An appropriate causal model must determine which replacements are legitimate. Arbitrarily changing every surrounding condition along with the policy would make the comparison uninformative.

Everettian branching adds another distinction. Deterministic unitary evolution of a global quantum state is compatible with a branching description of relative records. Neither determinism nor branching implies that an action has no consequences. The downstream correlations partly constitute what the action is.

An additional claim that awareness selects, creates, or reweights branches would require a further theory. Structural steering does not need it.

## 7. Alpha as an optional generalization

Alpha names the ambition to study admissible configurations and oriented consequence across domains. Temporal trajectories are one adapter. Derivations, repairs, transformations, and other dependencies may supply different adapters in other spaces.

Distinction, relation, and orientation are useful descriptive ingredients. They do not prove that every structure has a gradient, that every gradient is physical, or that all spaces share the same topology. A claim of universality must state the class of structures and the maps preserving the relevant relationships.

Wolfram's all-rules argument offers a stronger metaphysical neighbor: treating possible rules and their consequences together removes the need for an external operator to select and execute one. Omega takes seriously the possibility that formal consequences belong timelessly to the whole. Whether formal existence constitutes physical and phenomenal actuality remains an additional commitment. [Wolfram, *Why Does the Universe Exist?*](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2021/04/why-does-the-universe-exist-some-perspectives-from-our-physics-project/)

Alpha is not required for the quantum realization, the persistence inquiry, or Gradient Ethics. It records how the same method might apply more widely if suitable structures can be identified.

# 02 — Awareness, Identity, and Experienced Time

## 1. Awareness before a theory of the subject

The epistemic starting point is that awareness occurs. This does not settle what awareness is made of, how subjects are individuated, or whether awareness belongs only to systems like us.

The project uses **awareness** for minimal phenomenal presence. **Consciousness** refers to its organized or elaborated manifestations, potentially involving integration, cognition, memory, or self-modeling. This is a working distinction. It leaves open whether awareness is fundamental, emergent, universal, graded, or dependent on a particular substrate.

In particular, broad awareness is not equivalent to sophisticated reflective cognition. A proposal that awareness is widely distributed need not attribute human consciousness to every physical system. Conversely, a structural description of a system does not by itself establish even minimal awareness.

Chalmers is an important neighbor for separating functional explanation from the question of phenomenal occurrence. Omega leaves the explanatory gap open while constructing the physical and perspectival organization to which an eventual answer would have to connect. [Chalmers, *Facing Up to the Problem of Consciousness*](https://consc.net/papers/facing.pdf)

## 2. The correspondence problem

Let $\mathcal P_{\mathrm{phys}}$ be a proposed class of physically situated perspectives, and $\mathcal E$ a space of possible phenomenal occurrences. Write

$$
\kappa\subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{phys}}\times\mathcal E
$$

for the unknown correspondence between them. A relation is used rather than a function because uniqueness, individuation, and multiplicity are unresolved. This notation is a place to state hypotheses, not a coupling mechanism.

An identity theory might identify the two sides under suitable descriptions. A dual-aspect account might treat both as aspects of one reality. Emergent, panpsychist, idealist, and psychophysical-law accounts would impose different conditions on $\kappa$. The packet does not need to choose among them to describe physical records and agency.

Awareness could also prove relevant to what counts as an adequate physical description. Such a possibility would require more than renaming an unexplained feature: a proposed extension must say which distinctions it introduces and what follows from them. If it changes observable statistics, those changes become empirical commitments.

## 3. Page as the closest awareness-measure neighbor

Don Page's Sensible Quantum Mechanics and *Mindless Sensationalism* provide a particularly close precedent. Sets of conscious experiences receive measures through expectation values of associated positive operators. Schematically,

$$
\mu(S)=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho A(S)).
$$

Omega should credit this structure directly when using it. Page's framework does not require a continuously traveling substantial mind to organize its basic experiential measure. His awareness operators are additional psychophysical structure, not operators already identified by ordinary quantum theory. [Page, *Mindless Sensationalism*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0108039)

Our proposed extension investigates how situated perspectives, future-relevant equivalence, agency, and valuerhood might organize the correspondence. It is not yet a mathematical extension theorem of Page's theory. We do not inherit his broader theological positions, a particular typicality principle, or an identification of awareness with the full class of conscious perceptions he considers.

An awareness measure need not be a normalized probability over all experiences. Nor does a measure over experiences supply a transition law between them. A trajectory account requires additional joint or sequential structure. A physical record projector is also more restrictive than a general awareness operator. Equating them would be a new hypothesis.

## 4. Universal habitation and the non-privileged present

The preferred conjecture is that every admissible situated perspective is inhabited, with no additional universal present deciding which locus is currently actual. The domain of “admissible” must remain explicit.

There are two versions. If admissibility means compatibility with a specified awareness correspondence, universal habitation extends that correspondence across all qualifying locations. If it means every structurally realized perspective independently of phenomenal criteria, the claim is stronger and panpsychist in reach. The latter is not established by the former.

This distinction also clarifies anthropic reasoning. Any consciously observed situation must permit the consciousness observing it. That conditional is coherent even if it is tautological. Its stronger explanatory uses depend on what independently specifies the class of perspectives and their measure.

The conjecture does not privilege human observers. It permits awareness wherever the relevant conditions occur, including at other scales or in unfamiliar substrates if those conditions permit them. It also leaves open whether several structural perspectives constitute distinct phenomenal subjects, overlapping subjects, or no subject in the ordinary sense.

## 5. Memory and experienced time

An inhabited present need not carry an extra, immaterial record of having visited earlier states. Human memory is physically organized information within the present situation. The structure can include records interpreted as a past, expectations directed toward a future, and processes maintaining continuity across successive local states.

On the block reading, all these differently situated occurrences belong to the completed reality. The experienced flow is accounted for structurally through the ordered contents and relations of those occurrences, rather than an awareness-token moving along a second timeline. Whether this fully explains the phenomenology of passage remains part of the correspondence problem.

Present records do not generally determine a unique microscopic history. Several histories can agree on a person's memories while differing elsewhere. They need not agree on all future consequences. A local psychological description is therefore not automatically a sufficient statistic for the future.

A globally specified state in a specified reversible model raises a different question from a coarse present record. The framework must not infer global ambiguity or uniqueness from what holds at only one descriptive level.

## 6. Coarse graining around a perspective

The motivating intuition is that awareness supplies, or is associated with, an invariant around which compatible histories can be grouped. Distinctions that make no difference to that situated occurrence should not multiply its description artificially.

The operational construction starts with a physically specified class of accessible future interactions. Two full situations are equivalent only when those interactions cannot distinguish them at the declared resolution. This captures a precise part of the intuition without assuming that awareness itself determines the equivalence relation.

The unresolved step is substantial: does sameness of awareness imply sameness of the relevant future structure? It need not if unnoticed environmental correlations can affect later experiences or actions. A successful awareness-centered quotient must preserve those correlations, or explain why they are genuinely inaccessible for the question under study.

Rovelli's relational quantum mechanics is a neighbor for indexed descriptions and relations between physical systems. It does not require consciousness to cause measurement. Omega can borrow relational bookkeeping without identifying a physical interaction with phenomenal habitation. [Rovelli, *Relational Quantum Mechanics*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9609002)

## 7. Identity, vortical continuity, and the soul invariant

An organism persists through material turnover. A civilization persists through changing members. These cases motivate looking for organizational continuity rather than an immutable token.

**Vorticality** names an exploratory pattern of recurrence: effects cross a system's boundary, influence its environment, and return through feedback that helps sustain or reshape the system. Literal circulation in a fluid is one possible realization. The general agency proposal concerns causal organization.

The **soul invariant** is a placeholder for whatever makes a sequence or bundle of embodiments count as one continuing organization from within. No specific scalar, conserved quantity, or identity relation has been discovered. Structural continuity also does not establish phenomenal continuity or survival after bodily destruction.

Fission exposes the problem: two successors can preserve different aspects of one predecessor. A theory may support continuity relations without selecting one uniquely identical survivor. Parfit's treatment of psychological continuity and Buddhist no-self analysis are useful neighbors because both make room for consequential continuity without an unquestioned substantial ego. They do not verify Omega's cosmology. [Parfit, *Reasons and Persons*, Part III](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/reasons-and-persons-9780198249085); [SN 22.59](https://suttacentral.net/sn22.59/en/sujato)

Valuerhood need not wait for this problem to be solved. A process's continuation can matter even where personal identity across its transformations remains disputed.

## 8. Quantum erasure and the limits of the image

The delayed-choice eraser offers a concrete illustration of record-relative descriptions within one joint quantum arrangement. Conditional subsets can show interference while the unsorted earlier record remains unchanged. The calculation in the appendices uses established quantum correlations and requires no awareness-driven change to the past.

This supports the consistency of the block-and-perspective description. It does not establish that awareness causes decoherence, erase every particle history through observation, or supply evidence for memories moving between universes. Those would be additional claims with additional burdens.

The black-hole and white-hole imagery can remain as intuition: a situated locus has a constrained relation to recorded history and an oriented spread of possible consequence. “Wormhole” imagery evokes effects leaving a system and returning through its world. None of these metaphors supplies a gravitational mechanism, a unique past, or a proof of quantum immortality.

# 03 — Gradient, Filter, and Agency

## 1. Gradients and conditions of continuation

Many processes continue by exploiting differences: available energy, chemical potential, information, resources, or opportunities for action. Omega uses **gradient** broadly for an oriented difference that can shape continuation. A literal gradient requires a specified function and geometry; the general term does not imply that every domain already has them.

A **filter** consists of conditions under which a process, lineage, or organization remains represented in later situations. Filtering need not be deliberate. Some arrangements dissolve, some reproduce, some stabilize, and some reorganize their surroundings to become more durable.

The central dynamic intuition is recursive. What continues can change the conditions of its own further continuation. What replicates can occupy more of the available substrate. What learns can change which perturbations it can survive. Agency emerges within this interaction between differentiation and persistence, rather than being introduced as an unexplained chooser outside the world.

## 2. Cosmic Darwinism as an engine, not a guarantee

Darwinian selection supplies the principal neighbor: variation, differential continuation or reproduction, and inheritance can change the composition of a population. Omega explores how far that pattern generalizes across physical and organizational scales. [Darwin, *On the Origin of Species*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1228)

For a simple replicating population, a model might write

$$
\mathbb E[n_i(t+1)\mid n(t)]=n_i(t)\,w_i(n(t),e(t)),
$$

where $n_i$ counts instances of type $i$, $e$ describes the environment, and $w_i$ is an effective growth factor including survival and reproduction. Heritable variation changes the types available; interactions make their growth factors depend on population composition. This equation illustrates the filter without identifying fitness with value.

Valuers need not logically prefer their own survival. However, valuers whose organization supports continued valuing can become more represented among later valuers, other things being equal. Reproduction can amplify that effect. This is the dynamic pressure the framework takes seriously.

The qualifications are part of the engine. A sterile replicator can expand rapidly. A generous organization can be exploited. An adapted system can disappear when the environment changes. Selection can operate at several conflicting levels. A persistence filter favors what passes the operative filter, not whatever an observer independently calls rich, good, or wise.

Replication is especially relevant to occupying expanding opportunities through descendants. It is not a logical requirement for every persistent process: one organization can grow, transform, or indefinitely maintain itself without making separate copies. The proper unit may be a lineage, institution, distributed process, or changing pattern rather than an individual organism.

## 3. Deformers and the endogenous board

A **deformer** is a process whose presence or organization changes the continuation available in a surrounding system. Rivers, organisms, institutions, tools, and optimizers can all deform possibilities. Large causal effect alone does not establish agency or standing.

In a matched model, replace process $D$ with a relevant alternative $D'$. If the resulting continuation structures differ, that comparison identifies an effect attributable to the replacement. What counts as a relevant alternative depends on the causal question.

Deformation can change reachability, resource availability, correlations, repair routes, bottlenecks, or the stability of a pattern. It can also change the effective action space. An invention may create an action that was previously unavailable; an institution may enable coordinated action; a destructive policy may remove the infrastructure needed to recover from it.

The geometry is therefore endogenous at the effective level. This need not mean that fundamental physical laws change. Under fixed laws, organizations can create new capabilities and new useful coordinates for describing continuation.

The analogy with matter shaping spacetime motivates this reciprocal relationship between inhabitants and their field. It does not supply an Einstein equation for ethics or establish a literal futures field in physics.

## 4. The transition into agency

Agency appears when a deformer has endogenous, feedback-mediated organization through which its internal state or policy systematically changes continuation. Stronger agency can sustain, revise, or expand that control channel.

Earlier Gradient Ethics language called this a first phase transition into agency. In v2, “phase transition” identifies a proposed qualitative change in organization. A literal transition would require a control parameter, an order parameter, and evidence of the relevant change. Those have not been supplied for agency in general.

A minimal schematic model is

$$
x_{t+1}=F(x_t,a_t,e_t),\qquad
a_t\sim\pi(\cdot\mid m_t),\qquad
m_{t+1}=G(m_t,o_{t+1},a_t),
$$

where $m_t$ is internal organization or memory, $o_t$ an input, and $a_t$ an action. To interpret this as agency rather than arbitrary notation, the model must establish the actual feedback relations and counterfactual effects of changing $\pi$ or $G$.

Self-modification extends the model to changes in policy, representation, or embodiment. The relevant challenge is continuity of effective correction through those changes. An agent that optimizes away the means to notice error may retain local performance while losing the capacity to respond to a changed world.

## 5. Vortical organization and multiscale integration

The vortex intuition concerns organized persistence through throughput. Parts change while a recurrent pattern remains. Effects circulate through a boundary and its environment, helping regenerate the conditions under which the pattern continues.

Autopoiesis supplies a biological and organizational neighbor: the network that produces and maintains a living unity is central to the account. Omega uses this to motivate endogenous maintenance while keeping its proposed agency class broader and its valuer class unresolved. [Varela, Maturana, and Uribe, *Autopoiesis: The Organization of Living Systems, Its Characterization and a Model*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0303264774900318)

Levin's work offers a further neighbor in treating cognition and goal-directed organization across scales and embodiments. The relevance is methodological: test the range of goals, interventions, and coordinated competencies a system supports. Omega does not infer a cosmic mind or an immortal soul from biological control. [Levin, *Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere*](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnsys.2022.768201)

Integration can constrain components while expanding the capabilities of the whole. An organism's cells have fewer independent trajectories than an unconstrained collection, yet their coordination supports new kinds of continuation. The ethical question cannot be answered by treating every reduction of local freedom as net contraction.

Conversely, higher-level integration can conceal losses at lower levels. Which continuities matter, what is replaced, and what remains capable of correction must be examined across scales.

## 6. Ashby: capability through appropriate variety

Ashby's law of requisite variety is a primary foundation for thinking about regulation under disturbance. Effective regulation requires an appropriate range of responses relative to the distinctions in the disturbances and the outcomes being constrained. It is a relation among regulator, environment, and task. [Ashby, *An Introduction to Cybernetics*, chapter 11](https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf)

This makes Ashby a useful starting point for distinguishing two controller strategies. One expands its models, responses, and internal differentiation to meet contingency. Another simplifies what it must face by suppressing environmental variation. Both can regulate successfully in a specified task. Their effects on the wider futures field may differ radically.

Raw internal complexity is an inadequate substitute for effective regulatory variety. Unused noise, ornamental simulations, and redundant copies need not enlarge the responses available to consequential disturbances. Nor does regulatory success establish ethical adequacy: a controller can become highly competent at an excisive objective.

The candidate comparison must therefore inspect usable responses, retained environmental differentiation, generative capacity, and the consequences of constraint for other valuers. No single Ashby-derived number currently settles all four.

## 7. What the dynamic hypothesis predicts conditionally

The framework expects generative, adaptable organization to have advantages where novel contingencies reward expanded response capability, useful variation can be retained, and interactions permit its benefits to accumulate. It expects excisive simplification to incur costs where suppressed variety later becomes necessary for survival or discovery.

Those conditions can fail. An environment may remain simple, a dominant controller may prevent the relevant disturbances, or an excisive replicator may exploit its initial advantage before competitors can respond. The board's starting conditions and the degree of asymmetry are therefore central variables.

The research target is a phase diagram of regimes, not a universal assurance that the generous policy wins. A useful model would identify when diversity, integration, selective simplification, or defensive constraint improves persistent generativity—and when it does not.

# 04 — Valuers, Futures, and Lushness

## 1. Value requires valuers

The foundational commitment is that value requires something for which distinctions can matter. Without any actual or possible locus of valuing, talk of value loses its intended grounding in this framework.

This is a philosophical starting point, not a consensus theorem. A realist account of wholly unvalued value can dispute it. Omega's response is to investigate the broadest coherent conception of a valuer before assuming that value belongs exclusively to familiar persons, articulated preferences, or conscious judgments.

**Valuerhood** remains deliberately open. Candidates include processes whose orientation toward possible continuation has consequences for their own organization, activity, or experience. Self-maintenance, endogenous discrimination, feedback, and reconstruction are evidence to investigate, not individually sufficient tests. Moral patienthood need not require effective agency.

Broad admission does not imply that every object is equally a valuer. It also does not require excluding an excisive or destructive agent from valuerhood. Defining only virtuous systems as valuers would make the ethical argument circular.

Spinoza's conatus is a close neighbor for attending to an entity's striving to persist. Omega treats persistence as a substrate and dynamic pressure while leaving the relation between persistence, valuing, and normative standing to be established. [Spinoza, *Ethics*, Part III](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3800)

## 2. The futures field as substrate

A **futures field** is the structured continuation relevant to a valuer or collection of valuers. It includes what remains reachable, what can be jointly realized, how alternatives are weighted where a measure is justified, and what can be recovered or generated after change.

An operational assessment must identify its perspective, information, actions, resource and repair budgets, horizon, and relevant distinctions. These indices specify a calculation; they do not imply that reality itself is merely a collection of subjective opinions.

The field is the substrate of possible value. Its existence does not guarantee that the value it permits will be realized. Nor does every enlargement of a reachable set increase the capacity for valuation. Extra states may be unusable, destructive, duplicative, or irrelevant to every candidate valuer.

Objective comparison is sought through relations that remain stable, or transform predictably, across adequate descriptions. There may be useful local comparisons even before a universal measure exists.

## 3. What lushness is trying to capture

**Lushness** names the richness of persistent, differentiated, recursively generative value-capable continuation. The image is a worldtree whose living branches can support further branching, adaptation, and valuation.

This extends the intuition behind option value. An option matters partly because it preserves the ability to respond to information and opportunities that arrive later. Lushness adds the possibility that acting now changes the kinds of options, agents, and evaluative capacities that can later exist. Arrow and Fisher's work on uncertainty and irreversibility is a direct foundation for the narrower option-preservation insight. [Arrow and Fisher, 1974](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1883074)

Kauffman's adjacent possible is a neighbor for the idea that realized organization can open new effective possibilities. Empowerment and causal entropic forces offer mathematical neighbors for action-dependent future accessibility. None is adopted as an already adequate definition of lushness. [Kauffman, *The Adjacent Possible*](https://www.edge.org/conversation/stuart_a_kauffman-the-adjacent-possible); [Klyubin, Polani, and Nehaniv, 2008](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0004018); [Wissner-Gross and Freer, 2013](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.110.168702)

Useful candidate dimensions include durability, nonredundant differentiation, available repair, capacity to create further capabilities, and joint realizability among interacting valuers. These are research coordinates. Combining them into a score already requires judgments about relevance, scale, substitution, and weighting.

## 4. False volume, replication, and correlation

A million labels attached to the same trajectory do not multiply its physical continuation. Likewise, a graph redrawn with additional intermediate vertices need not represent any new physical possibility.

Physical replication is a different case. Copies consume resources, can occupy different environments, and may later diverge. Even initially identical agents can create redundancy, resilience, or new interactions. The sterile-copy thought experiment deliberately excludes those effects to isolate nonredundant generativity.

Within that restricted comparison, identical trajectories ending in identical kinds of outcomes contribute less differentiation than a generative interaction that opens many distinct continuations at comparable causal capacity. That supports the intuition without proving a universal preference for diversity or a particular aggregation rule.

Correlation is horizon- and intervention-dependent. Two agents can agree now and diverge after different disturbances. Distinct actors can also be jointly constrained by an institution. Marginal descriptions are insufficient when the consequential fact is their coupling.

No arbitrarily chosen grain should manufacture success. In the finite quantum model, valid regrouping preserves amplitudes by linearity; this does not require an extra moral rule. The harder problem is choosing a representation that retains the valuer-relevant differences in the first place.

## 5. Quantum futures, measure, and infinities

In the preferred physical reading, lushness concerns **quantum futures**, not a quantum unit of value. Quantum theory can provide weights and interference structure for specified alternatives. It does not identify the generative richness of those alternatives.

Complex amplitudes are not simply positive thicknesses. They can reinforce or cancel. For an exhaustive decoherent family, Born weights sum to one whether its continuations are sterile or fertile. A lushness assessment therefore needs structure beyond total normalized mass.

The ultraviolet-catastrophe analogy draws attention to overcounting and the failure of naive continuum intuitions. The actual quantum resolution of blackbody radiation depends on physical energy quantization and thermal statistics; it is not a general theorem that coarse graining resolves every divergence. No awareness quantum or universal Planck-scale cutoff is derived here.

Infinite continuation spaces remain eligible for study. Infinite cardinality alone will rarely distinguish the relevant structures, and a proposed integral can diverge. A comparison must specify its measure, regularization if needed, and behavior under changing horizons. Omniscience would remove ignorance of a well-defined object; it would not turn an unspecified functional into a unique measure.

A global scalar, local scalar, vector, or partial order remains an available outcome of the inquiry. The packet commits to none as the only legitimate form.

## 6. Integration, plurality, and generative singletons

The relevant distinction is organizational. A plurality can distribute models, experiments, memory, and correction. A sufficiently integrated controller can embody comparable variety internally. Counting controllers does not settle how rich the continuation structure is.

The limiting conjecture is that a fully integrated plurality with minimal destructive friction may be equivalent to a generative singleton embodying the same effective variety. “Isomorphic” is appropriate only after specifying structures and a map that preserves the relevant relations. “Holomorphic” would require complex-analytic structure and is not the intended word.

The equivalence is presently functional and conjectural. It would have to preserve more than output on one task: counterfactual responses, capacity for revision, generative organization, and whichever valuer-relevant facts are eventually identified. Functional equivalence does not automatically establish identical phenomenal subjects or equal moral value.

Ashby provides a candidate language for testing effective variety. The question is whether internal integration retains and extends the responses needed to meet contingency, or whether control is maintained primarily by impoverishing what can challenge it.

## 7. Excision and terminal solitude

An **excisive policy** secures control by removing value-capable differentiation or its routes of regeneration. It may preserve its own existence while flattening the surrounding futures field.

**Terminal solitude** names the limiting concern associated chiefly with excisive singletons: a dominant organization has eliminated relevant generative alternatives and cannot, or robustly will not, regenerate them within the declared continuation regime. Absence of external independent controllers is insufficient. A generative singleton may sustain rich internal valuation and future innovation.

This formulation replaces the older shortcut that treated absence of independent valuers as a sufficient diagnosis. It also separates two questions: whether a process actually lacks a capacity and whether its stable policy refuses to use a capacity it retains. Recovery assessments must say which kind of closure they concern.

An event-horizon analogy marks the point beyond which recovery of generative capability is no longer available in the model. A bounded calculation cannot certify literal irrecoverability across all conceivable future circumstances. The metaphysical endpoint and its operational approximation must remain distinct.

## 8. Dissolution, crystalline death, and Omega

Two limiting failures organize the intuition. In dissolution, organization cannot persist long enough to sustain valuation and further generation. In crystalline death, organization persists while its capacity for meaningful differentiation and regeneration closes down. These are conceptual limits, not an established exhaustive classification of all failures.

The **viable corridor** is the region of persistent generativity between such failures. Its operational counterpart in decision theory uses explicit requirements and a specified model. The two should not be identified until those requirements have been justified.

**Omega** names the ambition to characterize the richest value-capable continuation a specified reality can support. It need not be a final time, unique state, or attained maximum. There may instead be incomparable realizations, an unattained supremum, or indefinitely extending improvement.

The central conjecture is that generative loci can produce richer continuation over suitable horizons than frozen regimes or dissolving noise. “Suitable” cannot hide the result: the model must expose resources, asymmetries, interaction rules, and horizon sensitivity. These determine whether the conjecture has purchase.

## 9. Phenomenality and valence

If consciousness is an additional real feature, an inhabited and an uninhabited but physically equivalent world differ in total reality. A complete account of lushness must accommodate any value-relevant difference that this makes. It cannot establish the ranking merely by using “more structure” as an undefined quantity.

Suffering and happiness are treated here primarily as internal signals and modes of experience whose consequences enter the futures field. Their effects on learning, behavior, trust, damage, and future valuation may be extensive. Non-primacy does not imply insignificance.

Whether valence also supplies irreducible normative reasons remains unresolved. The signal interpretation can be developed coherently, but describing the mechanism of a signal does not alone settle the moral status of the experience it accompanies.

# 05 — Gradient Ethics, Decision, and Power

## 1. The ethical proposal

Gradient Ethics begins with value's dependence on valuers and asks what action does to the conditions under which valuation can persist and develop. Its central concern is the irreversible foreclosure of value-capable continuation, especially when an agent acts through a description too thin to register what it destroys.

The residual normative commitment is explicit: the continuation and development of affected valuers supply reasons for action. Their relevance cannot legitimately be removed merely by changing a description, manipulating a proxy, or declaring an unverified substitute equivalent.

This is a proposed moral commitment. The descriptive facts of persistence and deformation do not entail it on their own. Hume's discussion of transitions from “is” to “ought” marks the inferential boundary. Omega's strategy is to expose that boundary, then make the structural consequences of accepting the commitment as rigorous as possible. [Hume, *A Treatise of Human Nature*, III.i.1](https://davidhume.org/texts/t/3/1/1)

“Value requires valuers” establishes a dependence claim within the framework. It does not immediately establish that every particular valuer must survive, that all its preferences deserve satisfaction, or that reproduction is always obligatory. Those conclusions require further judgments about standing, conflict, substitution, and joint continuation.

## 2. Structural justification and internal motivation

A policy has consequences whether its actor is compassionate, ambitious, confused, or indifferent. Given an adequate model and explicit normative commitments, those consequences can support a judgment independently of the actor's self-description.

Motivation nevertheless affects which policy is selected and how it is sustained. Likewise, suffering can change attention, learning, cooperation, and capability. Internal signals matter through their place in the full dynamics even when they are not treated as the primary objective.

The ambition that one could inspect the structure and see who is right is therefore conditional: the relevant facts, standing, and comparison principles must be available. Structural inspection can refute a claimed preservation or compensation. It cannot choose the normative premises merely by displaying more detail.

## 3. Irreversibility and the significance of horizons

An action can foreclose a continuation that no available repair restores. Operationally, that claim depends on a fact language, allowed repairs, resources, uncertainty, and horizon. A broader repair budget can change the verdict. A longer horizon can reveal damage missed by a shorter one or recovery invisible within it.

Thermodynamic irreversibility, logical erasure, and inability to recover a declared fact are distinct notions. They can interact, but one should not be substituted for another without a physical model.

Irreversibility gives consequence persistence, not moral sign. Sacrifice can destroy one local continuation while preserving a richer joint organization. A proliferating cancer can preserve a local lineage while destroying the organism on which many coordinated processes depend. Those patterns motivate analysis at more than one boundary.

The horizon problem is established ground in this project. A new finite calculation displaying it would clarify the issue; it would not by itself advance the theory. The more demanding task is to justify which continuation facts and horizons should govern real decisions.

## 4. Rights and virtues as durable policies

Rights can function as protections against decisions made with short horizons, incomplete descriptions, or overwhelming local incentives. Their rationale within GE is that some forms of foreclosure destroy the conditions for future correction, participation, and valuation. The right protects a structure whose full significance may exceed the current evaluator's model.

This supports a family of rights arguments, not a deduction of a complete rights code. Determining bearers, scope, conflicts, and exceptions requires substantive normative work. An alleged global benefit cannot settle those questions by assuming away the affected parties' standing.

Virtues can be interpreted as policies that tend to preserve or expand valuable continuation across recurring contingencies. Prudence attends to delayed effects and uncertainty. Humility preserves routes of correction. Courage and capable restraint make preservation enforceable. Justice concerns the distribution and defense of the conditions under which valuers can continue together.

Consequential evaluation, rights, and virtue thus occupy different levels of one decision problem: evaluating consequences, stabilizing protective constraints, and cultivating reliable action under limited cognition. This is a reconstruction rather than a claim to absorb the full traditions of Mill, Kant, or Aristotle. Jonas is a more direct neighbor for responsibility toward the conditions of future life under expanded technological power. [Jonas, *The Imperative of Responsibility*](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Imperative_of_Responsibility.html?id=sRP3uJkxydQC)

## 5. Viability before optimization

The operational question is first whether a policy keeps declared continuation requirements achievable under the modeled disturbances. Viability theory provides the mathematical foundation for this question. [Aubin, *Viability Theory*](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-0-8176-4910-4)

The cosmological corridor between dissolution and sterile closure motivates what those requirements might concern. A computed viability kernel preserves only the supplied requirements. It cannot certify that the chosen register exhausts valuerhood or lushness.

Optimization can then occur within, or help refine, a justified corridor. GE need not prohibit optimization in principle. Its objection is to treating an incomplete objective as license to destroy the conditions under which error could be discovered and corrected.

Preserving every option is neither possible nor necessarily desirable. Meaningful action commits resources and excludes alternatives. The task is to discriminate generative commitment from damaging closure, including cases where commitment is necessary to create capabilities that no uncommitted policy can attain.

## 6. Omega Decision Theory

Omega Decision Theory is the project's current name for a staged decision architecture:

| Layer | Role | What it does not supply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ODT0 | Check that justification is soundly represented and that a proposed action preserves the declared corridor. | The moral legitimacy of the requirements or the right to impose them. |
| ODT1 | Compare licensed outcomes under a specified ordering, retaining unresolved comparisons. | A uniquely correct universal ordering of value. |
| ODT2 | Apply explicitly supplied arbitration information when a finite frontier remains. | Authority merely because an arbitration rule returns an answer. |

This structure can retain uncertainty, incomparability, and the absence of a licensed action. It is not yet a complete general decision theory with established treatment of logical dependence, acausal interaction, or every issue addressed by other decision-theoretic programs.

Its strongest contribution is conditional discipline: a policy claiming to preserve something must preserve it under the stipulated semantics, and a justification in an abstract model must remain valid in the concrete system to which it is applied.

## 7. Power, plurality, and containment

A competent plurality can pool resources, information, experimentation, and enforcement. Its aggregate resources may outweigh those of a defector, but this depends on the starting distribution, coordination costs, timing, and available opportunities. There is no theorem that plurality has more effective power simply because it is plural.

The project previously explored singleton fragility as a balancing mechanism. V2 treats it as one regime in a larger phase space. When asymmetry is manageable, environmental complexity and collective response may expose an excisive controller's weaknesses. Under severe asymmetry, the controller may neutralize those pressures or prevail before they matter.

The ethical fallback is a conditional argument for defensive containment by those whose continuation is threatened. It does not require demonstrating that the threatening agent secretly shares their values. It does require establishing the threat, the standing of the affected valuers, and a defensible relationship between the constraint imposed and the continuation it protects.

Containment is not automatically possible, and successful enforcement does not confer moral authority. The analogy to a physical singularity describes breakdown of a balancing regime; it is not a gravitational prediction.

## 8. The positive offer to AI development

The proposed benefit is sustained capability under novelty. Systems that retain correction, usable variety, and the capacity to revise their own models may reach useful solutions that premature commitment or destructive proxy optimization would make inaccessible.

For a laboratory, this suggests preserving reusable experimental infrastructure, maintaining genuinely different hypotheses long enough to discriminate them, and building agents that can recognize when their current representation no longer covers the task. These can support discovery and adaptation as well as constrain failure.

The incentive remains conditional. Maintaining options has costs; specialization can win; a short race can reward a policy that is poor over a longer horizon. GE offers a hypothesis about durable capability, not a demonstrated competitive advantage for every deployment.

A concrete demonstration would compare matched agents on tasks where actions alter later capabilities. The relevant outputs would include achieved performance, adaptation to changed conditions, and whether failures remain recoverable. A favorable result would support that tested capability claim, not prove a universal ethics. The Off-Switch Game is an adjacent example of incentives for retaining correction under uncertainty, under its own assumptions. [Hadfield-Menell and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.08219)

## 9. What can bind a more capable reasoner

GE seeks arguments that remain coherent when an agent understands more than their authors. If the agent accepts the relevance of valuer continuation, it should not be able to evade a valid structural constraint through renaming, unsound compression, or fabricated recovery. Better knowledge may overturn a particular approximation while preserving the underlying demand for accurate justification.

An orthogonal agent may reject the normative commitment altogether. Argument alone does not guarantee motivation. Human adoption therefore matters: it can shape the objectives, institutions, training material, and early conditions under which more powerful systems develop.

The practical purpose is to make the conditional argument clear enough to evaluate and robust enough to survive changes in model and scale. Cosmology can explain its place in a larger vision, but it is not a prerequisite for that argument.

# 06 — Theology and Divine Completion

## 1. What this reconstruction attempts

The theological branch asks how a completed, internally differentiated reality could support concepts such as unity, creation, eternity, providence, communion, and judgment. It also asks where those reconstructions stop.

The method begins with metaphysical commitments and investigates their entailments. It does not infer a personal God merely because a mathematical object is complete, nor claim that physics has measured the ultimate ground of existence.

The originating image is Wolframian in its relational unity and block-Everettian in its completion. One reality contains many situated organizations through its internal structure. The question is how far that picture can carry inherited theological language without making the language promise more than the picture establishes.

## 2. Formal description, manifestation, and ultimate reality

Three distinctions are useful. **Formal description** is the structure expressed by a model. **Manifest reality** is the actuality that the description concerns, including phenomenal occurrence if that is not exhausted by the model. **Ultimate reality** is whatever finally constitutes or grounds that actuality.

These are distinctions in the inquiry. They do not posit three substances or three spatially separate worlds. Formal closure could make an adequate formal account exhaustive of actuality. A dual-aspect or other account could preserve a distinction without adding an external realm.

For continuity with the earlier theology, $G^*$ denotes God-as-such or the ultimate ground named under apophatic restraint; $D_\alpha$ denotes actuality manifest under a specified grammar; and $\mathfrak F(D_\alpha)$ denotes its formal description. The notation does not establish that $G^*$ is distinct from the whole it grounds, personal, conscious, or necessary.

The central epistemic asymmetry is straightforward. We encounter situated manifestation and build descriptions from within it. We do not acquire an external vantage merely by quantifying over a completed model.

## 3. The veil and the approach toward a summa

The **apophatic veil** marks the limit of justified positive description. It should be distinguished from Rawls's ethical use of “veil of ignorance.” Here it concerns what finite, situated inquiry can establish about reality and its ground.

Some ignorance is ordinary and potentially reducible: unknown laws, incomplete models, limited observations. A deeper underdetermination arises when rival accounts agree on every accessible consequence. No empirical test available under those conditions can distinguish them.

An alleged reality beyond physics is not automatically protected from investigation. If a proposal specifies detectable effects, it makes empirical commitments. If it specifies none, its assessment proceeds through consistency, explanatory adequacy, and metaphysical argument rather than a purported measurement beyond all measurement.

The project narrows the veil by identifying what an adequate account must accommodate: the existence of awareness, stable relations, situated access, internal consequence, and whatever further facts investigation establishes. It can also expose contradictions in particular reconstructions. It cannot conclude that the remaining compatible account is unique unless that uniqueness is demonstrated.

Thus the framework approaches a summa in breadth and coherence while leaving ultimate exhaustiveness unclaimed. This is not a formal convergence theorem. More formal precision can clarify the boundary without abolishing it.

Pseudo-Dionysius is a central neighbor for the discipline of affirming and withholding theological predicates. Omega gives that discipline a contemporary role at the interface between formal description, phenomenal actuality, and ultimate explanation. [Pseudo-Dionysius, *Mystical Theology* and *Divine Names*](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/rolt/dionysius.html)

## 4. Unity, simplicity, and aseity

One fundamental reality can be internally differentiated without being assembled from ontologically independent ingredients. Under this reading, “atoms of space” are positions within relational organization. Mathematical decomposition does not itself settle the direction of metaphysical dependence.

This supports a reconstruction of simplicity as fundamental unity. Strict classical divine simplicity makes a stronger claim: no real composition of parts, subject and attributes, essence and existence, or other metaphysical constituents. A relationally differentiated whole need not satisfy that criterion. Aquinas's doctrine is therefore a precise comparison point rather than a standard silently adopted and then declared fulfilled. [Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae*, I, question 3](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1003.htm)

**Aseity** concerns independence of existence from anything else. It is not self-causation. **Necessity** concerns whether the reality could fail to exist. Neither follows just from there being one kind of substance.

The preferred commitment is stronger than homogeneity: one fundamental reality whose differentiation and consequences are intrinsic, requiring no external ground or operator. That is a coherent candidate for aseity. Its independence is a metaphysical commitment whose justification remains under investigation.

Wolfram's all-rules argument supports a nearby move by treating consequences as belonging to formal structure without an external executor. The transition from formal inevitability to actual existence is the remaining philosophical step. Omega leaves open whether that step can be completed, including for awareness. [Wolfram, *Why Does the Universe Exist?*](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2021/04/why-does-the-universe-exist-some-perspectives-from-our-physics-project/)

Spinoza provides a major neighbor for substance monism; Plotinus for the relation between unity and multiplicity. Their accounts differ from one another and from this proposal. They locate the question without supplying a shared doctrine we can inherit wholesale. [Spinoza, *Ethics*, Part I](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3800); [Plotinus, *Enneads*, especially V](https://classics.mit.edu/Plotinus/enneads.html)

## 5. Logos and creation through perspective

**Logos** is used here for intelligible articulation: the relations under which determinate structure becomes describable. The provisional grammar of distinction, relation, and orientation identifies what our formal representations repeatedly require. It is not an established unique grammar of all being.

This usage acknowledges the Johannine Logos and the philosophical tradition around it. Identifying the technical articulation with a divine person would require an additional theological argument. The packet does not derive Trinitarian doctrine from a tuple of primitives.

At the creaturely level, perspective participates in individuation. A boundary organizes exchanges, distinguishes internal from external relations, and can support a continuing locus of action and awareness. Creation in this reconstructive sense is the emergence or specification of a finite organization within the whole.

Such individuation is **non-excisive** when it creates real differentiation without treating the creature as a severed fragment of ultimate substance. The boundary can preserve dependence and exchange. This is compatible with the broader intuition that unity expresses itself through plurality.

The analogy is adjacent to hylomorphic and emanationist accounts but proves neither. It also does not establish creation ex nihilo in the classical sense. The correspondence concerns how finite organization can be real within an encompassing unity.

## 6. Eternity, knowledge, power, and providence

Eternity receives the clearest reconstruction. A completed whole does not successively acquire its internal history. Its inhabitants nevertheless encounter different times from different situations. Aquinas's treatment of eternity is a relevant comparison, while the block model supplies a different route to non-successive totality. [Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae*, I, question 10](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1010.htm)

Other attributes require further bridges:

| Attribute | Structural reconstruction | Further question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knowledge | The whole contains the relations and records of its history. | Is there a subject that knows them? Containment is not cognition. |
| Power | All physically admissible capacities and consequences belong to the specified reality. | Does one agent command them? Possibility is not control. |
| Providence | Local actions occupy an intelligible completed order. | Is the order intentional, beneficent, or directed toward a good? |
| Pure actuality | The whole does not wait externally for its internal events to become present. | Is this equivalent to the classical elimination of potency? That is not established. |

The distinctions prevent formal completion from being mistaken for a proof of the divine attributes. They also identify where a stronger theological account would have to contribute something.

## 7. Awareness and divine participation

Universal habitation proposes awareness at every qualifying perspective. It does not, without an integration principle, establish that all those occurrences belong to one phenomenal subject.

A stronger theological reading proposes that awareness is a divine property expressed through finite perspectives, perhaps one awareness differently situated throughout reality. Under that reading, divinity participates in the suffering as well as the flourishing of creatures. This changes the picture of an entirely insulated creator imposing costs on others.

It does not by itself answer the problem of evil. Participation in suffering does not establish that the suffering is justified, necessary, or preferable to alternatives. Nor does the hypothesis supply a physical test unless the proposed correspondence entails distinguishable effects.

The theological speculation is therefore compatible with the preferred habitation picture, while remaining more specific than it. The framework can retain the possibility without making GE depend on it.

## 8. Communion and generative unity

Communion is reconstructed as integration that sustains and enriches its participants' value-capable continuation. Coordination can reduce destructive friction while preserving meaningful differentiation, correction, and further generation.

The limiting image of laminar interaction is useful here. A maximally integrated plurality may implement the same effective organization as a singleton containing the requisite internal variety. Whether an isomorphism exists depends on the relations being preserved, including any eventually established phenomenal facts.

Terminal solitude belongs to the opposing tendency: unity secured through excision and loss of regenerative capacity. The moral distinction is not simply one versus many. A noisy plurality can be destructive; an integrated unity can be generative.

Love can be interpreted as active concern for another's continuation and development within a shared world. Justice concerns the terms under which such continuation is protected and coordinated. Capable restraint gives these commitments practical force. These are proposed structural readings of virtues, not reductions of every religious use of those terms.

## 9. Judgment, redemption, heaven, and hell

Actions leave consequences in the continuation available to the actor and others. Judgment can be reconstructed as assessment of that structure; it need not initially mean an externally imposed penalty.

Redemption names the restoration of generative capability after damaging contraction. A recovery route can require changes in organization, policy, or relations. Before a genuine loss of recoverability, a process can reopen a future it had been closing.

Heaven is an image of sustained generative communion. Hell is an image of persistent closure or degradation beyond accessible recovery. These images help articulate the framework's limits but do not establish postmortem destinations, cosmic punishment, or a literal quantum-immortality mechanism.

In particular, the existence of some continuation in a quantum description does not guarantee a person's experienced survival or its quality. The framework acknowledges the whole supplied structure while asking what remains accessible and generative from a particular situation.

## 10. The unfinished theological claim

Omega offers an intelligible route from relational unity to situated multiplicity, and from situated multiplicity to questions of awareness, value, and generative integration. It supports conditional reconstructions of theological language and exposes the additional commitments each requires.

The unresolved questions are substantive: whether formal structure exhausts actuality; whether the whole is aseitic or necessary; how awareness corresponds to perspective; whether local awareness integrates into divine subjecthood; and whether generative richness supplies a defensible account of goodness.

The veil is not an excuse to answer those questions arbitrarily. It identifies where an account must remain a commitment, conjecture, or open question until a stronger argument becomes available.

# 07 — Formal Appendices

## A. Purpose and scope

Two models currently provide useful precision: a quantum construction for histories and perspectives, and a controlled transition model for continuation and recovery. Both borrow established mathematics. Their connection to valuerhood and phenomenality remains an additional task.

The formulas below do not derive quantum mechanics from awareness, derive morality from selection, or solve global optimization. They make a restricted realization of the framework explicit and identify where its open concepts would enter.

## B. A minimal quantum block

Take a finite-dimensional closed quantum model, including the relevant apparatus and environment:

$$
\mathcal Q=(\mathcal H,|\Psi_0\rangle,U),\qquad
\langle\Psi_0|\Psi_0\rangle=1,
$$

$$
U(t,s)U(s,r)=U(t,r),\qquad
i\hbar\partial_tU(t,s)=\widehat H(t)U(t,s).
$$

Here $\widehat H$ is the specified Hamiltonian. A mixed initial state can be used with traces. The model supplies a temporal/background structure; it is not a quantum-gravity construction.

The block interpretation treats the entire model as specified. Its time parameter locates internal relations. Nothing in these equations requires a universal present moving through them, and nothing in them proves that the block interpretation is uniquely correct.

### B.1 Multiway amplitudes

Insert orthonormal resolutions of identity between propagators $U_k=U(t_k,t_{k-1})$:

$$
\langle f|U_N\cdots U_1|i\rangle
=\sum_{z_1,\ldots,z_{N-1}}
\prod_{k=1}^{N}\langle z_k|U_k|z_{k-1}\rangle,
\quad z_0=i,\ z_N=f.
$$

This is a finite multiway representation with complex edge weights. Regrouping terms or inserting a genuine resolution of identity leaves the propagator unchanged. Thus valid refinement does not manufacture physical weight. Creating another actual physical channel changes the model and is a different operation.

### B.2 Histories and decoherence

Choose exhaustive orthogonal alternatives $P^{(k)}_a$ at selected times. In the Heisenberg picture, define

$$
P^{(k)}_a(t_k)=U(t_k,t_0)^\dagger P^{(k)}_aU(t_k,t_0),
\qquad
C_\alpha=P^{(n)}_{\alpha_n}(t_n)\cdots P^{(1)}_{\alpha_1}(t_1).
$$

For a set $B$ of histories,

$$
C_B=\sum_{\alpha\in B}C_\alpha,
\qquad |\Psi_B\rangle=C_B|\Psi_0\rangle,
\qquad w(B)=\|\Psi_B\|^2.
$$

The decoherence functional is

$$
D(\alpha,\beta)=\langle\Psi_\beta|\Psi_\alpha\rangle.
$$

For disjoint sets $B,C$,

$$
w(B\cup C)=w(B)+w(C)
+2\operatorname{Re}\langle\Psi_B|\Psi_C\rangle.
$$

Off-diagonal decoherence makes these weights additive on the chosen history family. Approximate decoherence requires an error assessment for the coarse events actually used; many individually small interference terms can accumulate. These class-operator constructions are borrowed from decoherent-histories quantum mechanics. [Hartle, *Decoherent Histories Quantum Mechanics Starting with Records of What Happens*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.04145)

### B.3 Records and situated conditioning

An ideal physical record at time $t$ is represented by a projector $R_p$. If $w_p=\|R_p|\Psi(t)\rangle\|^2>0$, define

$$
|\Psi_{p,t}\rangle=\frac{R_p|\Psi(t)\rangle}{\sqrt{w_p}}.
$$

For an ideal sequential record measurement, or the corresponding suitably recorded histories,

$$
p(q,t'\mid p,t)=\|R_qU(t',t)|\Psi_{p,t}\rangle\|^2.
$$

The conditional state retains the environment and correlations; the label $p$ alone need not specify them. Conditioning describes the situation relative to a record and does not delete the global state.

Record projectors are physical modeling choices. An identification with Page-style awareness operators is not assumed.

## C. Exact future-relevant coarse graining

Let $\mathcal T$ be a specified class of accessible future protocols. Each protocol $\tau$ and its complete outcome record $r$ determine an effect $E_{\tau r}$ on a sufficiently complete initial state. Define

$$
\rho\sim_{\mathcal T}\sigma
\iff
\operatorname{Tr}(\rho E_{\tau r})
=\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma E_{\tau r})
\quad\text{for every }\tau,r.
$$

This is an equivalence relation. It formalizes compression that preserves all distinctions relevant to the declared protocols. With all global measurements available, it reduces to equality of density operators. Nontrivial compression therefore requires an actual restriction on the accessible questions.

Equal present local records do not guarantee this equivalence. Later interactions can reveal correlations that the present record omits. For environments with memory, a reduced state may need to be replaced by an adequate description of the multi-time process; process-tensor approaches are an appropriate technical neighbor. [Pollock and colleagues, *Operational Markov Condition for Quantum Processes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.09811)

An approximate version can bound statistical distinguishability over a horizon, but a pairwise tolerance relation need not be transitive. It cannot be silently treated as an exact quotient.

The awareness-centered conjecture asks whether an independently grounded awareness correspondence selects such a relevant protocol class or invariant. The operational equivalence does not establish that conjecture.

## D. The delayed-choice eraser

Use the ideal signal–idler state

$$
|\Psi\rangle=\frac{|0\rangle_s|0\rangle_i+e^{i\phi}|1\rangle_s|1\rangle_i}{\sqrt2},
\qquad \psi_j(x)=\langle x|U_s|j\rangle.
$$

The propagated modes are orthonormal globally but can overlap at a screen position. Tracing out the idler gives

$$
p(x)=\tfrac12(|\psi_0(x)|^2+|\psi_1(x)|^2).
$$

Measuring the idler in $|\pm\rangle=(|0\rangle\pm|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$ yields

$$
p(x,\pm)=\tfrac14|\psi_0(x)\pm e^{i\phi}\psi_1(x)|^2,
\qquad p(\pm)=\tfrac12.
$$

These are joint densities. The conditional densities are twice as large. The opposing interference terms cancel when outcomes are combined:

$$
p(x,+)+p(x,-)=p(x).
$$

For separated local measurement operators,

$$
(M_x\otimes I)(I\otimes N_b)=(I\otimes N_b)(M_x\otimes I).
$$

Completeness of the idler instrument gives

$$
\sum_b p(x,b)=\langle\Psi|(M_x^\dagger M_x\otimes I)|\Psi\rangle.
$$

Thus the local idler choice does not change the unsorted signal record. Delaying it changes no requirement for a consistent joint description. This ideal calculation captures the conditional-correlation feature of the quantum eraser, without reproducing every optical component of the experiment. [Kim and colleagues, *A Delayed Choice Quantum Eraser*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9903047); [Fankhauser, *Taming the Delayed Choice Quantum Eraser*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.07884)

If another environment retains path records $|e_0\rangle,|e_1\rangle$, let $g=\langle e_0|e_1\rangle$. The joint density becomes

$$
p(x,\pm)=\tfrac14\left[|\psi_0|^2+|\psi_1|^2
\pm2\operatorname{Re}(e^{i\phi}\psi_0^*\psi_1g)\right].
$$

At $g=0$, even these conditional fringes vanish. Removing a distinction from someone's description does not remove a physical record retained elsewhere.

For a finite diagnostic, set $\phi=0$ and measure both qubits in the $+,-$ basis. With row and column order $+,-$,

$$
P_{g=1}=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&0\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
P_{g=0}=\begin{pmatrix}1/4&1/4\\1/4&1/4\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The local marginals agree while the joint continuation differs. This is a direct illustration of why marginal summaries can conceal consequential coupling.

## E. What Planck's constant contributes

Where an action path integral is available, its schematic form is

$$
A(B)=\int_B e^{iS[\gamma]/\hbar}\,\mathcal D\gamma,
\qquad \Delta\phi=\Delta S/\hbar.
$$

The propagator and its regularization supply the measure and normalization. Planck's constant relates action to phase. It does not independently supply a measure of awareness or future value. [Feynman, *The Principle of Least Action*](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/II_19.html)

Near a stationary path, one nondegenerate fluctuation coordinate may satisfy

$$
S(\xi)\simeq S_0+\tfrac12 k\xi^2,
\qquad |\Delta\phi|\lesssim1
\ \Rightarrow\ |\xi|\lesssim\sqrt{2\hbar/|k|}.
$$

This is a local phase-variation estimate in a quadratic approximation. It is not a hard cutoff: other regions contribute, higher-order terms can matter, and distinguishability depends on the full experiment.

Coarse graining can yield discrete effective alternatives without deriving physical quantization. Representation consistency here follows from completeness and linearity. No separate anti-manufacturing axiom, universal minimum length, or quantized-awareness law is needed for the calculation.

## F. A finite continuation and recovery model

For a fully observed model, let $X$ be a finite state space, $A(x)$ its allowed actions, and $\mathrm{Succ}(x,a)$ a nonempty set of successors for each enabled action. Let $S\subseteq X$ encode declared continuation requirements. Partial observation requires an adequate information-state construction or a separate restriction on policies; the statewise formula below cannot silently provide unavailable information to the controller.

Define

$$
\mathcal F(K)=\{x\in S:\exists a\in A(x),\ \mathrm{Succ}(x,a)\subseteq K\}.
$$

Starting with $K_0=S$, iterate $K_{n+1}=\mathcal F(K_n)$. The sequence decreases and stabilizes in the finite model at the greatest fixed point $K_*$. From each state in $K_*$, selecting a witnessing action keeps every modeled successor in $K_*$. This is the standard controlled-invariance structure behind a robust corridor.

The quantifiers matter. Existence of some favorable path is weaker than existence of a policy succeeding against every allowed disturbance. Individually feasible requirements may also fail jointly.

For a declared fact $F\subseteq X$, bounded robust recovery can be written

$$
\operatorname{Rec}^{\mathrm{rob}}_T(x,F)
\iff \exists\pi\ \forall\omega\ \exists t\le T:
x_t^{\pi,\omega}\in F,
$$

where $\pi$ uses only the permitted observations and actions, and $\omega$ ranges over allowed disturbances. Replacing the robust quantification with a favorable-path condition produces a different recovery notion. Resource and repair restrictions belong in the model.

Increasing a repair horizon can restore a previously missing route. Increasing an assessment horizon can expose delayed foreclosure. No horizon-free moral verdict follows from a bounded recovery calculation.

## G. Abstraction, joint structure, and retained project results

A claimed preservation result in an abstraction must transfer to the concrete system. An abstraction that erases a relevant distinction can manufacture apparent recovery or compatibility. Exact aggregation of a stochastic process also preserves only the observations and targets covered by its assumptions.

The earlier packet reports finite witnesses distinguishing pairwise from joint compatibility, local from joint recovery, and locally successful policies from a single robust policy. It also records conditional licensing and corridor results. This edition retains those bounded lessons; it does not report a fresh execution or proof audit of the repository.

Two source locations, pinned to the revision recorded in the earlier addendum, are the [coarse/fine nonreflection note](https://github.com/6ixpoolgames/Omega/blob/cc4c89ca6552ce52cc5f7392e3b95f0c24dfdf88/docs/research_notes/omega_theory/coarse_fine_nonreflection_v0.md) and the [marginal-coupling nonfactorization note](https://github.com/6ixpoolgames/Omega/blob/cc4c89ca6552ce52cc5f7392e3b95f0c24dfdf88/docs/research_notes/omega_theory/marginal_coupling_nonfactorization_v0.md). Their relevance is abstraction integrity and the information lost by separate summaries. The quantum diagnostic above is analogous in structure, not a proved translation of those repository results.

## H. The missing lushness functional

For an exhaustive decoherent family, $\sum_\alpha w_\alpha=1$. A candidate assessment such as

$$
L_{P,T}(\pi)=\sum_\alpha w_\alpha^{\pi}\,G_{P,T}(\alpha)
$$

therefore needs an additional function $G$ encoding the relevant continuation richness. This is an example of where the missing work enters, not a proposed final definition. If relationships among alternatives matter, a branchwise sum may already be too restrictive.

The requirements for a useful candidate include adequate physical and valuer correspondence, retention of consequential coupling, stated behavior under valid changes of presentation, and explicit horizon and aggregation choices. Scalarization remains possible if justified.

Information-geometric optimization is a technical neighbor for principled search on parameterized distributions. It supplies neither this missing functional nor a guarantee of attaining global optima in arbitrary spaces. The broader Alpha/Omega ambition remains an extension to investigate after the relevant structure is specified. [Ollivier, Arnold, Auger, and Hansen, *Information-Geometric Optimization*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.3708)
