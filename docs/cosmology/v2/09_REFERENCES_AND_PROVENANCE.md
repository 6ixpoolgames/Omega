# 09 — References and Provenance

## 1. How attribution works

Each source is included for a particular contribution. Proximity does not mean agreement with an author's entire system, and a newly recognized intellectual neighbor is not automatically a historical influence. The packet's claims about its own extensions remain separate from its descriptions of borrowed work.

Links lead to primary texts, author pages, or publication records. They are intended as compact reconstruction routes: a reader can identify the relevant tradition and pursue it without the packet reproducing a literature review.

## 3. Core source routes

### Reality and quantum structure

- **Hermann Minkowski — [*Space and Time*](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Space_and_Time_(Hermann_Minkowski)).** Used for the spacetime representation. The completed metaphysical reading is a further interpretation.
- **Hugh Everett III — [“Relative State” Formulation of Quantum Mechanics (1957)](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.29.454).** Used for relative states in a universal quantum description. Omega adds no new collapse law.
- **Stephen Wolfram — [The Wolfram Physics Project: A One-Year Update](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2021/04/the-wolfram-physics-project-a-one-year-update/).** Used for relational atoms of space and the multiway orientation. Its proposed fundamental physics remains a proposal here.
- **Stephen Wolfram — [Observer Theory](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2023/12/observer-theory/).** Used as a neighbor for bounded observers and effective descriptions; not a derivation of habitation.
- **Stephen Wolfram — [Why Does the Universe Exist?](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2021/04/why-does-the-universe-exist-some-perspectives-from-our-physics-project/).** Used for the all-rules and intrinsic-consequences argument. The actuality inference remains a metaphysical commitment.
- **James B. Hartle — [Decoherent Histories Quantum Mechanics Starting with Records of What Happens (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.04145).** Used for histories, records, and the conditions under which probabilities apply.

### Awareness, experience, and identity

- **Don N. Page — [Sensible Quantum Mechanics: Are Only Perceptions Probabilistic? (1995)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9506010); [Mindless Sensationalism: A Quantum Framework for Consciousness (2001)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0108039).** Primary precedent for positive-operator experiential measures. Omega's proposed perspective and continuation correspondence is an additional research program.
- **Carlo Rovelli — [Relational Quantum Mechanics (1996)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9609002).** Used for relational indexing, without identifying measurement with consciousness.
- **David Chalmers — [Facing Up to the Problem of Consciousness (1995)](https://consc.net/papers/facing.pdf).** Used to locate the formal–phenomenal explanatory question.
- **Derek Parfit — [Reasons and Persons, Part III](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/reasons-and-persons-9780198249085).** A neighbor for continuity and identity under branching or division.
- **Saṃyutta Nikāya 22.59 — [The Characteristic of Not-Self, translated by Bhikkhu Sujato](https://suttacentral.net/sn22.59/en/sujato).** A philosophical neighbor for questioning a fixed substantial self; not empirical evidence for a multiverse.

### Persistence, control, and generativity

- **Charles Darwin — [On the Origin of Species](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1228).** Foundation for the selection engine. Selection does not by itself supply a moral ordering.
- **W. Ross Ashby — [An Introduction to Cybernetics (1956)](https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf).** Foundation for regulatory variety. The relevant variety is relative to disturbances, responses, and acceptable outcomes.
- **Francisco Varela, Humberto Maturana, and Ricardo Uribe — [Autopoiesis: The Organization of Living Systems, Its Characterization and a Model (1974)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0303264774900318).** Neighbor for organizational self-maintenance.
- **Michael Levin — [Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere (2022)](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnsys.2022.768201).** Neighbor for investigating agency and cognition across scales and embodiments.
- **Stuart Kauffman — [The Adjacent Possible](https://www.edge.org/conversation/stuart_a_kauffman-the-adjacent-possible), alongside *Investigations*.** Neighbor for capabilities that open further effective possibilities.
- **Alexander Klyubin, Daniel Polani, and Chrystopher Nehaniv — [2008 paper on empowerment](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0004018).** Candidate formal neighbor for action-dependent future influence; not a measure of moral value.
- **Alexander Wissner-Gross and Cameron Freer — [Causal Entropic Forces (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.110.168702).** Candidate mathematical neighbor for future accessibility; its objective is not identified with lushness.
- **Karl Friston — [The Free-Energy Principle: A Unified Brain Theory? (2010)](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn2787).** Further reading for the self-maintenance and inference branch. V2 does not use it as a universal premise about agency.

### Ethics, option value, and decision

- **Kenneth Arrow and Anthony Fisher — [Environmental Preservation, Uncertainty, and Irreversibility (1974)](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1883074).** Direct foundation for the option-preservation intuition under uncertainty.
- **Amartya Sen — [Equality of What?](https://sen.scholars.harvard.edu/publications/equality-what).** Neighbor for assessing capabilities. Omega's substrate-neutral futures inquiry extends beyond this human ethical setting.
- **David Hume — [A Treatise of Human Nature, III.i.1](https://davidhume.org/texts/t/3/1/1).** Marks the normative bridge that structural description alone does not cross.
- **Jean-Pierre Aubin — [Viability Theory](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-0-8176-4910-4).** Foundation for continuation under specified constraints; the linked edition is the 2009 reissue.
- **Hans Jonas — [The Imperative of Responsibility](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Imperative_of_Responsibility.html?id=sRP3uJkxydQC).** Neighbor for responsibility under technological power and for future life.
- **Dylan Hadfield-Menell, Anca Dragan, Pieter Abbeel, and Stuart Russell — [The Off-Switch Game](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.08219).** A conditional model of retaining human correction under uncertainty, not proof of GE's wider incentives.
- **Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics*; Immanuel Kant, *Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals*; John Stuart Mill, *Utilitarianism*.** Comparison traditions for virtue, duty, and consequential assessment. They are not being claimed as one doctrine under different names.

### Theology and metaphysical unity

- **Plotinus — [Enneads, especially V](https://classics.mit.edu/Plotinus/enneads.html).** Neighbor for unity and multiplicity.
- **Pseudo-Dionysius — [Divine Names and Mystical Theology](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/rolt/dionysius.html).** Principal neighbor for apophatic discipline.

- **Baruch Spinoza — [Ethics, Parts I and III](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3800).** Neighbors for substance monism and conatus, with different roles in Chapters 06 and 04.
- **Gospel of John 1:1–18.** Scriptural provenance for the Logos vocabulary. The technical articulation in this packet does not establish its personal or doctrinal interpretation.

### Additional mathematical routes

## 4. The project's own provenance

The project developed through a sequence of intuitions about spaces of meaning, geometric intelligence, persistence, and continuation. Gradient Ethics made value-capable futures and irreversible stakes central. Omega then became a laboratory for finite examples, abstractions, recovery conditions, compatibility, and decision procedures. Alpha generalized the language of continuation. The theological branch returned to the larger questions of reality, perspective, and awareness when narrower formal work ceased to capture enough of the motivating object.

That sequence is recorded in the earlier packet's lineage account. V2 has reviewed that packet and the two September addenda; it is not a claim to have freshly audited every underlying conversation, source manuscript, or proof file.

The originator supplied the persistent intuitive object and repeatedly corrected formulations that changed its meaning. AI systems served as drafting partners, literature guides, and formal prostheses. They also produced overclaims and definitions that sometimes made a thin result appear to answer a larger question. Retaining such experiments as records did not constitute endorsement of their interpretation.

This history matters to the method. An intuition can be imprecise while still distinguishing a faithful formalization from an unfaithful one. The remedy is to specify the translation and its losses, not assume that the first tractable formal object must be what the originator meant.

The [Omega repository](https://github.com/6ixpoolgames/Omega) remains the source for its own proof-bearing artifacts.
