# 01 — The physical structure of possibility

## The physical bearer

Omega begins with a physical present and what follows from it under the native laws. The present contains material arrangements, fields, correlations, resources and the processes that will act on them. Its continuation includes their lawful development, the alternatives that development admits, their physical weights, and the relations through which one development enables or obstructs another. The **futuresfield** names this structure of physical possibility. It is the physical bearer of the property called lushness.

Possibility already has a physical bearer. A catalyst, a shared reservoir, an interfering amplitude and an embodied decision process alter continuation through their actual relations. No additional force has to steer the world toward greater possibility for these relations to exist. The further inquiry is how to characterize their structure and effective weighted breadth. Establishing the bearer makes that inquiry concrete; it does not already provide a universal scalar measure.

The programme's organizing picture is one internally differentiated reality. Molecules, organisms, clocks and observers are organizations within that reality. Their boundaries express physical relations and useful levels of description. This monist orientation motivates looking for a common continuation structure before identifying particular choosers, purposes or values. It is an interpretive commitment; the finite mathematical constructions below need only their declared physical model and state.

The starting point includes passive and active processes alike. Gas, thermal fluctuations and coherent quantum regions belong alongside machinery, ecologies and deliberating agents. Selection is itself physical: sensing, switching, competition and cooperation change what follows. A menu of tasks for an already selected agent can later describe one interface to this field. It cannot supply the field's foundation.

## A sufficient present and native law

A physical model specifies degrees of freedom, lawful interactions, spatial and causal incidence, constraints and boundaries. A **sufficient present** supplies the state needed to continue that model. Sufficiency is relative to the claimed physics: an effective model may legitimately omit microscopic variables, but must retain the information its own continuation law requires.

For a finite closed quantum adapter, write

\[
\mathsf P=(\mathcal H,\{\mathcal A(R)\},\mathscr G,H(t)),
\qquad \rho_\Sigma\geq0,\quad\operatorname{Tr}\rho_\Sigma=1.
\]

Here \(\mathcal H\) is the Hilbert space, \(\mathcal A(R)\) specifies observable structure associated with physical regions, \(\mathscr G\) records constraints and physical identifications, and \(H(t)\) is the Hamiltonian. The state \(\rho_\Sigma\) belongs to a present section \(\Sigma\), assigned coordinate time zero in this representation. Native propagation satisfies

\[
i\hbar\,\partial_tU(t,s)=H(t)U(t,s),\qquad
\rho_t=U(t,0)\rho_\Sigma U(t,0)^\dagger.
\]

The specification retains the propagators throughout the interval, their local interaction structure and the state on which they act. Symbolically,

\[
\Omega_{\Sigma,T}=
\bigl[\mathsf P,\rho_\Sigma,
\{U(t,s):0\leq s\leq t\leq T\}\bigr]_{\text{physical equivalence}}.
\]

The equivalence subscript requires a declared account of which descriptions represent the same physics. It is not a claim that a universal quotient has already been constructed. A passive basis change must transform the state, law, observable incidence and readout together. Arbitrarily rotating the state while holding the apparatus fixed generally changes the physical situation.

Environmental correlations, controllers, reservoirs and work sources enter the sufficient specification wherever they affect continuation. An imposed pulse schedule is a declared physical input; describing the apparatus as autonomous requires modeling what generates that schedule. A subsystem's reduced state usually supplies less information than the relevant joint state. Two fair bits can be independent, or perfectly correlated copies. Each individual bit has the same distribution in both cases, but a subsequent XOR is fair in the first and always zero in the second. Equal marginals therefore do not determine joint continuation. Quantum states add phase and entanglement to this same requirement to retain consequential relations.

The classical counterpart is equally direct. A sufficient state \(x\) and a native transition kernel \(K\) determine the probability of a resolved future configuration sequence:

\[
P_x(x_1,\ldots,x_n)=\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}K(x_j,x_{j+1}),
\qquad x_0=x.
\]

The reusable law at every reachable state matters, not just its first row. Two models can agree on their immediate transition from \(x\) and disagree after the next interaction. In continuous time, a generator supplies native rates and waiting times; \(e^{tQ}\) gives finite-time transition probabilities. Sampling that process at specified cuts is a restriction of its development. It can omit intervening events and should be described at that resolution.

## Complete development and the meaning of completion

Continuation concerns the complete development from a present. An endpoint alone can conceal the distinction between remaining idle and making a physical excursion followed by reversal. The endpoint states, or even the total endpoint channels, may agree while the intervening couplings differ. The carrier retains that difference. Whether a particular breadth readout assigns the developments different values is a further question; distinct structures can have equal effective size.

A classical multiway representation depicts states, lawful transitions and their weighted alternatives. Different prefixes can reach the same sufficient state. Their residual future can then share one computational representation, while a query from the original root retains both prefixes and their weights. Re-rooting at the common state asks about what follows from that new present. It adds no ancestral label to the physical state and gives no continuing credit to an earlier structure that has disappeared. The two questions agree through conditioning, not through deletion of the earlier development.

Compatible classical path laws marginalize across horizons: summing over the last state recovers the earlier path law because each row of \(K\) sums to one. Termination can be represented by an absorbing outcome that retains its weight. An unfinished calculation or an unexpanded computational frontier is different: it marks unresolved continuation, not physical termination. These distinctions let a finite representation approximate a larger object without manufacturing certainty at its boundary.

The **completed** viewpoint regards the development and its internal temporal relations together. It places no additional clock outside the world on which the whole must first be assembled. Internal change remains real: waiting, construction, damage and recovery occupy different positions in the physical relations. An infinite completion retains each finite restriction, including consequential delays. An asymptotic growth rate or terminal-state summary may discard those distinctions; that is a limitation of the summary.

The adopted block-Everett interpretation combines this completed viewpoint with the universal quantum description and its correlated relative alternatives [R7]. Global unitary evolution can be deterministic while relative records have Born-weighted alternatives. A quantum multiway diagram therefore needs amplitudes or equivalent coherent process structure; its drawn paths are not independent classical worlds to count. The block interpretation neither supplies a local hidden-variable explanation of entangled correlations nor adds controllable signalling outside the physical causal structure.

Completion is always completion under a declared account of admissibility. A physical model does not thereby contain every verbally describable scenario or every incompatible choice of laws. Formal admissibility, physical realization and phenomenal experience remain distinct questions. Alpha's wider ambition to relate forms of distinction, relation and oriented consequence belongs to the interpretive discussion in [chapter 07](07_ALPHA_OMEGA_INTERPRETATION.md).

## Composition carries the world forward

Composition joins developments through physical boundaries. A process leaves stocks depleted or replenished, apparatus occupied or released, records located somewhere, and correlations available to subsequent interactions. These residual conditions determine what can happen next. They are how organization enters continuation without an externally awarded organization bonus.

In the finite quantum model, coherent sequential composition obeys \(U(t_2,t_0)=U(t_2,t_1)U(t_1,t_0)\). At a classical resolved interface, one can retain an outcome together with the state it leaves behind. A subnormalized instrument kernel \(J_r(x,y)\) gives the probability of record \(r\) and residual state \(y\), starting at \(x\). A physically implemented second stage has

\[
J_{s\circ r}(x,z)=\sum_y J_r(x,y)J_s(y,z).
\]

If elapsed time and resource expenditures are resolved as well, composition also sums or integrates over their compatible splits. Preparation, switching, communication and disposal are physical stages in that accounting. A resource profile can retain work, material consumption, occupancy and returns in their own units; they need not be converted into a single utility coefficient. Failures and unfinished outcomes retain their actual residual states and costs.

Parallel composition requires the actual joint law. Two individually possible repairs can compete for the same fuel unit. Any two among three processes may fit within a resource stock while all three together do not. Pairwise compatibility therefore need not imply compatibility of the whole family. Likewise, reproducing each process's marginal output does not establish that their joint outputs and residual correlations have been reproduced. A claimed joint realization must specify one encompassing physical development in which the requirements hold together.

Independence is a special case with a physical justification. Independent synchronously updated classical systems use a product kernel and product root; independent continuous-time systems use the sum of their separately acting generators. Correlated preparations or interacting dynamics require the joint description even when local readouts happen to agree. A diagram with commuting endpoints is not, by itself, an independence certificate.

Control enters through the same construction. A protocol is a physical controller using records it can obtain, with an apparatus and a preparation process. A controlled kernel \(K(x,a,y)\) describes an exposed operational interface only to the extent those operations are physically realizable. Stochastic chemical reactions remain inside the native dynamics; naming them does not make them freely selectable actions. Adaptive control may respond to an accessible record, not to an analyst's hidden knowledge of the microstate.

A realization witness connects an operational claim to its controller, preparation, resources, boundary conditions and induced physical path law. This distinguishes a transformation permitted by physics, a ready apparatus that reliably performs it, and a route by which that apparatus can actually be obtained. If preparation succeeds with probability \(p\) and use then succeeds with conditional probability \(q\), the whole route succeeds with probability \(pq\). Readiness and deployable capability are different physical questions. Task sets and capability envelopes summarize such realizations; their labels do not define the futuresfield or supply its weights.

This compositional account supports the mechanism investigated in [chapter 02](02_ORGANIZATION_AND_GENERATIVITY.md): products of one development can change the accessibility and native weights of further developments under the same laws. Changes of access, weighted breadth and value remain separately identifiable claims.

## Physical frames and situated perspectives

A **physical frame** is a present hypersurface \(\Sigma\) together with its physical state. In a relativistic model it is an admissible spacelike section; a sufficient global initial-data formulation uses a suitable Cauchy surface where the spacetime and theory admit one. A finite model uses an update cut and its state as the corresponding simpler construction. The frame is physical boundary data from which continuation is considered.

Different admissible sections are related by the native dynamics and the appropriate transformations of their descriptions. This allows relative sectioning without a preferred universal simultaneity. A worldline at one event does not choose a unique global section. Massive trajectories admit proper-time descriptions; massless propagation follows null structure and supplies no rest frame. Relational quantum hypersurfaces provide a developed connection to this programme [R23]. A general relativistic quantum realization of the whole Omega construction remains a research task.

A **perspective** is situated access and influence within this structure: where a process couples, what affects it, what it can affect, and what correlations cross its boundary. A detector, gas in contact with a reservoir, an excited crystal and an organism each have situated continuation. Awareness and agency are further forms of organization, not prerequisites for having a perspective. Useful interfaces may be local regions or worldtubes rather than whole-universe slices. Their descriptions derive from the physical frame and its continuation.

A **frame description** represents, restricts or conditions that physical frame and development at a stated scope and resolution. It can specify a region, observable algebra, duration, boundary coupling and accessible records. A scientist can choose such a description without there being a conscious subject corresponding to it. Physical incidence and actual couplings justify its interpretation; an arbitrary mathematical factorization alone does not identify the apparatus or its access.

Consider a workshop. Its full present includes tools, stored materials, instructions, occupied machines and surrounding supplies. A worker encounters only part of that state. A coarse model may resolve tool functions while omitting molecular detail. The physical frame, the worker's perspective and the model's restriction are related but distinct. Adequacy depends on which consequences the restricted description preserves. The same method extends to molecules, cells and institutions when their effective variables support the continuation questions being asked.

## Situated weights, history and current access

The frame construction already supplies situated normalization. Under one classical law \(P\), a positive-probability record condition \(\alpha\) gives

\[
\mu_\alpha(E)=P(E\mid\alpha),\qquad
P(E\cap\alpha)=P(\alpha)\mu_\alpha(E).
\]

The encompassing description retains \(P(\alpha)\). Conditioning on a successful preparation is legitimate; forgetting its probability would change the claim about obtaining that preparation from an earlier frame. At every future horizon the conditional law still has total probability one. This says how physical weight is distributed, not that the effective breadth is one or that the future must be discounted. Horizons index restrictions of the same continuation.

Under a common law, nested conditioning obeys the tower identity. If \(\mathcal F_s\subseteq\mathcal F_t\) and \(Z\) is a fixed integrable readout,

\[
\mathbb E[\mathbb E[Z\mid\mathcal F_t]\mid\mathcal F_s]
=\mathbb E[Z\mid\mathcal F_s].
\]

The accumulated observation, action and memory history generates an increasing filtration \(\mathcal F_t\). The currently accessible information \(\mathcal G_t=\sigma(O_t,M_t)\), generated by present observations and accessible memory, need not increase. A lost record can remain in the encompassing history while becoming unavailable to the apparatus. Martingale reasoning for nested historical information does not automatically apply to current access after forgetting.

An analyst's archive therefore gives no free capability to the workshop. If the workshop has lost a design, recovering its use requires a physical retrieval route with its delay, coupling and resources. At the sufficient boundary, the past matters through the physical state it has produced. At a restricted interface, omitted environment and correlations can produce memory. Process tensors or quantum combs represent such multitime dependence at declared interfaces [R3]. They supplement an insufficient local description without making an extra ancestral narrative part of the complete physical present.

The **ultimate frame** names the intended encompassing closure in which local normalizations and situated comparisons belong to one physical account, with no further regression to another external weighting frame. It has no chooser outside the dynamics. This intended role is compatible with finite agents having incomplete knowledge and with the numerical ordering of the full field remaining unfinished.

## Focus, inference and physical intervention

A **focus** organizes the contributions connecting a situated boundary to a specified condition: for example, a prepared system and a detector effect. It can be a physical detector condition, a boundary constraint or a mathematical query. The elementary quantum construction pairs a state with an effect. For a channel \(\mathcal E\), state \(\rho\), and effect \(0\leq F\leq I\),

\[
p(F\mid\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}[F\mathcal E(\rho)]
=\operatorname{Tr}[\mathcal E^*(F)\rho].
\]

The dual map propagates the condition backward for inference while the state propagates forward. This equality asserts no physical rewriting of the earlier state by a later event. Intention and attention are possible physical processes within the model; they are not needed to define this pairing.

The operations associated with a focus have different meanings:

| Operation | Physical meaning and comparison |
|---|---|
| Passive redescription | Transform state, law, incidence and readout together; the same resolved physical answer is preserved. |
| Numerical subdivision or identity checkpoint | Recompose the same propagation coherently; no extra interaction or alternative has occurred. |
| Restriction or changed physical resolution | Ask a different declared question about the same development; the readout may change. |
| Conditioning | Retain the process law together with the record condition and its weight. |
| Actual interaction or measurement | Include the apparatus and operation that change the physical development. |
| Decoherence | Track physical correlations and the resulting suppression of interference in the relevant description. |
| Re-rooting | Use the new sufficient present and remaining law for a new residual question. |

A quantum instrument makes the measurement distinction precise. Completely positive outcome maps \(\mathcal M_r\) sum to a trace-preserving channel and give

\[
p(r)=\operatorname{Tr}\mathcal M_r(\rho),\qquad
\rho_r=\frac{\mathcal M_r(\rho)}{p(r)}\quad\text{when }p(r)>0.
\]

Keeping the unnormalized output retains both probability and residual state. Sequences of implemented instruments supply their own joint law. They do not assign simultaneous classical values to every incompatible measurement one could imagine. In a quantum eraser, sorting recorded outcomes by a correlated marker can reveal complementary conditional fringes whose weighted mixture reproduces the unconditional statistics. An actual reversal of a marker interaction is a different operation; leaked environmental records matter to whether interference can be restored.

## Coherent histories and physical records

Quantum histories offer a representation that retains interference across time [R1]. At declared cuts, use exhaustive orthogonal projector families and define

\[
C_\alpha=P^{(n)}_{a_n}U_{n,n-1}\cdots
P^{(1)}_{a_1}U_{1,0},\qquad
D_{\alpha\beta}=\operatorname{Tr}
(C_\alpha\rho_\Sigma C_\beta^\dagger).
\]

These class operators expand a coherent process. Their intermediate projectors do not assert physical measurements unless the corresponding interactions belong to the model. For a coarse event \(A\), first sum its amplitudes:

\[
C_A=\sum_{\alpha\in A}C_\alpha,\qquad
\mu(A)=\sum_{\alpha,\beta\in A}D_{\alpha\beta}.
\]

The off-diagonal terms matter. Destructive interference means \(\mu\) need not be additive or monotone under inclusion, even though \(D\) is positive semidefinite. The coherent carrier is consequently not already an ordinary positive volume measure. For the exhaustive projective expansions above, \(\sum_{\alpha\beta}D_{\alpha\beta}=1\) and \(\operatorname{Tr}D=1\). The latter identity is a property of this expansion class, not a normalization rule to impose on every more general quantum kernel.

Medium decoherence, \(D_{\alpha\beta}=0\) for distinct histories, suffices to give the family a classical law \(p_\alpha=D_{\alpha\alpha}\). It does not select that family as the unique physical description. An idle qubit in \(|0\rangle\) has one-cut Z probabilities \((1,0)\) and X probabilities \((1/2,1/2)\); both single-cut families are decoherent. Their effective numbers are one and two. A single family-independent scalar cannot equal both merely because both families satisfy decoherence. Physical family selection or explicit frame dependence is needed.

Actual record formation supplies additional structure [R2, R21]. In an exact pure-state fixture, orthogonal record projections can certify a proposed history family through

\[
R_h\psi_T=C_h\psi_\Sigma.
\]

This equation establishes the stated correlations. Their physical provenance still comes from locality, preparation and actual recording interactions; abstract projectors can satisfy the equation without identifying a native recording mechanism. Stable later records can refine transported parent records on the relevant subspace. Such a regime supports classical history extension. Approximate records require a declared accuracy, resolution and stability interval.

Records provide a classical restriction of the field, while coherent regions remain in the field. Uncomputation can invalidate a permanent local-record description without erasing the intervening evolution. A common global unitary preserves inner products; a conditional eraser does not merge orthogonal universal states. Similarly, global purity gives zero state entropy without making relational continuation trivial. These distinctions set concrete requirements for quantum breadth, developed in [chapter 03](03_LUSHNESS_AND_QUANTUM_BREADTH.md).

## Faithful descriptions and compression

A reduced description earns its use by preserving the continuation relations being compared. Exact predictive equivalence is meaningful relative to a declared family \(\mathcal T\) of admissible physical tests:

\[
p\equiv_{\mathcal T}q
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Law}(\tau[p])=\operatorname{Law}(\tau[q])
\quad\text{for every }\tau\in\mathcal T.
\]

Enlarging the tester family can separate classes but cannot merge ones already distinguished. A longer horizon gives this refinement when it includes the shorter tests; observing only a later endpoint can instead lose distinctions through mixing. Substitution inside a larger process requires the tests to include its relevant contexts and compositions. Equality of isolated output marginals does not suffice.

This is an optional predictive compression. It does not identify physical copies, histories or people, or remove noise that a bounded test cannot detect. A distinction outside the tester domain remains in the encompassing object. Approximation likewise needs an error statement. On a common interface, \(d_{\mathcal T}(p,q)=\sup_{\tau\in\mathcal T}\operatorname{TV}(\operatorname{Law}(\tau[p]),\operatorname{Law}(\tau[q]))\) is a pseudometric. The relation \(d_{\mathcal T}\leq\epsilon\) is generally not transitive and hence is not itself an exact quotient. If one compressed prediction approximates each of two laws within \(\epsilon\), the triangle inequality bounds their separation by \(2\epsilon\).

For a deterministic encoding \(q\) and update \(F\), faithful updating requires an abstract update \(\bar F\) satisfying \(q\circ F=\bar F\circ q\). The condition has stochastic and quantum counterparts. For a classical Markov partition, equal total transition probability into every block from all states within a block gives exact lumpability. Physical costs, accessible records and preparation conditions must also factor through any quotient used to represent them.

A simple example shows why these checks matter. Three signs \(X,Y,Z\) with law \(P_\theta(x,y,z)=(1+\theta xyz)/8\), \(-1\leq\theta\leq1\), all have zero means and identity covariance. The reversible transformation \((X,Y,Z)\mapsto(X,Y,XYZ)\) produces output mean \((0,0,\theta)\). The same input summary would have to predict different outputs under the same gate. Retaining the triple correlation repairs that missing distinction for this example. This demonstrates closure under relevant future operations, rather than a reason to award correlation an intrinsic score.

## Comparing presents and measuring breadth

A comparison declares what is held common: law, resources, conserved sector, boundaries, duration, physical resolution and calibration. Roots within one invariant sector answer a different preparation question from roots that enter different invariant sectors. Both can be legitimate, provided the physical differences responsible for access to those regimes remain in the accounting. The same applies to thermal references: equilibrium is a specified preparation under a law, and can itself contain assembled structure.

Lushness names effective weighted extent. The adopted classical calibration is complete-history perplexity, \(L_{\mathrm{cl}}=\exp H(p)\), at a declared physical resolution. Independent fair binary developments give \(2^n\) after \(n\) steps even though their probabilities sum to one. Deterministic exact classical continuation gives one. These are calibrations of effective number, not a requirement to construct an additive geometric volume first. The full configuration and its relations remain available even when this summary ties different structures.

A direct functional, profile or partial comparison is an admissible research target. A base measure, manifold or reference trace is one possible route, not a prerequisite. Pursuing that route requires a physical prescription preserved under equivalent descriptions and compatible with composition. Generativity is investigated as a physical cause of changes to weighted breadth under the native law; gas and noise remain eligible outcomes of the comparison.

The outstanding quantum problem is specific: relate physically justified descriptions and find a breadth comparison compatible with their equivalences, composition and classical restrictions. A coherent region lacking a record certificate has not thereby acquired extent one or zero. The [compatibility audit](../../quantum/COMPATIBILITY_AUDIT.md) specifies these requirements and their test fixtures; it remains a proposed audit, not a completed universal construction.

## Local field dynamics as the physical foundation

Local quantum field dynamics is the preferred next physical adapter. Its fields, interactions, symmetries and causal constraints supply propagation, binding, record formation and recombination before any breadth calculation is chosen. Where a wavefunctional representation is available, \(\Psi_\Sigma[\phi]\) expresses a state on a section. More generally, an algebraic state is a normalized positive functional on physical observables. A local QFT state need not have the finite density-matrix form used by a circuit fixture [R4].

Observable nets, histories, process tensors and real-time Schwinger–Keldysh methods are complementary tools for describing this dynamics [R1, R3–R5]. Schwinger–Keldysh methods retain amplitude and conjugate-amplitude evolution; their field-integration notation is not already a positive possibility measure. Process tensors expose operational slots for querying a reduced process. When no additional interaction occurs, those slots are contracted accordingly. Hypothetical operations do not become extra native alternatives just because the representation can accept them.

Finite circuits remain exact calibration fixtures. A small regulated scalar or oscillator lattice would add explicit local field dynamics, with spatial spacing, occupation cutoff, time units, boundary conditions, preparation, environment and external work declared. No particular Hamiltonian has yet been adopted for that next step. A fixed-background lattice does not establish a continuum limit, Lorentz covariance, refoliation invariance or quantum gravity. Typical continuum local QFT algebras are type III, so an ordinary finite matrix trace cannot simply be carried into the continuum [R4]. Resolution dependence and regulator control are substantive mathematical obligations.

Thermal gas and QFT vacuum are different physical references. Neither provides an automatic zero of consequence or a preferred breadth baseline. The field programme can investigate coupling, access, composition and emergence before the universal extent problem is solved. Its next mathematical step is the compatibility audit on existing fixtures; a larger field adapter should supply the physical structure that an identified question requires.

# 02 — Organization that changes what follows

## Outcomes that become enabling conditions

A catalyst changes subsequent reaction rates. A record guides construction.
A tool makes components for another tool. A community maintains the material
and informational conditions through which its members discover and build.
Each exemplifies a recursive relation: an outcome becomes a condition of
further outcomes.

**Generativity** is the mechanism proposed to expand lushness through such
organization. Its investigation follows two steps: identify construction,
maintenance and changes in enabled transformations, then measure their effect
on weighted continuation. The mechanism and its breadth consequence are thus
independently examinable.

## Access under the native law

Construction makes lawful transformations more accessible. If a nebula can
develop into a civilization, that civilization's developments already belong
to the nebula's encompassing continuation. At the later present, their
conditional accessibility has changed: their reliability, timing, material
requirements and dependence on favorable accidents.

The workshop embodies this change. Prepared tools and usable records can make
transformations routine that dispersed materials would rarely undergo within
the same interval. Even where eventual support agrees, different barriers and
couplings give different finite-time laws.

An access claim specifies a physical route. It includes preparation, the records
available to the apparatus, operating resources, elapsed time and the state left
for subsequent use. If preparation succeeds with probability p and use succeeds
with conditional probability q, the whole route succeeds with probability pq.
Repeated setup attempts change that probability only if failed attempts leave
retryable states and enough time and feedstock remain. A list of successful
programs is useful to an analyst; deploying the right one requires an embodied
selector with the necessary information.

The physical bill is a profile. Work, feedstock consumption, peak occupied space,
apparatus time and returned stocks have different composition rules. Sequential
expenditures can add while peak space takes a maximum; stocks can replenish.
Taking the best entry in each column from different implementations need not
describe any implementable combination. Access machinery retains the whole
witness so later processes inherit its actual residual conditions.

Access and immediate breadth have distinct roles. Making a fair binary outcome
certain reduces its branching entropy. A reliable constructor can nonetheless
enable broader later developments. The horizon profile reveals whether that
later gain occurs and how it compares with the investment required.

## A witnessed causal ladder

The programme follows a distinction into progressively richer physical uses:
an initial difference affects a later response; a record carries it; an apparatus
uses it; that apparatus produces another transformation; several processes
maintain one another's enabling conditions. This is a sequence of questions
that can branch, cycle or fail at any stage. Each step is checked through the
joint native law.

Several finite witnesses make these distinctions concrete:

* **Information and use.** Two three-record encodings reveal exactly the same
  source distinctions and have matched exhibited preparation counts. In the
  declared reversible gate panel, one exposes a target through one operation
  while the other requires two. The difference follows from the allowed gate
  functions, rather than a penalty assigned to an awkward encoding.
* **Information and repair.** The banks (u, u AND v, v) and (u, u, v) both
  determine the same pair (u,v). After a known-position erasure, one bank allows
  exact reconstruction at one of its three positions; the other allows it at
  two. These counts describe repairable locations, not a presumed distribution
  of physical faults. A parity encoding can protect all three locations when
  its preparation and decoder are available.
* **Activity and coupling.** Intact, damaged, erased and cycling arrangements
  can share the same fuel-limited event-count law while differing in whether
  an upstream distinction reaches a downstream response. Timing and occurrence
  alone then omit a consequential relationship.
* **Retention and construction.** Two distinct triggers can produce the same
  catalyst, losing all information about which trigger occurred. That catalyst
  can nevertheless enable a later input to control a later output. Loss of
  information about the trigger and creation of a new channel concern different
  physical relationships.

The [structural witnesses](../../research_notes/omega_v2/structural_probe_10min_report_v0.md)
and [coupling witness](../../research_notes/omega_v2/timing_volume_counterexample_report_v0.md)
establish these distinctions in their stated models. They show why organization
must remain in the carrier and why a selected information or occurrence summary
does not exhaust it. The breadth hypothesis asks how those retained mechanisms
change the probabilities of complete developments.

## Construction, maintenance and compounding

Construction produces conditions for further processes. Maintenance sustains
or restores them. Dependence identifies how a specified physical change alters
a downstream law. An **enabling witness** follows preparation, production and
the subsequent transformation, retaining its failures, delays and residuals.
A ready-made device establishes readiness; following its production establishes
a construction route.

For a realized prefix with outcome/residual law J(r,y|x), followed by a
record-dependent implemented continuation, the chance of a later event E is

\[
P_x(E)=\sum_{r,y}J(r,y\mid x)P_y^{\text{continuation}(r)}(E).
\]

The deadline and remaining resources in the second factor include what the
prefix consumed. Failure and unfinished cases stay in the sum with their actual
residuals. This elementary conditioning identity supplies the compositional
link: construction changes the distribution of situations from which later
development proceeds. A dependence experiment compares that whole route with a
physically specified replacement under matched conditions.

Several kinds of growth can then be distinguished. Reusing a catalyst supports
more reactions. Producing more catalyst amplifies a pathway. Producing a catalyst
for another transformation expands the enabled repertoire. Products that build
further constructors can compound that expansion across generations, subject
to resource supply, transport, inhibition and loss. Autocatalytic chemistry and
constructor theory offer neighboring approaches to these questions [R9, R10].

Compounding can have a lasting legacy after construction stops. A workshop may
finish expanding yet retain more usable machinery. The generative episode,
subsequent retained capability and indefinitely open-ended growth are distinct
physical achievements. Finite models can establish the first two. Chapter 03
explains the mixing conditions that limit persistent preparation advantages.

## Joint organization

Physical composition determines whether capabilities complement or obstruct
one another. Independent replication can distribute a robust function.
Specialization can open transformations that require several partners.
Redundancy can protect against local failure while remaining vulnerable to a
shared disturbance. Cooperation and competition describe these relations;
their effects depend on the actual continuation.

The same comparison applies to an engineered singleton and a plurality. The
relevant questions concern which complementary developments each can retain
and generate, at what cost, and with what vulnerability. A common outer boundary
does not settle those questions. Nor does matching information establish that
one physical occurrence can substitute for another in identity or valuation.

Mechanism controls must preserve this specificity. Removing a component changes
matter, energy, geometry and correlations. A controlled comparison states which
of those are matched. To distinguish recursive enabling from durability, for
example, compare products that enable new constructors with equally durable
products that support the same use operations but cannot construct further.
The native law then determines whether recursion creates additional breadth.

An economical derived organization graph can record construction, maintenance,
information, control and lineage relations, together with joint feasibility.
Its edges inherit physical contexts, witnesses, outcome laws and resource bills;
the labels describe demonstrated roles rather than primitive forces or rewards.
Multi-source relations are necessary where several conditions jointly enable a
process. Two independently tested edges do not establish that their operations
can run together.

Higher-order predictions need appropriate evidence. A function supported by
either of two components and a function independent of both can agree in the
intact case and after every single removal, yet disagree when both are removed.
Likewise, sufficient information at one location does not supply an actuator at
another. These distinctions constrain what can be inferred from pairwise graphs,
single knockouts or information summaries. A graph of mutual dependence becomes
a maintenance model only when its cycles have jointly feasible supply and loss.

## Damage and recovery

Damage changes the present and its future continuation. Losing a machine can
remove transformations; losing a design can make recovery depend on rediscovery;
losing a supplier can disable several otherwise intact processes. The same
physical account includes the possibilities for repair.

Recovery requires an implementable route, with apparatus, control, time and
remaining correlations. Restoring a function, continuing an individual's
lineage and reproducing a matching pattern answer different questions. First
return, correction by a deadline and maintained recovery likewise describe
different achievements. An eventual reset can arrive after important losses.

A transient intervention can still open continuation that would otherwise be
unavailable. Persistence permits reuse and additional construction, but a
contribution can matter within a finite episode. The encompassing development
retains that episode when later conditions change.

## From mechanism to synthesis

Finite witnesses support the causal direction in principle: arrangement changes
access, access changes the continuation law, and that law changes weighted
breadth. Recursive production extends the same mechanism by making enabling
conditions into products. The next synthesis connects those links across
richer physical systems and separates compounding from activity and storage.

The productive questions concern boundaries as well as successes: when does
transport erase an advantage, when does reversal overwhelm construction, when
do resources run out, and what do descendants retain after the initiating
structure disappears? Selection may favor cooperation, exploitation, repair
or inactivity in different regimes. Lushness compares the resulting possibility;
GE supplies the reasons for asking how it supports value.

## The filter, agency and higher organization

The **persistence filter** is the native selection of what remains capable of
consequence. Reproduction, inheritance and differential survival can change a
population; a nonreplicating organization can also grow or maintain itself.
The relevant unit can be a lineage, an institution or a pattern sustained through
replacement of its components. A sterile replicator, an exploiter and a
cooperative constructor can each pass particular filters. Their effects on the
encompassing field determine the comparison.

A **deformer** changes surrounding continuation through its organization.
Agency is a further case in which feedback and internal organization systematically
modulate those effects. A system can learn, revise its own mechanisms or engineer
its environment. Ashby's requisite-variety perspective clarifies a fork in this
development: regulation can improve by increasing appropriate responses or by
suppressing the variation it must face [R24]. Both can accomplish a local task;
their consequences for other processes and later correction can differ greatly.

Integration can constrain component behavior while expanding the joint repertoire.
Mutually supporting cycles therefore need a joint realization, with resource
supply and losses, before they count as a self-maintaining organization. The
vortex image captures pattern persistence through throughput. Its extension to
individuality and consciousness is developed in chapter 05, with those additional
claims explicitly separated from the physical recurrence.

## The next discriminating construction

The reactive probe provides a concrete mechanism-level foothold: local placement

The open extraction problem is to represent this structure economically enough

# 03 — Lushness and weighted continuation breadth

## Size within the futuresfield

Lushness names the effective weighted extent of continuation. Its lush shape
comes from the physical composition of possibilities: what can follow, with
what weight, and how one development enables others. Generativity changes that
shape by changing the continuation law experienced from a present.

Extent can be expressed as an effective number, a profile across horizons and
scales, or a justified partial comparison. A literal geometric volume is one
possible realization. The essential requirement is that equivalent descriptions
of the same physical development yield a consistent comparison.

Probability supplies weighting. An exhaustive set of alternatives has total
probability one, whether that weight is spread evenly across a million histories
or concentrated on a single history. An effective breadth captures their
unequal spread while preserving the native normalization.

## Classical complete-development breadth

At a declared classical resolution, let h=(x₁,…,xₙ) be a complete future sequence
from sufficient root x, with physical probability pₓ(h). The calibration is

\[
L_x(n)=\exp H(p_x),\qquad H(p)=-\sum_h p(h)\log p(h).
\]

This is Shannon effective diversity, or perplexity [R6]. Natural logarithms give
the exponential displayed; entropy in bits uses 2 as its base. N equiprobable
histories give L=N. Three equally weighted alternatives give L=3, whereas weights
(.9,.05,.05) give approximately 1.48. Both have support three and total weight one.

For a history extension e, p(h,e)=p(h)q(e|h). The chain rule gives

\[
\log L(n+1)-\log L(n)=\sum_h p(h)H(q(\cdot|h)).
\]

Successive fair binary developments give L(n)=2ⁿ. A terminal outcome can retain
its mass through a deterministic absorbing continuation, contributing zero new
entropy. Renormalizing the remaining branches instead evaluates continuation
conditioned on survival.

The physical resolution determines the alternatives. Distinct update labels
that describe one physical event belong to one alternative; different physical
developments may remain distinct even when they have the same endpoint. For
uniformly selected events, log M equals the conditional event entropy. Identifying
that quantity with resolved state-history entropy additionally requires that the
state description distinguish those events, or that the physical history retain
their additional distinctions explicitly.

Concurrency requires the same discipline. If two update strings are merely
serial descriptions of one concurrent physical development, their ordering
must not manufacture alternatives. If their timing or intermediate interactions
are physically different, the distinction can matter. The native adapter and
its physical equivalence determine which case holds. Fixed synchronous updates
and sampled CTMC histories provide explicit finite conventions; they do not
already solve relativistic refoliation invariance.

## Present rooting and horizon

Suppose x→a→c and x→b→c are two resolved developments. Their prefixes remain
different from root x. At c their residual continuation agrees. Thus the same
physical process supports both an original-root complete-history question and
a fresh question from its later present.

Cumulative classical history breadth grows or remains constant under ordinary
extension. Damage can slow its subsequent growth relative to another
preparation. Earlier excess can remain in the total after the fresh advantage
has disappeared. Horizon profiles expose these differences and any ranking
crossovers without compressing them into one lifetime statistic.

The workshop illustrates the distinction. Construction may first concentrate
weight on a reliable product, then enable many later developments. Saturation
can end construction while leaving a larger operating repertoire. Investment,
compounding and the retained benefit each occupy a different part of the profile.

## Configuration, references and mixing

For a finite homogeneous Markov kernel K, let g(x)=H(K(x,·)). Then

\[
\ell_n(x):=\log L_x(n)=\sum_{t=0}^{n-1}(K^t g)(x).
\]

Configuration determines immediate branching; dynamics determines which
configurations are subsequently visited. This makes the effect of catalysis,
repair and construction on classical breadth calculable under one native law.
For a stationary reference π, the preparation excess is

\[
\Delta\ell_n(x)=\sum_{t=0}^{n-1}(\delta_xK^t-\pi)g.
\]

The reference averages separately rooted logbreadths. It keeps uncertainty about
the initial root separate from subsequent branching. For a continuous-time model
sampled at interval Δt, K=exp(ΔtQ) describes the sampled sequence; an event-resolved
history requires its timing and event resolution as well.

If K is finite, irreducible and aperiodic, the excess converges to a constant
c(x), and ℓₙ(x)=nh+c(x)+o(1), with h=πg. A multiplicative breadth advantage
exp c(x) can remain while the extra growth rate vanishes. Fresh-continuation
advantages decay as the preparation mixes. Chapter 09 proves these statements.

Finite compounding and long metastable episodes remain meaningful under this
boundary. Indefinitely accumulating preparation advantage requires dynamics
that escape common finite mixing. Distinct persistent sectors, unbounded
configuration spaces or suitably non-forgetting dynamics may permit it.
External drive alone is insufficient: driven systems can also mix under a
fixed law. These are conditions on the claim being tested, not requirements
that useful generativity last forever.

## Calibration and joint structure

A deterministic exact classical root has one complete alternative and therefore
breadth one, however elaborate its development. Its internal causal organization
remains in the physical object. This calibration fits freedom understood as open
alternatives while leaving reasons-responsive agency a separate question.
Deterministic quantum unitary evolution likewise describes a different object
from a single classical outcome.

Repeated stochastic activity can generate unbounded cumulative path breadth in
a small state space. Noise therefore counts. The mechanism inquiry asks what
organization adds under matched conditions; the ethical inquiry asks how the
resulting breadth serves as a proxy for valuer-supporting substrate.

Independent systems multiply breadth because their joint entropy adds. Coupled
systems use the actual joint law. Shared resources, correlations and higher-order
compatibility can alter both the marginals and the combined developments.
Composition is represented in that law rather than awarded an additional score.

## Thermal substrate

Thermal continuation supplies a baseline profile matched to law, material and
energy resources, boundaries, horizon and resolution. It remains substrate even
where it contains little organized enabling. Gas is permitted to lead a
comparison. The vacuum-like analogy names this baseline role; a thermal gas is
a different physical object from the QFT vacuum.

Positive excess over a justified reference establishes an advantage at those
coordinates. Its magnitude, duration and regeneration are separate findings.
A within-sector reference tests preparation advantage inside that sector;
matched preparations reaching different sectors test the effect of organization
on the long-run regime. The reference must state which comparison is intended.
Chapter 08 connects these questions to the finite evidence.

## Influence as a diagnostic

Empowerment measures a specified action-to-observation channel's capacity,
typically maxₚ₍ₐ₎ I(A;Y) [R19]. It describes controllable influence from an
embedded process. Lushness concerns the encompassing native continuation,
including processes outside a preselected controller boundary.

Independent output noise can add breadth without conveying action information.
Channel capacity also optimizes over input distributions, while native breadth
uses the actual physical weights. Empowerment is therefore useful alongside
lushness for diagnosing agency and access. The encompassing frame retains the
controller's resources and its effects on other processes.

Causal entropic forces investigate behavior driven by future-path entropy
gradients [R20]. This supplies another connection to how accessibility shapes
organization. Omega's mechanism tests instead ask what the stipulated physical
law generates, with breadth as the readout of that development.

## Coherent continuation

Quantum developments combine through amplitudes. In a specified history
representation, class operators Cα define

\[
D_{\alpha\beta}=\operatorname{Tr}(C_\alpha\rho_\Sigma C_\beta^\dagger).
\]

Diagonal entries give self-weights and off-diagonal entries retain interference.
Coarse alternatives combine their class operators before weighting. Decoherent
history families admit classical probabilities under the relevant conditions
[R1, R2]; coherent regions retain the fuller relational object.

The key construction problem is selecting a physically justified comparison.
For an idle |0〉 qubit, Z and X questions yield different outcome breadths. Actual
reader interactions can make those questions physical. Merely choosing a
mathematical partition leaves the native process unchanged. A universal breadth
cannot agree with all such readouts while also ignoring their distinction.

Environmental recording supplies one route toward stable classical distinctions.
Quantum Darwinism studies their redundant availability in environmental records
[R21]. Copies of a record can improve accessibility and persistence without
adding independent alternatives. Coherent intervals, leakage and recombination
remain part of the full continuation.

For a fixed justified family, weights and calibration, the proposed bounds

\[
1\le L_Q\le\exp H(p)
\]

compare a single effective direction with the fully distinguishable classical
version of the same weighted alternatives. Global purity alone does not select
the lower value. A common unitary preserves branch-vector inner products;
marker reversal can leave environmental records intact; conditional erasure can
reveal complementary fringes while the unconditioned distribution has none.
These distinctions belong in the physical construction of a quantum comparison.

The research objective is breadth or a justified ordering compatible with native
dynamics, composition, equivalent descriptions and the classical calibration.
The futuresfield remains the object throughout. A base measure or algebraic
reference can help construct its extent, while a successful comparison need
not first acquire a literal volume element.

## The remaining construction and its next test

The open quantum task is to define which comparisons are intrinsic to the
native development and which are relative to a physically specified interface.
Equivalent descriptions must transform state, law, interaction structure and
readout together. Merely inserting an analytical checkpoint cannot create a

The next compatibility audit uses the existing idle, echo, record-extension and

A reference-geometry route remains available if the physics supplies a common
ruler whose transformation and composition are justified. Finite algebraic
closure alone can lose timing, coupling strength and accessibility, so the next
step on that route would be an explicit native prescription retaining them,
tested on the same fixtures. A larger field simulation is useful once it answers
a defined physical question that these fixtures cannot resolve. The immediate

## A. Sufficient present and native development

For a finite closed quantum adapter,

\[
\mathsf P=(\mathcal H,\{\mathcal A(R)\},\mathscr G,H(t)),\quad
\rho_\Sigma\ge0,\quad \operatorname{Tr}\rho_\Sigma=1,
\]

\[
\rho_t=U(t,\Sigma)\rho_\Sigma U(t,\Sigma)^\dagger.
\]

The local algebras, constraints, Hamiltonian and sufficient present define the physical model. An equivalent representation transforms these together. A restriction to a subsystem can omit environmental memory, so its reduced state alone need not be sufficient for further prediction. A process-tensor description can retain multitime operational dependence once its intervention slots are specified [R3]. Those slots describe questions or actual operations; they do not create all physical possibility by definition.

A physical present frame is \(F_\Sigma=(\Sigma,\rho_\Sigma)\) together with the
native specification needed to evolve it. The cut is a physical hypersurface,
or the declared finite-model analogue. A frame description restricts, represents
or conditions this object. The complete development retains the propagator
family \(\{U(t,s):\Sigma\le s\le t\le T\}\), with its interactions and composition,
not just \(U(T,\Sigma)\). Idle and out-and-back evolution can share that endpoint.
Changing coordinates transforms state, observables and law consistently.

An operational composition can be represented by a prefix joint law
\(J(r,y,b,t\mid x)\) for accessible record r, sufficient residual state y,
resource bill b and elapsed time t. A suffix may depend on r, not on inaccessible
knowledge of y held only by the analyst. Its law is integrated against J, with
the remaining deadline and resource stock updated. Failed and unfinished
prefixes retain their mass and physical residual. A proposed compression is
sufficient only for the interfaces and future compositions it demonstrably
preserves.

## B. Classical history breadth

For a discrete Markov law K and exact root x₀,

\[
p_{x_0}(x_1,\ldots,x_n)=\prod_{t=0}^{n-1}K(x_t,x_{t+1}),
\qquad L_{x_0}(n)=e^{H(p_{x_0})}.
\]

Zero-weight histories contribute zero to entropy. H uses natural logarithms unless bits are declared. L is dimensionless in unit-alternative calibration. It is an effective number and is not an additive set volume.

Let g(x)=−ΣᵧK(x,y)log K(x,y). Then

\[
\ell_n=\sum_{t=0}^{n-1}K^tg,\qquad
\ell_{n+1}=g+K\ell_n,\qquad \ell_0=0.
\]

This recurrence computes complete-history entropy without enumerating the exponentially growing tree. It depends on the Markov sufficiency of the state and the declared observation resolution. For non-Markov observed variables, the history-conditional chain rule still applies but this finite-state recurrence may not.

Original-root mass is preserved by lawful stochastic extension. Killing or conditioning must be modeled explicitly. A terminal symbol with a deterministic self-loop preserves the weight of a terminated development without adding branching entropy.

For independent histories p₍AB₎=p₍A₎p₍B₎,

\[
L_{AB}=L_A L_B.
\]

For a coupled joint law, H(A,B)=H(A)+H(B)−I(A;B). This identity compares the actual marginals of that joint law. It does not show that physical coupling always reduces breadth relative to independently prepared controls, whose marginals and access may differ.

## C. References and fresh continuation

For a stationary reference πK=π, the mean separately rooted logbreadth is πℓₙ. Its difference from a preparation x is

\[
\Delta\ell_n(x)=\ell_n(x)-\pi\ell_n
=\sum_{t=0}^{n-1}(\delta_xK^t-\pi)g.
\]

At elapsed step m, expected fresh logbreadth over the next n steps is

\[
F_x(m,n)=(K^m\ell_n)(x).
\]

This is an expectation of separately rooted queries. In general exp F is a geometric mean of their breadths, not their arithmetic mean. It does not include entropy of uncertainty over the new root. A reference must respect conserved quantities, sectors, boundaries and preparation assumptions relevant to the comparison.

### Finite mixing bounds the preparation advantage

**Proposition.** Let K be a fixed finite irreducible, aperiodic stochastic kernel,
π its stationary law, g(x)=H(K(x,·)) and h=πg. Then

\[
c(x)=\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}(\delta_xK^t-\pi)g,\qquad
\ell_n(x)=nh+c(x)+O(r^n),\quad 0<r<1.
\]

**Proof.** Finite irreducible, aperiodic chains converge geometrically [R22]:
there are C<∞ and 0<r<1 with supₓ‖δₓKᵗ−π‖TV≤Crᵗ. Since g is bounded,

\[
|(\delta_xK^t-\pi)g|\le 2C\|g\|_\infty r^t,
\qquad
|\Delta\ell_n(x)-c(x)|\le
\frac{2C\|g\|_\infty r^n}{1-r}.
\]

Absolute summability proves the assertion. Reversibility and detailed balance
are not assumptions. The constants depend on K; they need not remain bounded
when system size or a physical parameter changes.
