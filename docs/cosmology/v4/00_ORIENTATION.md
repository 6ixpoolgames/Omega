# 00 — The programme and its north star

Omega investigates the physical structure of possibility. It asks how a present
configuration supports what can follow, how its components jointly shape that
continuation, and how some configurations build the conditions for further
construction, valuation and change.

The north star is:

> Characterize physical possibility well enough to explain how organization
> reshapes continuation, compare its effective weighted breadth, and investigate
> how processes within reality can preserve and enrich the conditions for value.

This is useful physics even before it becomes ethics. A description that reveals
hidden dependence, obstruction, recoverability or the propagation of distinctions
can teach us something about a system without supplying a universal ranking of
worlds. The ethical argument enters through two stated premises: **value requires
valuers, and normativity is conditional on valuing**. Alpha–Omega cosmology then
offers a wider interpretation of the same picture. Accepting that interpretation
is not a prerequisite for either the physical inquiry or Gradient Ethics.

## The motivating difference

Compare dispersed materials with a workshop made from those materials. Both obey
the same laws. The workshop creates no exemption from physics. Its machinery,
records, couplings and resource stocks change which transformations occur readily,
how they fit together, and what further machinery can be produced afterward.

Now compare two workshops with the same immediate output. One consumes the tool
that produced it. The other leaves a tool capable of making more tools. Their
current output does not specify their continuation. Repairing a damaged tool,
communicating its design, or sharing a limited power source changes the joint
development again. The board on which later processes operate is itself an
outcome of earlier processes.

That recursion is the programme's common spine. **Generativity** concerns
processes that establish, maintain or expand conditions for further processes.
**Lushness** names the intended effective weighted breadth of continuation.
Generativity may drive lushness; it receives no independent reward in the measure.
A workshop is an example to investigate, not a structure guaranteed to win.

## Three connected projects

The physical project starts from native dynamics and sufficient states. It keeps
joint relations, timing, resources, environmental correlations and quantum
interference where required. A future is generated from an actual present, not
assembled from separately attractive outcomes that cannot occur together.

The comparison project asks how much weighted possibility that structure holds.
In declared classical regimes, the current calibration is the effective number
of complete future sequences, obtained by exponentiating their Shannon entropy.
The corresponding general quantum comparison is open. A literal geometric volume
is one possible construction, not a prerequisite. A profile or a justified
partial comparison can be a useful intermediate answer.

The ethical project asks how physical changes bear on the conditions of valuation.
Under uncertainty about unfamiliar or future values, lushness is proposed as a
substrate proxy. Better knowledge should support more discriminating steering.
The research tests how faithfully that proxy tracks opportunities for value.

## What this draft commits to

The physical object comes first. Organisms, controllers, valuers and observers
are configurations within it. No prior list of preferred agents defines what
is admitted. Noise, thermal activity, temporary structures and inaccessible
correlations remain part of the account.

Frames supply situated descriptions and conditional weights. They do not create
physical equivalence merely by normalizing each description. Horizons index
restrictions of a development; they do not automatically discount later effects
or replace the whole by its long-run growth rate.

The programme permits failure. An inaccurate representation, a defective breadth
rule and an inadequate ethical proxy are different failures. Keeping all the
physics beside a poor scalar does not vindicate the scalar. Equally, a failed
scalar does not erase the physical object or make its characterization pointless.
