# 00 — The programme and its north star

Omega investigates the physical structure of possibility: how a present supports
what can follow, how its components jointly shape that continuation, and how
some configurations build the conditions for further construction, valuation
and change. Lushness names the effective weighted breadth of that possibility.
Generativity describes processes that can expand it.

The programme's north star is to characterize this physical substrate well
enough to understand how organization enriches continuation and how inhabitants
of reality can preserve and develop the conditions for value.

The preferred cosmological picture is a completed reality encountered from
within. Its inhabitants distinguish presents, remember, anticipate and act;
their actions belong to the reality whose continuation they help constitute.
Completion contains those changes and their consequences. This picture gives
the programme its wider orientation, while the physical inquiry and ethical
argument have premises that can be assessed in their own right.

## From physical possibility to value

The argument begins with the world and its lawful development. A physical
present, together with the law, supports a structured continuation containing
interactions, alternatives, correlations and, where relevant, quantum coherence.
The futuresfield names this object. Identifying it within physics grounds the
subject whose lushness the programme seeks to measure.

A physical frame is a present section with its physical state. In a relativistic
description the section is an admissible spacelike hypersurface; in a finite
model it has a declared cut or update analogue. Local perspectives and bounded
descriptions are restrictions of this encompassing construction. Conditioning
can give each description normalized probabilities while preserving the
different breadths and physical relations of the futures it describes. The
ultimate frame names the intended encompassing comparison where this regression
of situated weighting closes.

Value requires valuers. The substrate that supports valuers therefore supports
the possibility of value itself. Where the forms of value remain uncertain,
GE proposes lushness as a broad substrate proxy: keep the flowerbed expansively
fertile for value to blossom. Gas belongs in that account alongside organisms
and constructors. Diffuse substrate can be thin in organized capability while
still supplying the material and possibility from which richer structures arise.

This uncertainty motivates broad admission. Any consequential process can enter
the inquiry as a potential valuer; familiar organisms, conscious reports and
declared preferences are not prerequisites. Whether a process actually values,
what it values and how much ethical significance its organization has are further
questions. The programme seeks physical and functional grounds for answering
them without excluding unfamiliar forms at the outset.

Conditional normativity connects this account to reasons for action. Valuing
gives reasons to attend to the conditions on which value depends. As knowledge
improves, those reasons support more informed steering within the same physical
possibility structure. Lushness is ODT's intended arbitrator; constructing a
justified comparison is consequently central to the decision programme.

The Alpha–Omega interpretation places these questions within a wider cosmology.
Its proposals about completion, consciousness and theology identify connections
for downstream research. The physical and ethical arguments can be developed
while those interpretive questions remain open.

## A workshop and its continuation

The book follows a common chain: **present organization → lawful joint
continuation → changed conditions of access → further continuation → conditions
for valuers and value.** Every link is internal to the physical process.

Consider dispersed materials and a workshop assembled from them. Both follow
the same law. The workshop's machinery, records and resource stocks change which
transformations occur readily and which further constructions can follow.

Now compare two workshops making the same part. One exhausts the tool that made
it. The other leaves machinery capable of making further tools. Their immediate
output agrees, but their continuations differ. Repairing a damaged machine,
sharing a power source or communicating a design changes the joint development
again. Conditions for later possibilities are themselves products of the process.

This example connects the book's questions. The physical description identifies
the machinery and its dependencies. The generativity inquiry follows construction
into subsequent enabling. The breadth comparison uses the actual law and matched
resources. GE asks what this continuation supports for valuers. ODT studies how
an embedded decision can realize and compare alternatives. The example motivates
the inquiry; its ranking is something the model must determine.

Persistence supplies an endogenous filter. What is still capable of affecting
a later situation has passed through conditions of continuation. Competition,
cooperation, capture, breakdown and repair all participate in that filter.
Survival alone establishes neither ethical success nor a universal tendency
toward improvement. It helps explain why particular organizations and their
consequences remain in the field long enough to matter.

The same argument connects scales. Molecular interactions alter reaction
networks; networks can maintain organisms; organisms construct practices,
institutions and tools that alter later access. The programme has demonstrated
bounded links in this ladder in principle. A stronger synthesis must show which
mechanisms survive composition, scale and physical comparison. Greater
organization earns its role through the continuation it supports.

## Completion, finite horizons and situated knowledge

The target includes the complete development and its finite consequential
structure. A construction followed by dissolution still occurred. A delay can
make a response unavailable when it was needed. Recovery can reopen access
without making the intervening loss disappear. Extending the horizon retains
those episodes; rerooting at a later present asks a new residual question.

Finite horizons, local descriptions and coarse resolutions make calculations
tractable. They retain their relationship to the physical object they describe.
A normalized local law does not erase how that situation was weighted from a
larger frame. An asymptotic rate need not contain every finite difference.
A useful summary can discard information, so its sufficiency must be checked
for the comparison being made.

This matters especially for bounded decision makers. Their descriptions, models
and interventions are physical parts of the same reality. ODT studies how they
can compare implementable continuations using lushness while accounting for
information, resources, dependencies and their own implementation. Its ambition
is an encompassing comparison; practical instruments expose what a bounded
calculation leaves unresolved.

## Reading the argument

Chapters 01–03 develop physical possibility, generativity and lushness. Chapter
04 connects substrate to GE. Chapters 05–06 consider individuals and decisions
within it. Chapter 07 develops the interpretive cosmology. Evidence and future
tests are collected in chapter 08, the formal core in chapter 09, and references
and provenance in chapters 10–11.

The scientific value of characterizing possibility also stands independently.
Dependencies, recoverability and the propagation of distinctions can teach us
about a system before a complete breadth comparison is available. Each such
result contributes to the common object on which the wider programme builds.

Each chapter supplies the definitions needed for its own argument and points
to the evidence or formal assumptions it uses. The main text presents the
current construction. Evidence and provenance preserve the route by which it
was constrained. Replaced formulas remain in the source archive; open routes
state their remaining problem and next step.

The larger interpretive ambition is a connected account of physical process,
inhabited perspective, ethics and cosmology. Its theological connections retain
an apophatic limit: accounts agreeing on all accessible consequences cannot be
separated by those consequences alone. The programme aims to clarify that shared
structure and make the remaining questions more precise.
