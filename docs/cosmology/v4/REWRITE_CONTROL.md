## Authority and editorial rule

Current user instructions; preserved brief; dated adopted updates; historical
standalone editions. Actual evidence retains its reported scope. Read relevant
sources rather than relying on titles or assistant summaries. External reports
are unreproduced until verified.

Omit superseded formulas when a replacement exists. Preserve their history in
source archives and audit maps. For an unresolved route, state the surviving
question, its obstruction and the intended next construction or test. Do not
present a menu of discarded candidates as the current theory.

## Three passes for every chapter

A. Coverage: record sources read and map developments to retained text,
relocation or justified supersession. Missing sources stay explicitly missing.

B. Verification: check definitions against brief, mathematics by derivation,
numbers against reports and citations against scope. Mark claim status.

C. Coherence: standalone chapter, clear prerequisites, consistent terminology
and neighboring chapters, positive argument rather than correction chronology.
