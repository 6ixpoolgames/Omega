## Physical perspective and descriptive frame

A **perspective** is situated physical organization: where a process couples,
what distinctions can affect it, what it can affect, and which correlations cross
its boundary. A perspective need not be a mind. A chemical apparatus, a crystal
carrying an excitation and an organism have different forms of situated access.

A **frame** is a description or conditioning of the encompassing process at a
declared region, present, resolution and scope. An analytical question does not
by itself instantiate a detector or observer. Where the question concerns an
actual reader, that reader and its interaction belong to the physical model.

For a classical law and a positive-probability record α, frame weighting uses
P(E|α). The probability of the record remains available when comparing back to
the encompassing frame. A successful preparation with probability p followed by
conditional success q has overall success pq. Reframing at success is legitimate;
presenting q as the original probability is not.

Compatible decoherent quantum alternatives permit corresponding classical
conditioning. General coherent situations require the appropriate quantum
state, effects or instruments; incompatible questions cannot simply be pooled
into one classical sample space. These are established restrictions of the
machinery, not new exceptions invented for Omega [R1, R2].

## Four changes that must not be confused

Renaming coordinates preserves the physical process when state, law, apparatus
and readout transform together. A numerical subdivision or identity checkpoint
also leaves the process unchanged when its contributions are recomposed exactly.

Changing the resolved question can legitimately change a readout. Asking about
position rather than momentum, or a local record rather than the whole joint
system, is not merely relabeling coordinates. The comparison must say which
question it concerns.

Introducing an actual interaction changes the physical development. A detector
can correlate with a system and alter what happens later. Writing its projector
on paper does not have that physical effect.

Finally, **rerooting** selects a new sufficient present and asks about its residual
future. It does not revise the earlier-root development or delete its prefixes.
These distinctions prevent representation changes from masquerading as creation
or destruction of possibility.

## Present sufficiency and memory

The past matters to future prediction through what it has left in the sufficient
present. A subsystem's reduced state may omit that information. Two joint
system–environment states can have the same subsystem marginal yet behave
differently when the environment interacts again.

The repair is to keep a sufficient joint state or a suitable multitime reduced
process description. Process tensors provide one established way to represent
such memory at an interface [R3]. They are tools for representing the process,
not a license to count every hypothetical intervention as another native future.

An observer's currently accessible memory is also different from the complete
record an analyst has retained. Forgetting can remove local access without
erasing physical correlations elsewhere. A description that keeps an archive
does not supply the archive to an embodied controller without a retrieval route.

## Composition precedes aggregation

Physical composition joins processes through their actual boundaries, shared
resources and correlations. Two individually possible activities need not be
jointly possible. Two alternative plans may each consume the same unique fuel
unit. Pairwise compatibility among several repairs may still fail for the whole
set. A product of subsystem laws is justified only by the required independence.

This is why continuation must retain joint structure. Equal marginal outputs
can hide a crucial correlation. Two fair bits may be independent or copies;
the downstream effect of an XOR operation distinguishes them. No tally of their
separate entropies reconstructs that difference.

Sequential composition uses the actual residual state, including depleted
stocks, occupied apparatus, memory and environmental correlations. It is not a
concatenation of separately optimized best cases. Resource accounting constrains
the law and the comparison; it is not a reward term added to lushness.

## A common physical reference

Different roots should be compared under declared matching conditions: law,
resources, conserved sector, boundaries, duration and resolution. Their local
normalizations do not manufacture a common physical unit. The encompassing
description retains how the frames relate.

The **ultimate frame** names the intended encompassing standpoint at which no
further regression of situated normalized weighting remains. It is not an ideal
agent outside the dynamics. This fixes the programme's reference ambition;
it does not yet provide a complete numerical ordering of every continuation.

## Why local field theory is the next physical direction

The current foundation points toward local field dynamics: actual degrees of
freedom interact, propagate distinctions, form records and recombine. QFT is
the preferred physical adapter direction. Observable algebras and real-time
field methods offer useful representations [R4, R5]. Multiway graphs remain
useful when they faithfully encode the native law, including coherence.

A small regulated field model would be a next experimental substrate, not a
derivation of fundamental physics. A lattice cutoff is not automatically a
Planck cell, a timestep is not automatically relativistic time, and typical
continuum local QFT algebras do not inherit a finite-dimensional trace unchanged
[R4]. A thermal gas reference is distinct from the quantum vacuum.

The goal is a tractable description that preserves the consequential relations
under investigation. The full state and law are the reference; a useful reduced
description must earn its compression through explicit preservation or error
bounds. Better computation changes what we can infer, not what physically exists.
