## B. Verification

Ethical commitments are not presented as theorems of physics. Inclusive concern
is stated rather than inferred from one actor's preference. Admission is not
equal weight or a proof that all processes value. A singleton is not condemned
by its boundary. Physical obstruction alone does not determine ethical sign.
The adversarial proxy test concerns the operational comparison at declared scope.

## C. Coherence

Lushness is ODT's intended arbitrator, with construction tasks identified.
Cosmology is not substituted for the ethical bridge. Repeated historical
correction language has been removed.
