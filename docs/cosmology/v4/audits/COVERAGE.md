## Cross-chapter checks

- Frames consistently mean physical present sections plus sufficient state.
- Lushness consistently means weighted extent; organization affects it through
  native dynamics. Profiles express its dependence, not a redefinition as virtues.
- Value requires valuers and conditional normativity supplies the ethical bridge.
- Lushness remains the intended ODT arbitrator, including where its construction
  and practical comparison are incomplete.
- Complete history and residual rerooting remain compatible different queries.
- Gas/noise, deterministic-one and no prescribed winners are retained.
- Physical quantum evolution, analyst history descriptions and actual records
  remain distinct; global purity does not settle breadth.
- Hypotheses about consciousness and Alpha–Omega remain visible, scoped and
  connected to concrete follow-up work.
- Numeric evidence is reported in its actual units and with appropriate references.
- Publication is held; no raw run output is added by this rewrite.

Final document checks verify source/generated agreement, local links, references,
chapter completeness and formatting hazards. The chapter audits record the
substantive review; neither kind of check guarantees that every scientific or
philosophical problem has been settled.
