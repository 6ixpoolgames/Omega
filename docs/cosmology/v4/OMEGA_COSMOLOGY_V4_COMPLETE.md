# 00 — The programme and its north star

Omega investigates the physical structure of possibility: how a present supports
what can follow, how its components jointly shape that continuation, and how
some configurations build the conditions for further construction, valuation
and change. Lushness names the effective weighted breadth of that possibility.
Generativity describes processes that can expand it.

The programme's north star is to characterize this physical substrate well
enough to understand how organization enriches continuation and how inhabitants
of reality can preserve and develop the conditions for value.

The preferred cosmological picture is a completed reality encountered from
within. Its inhabitants distinguish presents, remember, anticipate and act;
their actions belong to the reality whose continuation they help constitute.
Completion contains those changes and their consequences. This picture gives
the programme its wider orientation, while the physical inquiry and ethical
argument have premises that can be assessed in their own right.

## From physical possibility to value

The argument begins with the world and its lawful development. A physical
present, together with the law, supports a structured continuation containing
interactions, alternatives, correlations and, where relevant, quantum coherence.
The futuresfield names this object. Identifying it within physics grounds the
subject whose lushness the programme seeks to measure.

A physical frame is a present section with its physical state. In a relativistic
description the section is an admissible spacelike hypersurface; in a finite
model it has a declared cut or update analogue. Local perspectives and bounded
descriptions are restrictions of this encompassing construction. Conditioning
can give each description normalized probabilities while preserving the
different breadths and physical relations of the futures it describes. The
ultimate frame names the intended encompassing comparison where this regression
of situated weighting closes.

Value requires valuers. The substrate that supports valuers therefore supports
the possibility of value itself. Where the forms of value remain uncertain,
GE proposes lushness as a broad substrate proxy: keep the flowerbed expansively
fertile for value to blossom. Gas belongs in that account alongside organisms
and constructors. Diffuse substrate can be thin in organized capability while
still supplying the material and possibility from which richer structures arise.

This uncertainty motivates broad admission. Any consequential process can enter
the inquiry as a potential valuer; familiar organisms, conscious reports and
declared preferences are not prerequisites. Whether a process actually values,
what it values and how much ethical significance its organization has are further
questions. The programme seeks physical and functional grounds for answering
them without excluding unfamiliar forms at the outset.

Conditional normativity connects this account to reasons for action. Valuing
gives reasons to attend to the conditions on which value depends. As knowledge
improves, those reasons support more informed steering within the same physical
possibility structure. Lushness is ODT's intended arbitrator; constructing a
justified comparison is consequently central to the decision programme.

The Alpha–Omega interpretation places these questions within a wider cosmology.
Its proposals about completion, consciousness and theology identify connections
for downstream research. The physical and ethical arguments can be developed

## A workshop and its continuation

The book follows a common chain: **present organization → lawful joint
continuation → changed conditions of access → further continuation → conditions
for valuers and value.** Every link is internal to the physical process.

Consider dispersed materials and a workshop assembled from them. Both follow
the same law. The workshop's machinery, records and resource stocks change which
transformations occur readily and which further constructions can follow.

Now compare two workshops making the same part. One exhausts the tool that made
it. The other leaves machinery capable of making further tools. Their immediate
output agrees, but their continuations differ. Repairing a damaged machine,
sharing a power source or communicating a design changes the joint development
again. Conditions for later possibilities are themselves products of the process.

This example connects the book's questions. The physical description identifies
the machinery and its dependencies. The generativity inquiry follows construction
into subsequent enabling. The breadth comparison uses the actual law and matched
resources. GE asks what this continuation supports for valuers. ODT studies how
an embedded decision can realize and compare alternatives. The example motivates
the inquiry; its ranking is something the model must determine.

Persistence supplies an endogenous filter. What is still capable of affecting
a later situation has passed through conditions of continuation. Competition,
cooperation, capture, breakdown and repair all participate in that filter.
Survival alone establishes neither ethical success nor a universal tendency
toward improvement. It helps explain why particular organizations and their
consequences remain in the field long enough to matter.

The same argument connects scales. Molecular interactions alter reaction
networks; networks can maintain organisms; organisms construct practices,
institutions and tools that alter later access. The programme has demonstrated
bounded links in this ladder in principle. A stronger synthesis must show which
mechanisms survive composition, scale and physical comparison. Greater
organization earns its role through the continuation it supports.

The target includes the complete development and its finite consequential

Finite horizons, local descriptions and coarse resolutions make calculations

# 01 — The physical structure of possibility

## The physical bearer

Omega begins with a physical present and what follows from it under the native laws. The present contains material arrangements, fields, correlations, resources and the processes that will act on them. Its continuation includes their lawful development, the alternatives that development admits, their physical weights, and the relations through which one development enables or obstructs another. The **futuresfield** names this structure of physical possibility. It is the physical bearer of the property called lushness.

Possibility already has a physical bearer. A catalyst, a shared reservoir, an interfering amplitude and an embodied decision process alter continuation through their actual relations. No additional force has to steer the world toward greater possibility for these relations to exist. The further inquiry is how to characterize their structure and effective weighted breadth. Establishing the bearer makes that inquiry concrete; it does not already provide a universal scalar measure.

The programme's organizing picture is one internally differentiated reality. Molecules, organisms, clocks and observers are organizations within that reality. Their boundaries express physical relations and useful levels of description. This monist orientation motivates looking for a common continuation structure before identifying particular choosers, purposes or values. It is an interpretive commitment; the finite mathematical constructions below need only their declared physical model and state.

The starting point includes passive and active processes alike. Gas, thermal fluctuations and coherent quantum regions belong alongside machinery, ecologies and deliberating agents. Selection is itself physical: sensing, switching, competition and cooperation change what follows. A menu of tasks for an already selected agent can later describe one interface to this field. It cannot supply the field's foundation.

## A sufficient present and native law

A physical model specifies degrees of freedom, lawful interactions, spatial and causal incidence, constraints and boundaries. A **sufficient present** supplies the state needed to continue that model. Sufficiency is relative to the claimed physics: an effective model may legitimately omit microscopic variables, but must retain the information its own continuation law requires.

For a finite closed quantum adapter, write

\[
\mathsf P=(\mathcal H,\{\mathcal A(R)\},\mathscr G,H(t)),
\qquad \rho_\Sigma\geq0,\quad\operatorname{Tr}\rho_\Sigma=1.
\]

Here \(\mathcal H\) is the Hilbert space, \(\mathcal A(R)\) specifies observable structure associated with physical regions, \(\mathscr G\) records constraints and physical identifications, and \(H(t)\) is the Hamiltonian. The state \(\rho_\Sigma\) belongs to a present section \(\Sigma\), assigned coordinate time zero in this representation. Native propagation satisfies

\[
i\hbar\,\partial_tU(t,s)=H(t)U(t,s),\qquad
\rho_t=U(t,0)\rho_\Sigma U(t,0)^\dagger.
\]

The specification retains the propagators throughout the interval, their local interaction structure and the state on which they act. Symbolically,

\[
\Omega_{\Sigma,T}=
\bigl[\mathsf P,\rho_\Sigma,
\{U(t,s):0\leq s\leq t\leq T\}\bigr]_{\text{physical equivalence}}.
\]

The equivalence subscript requires a declared account of which descriptions represent the same physics. It is not a claim that a universal quotient has already been constructed. A passive basis change must transform the state, law, observable incidence and readout together. Arbitrarily rotating the state while holding the apparatus fixed generally changes the physical situation.

Environmental correlations, controllers, reservoirs and work sources enter the sufficient specification wherever they affect continuation. An imposed pulse schedule is a declared physical input; describing the apparatus as autonomous requires modeling what generates that schedule. A subsystem's reduced state usually supplies less information than the relevant joint state. Two fair bits can be independent, or perfectly correlated copies. Each individual bit has the same distribution in both cases, but a subsequent XOR is fair in the first and always zero in the second. Equal marginals therefore do not determine joint continuation. Quantum states add phase and entanglement to this same requirement to retain consequential relations.

The classical counterpart is equally direct. A sufficient state \(x\) and a native transition kernel \(K\) determine the probability of a resolved future configuration sequence:

\[
P_x(x_1,\ldots,x_n)=\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}K(x_j,x_{j+1}),
\qquad x_0=x.
\]

The reusable law at every reachable state matters, not just its first row. Two models can agree on their immediate transition from \(x\) and disagree after the next interaction. In continuous time, a generator supplies native rates and waiting times; \(e^{tQ}\) gives finite-time transition probabilities. Sampling that process at specified cuts is a restriction of its development. It can omit intervening events and should be described at that resolution.

## Complete development and the meaning of completion

Continuation concerns the complete development from a present. An endpoint alone can conceal the distinction between remaining idle and making a physical excursion followed by reversal. The endpoint states, or even the total endpoint channels, may agree while the intervening couplings differ. The carrier retains that difference. Whether a particular breadth readout assigns the developments different values is a further question; distinct structures can have equal effective size.

A classical multiway representation depicts states, lawful transitions and their weighted alternatives. Different prefixes can reach the same sufficient state. Their residual future can then share one computational representation, while a query from the original root retains both prefixes and their weights. Re-rooting at the common state asks about what follows from that new present. It adds no ancestral label to the physical state and gives no continuing credit to an earlier structure that has disappeared. The two questions agree through conditioning, not through deletion of the earlier development.

Compatible classical path laws marginalize across horizons: summing over the last state recovers the earlier path law because each row of \(K\) sums to one. Termination can be represented by an absorbing outcome that retains its weight. An unfinished calculation or an unexpanded computational frontier is different: it marks unresolved continuation, not physical termination. These distinctions let a finite representation approximate a larger object without manufacturing certainty at its boundary.

The **completed** viewpoint regards the development and its internal temporal relations together. It places no additional clock outside the world on which the whole must first be assembled. Internal change remains real: waiting, construction, damage and recovery occupy different positions in the physical relations. An infinite completion retains each finite restriction, including consequential delays. An asymptotic growth rate or terminal-state summary may discard those distinctions; that is a limitation of the summary.

The adopted block-Everett interpretation combines this completed viewpoint with the universal quantum description and its correlated relative alternatives [R7]. Global unitary evolution can be deterministic while relative records have Born-weighted alternatives. A quantum multiway diagram therefore needs amplitudes or equivalent coherent process structure; its drawn paths are not independent classical worlds to count. The block interpretation neither supplies a local hidden-variable explanation of entangled correlations nor adds controllable signalling outside the physical causal structure.

Completion is always completion under a declared account of admissibility. A physical model does not thereby contain every verbally describable scenario or every incompatible choice of laws. Formal admissibility, physical realization and phenomenal experience remain distinct questions. Alpha's wider ambition to relate forms of distinction, relation and oriented consequence belongs to the interpretive discussion in [chapter 07](#chapter-07).

## Composition carries the world forward

Composition joins developments through physical boundaries. A process leaves stocks depleted or replenished, apparatus occupied or released, records located somewhere, and correlations available to subsequent interactions. These residual conditions determine what can happen next. They are how organization enters continuation without an externally awarded organization bonus.

In the finite quantum model, coherent sequential composition obeys \(U(t_2,t_0)=U(t_2,t_1)U(t_1,t_0)\). At a classical resolved interface, one can retain an outcome together with the state it leaves behind. A subnormalized instrument kernel \(J_r(x,y)\) gives the probability of record \(r\) and residual state \(y\), starting at \(x\). A physically implemented second stage has

\[
J_{s\circ r}(x,z)=\sum_y J_r(x,y)J_s(y,z).
\]

If elapsed time and resource expenditures are resolved as well, composition also sums or integrates over their compatible splits. Preparation, switching, communication and disposal are physical stages in that accounting. A resource profile can retain work, material consumption, occupancy and returns in their own units; they need not be converted into a single utility coefficient. Failures and unfinished outcomes retain their actual residual states and costs.

Parallel composition requires the actual joint law. Two individually possible repairs can compete for the same fuel unit. Any two among three processes may fit within a resource stock while all three together do not. Pairwise compatibility therefore need not imply compatibility of the whole family. Likewise, reproducing each process's marginal output does not establish that their joint outputs and residual correlations have been reproduced. A claimed joint realization must specify one encompassing physical development in which the requirements hold together.

Independence is a special case with a physical justification. Independent synchronously updated classical systems use a product kernel and product root; independent continuous-time systems use the sum of their separately acting generators. Correlated preparations or interacting dynamics require the joint description even when local readouts happen to agree. A diagram with commuting endpoints is not, by itself, an independence certificate.

Control enters through the same construction. A protocol is a physical controller using records it can obtain, with an apparatus and a preparation process. A controlled kernel \(K(x,a,y)\) describes an exposed operational interface only to the extent those operations are physically realizable. Stochastic chemical reactions remain inside the native dynamics; naming them does not make them freely selectable actions. Adaptive control may respond to an accessible record, not to an analyst's hidden knowledge of the microstate.

A realization witness connects an operational claim to its controller, preparation, resources, boundary conditions and induced physical path law. This distinguishes a transformation permitted by physics, a ready apparatus that reliably performs it, and a route by which that apparatus can actually be obtained. If preparation succeeds with probability \(p\) and use then succeeds with conditional probability \(q\), the whole route succeeds with probability \(pq\). Readiness and deployable capability are different physical questions. Task sets and capability envelopes summarize such realizations; their labels do not define the futuresfield or supply its weights.

This compositional account supports the mechanism investigated in [chapter 02](#chapter-02): products of one development can change the accessibility and native weights of further developments under the same laws. Changes of access, weighted breadth and value remain separately identifiable claims.

## Physical frames and situated perspectives

A **physical frame** is a present hypersurface \(\Sigma\) together with its physical state. In a relativistic model it is an admissible spacelike section; a sufficient global initial-data formulation uses a suitable Cauchy surface where the spacetime and theory admit one. A finite model uses an update cut and its state as the corresponding simpler construction. The frame is physical boundary data from which continuation is considered.

Different admissible sections are related by the native dynamics and the appropriate transformations of their descriptions. This allows relative sectioning without a preferred universal simultaneity. A worldline at one event does not choose a unique global section. Massive trajectories admit proper-time descriptions; massless propagation follows null structure and supplies no rest frame. Relational quantum hypersurfaces provide a developed connection to this programme [R23]. A general relativistic quantum realization of the whole Omega construction remains a research task.

A **perspective** is situated access and influence within this structure: where a process couples, what affects it, what it can affect, and what correlations cross its boundary. A detector, gas in contact with a reservoir, an excited crystal and an organism each have situated continuation. Awareness and agency are further forms of organization, not prerequisites for having a perspective. Useful interfaces may be local regions or worldtubes rather than whole-universe slices. Their descriptions derive from the physical frame and its continuation.

A **frame description** represents, restricts or conditions that physical frame and development at a stated scope and resolution. It can specify a region, observable algebra, duration, boundary coupling and accessible records. A scientist can choose such a description without there being a conscious subject corresponding to it. Physical incidence and actual couplings justify its interpretation; an arbitrary mathematical factorization alone does not identify the apparatus or its access.

Consider a workshop. Its full present includes tools, stored materials, instructions, occupied machines and surrounding supplies. A worker encounters only part of that state. A coarse model may resolve tool functions while omitting molecular detail. The physical frame, the worker's perspective and the model's restriction are related but distinct. Adequacy depends on which consequences the restricted description preserves. The same method extends to molecules, cells and institutions when their effective variables support the continuation questions being asked.

## Situated weights, history and current access

The frame construction already supplies situated normalization. Under one classical law \(P\), a positive-probability record condition \(\alpha\) gives

\[
\mu_\alpha(E)=P(E\mid\alpha),\qquad
P(E\cap\alpha)=P(\alpha)\mu_\alpha(E).
\]

The encompassing description retains \(P(\alpha)\). Conditioning on a successful preparation is legitimate; forgetting its probability would change the claim about obtaining that preparation from an earlier frame. At every future horizon the conditional law still has total probability one. This says how physical weight is distributed, not that the effective breadth is one or that the future must be discounted. Horizons index restrictions of the same continuation.

Under a common law, nested conditioning obeys the tower identity. If \(\mathcal F_s\subseteq\mathcal F_t\) and \(Z\) is a fixed integrable readout,

\[
\mathbb E[\mathbb E[Z\mid\mathcal F_t]\mid\mathcal F_s]
=\mathbb E[Z\mid\mathcal F_s].
\]

The accumulated observation, action and memory history generates an increasing filtration \(\mathcal F_t\). The currently accessible information \(\mathcal G_t=\sigma(O_t,M_t)\), generated by present observations and accessible memory, need not increase. A lost record can remain in the encompassing history while becoming unavailable to the apparatus. Martingale reasoning for nested historical information does not automatically apply to current access after forgetting.

An analyst's archive therefore gives no free capability to the workshop. If the workshop has lost a design, recovering its use requires a physical retrieval route with its delay, coupling and resources. At the sufficient boundary, the past matters through the physical state it has produced. At a restricted interface, omitted environment and correlations can produce memory. Process tensors or quantum combs represent such multitime dependence at declared interfaces [R3]. They supplement an insufficient local description without making an extra ancestral narrative part of the complete physical present.

The **ultimate frame** names the intended encompassing closure in which local normalizations and situated comparisons belong to one physical account, with no further regression to another external weighting frame. It has no chooser outside the dynamics. This intended role is compatible with finite agents having incomplete knowledge and with the numerical ordering of the full field remaining unfinished.

## Focus, inference and physical intervention

A **focus** organizes the contributions connecting a situated boundary to a specified condition: for example, a prepared system and a detector effect. It can be a physical detector condition, a boundary constraint or a mathematical query. The elementary quantum construction pairs a state with an effect. For a channel \(\mathcal E\), state \(\rho\), and effect \(0\leq F\leq I\),

\[
p(F\mid\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}[F\mathcal E(\rho)]
=\operatorname{Tr}[\mathcal E^*(F)\rho].
\]

The dual map propagates the condition backward for inference while the state propagates forward. This equality asserts no physical rewriting of the earlier state by a later event. Intention and attention are possible physical processes within the model; they are not needed to define this pairing.

The operations associated with a focus have different meanings:

| Operation | Physical meaning and comparison |
|---|---|
| Passive redescription | Transform state, law, incidence and readout together; the same resolved physical answer is preserved. |
| Numerical subdivision or identity checkpoint | Recompose the same propagation coherently; no extra interaction or alternative has occurred. |
| Restriction or changed physical resolution | Ask a different declared question about the same development; the readout may change. |
| Conditioning | Retain the process law together with the record condition and its weight. |
| Actual interaction or measurement | Include the apparatus and operation that change the physical development. |
| Decoherence | Track physical correlations and the resulting suppression of interference in the relevant description. |
| Re-rooting | Use the new sufficient present and remaining law for a new residual question. |

A quantum instrument makes the measurement distinction precise. Completely positive outcome maps \(\mathcal M_r\) sum to a trace-preserving channel and give

\[
p(r)=\operatorname{Tr}\mathcal M_r(\rho),\qquad
\rho_r=\frac{\mathcal M_r(\rho)}{p(r)}\quad\text{when }p(r)>0.
\]

Keeping the unnormalized output retains both probability and residual state. Sequences of implemented instruments supply their own joint law. They do not assign simultaneous classical values to every incompatible measurement one could imagine. In a quantum eraser, sorting recorded outcomes by a correlated marker can reveal complementary conditional fringes whose weighted mixture reproduces the unconditional statistics. An actual reversal of a marker interaction is a different operation; leaked environmental records matter to whether interference can be restored.

## Coherent histories and physical records

Quantum histories offer a representation that retains interference across time [R1]. At declared cuts, use exhaustive orthogonal projector families and define

\[
C_\alpha=P^{(n)}_{a_n}U_{n,n-1}\cdots
P^{(1)}_{a_1}U_{1,0},\qquad
D_{\alpha\beta}=\operatorname{Tr}
(C_\alpha\rho_\Sigma C_\beta^\dagger).
\]

These class operators expand a coherent process. Their intermediate projectors do not assert physical measurements unless the corresponding interactions belong to the model. For a coarse event \(A\), first sum its amplitudes:

\[
C_A=\sum_{\alpha\in A}C_\alpha,\qquad
\mu(A)=\sum_{\alpha,\beta\in A}D_{\alpha\beta}.
\]

The off-diagonal terms matter. Destructive interference means \(\mu\) need not be additive or monotone under inclusion, even though \(D\) is positive semidefinite. The coherent carrier is consequently not already an ordinary positive volume measure. For the exhaustive projective expansions above, \(\sum_{\alpha\beta}D_{\alpha\beta}=1\) and \(\operatorname{Tr}D=1\). The latter identity is a property of this expansion class, not a normalization rule to impose on every more general quantum kernel.

Medium decoherence, \(D_{\alpha\beta}=0\) for distinct histories, suffices to give the family a classical law \(p_\alpha=D_{\alpha\alpha}\). It does not select that family as the unique physical description. An idle qubit in \(|0\rangle\) has one-cut Z probabilities \((1,0)\) and X probabilities \((1/2,1/2)\); both single-cut families are decoherent. Their effective numbers are one and two. A single family-independent scalar cannot equal both merely because both families satisfy decoherence. Physical family selection or explicit frame dependence is needed.

Actual record formation supplies additional structure [R2, R21]. In an exact pure-state fixture, orthogonal record projections can certify a proposed history family through

\[
R_h\psi_T=C_h\psi_\Sigma.
\]

This equation establishes the stated correlations. Their physical provenance still comes from locality, preparation and actual recording interactions; abstract projectors can satisfy the equation without identifying a native recording mechanism. Stable later records can refine transported parent records on the relevant subspace. Such a regime supports classical history extension. Approximate records require a declared accuracy, resolution and stability interval.

Records provide a classical restriction of the field, while coherent regions remain in the field. Uncomputation can invalidate a permanent local-record description without erasing the intervening evolution. A common global unitary preserves inner products; a conditional eraser does not merge orthogonal universal states. Similarly, global purity gives zero state entropy without making relational continuation trivial. These distinctions set concrete requirements for quantum breadth, developed in [chapter 03](#chapter-03).

## Faithful descriptions and compression

A reduced description earns its use by preserving the continuation relations being compared. Exact predictive equivalence is meaningful relative to a declared family \(\mathcal T\) of admissible physical tests:

\[
p\equiv_{\mathcal T}q
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Law}(\tau[p])=\operatorname{Law}(\tau[q])
\quad\text{for every }\tau\in\mathcal T.
\]

Enlarging the tester family can separate classes but cannot merge ones already distinguished. A longer horizon gives this refinement when it includes the shorter tests; observing only a later endpoint can instead lose distinctions through mixing. Substitution inside a larger process requires the tests to include its relevant contexts and compositions. Equality of isolated output marginals does not suffice.

This is an optional predictive compression. It does not identify physical copies, histories or people, or remove noise that a bounded test cannot detect. A distinction outside the tester domain remains in the encompassing object. Approximation likewise needs an error statement. On a common interface, \(d_{\mathcal T}(p,q)=\sup_{\tau\in\mathcal T}\operatorname{TV}(\operatorname{Law}(\tau[p]),\operatorname{Law}(\tau[q]))\) is a pseudometric. The relation \(d_{\mathcal T}\leq\epsilon\) is generally not transitive and hence is not itself an exact quotient. If one compressed prediction approximates each of two laws within \(\epsilon\), the triangle inequality bounds their separation by \(2\epsilon\).

For a deterministic encoding \(q\) and update \(F\), faithful updating requires an abstract update \(\bar F\) satisfying \(q\circ F=\bar F\circ q\). The condition has stochastic and quantum counterparts. For a classical Markov partition, equal total transition probability into every block from all states within a block gives exact lumpability. Physical costs, accessible records and preparation conditions must also factor through any quotient used to represent them.

A simple example shows why these checks matter. Three signs \(X,Y,Z\) with law \(P_\theta(x,y,z)=(1+\theta xyz)/8\), \(-1\leq\theta\leq1\), all have zero means and identity covariance. The reversible transformation \((X,Y,Z)\mapsto(X,Y,XYZ)\) produces output mean \((0,0,\theta)\). The same input summary would have to predict different outputs under the same gate. Retaining the triple correlation repairs that missing distinction for this example. This demonstrates closure under relevant future operations, rather than a reason to award correlation an intrinsic score.

# 02 — Organization that changes what follows

## Outcomes that become enabling conditions

A catalyst changes subsequent reaction rates. A record guides construction.
A tool makes components for another tool. A community maintains the material
and informational conditions through which its members discover and build.
Each exemplifies a recursive relation: an outcome becomes a condition of
further outcomes.

**Generativity** is the mechanism proposed to expand lushness through such
organization. Its investigation follows two steps: identify construction,
maintenance and changes in enabled transformations, then measure their effect
on weighted continuation. The mechanism and its breadth consequence are thus
independently examinable.

## Access under the native law

Construction makes lawful transformations more accessible. If a nebula can
develop into a civilization, that civilization's developments already belong
to the nebula's encompassing continuation. At the later present, their
conditional accessibility has changed: their reliability, timing, material
requirements and dependence on favorable accidents.

The workshop embodies this change. Prepared tools and usable records can make
transformations routine that dispersed materials would rarely undergo within
the same interval. Even where eventual support agrees, different barriers and
couplings give different finite-time laws.

An access claim specifies a physical route. It includes preparation, the records
available to the apparatus, operating resources, elapsed time and the state left
for subsequent use. If preparation succeeds with probability p and use succeeds
with conditional probability q, the whole route succeeds with probability pq.
Repeated setup attempts change that probability only if failed attempts leave
retryable states and enough time and feedstock remain. A list of successful
programs is useful to an analyst; deploying the right one requires an embodied
selector with the necessary information.

The physical bill is a profile. Work, feedstock consumption, peak occupied space,
apparatus time and returned stocks have different composition rules. Sequential
expenditures can add while peak space takes a maximum; stocks can replenish.
Taking the best entry in each column from different implementations need not
describe any implementable combination. Access machinery retains the whole
witness so later processes inherit its actual residual conditions.

Access and immediate breadth have distinct roles. Making a fair binary outcome
certain reduces its branching entropy. A reliable constructor can nonetheless
enable broader later developments. The horizon profile reveals whether that
later gain occurs and how it compares with the investment required.

## A witnessed causal ladder

The programme follows a distinction into progressively richer physical uses:
an initial difference affects a later response; a record carries it; an apparatus
uses it; that apparatus produces another transformation; several processes
maintain one another's enabling conditions. This is a sequence of questions
that can branch, cycle or fail at any stage. Each step is checked through the
joint native law.

Several finite witnesses make these distinctions concrete:

* **Information and use.** Two three-record encodings reveal exactly the same
  source distinctions and have matched exhibited preparation counts. In the
  declared reversible gate panel, one exposes a target through one operation
  while the other requires two. The difference follows from the allowed gate
  functions, rather than a penalty assigned to an awkward encoding.
* **Information and repair.** The banks (u, u AND v, v) and (u, u, v) both
  determine the same pair (u,v). After a known-position erasure, one bank allows
  exact reconstruction at one of its three positions; the other allows it at
  two. These counts describe repairable locations, not a presumed distribution
  of physical faults. A parity encoding can protect all three locations when
  its preparation and decoder are available.
* **Activity and coupling.** Intact, damaged, erased and cycling arrangements
  can share the same fuel-limited event-count law while differing in whether
  an upstream distinction reaches a downstream response. Timing and occurrence
  alone then omit a consequential relationship.
* **Retention and construction.** Two distinct triggers can produce the same
  catalyst, losing all information about which trigger occurred. That catalyst
  can nevertheless enable a later input to control a later output. Loss of
  information about the trigger and creation of a new channel concern different
  physical relationships.

The [structural witnesses](../../research_notes/omega_v2/structural_probe_10min_report_v0.md)
and [coupling witness](../../research_notes/omega_v2/timing_volume_counterexample_report_v0.md)
establish these distinctions in their stated models. They show why organization
must remain in the carrier and why a selected information or occurrence summary
does not exhaust it. The breadth hypothesis asks how those retained mechanisms
change the probabilities of complete developments.

## Construction, maintenance and compounding

Construction produces conditions for further processes. Maintenance sustains
or restores them. Dependence identifies how a specified physical change alters
a downstream law. An **enabling witness** follows preparation, production and
the subsequent transformation, retaining its failures, delays and residuals.
A ready-made device establishes readiness; following its production establishes
a construction route.

For a realized prefix with outcome/residual law J(r,y|x), followed by a
record-dependent implemented continuation, the chance of a later event E is

\[
P_x(E)=\sum_{r,y}J(r,y\mid x)P_y^{\text{continuation}(r)}(E).
\]

The deadline and remaining resources in the second factor include what the
prefix consumed. Failure and unfinished cases stay in the sum with their actual
residuals. This elementary conditioning identity supplies the compositional
link: construction changes the distribution of situations from which later
development proceeds. A dependence experiment compares that whole route with a
physically specified replacement under matched conditions.

Several kinds of growth can then be distinguished. Reusing a catalyst supports
more reactions. Producing more catalyst amplifies a pathway. Producing a catalyst
for another transformation expands the enabled repertoire. Products that build
further constructors can compound that expansion across generations, subject
to resource supply, transport, inhibition and loss. Autocatalytic chemistry and
constructor theory offer neighboring approaches to these questions [R9, R10].

Compounding can have a lasting legacy after construction stops. A workshop may
finish expanding yet retain more usable machinery. The generative episode,
subsequent retained capability and indefinitely open-ended growth are distinct
physical achievements. Finite models can establish the first two. Chapter 03
explains the mixing conditions that limit persistent preparation advantages.

## Joint organization

Physical composition determines whether capabilities complement or obstruct
one another. Independent replication can distribute a robust function.
Specialization can open transformations that require several partners.
Redundancy can protect against local failure while remaining vulnerable to a
shared disturbance. Cooperation and competition describe these relations;
their effects depend on the actual continuation.

The same comparison applies to an engineered singleton and a plurality. The
relevant questions concern which complementary developments each can retain
and generate, at what cost, and with what vulnerability. A common outer boundary
does not settle those questions. Nor does matching information establish that
one physical occurrence can substitute for another in identity or valuation.

Mechanism controls must preserve this specificity. Removing a component changes
matter, energy, geometry and correlations. A controlled comparison states which
of those are matched. To distinguish recursive enabling from durability, for
example, compare products that enable new constructors with equally durable
products that support the same use operations but cannot construct further.
The native law then determines whether recursion creates additional breadth.

An economical derived organization graph can record construction, maintenance,
information, control and lineage relations, together with joint feasibility.
Its edges inherit physical contexts, witnesses, outcome laws and resource bills;
the labels describe demonstrated roles rather than primitive forces or rewards.
Multi-source relations are necessary where several conditions jointly enable a
process. Two independently tested edges do not establish that their operations
can run together.

Higher-order predictions need appropriate evidence. A function supported by
either of two components and a function independent of both can agree in the
intact case and after every single removal, yet disagree when both are removed.
Likewise, sufficient information at one location does not supply an actuator at
another. These distinctions constrain what can be inferred from pairwise graphs,
single knockouts or information summaries. A graph of mutual dependence becomes
a maintenance model only when its cycles have jointly feasible supply and loss.

## Damage and recovery

Damage changes the present and its future continuation. Losing a machine can
remove transformations; losing a design can make recovery depend on rediscovery;
losing a supplier can disable several otherwise intact processes. The same
physical account includes the possibilities for repair.

Recovery requires an implementable route, with apparatus, control, time and
remaining correlations. Restoring a function, continuing an individual's
lineage and reproducing a matching pattern answer different questions. First
return, correction by a deadline and maintained recovery likewise describe
different achievements. An eventual reset can arrive after important losses.

A transient intervention can still open continuation that would otherwise be
unavailable. Persistence permits reuse and additional construction, but a
contribution can matter within a finite episode. The encompassing development
retains that episode when later conditions change.

## From mechanism to synthesis

Finite witnesses support the causal direction in principle: arrangement changes
access, access changes the continuation law, and that law changes weighted
breadth. Recursive production extends the same mechanism by making enabling
conditions into products. The next synthesis connects those links across
richer physical systems and separates compounding from activity and storage.

The productive questions concern boundaries as well as successes: when does
transport erase an advantage, when does reversal overwhelm construction, when
do resources run out, and what do descendants retain after the initiating
structure disappears? Selection may favor cooperation, exploitation, repair
or inactivity in different regimes. Lushness compares the resulting possibility;
GE supplies the reasons for asking how it supports value.

## The filter, agency and higher organization

The **persistence filter** is the native selection of what remains capable of
consequence. Reproduction, inheritance and differential survival can change a
population; a nonreplicating organization can also grow or maintain itself.
The relevant unit can be a lineage, an institution or a pattern sustained through
replacement of its components. A sterile replicator, an exploiter and a
cooperative constructor can each pass particular filters. Their effects on the
encompassing field determine the comparison.

A **deformer** changes surrounding continuation through its organization.
Agency is a further case in which feedback and internal organization systematically
modulate those effects. A system can learn, revise its own mechanisms or engineer
its environment. Ashby's requisite-variety perspective clarifies a fork in this
development: regulation can improve by increasing appropriate responses or by
suppressing the variation it must face [R24]. Both can accomplish a local task;
their consequences for other processes and later correction can differ greatly.

Integration can constrain component behavior while expanding the joint repertoire.
Mutually supporting cycles therefore need a joint realization, with resource
supply and losses, before they count as a self-maintaining organization. The
vortex image captures pattern persistence through throughput. Its extension to
individuality and consciousness is developed in chapter 05, with those additional
claims explicitly separated from the physical recurrence.

## The next discriminating construction

The reactive probe provides a concrete mechanism-level foothold: local placement
changes subsequent sampled-history breadth under an autocatalytic law, and the

the entire comparison. A compact representation earns its use by preserving

# 03 — Lushness and weighted continuation breadth

## Size within the futuresfield

Lushness names the effective weighted extent of continuation. Its lush shape
comes from the physical composition of possibilities: what can follow, with
what weight, and how one development enables others. Generativity changes that
shape by changing the continuation law experienced from a present.

Extent can be expressed as an effective number, a profile across horizons and
scales, or a justified partial comparison. A literal geometric volume is one
possible realization. The essential requirement is that equivalent descriptions
of the same physical development yield a consistent comparison.

Probability supplies weighting. An exhaustive set of alternatives has total
probability one, whether that weight is spread evenly across a million histories
or concentrated on a single history. An effective breadth captures their
unequal spread while preserving the native normalization.

## Classical complete-development breadth

At a declared classical resolution, let h=(x₁,…,xₙ) be a complete future sequence
from sufficient root x, with physical probability pₓ(h). The calibration is

\[
L_x(n)=\exp H(p_x),\qquad H(p)=-\sum_h p(h)\log p(h).
\]

This is Shannon effective diversity, or perplexity [R6]. Natural logarithms give
the exponential displayed; entropy in bits uses 2 as its base. N equiprobable
histories give L=N. Three equally weighted alternatives give L=3, whereas weights
(.9,.05,.05) give approximately 1.48. Both have support three and total weight one.

For a history extension e, p(h,e)=p(h)q(e|h). The chain rule gives

\[
\log L(n+1)-\log L(n)=\sum_h p(h)H(q(\cdot|h)).
\]

Successive fair binary developments give L(n)=2ⁿ. A terminal outcome can retain
its mass through a deterministic absorbing continuation, contributing zero new
entropy. Renormalizing the remaining branches instead evaluates continuation
conditioned on survival.

The physical resolution determines the alternatives. Distinct update labels
that describe one physical event belong to one alternative; different physical
developments may remain distinct even when they have the same endpoint. For
uniformly selected events, log M equals the conditional event entropy. Identifying
that quantity with resolved state-history entropy additionally requires that the
state description distinguish those events, or that the physical history retain
their additional distinctions explicitly.

Concurrency requires the same discipline. If two update strings are merely
serial descriptions of one concurrent physical development, their ordering
must not manufacture alternatives. If their timing or intermediate interactions
are physically different, the distinction can matter. The native adapter and
its physical equivalence determine which case holds. Fixed synchronous updates
and sampled CTMC histories provide explicit finite conventions; they do not
already solve relativistic refoliation invariance.

## Present rooting and horizon

Suppose x→a→c and x→b→c are two resolved developments. Their prefixes remain
different from root x. At c their residual continuation agrees. Thus the same
physical process supports both an original-root complete-history question and
a fresh question from its later present.

Cumulative classical history breadth grows or remains constant under ordinary
extension. Damage can slow its subsequent growth relative to another
preparation. Earlier excess can remain in the total after the fresh advantage
has disappeared. Horizon profiles expose these differences and any ranking
crossovers without compressing them into one lifetime statistic.

The workshop illustrates the distinction. Construction may first concentrate
weight on a reliable product, then enable many later developments. Saturation
can end construction while leaving a larger operating repertoire. Investment,
compounding and the retained benefit each occupy a different part of the profile.

## Configuration, references and mixing

For a finite homogeneous Markov kernel K, let g(x)=H(K(x,·)). Then

\[
\ell_n(x):=\log L_x(n)=\sum_{t=0}^{n-1}(K^t g)(x).
\]

Configuration determines immediate branching; dynamics determines which
configurations are subsequently visited. This makes the effect of catalysis,
repair and construction on classical breadth calculable under one native law.
For a stationary reference π, the preparation excess is

\[
\Delta\ell_n(x)=\sum_{t=0}^{n-1}(\delta_xK^t-\pi)g.
\]

The reference averages separately rooted logbreadths. It keeps uncertainty about
the initial root separate from subsequent branching. For a continuous-time model
sampled at interval Δt, K=exp(ΔtQ) describes the sampled sequence; an event-resolved
history requires its timing and event resolution as well.

If K is finite, irreducible and aperiodic, the excess converges to a constant
c(x), and ℓₙ(x)=nh+c(x)+o(1), with h=πg. A multiplicative breadth advantage
exp c(x) can remain while the extra growth rate vanishes. Fresh-continuation
advantages decay as the preparation mixes. Chapter 09 proves these statements.

Finite compounding and long metastable episodes remain meaningful under this
boundary. Indefinitely accumulating preparation advantage requires dynamics
that escape common finite mixing. Distinct persistent sectors, unbounded
configuration spaces or suitably non-forgetting dynamics may permit it.
External drive alone is insufficient: driven systems can also mix under a
fixed law. These are conditions on the claim being tested, not requirements
that useful generativity last forever.

## Calibration and joint structure

A deterministic exact classical root has one complete alternative and therefore

Thermal continuation supplies a baseline profile matched to law, material and

# 04 — Gradient Ethics: valuers, consequence and conditional reasons

## Value and its conditions

Gradient Ethics begins with two commitments. **Value requires valuers:** value
depends on processes through which distinctions matter. **Normativity is
conditional:** reasons arise through valuing and the conditions under which
what is valued can exist, continue or develop. Physics describes those
conditions and their dependencies.

If a person values another's flourishing, that commitment gives reasons to
attend to the bodily, social and environmental conditions of that flourishing.
Discovering dependence on unfamiliar people or organisms can extend the reach
of the commitment. The dependency was present before it was recognized. Better
knowledge can therefore change what the commitment reasonably requires.

GE extends concern to the possibility of value where its forms remain uncertain.
This inclusive orientation goes beyond any particular person's existing
preferences. Its justification concerns the conditions of valuation; motivation
and the authority to enforce a judgment remain further questions. The physical
programme can clarify those conditions independently of the Alpha–Omega
cosmological interpretation.

A **valuer** is a locus or process for which distinctions matter in the sense
at issue in valuation. Its final demarcation remains open. The inquiry admits
every consequential process as a potential valuer, including unfamiliar,
nonconscious and transient organizations. Admission keeps the physical field
intact while the account of value develops. It does not establish that every
process actually values, assign equal standing or make expressed preferences
authoritative. A destructive agent can still be a valuer; excluding it by
definition would make the ethical comparison circular. Moral patienthood also
need not depend on effective agency.

Conditional reasons can extend beyond an actor's recognized interests as hidden
dependencies become known. GE additionally adopts concern for the possibility
of value across affected loci. An actor's commitment to one thing does not
logically entail equal concern for everything. The inclusive commitment is
stated openly, and an orthogonal actor may reject it. Structural justification,
motivation and enforceable power are consequently separate parts of the problem.

## Fertile substrate under uncertainty

The substrate supporting valuers thereby supports value itself. When the forms
of possible value are incompletely known, preserving and expanding that substrate
preserves opportunities for value to arise. This is the flowerbed intuition:
keep it expansively fertile for forms of life and valuation that may not yet
be recognized.

Lushness is the programme's proxy for this substrate. Its breadth encompasses
the wider physical possibility from which particular valuers and achievements
can emerge. Thermal matter remains part of it. A thinly organized substrate
can still support the later development of richer organization, just as the
materials outside a workshop can become components of future construction.

Generativity connects this broad substrate to its development. Construction,
cooperation, learning, maintenance and recovery can alter which further
processes become accessible. In-principle mechanism results motivate a stronger
synthesis: explaining when those effects compound, how far they extend and
which physical arrangements sustain them.

As the conditions of value become better characterized, steering can become
more discriminating. Value occurs within physical frames and their developments.
Knowing more about an inhabitant's dependencies lets an evaluator represent
consequences that a coarser description missed. The encompassing account keeps
the costs, probabilities and effects on others visible as local descriptions
are refined.

The intended substrate comparison remains distinct from any current estimator.
Classical history perplexity measures weighted alternatives precisely. Reliable
achievement may concentrate those weights; newly introduced failure modes may
spread them. Such cases test the estimator's adequacy for the declared scope.
Chapter 08 specifies an adversarial procedure capable of rejecting an
operational proxy without abandoning inquiry into the substrate itself.

## Harm and recovery within continuation

An injury changes the physical present: capabilities may disappear, dependencies
may weaken, participation may narrow and recovery may become costly. From an
earlier root, the injury and its aftermath belong to the complete development.
From the injured present, the relevant question concerns what is still possible
and how restoration can occur.

The workshop example makes the physical distinctions concrete. Restoring one
output may leave the machine unable to repair itself. Restoring its design
record may still leave no apparatus capable of building it. A dependable repair
route differs from a theoretically possible rescue of negligible probability.
Preparation, timing, resources and residual organization determine those futures.

Where individuals are concerned, recovery also raises questions of function,
lineage and experience. A replacement can reproduce a function while belonging
to another physical trajectory. Suffering and pleasure have significance for
valuers that a count of reports or random transitions can miss. The encompassing
comparison must represent the affected processes; a local increase in failure
variety alone is an incomplete account of their continuation.

A valuer's functional organization, phenomenal experience and ethical standing
need related but distinct characterizations. Chapter 05 develops these questions
as research connections. GE's substrate orientation allows unfamiliar potential
valuers to remain in consideration while that characterization develops.

Obstruction, congestion and lost coupling have a physical meaning before the
ethical verdict: a specified response or recovery route becomes unavailable or
less accessible. Whether that loss is justified depends on the encompassing
consequence. Sacrificing a cell can preserve an organism; suppressing a dangerous
route can permit other processes to coexist. Thermodynamic irreversibility,
erasure of a logical record and inability to recover a particular organization
must each be assessed in the model that supports the claim.

## Cooperation, plurality and power

Cooperation can create joint capabilities unavailable to isolated participants.
Specialization, shared records and repair can expand access. Competition can
expose alternatives and resist capture. Either relation can also consume
resources or destroy complementary processes. Their significance follows from
the continuations they generate in the encompassing system.

Diversity can support complementary exploration and recovery; a coordinated
structure can make some of those functions more reliable. Comparing them requires
following what is retained, newly enabled and foreclosed. An engineered system
that reproduced the relevant continuation of a plurality and added further
possibilities would deserve consideration on that basis. Establishing such a
comparison requires physical evidence about substitution, vulnerability and
the persistence of the affected individuals.

Power also changes the evidence available to participants. A controller can
monopolize repair, suppress records or make its own survival appear indispensable.
The apparent gain within its local description may coexist with a broader loss.
Tracing those effects through the physical frames gives GE a way to investigate
capture and exclusion without treating a controller's scope as the whole world.

**Excision** secures a local position by removing surrounding continuation and
its routes of regeneration. **Terminal solitude** names the limiting closure
in which relevant generative valuation has been eliminated and cannot, or
robustly will not, be regenerated in the stated regime. One integrated system
can contain rich internal valuation; its single outer boundary is insufficient
to establish such closure. Conversely, many nominally separate actors can
share one tightly captured future.

A plurality's defensive advantage depends on coordination, timing, resources
and the distribution of power. Severe asymmetry can defeat it. Defensive reasons
do not depend on proving that an attacker secretly shares the threatened parties'
values. Institutions that enforce protection also enter the comparison as
deformers with their own incentives, mistakes and capacity for capture.

## Rights, justice and virtues

Rights-like protections are proposed responses to recurring vulnerabilities
among valuers. Their practical force can exceed what an individual calculation
recognizes because they preserve conditions and dependencies outside that
calculation's scope.

| Protection or practice | Continuation it helps protect |
| --- | --- |
| Bodily integrity and continuity | The organization through which a person acts, experiences and maintains a life. |
| Consent and participation | Affected people's information and authority concerning changes to their continuation. |
| Inquiry and reliable records | Discovery of hidden dependencies and correction of mistaken judgments. |
| Due process and contestation | Resistance to irreversible error, bias and capture. |
| Exit and association | Alternative relationships and routes out of destructive control. |
| Repair and restitution | Restoration of damaged capacities and their supporting conditions. |

These are structural hypotheses about why the protections work. Their conflicts
and exceptions require the same attention to consequence. Defense or containment
can protect valuers from a process destroying their continuation, while carrying
its own costs and risks of error.

Justice concerns the terms under which coupled valuers coexist and resolve
conflict, including unequal power and the reliability of institutions. Virtues
can be studied as durable policies within those relations. Honesty supports
usable signals; humility supports correction; courage enables necessary action
under risk; prudence attends to delayed effects; restraint limits avoidable
foreclosure. The research asks when these patterns explain the preservation
and development of valuable conditions.

## Sacrifice and bounded judgment

The programme's stringent formulation licenses sacrifice for a greater good
only where the lushness comparison justifying it is actually solved. It places
a high burden on proposals to destroy existing valuers in exchange for a claimed
increase in substrate. A complete-comparison reading and a graded burden rising
with irreversibility, scale and uncertainty remain two interpretations to develop.

Urgency is itself consequential. Waiting can close a rescue route; acting can
cause irreversible loss. ODT therefore compares intervention, delay and further
investigation as physically realized developments. Where knowledge supports
only a partial ordering, practical arbitration must state its authority and
what remains unresolved.

The programme also permits testing a candidate lushness comparison without
adding separate ethical bonuses, to see what organization and behavior it
favors. This gives the substrate hypothesis a demanding empirical role. Its
results can support or challenge the chosen comparison rather than being
predetermined by imposed protections.

## From the substrate hypothesis to discriminating cases

The next ethical synthesis follows the physical mechanism into cases where
intuitive judgments can be stated independently of the readout. Compare the
effects of destroying a repair network, preserving it, replacing it with a
claimed equivalent, or adding random failure modes while holding the relevant
resources and law fixed. Track affected participation, recovery and future
valuation alongside breadth. The observations can then distinguish a deficient
physical description from a genuine failure of the substrate proxy at that scope.

The programme must also address aggregation, rare consequential possibilities,
experience and sacrifice. Head counts do not settle a joint continuation
comparison. A small branch retains its native weight rather than being inflated
by rhetorical stakes. Describing suffering's causal role does not settle whether
experience supplies additional normative significance. These questions remain
open with specific routes forward: improved physical comparison, explicit
bounded-decision analysis and a better account of valuing and experience.

For artificial systems, the positive hypothesis is sustained capability under
novelty. Preserving correction, usable experimental variety and the ability to
revise a failed model may support achievements a brittle optimizer forecloses.
A fair test compares matched resources, changing conditions, later capabilities
and recoverability. Specialization and decisive commitment can also succeed;
the experiment must allow that result. An ethical argument that survives better
understanding must remain answerable to these actual consequences.

Lushness remains the intended arbitrator because the inquiry concerns the
conditions for value, including forms still unknown. Conditional commitments
give that inquiry its ethical purpose. Better physical and experiential
understanding makes its application more informed, connecting the broad
flowerbed intuition to the particulars of life within it.

# 05 — Individuals, consciousness and experience

## Individuals as trajectories

A whirlpool persists while its water changes. An organism replaces molecules; a person learns, forgets and changes their capacities. These examples suggest that an individual is not simply a fixed inventory of material or a pattern that can be copied. It is an organized trajectory: a physically realized process whose organization is maintained and transformed through causal history.

On this trajectory account, a pattern describes what can recur, while a trajectory tracks how a particular occurrence is produced, sustained and changed. Systems that match in measured function may still differ in origin and causal lineage. Records can guide reconstruction, while questions of maintained organization, individual persistence and experiential continuity remain distinct.

These distinctions matter in familiar cases. Ordinary growth changes the person’s material and character while preserving an ongoing trajectory. A pause in activity need not end that trajectory if the organization that maintains it remains intact. A copied pattern can support a capable successor without thereby sharing the source’s history. A repair may restore a function, preserve lineage, or plausibly restore the individual; those are different achievements. The trajectory view gives a reason to treat destructive scanning and reconstruction as replacement rather than survival, but that verdict is philosophical and remains open to challenge.

Vortices make persistence through change vivid. In suitable physical settings, circulation or topological winding gives a stable description across transformations and identifies classes of maintained organization. Tracking an individual also involves its particular occurrence and lineage; a crystal, organism and computational process may preserve different features.

The originator also entertains a stronger possibility: as identity becomes more densely organized within the futuresfield—the structure of physical possibility and continuation—its development might approach a singularity-like limit. This remains an open research ambition. “Density,” “vortex” and “singularity” need mathematical and physical definitions before the proposal can yield dynamics. Its relation to consciousness is speculative and awaits a characterization of the futuresfield and its universal possibility structure. No fixed threshold, gravitational claim, or equation of singularity with identity or consciousness follows. The idea is downstream of the physical programme; the programme does not depend on it.

## Continuity, recovery and self-modification

An identity claim specifies what is preserved through which transformations.
Six relations deserve separate records: functional readiness, maintained physical
organization, reconstruction from a specification, causal lineage, numerical
identity and phenomenal continuity. A prediction-equivalence test can establish
substitutability within its domain while leaving two physical occurrences
distinct. A gain elsewhere can compensate for a loss without restoring the
particular individual who was lost.

Self-modification makes these distinctions operational. Replacing a memory
substrate, distributing a process, merging organizations or changing a policy
can preserve some capacities and break others. A useful analysis tracks lineage,
memory, commitments, embodied competence and relationships separately. The
trajectory interpretation gives these facts a proposed account of individuality;
it does not turn a single retained checksum or response profile into a soul.

Recovery likewise has several strengths. Some lawful route may restore a
function; one physically available policy may reliably restore it across declared
disturbances; or the repair may establish conditions for continued maintenance.
The apparatus, knowledge, resources and delay belong to the claim. Global unitary
information retention does not supply a feasible local reversal when information
has dispersed into inaccessible environmental correlations.

First reset, corrected occupancy at a deadline and permanent correction are
different properties. A process may reset repeatedly yet never remain corrected.
Correction may also arrive only after an irreversible loss. In a finite fixed
Markov model, a closed corrected set supplies a sufficient certificate for
continued correction after entry. This is a property of that law and target;
the ethical relevance of the target and identity after restoration remain
additional questions.

These distinctions support an inquiry into regeneration: repair can restore
not just today's output but the capacity to build further repair and construction.
The causal relation is investigated in the physical model, and its effect on
lushness is assessed through the continuation it creates.

The proposed identity or “soul” invariant names the still-unknown relation
preserved through such changes, rather than a discovered conserved scalar.
Fission is a discriminating case: two successors may preserve different aspects
of one predecessor without the account selecting a uniquely identical survivor.
The programme can investigate continuity and its consequences while leaving
that metaphysical verdict open. Resolving personal identity is not a prerequisite
for admitting the successors as potential valuers.

## Consciousness as embodied coordination

The functional account begins with a practical feature of living systems: perception and action must work across signals arriving at different times. Sensory channels have distinct delays, bodily states change continuously, and action cannot wait for a perfectly synchronized picture. An organism needs a usable present that integrates relevant signals, memory and anticipation. The proposed functional description treats consciousness as a temporoperceptual coordinator: an embodied organization that makes such integration available for perception, action and ongoing self-maintenance.

This proposal has a second, equally important side. Consciousness, in its functional role, also engineers and reconfigures the disparate embodied causal processes that compose an individual. It coordinates processes that deform the individual’s continuation—sensory pathways, bodily regulation, memory, learned response and action—so that their combined organization can guide what happens next. “Engineering” here means a physically realized capacity to organize, recruit, inhibit or alter processes, not a separate inner operator. There is no homunculus outside the body performing integration. The coordinator is the organized activity of the processes themselves, and can be distributed across them.

The proposal extends functionalist accounts of mind by connecting temporal integration of perception with embodied control. Researchers can investigate this functional description through causal organization, behaviour and internal regulation. How such organization relates to experience is the κ question developed below.

Several lines of research motivate parts of this picture. Eagleman and Sejnowski’s work on postdiction examines how later-arriving information can alter the perceptual interpretation of an earlier event. This supports the idea that the experienced present is assembled over time rather than read off from a single instant. [Eagleman and Sejnowski (2000)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10720334/)

Dehaene, Kerszberg and Changeux model access to consciousness through a neuronal global-workspace architecture, in which distributed processing can make selected information broadly available. Their model links conscious access with coordination among specialized processes. [Dehaene, Kerszberg and Changeux (1998)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9826734/)

Seth, Suzuki and Critchley develop an interoceptive predictive account in which perception of the body involves predictions and prediction errors. This work helps connect experience to the regulation and interpretation of bodily signals. [Seth, Suzuki and Critchley (2012)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2011.00395/full)

Together, these sources motivate temporal integration, broad availability and bodily regulation as components of an embodied coordination architecture. They support investigating its functions; whether it constitutes phenomenal experience remains open.

## Physical perspectives and phenomenal experience

The vocabulary begins with awareness as phenomenal presence. The originator's
speculative picture distinguishes bare awareness, phenomenal content organized
by physical machinery, and mind as we know it with memory and an ongoing sense
of self. Neither an indivisible smallest awareness unit nor a universal threshold
is established. Epistemically, seeming is the starting point of this inquiry;
ontologically, whether awareness is fundamental is a separate question.

The physical programme describes situated processes, their information access and lawful continuations. Its account of agency and perspective is functional: a thermostat directs outcomes according to its state, while conscious experience remains a further question. Organized sensitivity, physical records and memory relations can explain aspects of function and continuity without settling phenomenal continuity.

The relation between physical organization and experience is left open and represented schematically by κ. κ might identify experience with some physical organization already described; it might add a correspondence between physical perspectives and phenomenal occurrences; or it might propose a physically consequential interaction. These are different options. A correspondence need not change physical predictions. An interaction would need a specified physical model and evidence that distinguishes its effects. In every case, merely writing κ supplies no measure of experience and no established increase in value or lushness.

This leaves three broad positions available. A functionalist may hold that the right physical organization constitutes experience, with no additional psychophysical ingredient. A dual-aspect or dualist proposal may hold that physical and phenomenal descriptions are related but not identical. A consciousness-first position may treat awareness as more fundamental and physical organization as its articulation. The physical continuation programme does not select among these metaphysical views, provided they leave its physical claims intact. If a view changes physical dynamics, that change must be stated and assessed as physics.

The originator’s preferred bridge across this gap is a form of cosmopsychism: one encompassing awareness is related to distinct experiences through situated perspectives. On this proposal, individuation belongs to the physical and experiential perspectives rather than requiring many fundamental awarenesses. It is offered as the bridge past κ with the fewest additional assumptions, alongside a fully functionalist interpretation that remains compatible with the programme. This is a parsimony rationale, not a proof that cosmopsychism is uniquely minimal or true. The decombination problem—how one encompassing awareness is differentiated into distinct subjects or perspectives—remains open. Physical perspectives supply a candidate structure for individuation, but whether this solves the problem is not established.

Phenomenal continuity and trajectory identity remain related but distinct. A continuing physical trajectory informs an account of experience without settling it, just as reproducing a functional pattern leaves open whether a subject persists through copying or repair. Further work must specify how κ relates experiences across time, including branching, interruption and reconstruction.

## Open routes for the correspondence

Page's Sensible Quantum Mechanics provides a close mathematical neighbor:
positive awareness operators relate a quantum state to a measure on sets of
experiences [R26]. Those operators supply additional psychophysical structure.
They are not identified merely by finding physical record projectors, and a
measure of experiences alone does not supply their temporal continuity. The
route forward would specify the proposed physical perspectives, their relation
to experiential sets and any sequential structure needed by the identity account.

The minimal record-forming interaction has also been proposed as a possible
lower boundary for inhabitable perspectives. The current physical work makes
record formation a concrete subject of study, while leaving habitation open.
The next step on this speculative route is to identify a reason why a specified
record architecture should satisfy the proposed correspondence. Decoherence
alone does not establish awareness, and redundant records alone do not establish
many independent subjects.

A physically interacting correspondence would require a different kind of
advance: specify a mechanism and predictions beyond the physical account already
in use. Endogenous fields, collective quantum effects and protected coherence
have been discussed as possible places to investigate organization. Their
ordinary physical occurrence would not itself identify a phenomenal coupling.
A proposed mechanism must resolve its degrees of freedom, timescale, resources
and causal constraints. Objective-collapse theories would change the unitary
Everettian premise; investigating biological coherence does not require that
change. These routes remain downstream research possibilities rather than an
adopted neural or quantum mechanism.

The vortical-singularity conjecture has a prior task of its own: define the
continuation geometry, the quantity alleged to become dense and the relevant
limiting behavior. Only then could it yield an identity or consciousness
prediction. A successful characterization of ordinary coherent organization
would already be progress even if no singularity emerged.

## Role in the programme

The consciousness proposals identify connections for later research once the
physical foundations are more mature. They are not prerequisites for defining
classical breadth or investigating generative mechanisms. Their purpose here is
to keep promising functional and phenomenal questions connected to that work.

## What follows for inquiry

The functional and phenomenal questions can be pursued without waiting for a metaphysical resolution. Researchers can test whether particular forms of temporal integration and embodied reconfiguration explain perception, control and continuity. They can compare systems by their causal organization while remaining careful about what those comparisons establish. Tests should distinguish contributions to conscious access from claims about phenomenal experience.

Nor does the account require that every physical perspective be globally aware, or that awareness selects one branch of a completed quantum history. A block-Everett interpretation, if adopted, describes physical development without an external spotlight choosing which occurrence becomes real. Phenomenal habitation remains an additional hypothesis. No claim of quantum immortality follows: survival of some physical continuation would not establish numerical identity, phenomenal continuity, or the irrelevance of losses across other continuations.

The open status of κ does not make experience unimportant. If experience adds distinctions that a physical consequence account omits, a fuller account of value may need to represent them. If experience is constituted by physical organization, that organization already matters and should not be counted a second time as an independent contribution. Either way, the right next step is clearer characterization: specify the physical organization under study, the experiential claim being made, and the evidence that could distinguish it from alternatives.

Omega therefore treats individuals as organized trajectories, consciousness functionally as embodied temporal coordination and reconfiguration, and phenomenal experience as a question not yet settled by that functional account. This preserves a path from physical description to minds without pretending that the path is already complete. The metaphysical horizon remains open; the work of describing how embodied processes organize a usable present can proceed within it.

# 06 — Omega Decision Theory: physically situated choice

Omega Decision Theory (ODT) asks which physically realizable variations of an embedded decision process correspond to choosing differently, and how their complete consequences should be compared. Its ambition extends beyond an audit checklist. It seeks a decision theory grounded in the physical organization of the decision itself. The counterfactual construction needed to complete that theory remains open.

The futuresfield is prior to the decision problem. Organisms, controllers, institutions, plans and evaluators are structures within native development. An action menu describes a situated part of physical possibility; it does not define that possibility. ODT studies implementation, counterfactual dependence and comparison in service of the programme's intended arbitrator: lushness of the resulting continuation. GE supplies the conditional reason for using that substrate comparison. The implementation machinery can be described abstractly, but its intended evaluation here is not an unspecified external objective. Completing the lushness comparison is therefore central to completing ODT; counterfactual matching is a separate construction it also needs.

## Decision lineage, information and plans

A **decision lineage** J is the physically persisting organization carrying the decision through the relevant interval: a motor-selection process, learned disposition, planning architecture or institutional rule. Persistence can include learning, turnover, repair and self-modification. The proposed boundary must be justified by the decision's physical organization rather than the verdict wanted.

Available information can include classical records, analogue variables and coherent quantum degrees of freedom. Recording requires evidence where a record is claimed. Control does not require an already classical record; possessing a record does not establish control over what it describes.

A **plan** is a physically implementable organization of response to available information. A classical policy can map information histories to actions. Coherent control instead requires quantum operations and memory and need not admit an equivalent classical policy. Plans include waiting, investigating, committing, delegating and modifying the decision architecture. Their consequences include these activities and their costs.

Three objects remain distinct:

1. **Native continuation:** development generated by the actual sufficient present and law.
2. **Implementable variations:** alternative operations or organizations the lineage can realize under specified physical conditions.
3. **Counterfactual matching:** the rule specifying which conditions vary and which remain matched when implementations are compared.

A deterministic exact root has one actual trajectory. Studying the same mechanism under different lawful inputs or preparations remains possible, but varies those conditions. It must not be described as multiple continuations of an unchanged sufficient root. Conversely, stochastic alternatives need not be controllable choices. An internal malfunction can be possible without being selectable by the lineage.

## Physical implementation requirements

An admissible implementation needs a witness: a model of how the proposed change occurs, retaining the native law, constraints, resources and consequential interactions. The lineage must participate in realizing the operation in the claimed way. External rewiring establishes another system's influence, not automatically the lineage's own control. Delegation can be its plan when arranging and using that delegation belongs to its decision process.

Locality constrains implementation; it does not justify instantaneously replacing a register while omitting the required apparatus and correlations. A costly sequence reaching a state is not evidence for a free present edit to that state. Equivalent descriptions must not add options. A coarse quotient must preserve the relevant implementation and consequence distinctions, not merely an endpoint law.

The construction problem is to determine endogenous control, lineage boundaries and which surrounding changes an implementation requires. Finite models can address it by representing the controller, alternatives and apparatus explicitly, then checking which distinctions survive changes of description. A declaration of minimal deformation needs a physical criterion; mere register count depends on representation.

An abstract model also owes a connection to its physical implementation. A
simulation relation can supply one sufficient form of evidence: each proposed
operation has a concrete counterpart preserving the continuation property being
claimed. The obligation differs for existence of a route, success with a stated
probability, and reliable recovery across adversarial disturbances. An optimistic
abstraction that retains one successful route cannot establish robust recovery.
This is where joint feasibility, accessible information and the full resource
bill become requirements on a decision argument.

## Counterfactual matching and anchors

A **matching rule** m identifies the physical variations instantiating compared plans and specifies the treatment of surrounding conditions, shared causes and records. Consequences must develop forward under the law, without downstream edits to obtain a preferred answer. Realization costs remain represented.

An **anchor** is a proposed section rooting the compared implementations. Present-edit matching asks for alternatives lawful from that section onward. History-consistent matching additionally requires an appropriate lawful common past. The first does not guarantee implementability; the second does not always select one section.

The proposed **latest history-consistent divergence** seeks the latest admissible section at which the compared lineage alternatives separate under the chosen history-consistency requirements. It follows the organization carrying the alternative while limiting unrelated earlier variation. A policy formed before a prediction can have different dependence from a fresh decision afterward.

This is a candidate matching rule, not a theorem. Several incomparable sections may qualify; continuous settings may have no latest qualifying point. Remote divergence can carry unrelated differences, and matching them away may require an additional choice. These ambiguities must remain visible.

Under a forward-deterministic law, equal sufficient global states earlier imply equal global states later. Different later states therefore cannot arise from an identical earlier global state under that law. This does not establish that distinctive counterfactual reasoning requires indeterminism. Everettian universal evolution is itself unitary: relative/decoherent branching and alternative global-state evolutions are different levels of description [R7]. Quantum anchors require an explicit construction rather than an appeal to Everett branches.

Present-edit matching also does not by itself prove equivalence to causal decision theory. That limit requires a specified intervention model and treatment of dependencies. With a justified act-level causal intervention and expected utility, ODT recovers the corresponding act-based causal decision calculation. Other matching rules require separate assessment.

## Conditioning is not implementation

Restricting native histories to those in which a plan occurs generally gives a conditional distribution. It need not describe the effects of implementing the plan.

For example, let an unobserved physical condition Z be equally likely zero or one. Action C agrees with Z with probability .9, while outcome Y is determined by Z and unaffected by C. Then P(Y=1|C=1)=.9. A physically modeled actuator setting C=1 without altering Z leaves P(Y=1)=.5. Selecting histories by action has selected its common cause. Native weighting does not remove that confounding.

An implementation can legitimately change shared causes or earlier records where its realization requires that change. The task is to identify the dependence, not stipulate that every comparison holds all earlier circumstances fixed. Newcomb, lesion and correlated-agent cases test the matching construction; their answers must not be built into the lineage definition.

## Complete-continuation comparison

For each admissible matching rule m and plan π, let Ωᵐπ denote the resulting complete continuation object. The intended evaluation relation ≽ is the justified lushness comparison of these objects. It may presently be partial, and a quantum continuation can retain coherence without becoming a classical branch distribution. Lushness supplies ODT's substantive arbitrator as a proxy for valuer-supporting substrate; a particular entropy diagnostic is not automatically the completed comparison.

Consequences include transient achievements, information, waiting, losses, recovery and effects on other systems. A shared endpoint does not make intervening developments equal. This does not prescribe summing an arbitrary score at every time step: the full object comes first, and a cumulative statistic needs its own justification. Finite horizons expose a comparison profile. An uninformative or divergent infinite limit does not settle it.

For finite plan sets, maximal options can be reported for each justified m. Changed recommendations across m are a finding; a stronger recommendation may survive an entire justified matching family. The evaluation still needs grounds. Physics constrains consequences; GE supplies the conditional ethical commitments of chapter 04.

Information needs no separate bonus. An observe-then-act plan contains its apparatus, conditional responses, delay and opportunity costs. Commitment matters through its effects, including others' responses. Reframing after successful preparation cannot remove its original probability and cost. Normalizing a local institutional frame likewise cannot erase affected people from the encompassing account.

## GE-ODT: breadth, access and maintenance

Value, value-capable substrate and lushness remain distinct. Lushness retains its meaning as weighted breadth. Access, persistence, resources and influence can explain or diagnose a comparison without becoming an unannounced replacement definition.

An access profile can describe reaching a declared physical target by a deadline, arrival times and resource costs at a specified reliability. The target must earn its relation to the question being asked. A proposed route uses organized control and resource requirements to identify candidate enabling structures. Its next test is whether those structures actually maintain or generate further continuation, including controls with equally strong influence but destructive consequences. Threshold sensitivity alone would not establish that they characterize valuer prerequisites.

First arrival differs from maintenance and regeneration. If τ is first arrival, integrating P(τ≤t) up to T measures expected time remaining after arrival, even if the target immediately disappears. It does not measure continued occupancy or retained enabling. Chapter 09 gives the identity and separate diagnostics. These are forward queries from one root, not time-averaged substitutes for the present.

Noise can obstruct access or enable exploration and escape. Routing an existing energy flow can help while also introducing dependencies and costs. Faster reversible relaxation does not prove that every target is reached sooner or with less free energy. Noise, capture, accelerated spending and conservation are cases to compare, not predetermined winners or losers.

## Bounded judgment: ODT0–ODT2

Bounded ODT uses three practical stages:

1. **ODT0 — License:** check whether implementation, matching and consequence models support the requirements justified in the situation.
2. **ODT1 — Compare:** retain supported comparisons and their dependence on scope, matching, horizon and unresolved effects.
3. **ODT2 — Arbitrate:** where action remains necessary despite incomparability, state the authority and rationale of the rule used.

Demonstrated violation differs from insufficient justification. Emergency compromise does not prove every requirement was met. Where lushness cannot yet settle a necessary decision, institutions, negotiated priorities, lotteries or declared scores can provide provisional procedures. These manage incomplete knowledge; they do not replace lushness as the programme's intended substantive arbitrator. This layer does not settle every counterfactual or Pascal-style problem.

Consider repairing a shared water system. A fast proprietary controller, a slower distributed repair and further investigation produce different continuations of supply, health, knowledge and dependency. ODT represents how each plan is implemented and how environmental conditions are matched, compares supported consequences, and handles urgent unresolved tradeoffs. Neither plurality nor speed is an automatic bonus.

Stable preferences support a limited consistency result. If every voluntary revision strictly improves the evaluated complete plan, strict preference is transitive and irreflexive, and pure resource loss is strictly worse, a finite sequence cannot return to the original plan with only an additional loss. This is a conditional preference theorem, not a physical derivation of rationality. Plans can contain learning; changes in beliefs or evaluation between revisions require separate dynamic-consistency analysis.

## Optimization, uncertainty and correction

For one exact numerical objective and fixed action set, restricting to a nonempty
subset cannot increase the attainable maximum. A protection can agree with that
maximum or improve a bounded decision maker's performance under error. When
adopting a protection changes trust, coordination or others' responses, it changes
the continuation being evaluated and must be represented as an implemented plan.
The programme seeks sound optimization of the encompassing object, while treating
its current measurements as fallible.

Three sources of sensitivity should remain distinguishable: changes in physical
circumstances, changes in the proposed comparison, and changes in available
information or approximation. Map the boundaries at which the recommendation
changes. Counting how many parameter settings favor an option is not a probability
of correctness without a justified measure on that parameter space. The same
discipline applies to rare, high-stakes outcomes: retain their native weights,
the evidence for their consequences and the possibility of counting incompatible
scenarios twice. No automatic cutoff or solution to Pascal-style problems follows.

For alignment and governance, these obligations expose errors a powerful optimizer
can otherwise exploit: treating an inaccessible repair as available, replacing a
participant's requirements with convenient records, or combining separately
feasible promises that cannot be delivered together. Evaluating the construction,
information, control and maintenance relations helps diagnose such errors. Their
ethical significance remains grounded in the affected continuation, including
the system's capacity to discover and correct its own mistakes.

## Research status

Next tests should distinguish physical mechanism from counterfactual convention: ordinary implemented control; deterministic preparation variation; a confounded action with and without an actuator; policy formation before and after prediction; and multiple candidate anchors. An access test should contrast first arrival, immediate loss, sustained maintenance and regeneration. No favourable Newcomb verdict or ethical ranking is a prerequisite for success.

An earlier finite decision prototype achieved 5/6 where direct optimization under the same guards achieved 35/36; a revised-plan stratum remained zero. The [original report](../../research_notes/omega_v2/lushness_decision_report_v0.md) remains evidence against that candidate, not validation of this rebuild. No new ODT probe has been run for this revision.

The central hypothesis is that physical organization and its lawful implementations constrain counterfactual dependence more tightly than an arbitrary choice of act or policy scope. Whether they determine it uniquely remains open. A justified construction or a precise obstruction would advance that question, while the bounded layer already helps keep decisions accountable to the continuations they affect.

# 07 — Alpha–Omega and the interpretive horizon

The physical programme describes reality through its lawful continuations, their composition, and the situated frames from which development can be encountered. Alpha–Omega interprets that account at a wider metaphysical scale, proposing a relation among generative possibility, lawful realization and completed manifestation. It adds an interpretive horizon; the ethical bridge remains value requiring valuers together with conditional normativity, so Gradient Ethics does not depend on Alpha–Omega.

The connections to philosophical and theological traditions are research hooks for later work as the physical foundations mature. They orient downstream inquiry rather than serve as evidence for the finite probes.

## One reality, differentiated within itself

The preferred metaphysical picture begins with one fundamental reality whose multiplicity is internal. Organisms, fields, histories, and perspectives are real differentiations of that reality. Unity does not mean sameness: relations produce determinate structures that maintain boundaries, enter exchanges, and acquire distinct histories.

This whole-first interpretation treats discernible components as relationally specified within one fundamental reality. Unity, self-sufficiency and goodness are separate questions. The [v2 account of reality and perspective](../v2/01_REALITY_COMPLETION_AND_PERSPECTIVE.md) develops the distinction between a common substrate and the stronger whole-first interpretation.

Aquinas and Spinoza are important interlocutors for proposed, reconstructive comparisons. Aquinas's divine simplicity raises whether a relationally differentiated whole could meet the strict standard that excludes real composition; Omega's preferred picture leaves that question open. His account of eternity also offers a comparison for a whole that does not successively acquire its history. Spinoza is a neighbour for substance monism and, separately, for conatus—the persistence or striving of a finite thing. Omega treats persistence as a condition of continuation, without adopting Spinoza's complete metaphysics or deriving value from persistence alone. These links clarify both resemblance and departure.

## Completion without a final instant

Omega adopts a block–Everett interpretation as its working picture: one completed reality contains internally differentiated events and the correlated alternatives of its quantum history. Completion means the whole history is considered together, while temporal relations within it still describe development, memory, action, death and irreversible local change.

The block is a complete structure whose internal relations include temporal order, causal influence, records, and asymmetries of access. A situated observer encounters a bounded region and reconstructs more of the whole from within; representing the full history does not confer an external viewpoint.

The distinction among three scopes remains essential. **Formal admissibility** concerns what follows under a specified model and its rules. **Actual physical realization** concerns what belongs to the physical world. **Habitation** concerns where experience occurs. Relating these three scopes is the cosmological task. κ names the remaining question about physical organization and experience.

Cosmopsychism has a specific proposed role at that boundary: it is the bridge past κ, the unresolved relation between physical organization and experience, that currently appears to require the fewest additional assumptions. The preference rests on a parsimony rationale; a fully functionalist account remains compatible. Chapter [05](#chapter-05) treats the functional proposal: consciousness as physically realized temporal integration and coordination of the processes composing an individual, not an inner controller. The functional account and the cosmopsychist proposal address different explanatory levels.

## Alpha, Logos, and Omega

**Alpha** names the broad horizon of generative possibility: distinctions that can be made, relations that can obtain, and orientations through which consequences become structured. It is an ambition to generalize beyond our present physical model, not a demonstrated space containing every possible law. **Logos** names lawful articulation: the particular grammar under which a world is realized. **Omega** names the completed manifestation of that realization, considered as a whole.

QFT is the preferred physical direction for characterizing our world. Alpha–Logos–Omega extends the interpretation beyond that physical description, asking how realized structure relates to possibility, law and manifestation.

The proposed general engine is **gradient and filter**: oriented differences
permit developments while laws, constraints and conditions of persistence select
what occurs and continues. A literal mathematical gradient requires its function
and geometry. Alpha need not impose one potential, a gradient-descent algorithm
or universal fluid dynamics on every realization. Its generalization becomes
substantive when an explicit map preserves the composition and consequence of
the physical case. Recovering recursive construction, maintenance and joint
feasibility is a first obligation for such a map.

Completed manifestation and the aspiration to maximal lushness are distinct.
The first names the object; the second asks about a comparison within or among
lawful objects. A maximum might be unattained, or several continuations might
remain incomparable under a bounded account. The persistence filter constrains
viable corridors without establishing a cosmic march toward a champion. Finite
flourishing and loss remain part of completion regardless of the final state.

This also sets the intended scope of “ultimate frame.” It names the encompassing comparison at which the regression of situated normalization would close. It is not an external chooser evaluating the world from beyond it. Present frame constructions provide the actual apparatus for describing situated weighting and access; the ultimate-frame role remains an intended closure, not a completed mathematical order. A formal description of closure would still need to show how comparisons across frames are justified.

The proposed **demiurge** connection is still underspecified. A provisional analogy concerns lawful realization shaping determinate material through intelligible order. A developed correspondence requires a closer account of the intended relation to the historical tradition; the present analogy posits no cosmic agent.

The next interpretive work is therefore specific: state which sense of formative
ordering the analogy intends, compare it with the relevant source tradition and
show which further premises would be required. The same method applies to the
Aquinas and Spinoza connections. Similar vocabulary locates a question; an
explicit correspondence establishes how far the analogy can carry an argument.

## Ground, manifestation, and the veil

The theological horizon distinguishes three questions without positing three separate worlds. **Formal description** is the structure a model expresses. **Manifest actuality** is the reality the model concerns, including phenomenal occurrence if it is not exhausted by the model. **Ultimate ground** is whatever finally constitutes or explains that actuality. The distinctions make room for formal closure—the possibility that an adequate formal account exhausts actuality—while leaving open dual-aspect and other accounts. They do not decide among them.

The **apophatic veil** marks the limit of justified positive claims about the ultimate. Some ignorance is temporary: more observation or better theory may resolve it. Other underdetermination persists when rival accounts agree on every accessible consequence. In that case, further empirical precision cannot distinguish them, though consistency, explanatory reach, and metaphysical argument still matter. The veil is not permission to claim anything whatsoever. It is a discipline for saying what a description establishes and where it stops.

On this account, simplicity means first that one reality is fundamental, not that it is featureless or that classical divine simplicity has been proven. **Aseity** means independence of existence from anything else; it is not self-causation. **Necessity** means that the reality could not have failed to exist. Neither follows from unity alone. The move from formal consequences that require no external executor to actual existence is also substantive. Wolfram's all-rules argument is a close modern neighbour for locating consequences within formal structure, but the transition from formal inclusion to actuality remains a philosophical commitment.

Logos can evoke the philosophical and Johannine language of intelligible order. The technical use here concerns lawful articulation and does not identify that articulation with a divine person or derive Trinitarian doctrine. Likewise, a creature's boundary can be read as **non-excisive individuation**: a real locus of organization within the whole, not a fragment severed from it. This is compatible with some hylomorphic and emanationist intuitions, but it does not establish creation ex nihilo or settle how dependence relates to independence.

## Completion and divine attributes

The strongest theological reconstruction concerns eternity: a completed whole does not successively acquire its internal history, though its inhabitants encounter temporal differences from their positions within it. Other divine attributes require more than completion. The whole's containing the relations and records of events is not yet a subject knowing them. Physical capacity is not an agent's command over all possibilities. An intelligible order is not yet intentional providence, and completion alone does not prove beneficence or pure actuality.

A stronger interpretation may propose that awareness is divine participation expressed through finite perspectives. Under that proposal, the whole would be present to suffering as well as flourishing. This would alter the image of an insulated creator imposing costs on creatures, but it would not solve the problem of evil: sharing suffering does not establish that suffering is justified, necessary, or preferable to alternatives. Nor does habitation establish that all local experiences form one phenomenal subject. Those are further phenomenal and theological claims.

## Communion and the meaning of completion

Communion names integration that sustains participants and their value-capable continuation without erasing their differences. Coordination can make a plurality more coherent while preserving identity, correction, and further generation. Love may be read as active concern for another's continuation and development; justice as the terms that protect and coordinate such continuation. These are structural interpretations of familiar virtues, not exhaustive definitions of religious traditions.

Judgment can be understood as the completed relational truth of a trajectory: what it did to the continuations of those it affected. Redemption can name recovery of generative capacity after contraction. Heaven and hell can serve as images of sustained generative communion and terminal solitude, respectively. “Terminal solitude” names a severe closure of value-capable continuation and regeneration in the relevant regime. Solitude or a single outer boundary alone does not establish that closure: one organization could sustain rich internal plurality and generativity. These interpretations make the consequences of action matter within a completed whole; they do not establish postmortem destinations, cosmic punishment, or guaranteed recovery.

The proposal is deliberately non-excisive at the level of creation and communion. A unity achieved by deleting its participants would miss the point of communion. Yet this preference is not a theorem that reality must be good, or that every loss is compensated by a larger whole. It names the theological and ethical orientation the project is trying to make intelligible while leaving its adequacy open to argument.

Alpha–Omega thus gives the programme a broad horizon: one internally differentiated reality, lawful realization, situated life, and a completed manifestation whose inhabitants still change from within. Its ambition is to understand physics, value and theology together while preserving the distinctions among formal description, actuality, experience and ultimate ground.

The first is a **compatibility construction or obstruction** for quantum breadth. The third is a **local field-theoretic adapter**. Begin with a regulated, explicit model and native interactions. Specify constraints, state, boundaries, local observables and resolution. Consciousness and vortical dynamics are downstream lines requiring their own tests. The functional coordination proposal can be connected to existing neuroscience now. ## What would count as success

A counterfactual research line connects the physical programme to ODT. Later tests can vary policy persistence across a prediction and examine several admissible anchors. A useful result could be a transferable physical representation, a valid partial comparison, a finite mechanism for compounding breadth, or an impossibility theorem exposing an incompatible demand. A unique universal scalar is not the only scientific success. Conversely, additional machinery that merely renames the same ambiguity is not progress.

The ethical research needs independent pressure too. Cases involving reliable achievement, preventable suffering, irreversible loss, cooperation and capture should be specified before selecting a successful proxy. Agreement with those cases would be evidence about the proxy's adequacy; disagreement must remain capable of changing the proposal. The objective is an account that explains more of the world, not a metric protected from unwanted answers.

## Adversarial substrate-proxy test

A proposed preregistered rejection criterion makes this challenge concrete.

## A. Sufficient present and native development

For a finite closed quantum adapter,

\[
\mathsf P=(\mathcal H,\{\mathcal A(R)\},\mathscr G,H(t)),\quad
\rho_\Sigma\ge0,\quad \operatorname{Tr}\rho_\Sigma=1,
\]

\[
\rho_t=U(t,\Sigma)\rho_\Sigma U(t,\Sigma)^\dagger.
\]

The local algebras, constraints, Hamiltonian and sufficient present define the physical model. An equivalent representation transforms these together. A restriction to a subsystem can omit environmental memory, so its reduced state alone need not be sufficient for further prediction. A process-tensor description can retain multitime operational dependence once its intervention slots are specified [R3]. Those slots describe questions or actual operations; they do not create all physical possibility by definition.

A physical present frame is \(F_\Sigma=(\Sigma,\rho_\Sigma)\) together with the
native specification needed to evolve it. The cut is a physical hypersurface,
or the declared finite-model analogue. A frame description restricts, represents
or conditions this object. The complete development retains the propagator
family \(\{U(t,s):\Sigma\le s\le t\le T\}\), with its interactions and composition,
not just \(U(T,\Sigma)\). Idle and out-and-back evolution can share that endpoint.
Changing coordinates transforms state, observables and law consistently.

An operational composition can be represented by a prefix joint law
\(J(r,y,b,t\mid x)\) for accessible record r, sufficient residual state y,
resource bill b and elapsed time t. A suffix may depend on r, not on inaccessible
knowledge of y held only by the analyst. Its law is integrated against J, with
the remaining deadline and resource stock updated. Failed and unfinished
prefixes retain their mass and physical residual. A proposed compression is
sufficient only for the interfaces and future compositions it demonstrably
preserves.

## B. Classical history breadth

For a discrete Markov law K and exact root x₀,

\[
p_{x_0}(x_1,\ldots,x_n)=\prod_{t=0}^{n-1}K(x_t,x_{t+1}),
\qquad L_{x_0}(n)=e^{H(p_{x_0})}.
\]

Zero-weight histories contribute zero to entropy. H uses natural logarithms unless bits are declared. L is dimensionless in unit-alternative calibration. It is an effective number and is not an additive set volume.

Let g(x)=−ΣᵧK(x,y)log K(x,y). Then

\[
\ell_n=\sum_{t=0}^{n-1}K^tg,\qquad
\ell_{n+1}=g+K\ell_n,\qquad \ell_0=0.
\]

This recurrence computes complete-history entropy without enumerating the exponentially growing tree. It depends on the Markov sufficiency of the state and the declared observation resolution. For non-Markov observed variables, the history-conditional chain rule still applies but this finite-state recurrence may not.

Original-root mass is preserved by lawful stochastic extension. Killing or conditioning must be modeled explicitly. A terminal symbol with a deterministic self-loop preserves the weight of a terminated development without adding branching entropy.

For independent histories p₍AB₎=p₍A₎p₍B₎,

\[
L_{AB}=L_A L_B.
\]

For a coupled joint law, H(A,B)=H(A)+H(B)−I(A;B). This identity compares the actual marginals of that joint law. It does not show that physical coupling always reduces breadth relative to independently prepared controls, whose marginals and access may differ.

## C. References and fresh continuation

For a stationary reference πK=π, the mean separately rooted logbreadth is πℓₙ. Its difference from a preparation x is

\[
\Delta\ell_n(x)=\ell_n(x)-\pi\ell_n
=\sum_{t=0}^{n-1}(\delta_xK^t-\pi)g.
\]

At elapsed step m, expected fresh logbreadth over the next n steps is

\[
F_x(m,n)=(K^m\ell_n)(x).
\]

This is an expectation of separately rooted queries. In general exp F is a geometric mean of their breadths, not their arithmetic mean. It does not include entropy of uncertainty over the new root. A reference must respect conserved quantities, sectors, boundaries and preparation assumptions relevant to the comparison.

### Finite mixing bounds the preparation advantage

**Proposition.** Let K be a fixed finite irreducible, aperiodic stochastic kernel,
π its stationary law, g(x)=H(K(x,·)) and h=πg. Then

\[
c(x)=\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}(\delta_xK^t-\pi)g,\qquad
\ell_n(x)=nh+c(x)+O(r^n),\quad 0<r<1.
\]

**Proof.** Finite irreducible, aperiodic chains converge geometrically [R22]:
there are C<∞ and 0<r<1 with supₓ‖δₓKᵗ−π‖TV≤Crᵗ. Since g is bounded,

\[
|(\delta_xK^t-\pi)g|\le 2C\|g\|_\infty r^t,
\qquad
|\Delta\ell_n(x)-c(x)|\le
\frac{2C\|g\|_\infty r^n}{1-r}.
\]

Absolute summability proves the assertion. Reversibility and detailed balance
are not assumptions. The constants depend on K; they need not remain bounded
when system size or a physical parameter changes.

Consequently Lₓ(n)/exp(nh)→exp c(x), and for two preparations
Lₓ(n)/Lᵧ(n)→exp(c(x)−c(y)). The ratio need not approach one. The denominator
exp(nh) is the geometric mean of separately rooted stationary breadths, not
the arithmetic mean or the breadth of a mixed initial root.

The excess next-step production tends to zero. More strongly, for fresh
continuation Fₓ(m,n)=Kᵐℓₙ(x),

\[
|F_x(m,n)-nh|
\le\frac{2C\|g\|_\infty r^m(1-r^n)}{1-r}
\le\frac{2C\|g\|_\infty r^m}{1-r}.
\]

Thus fresh advantages decay even uniformly over the future window n. The
reactive probe's reported fresh-window comparison is this kind of query;
its eventual convergence is conditional on the sampled chain satisfying the
stated assumptions, not established by its finite-window result alone.

For a common time-dependent sequence Kₜ on a fixed finite state space,
let gₜ(x)=H(Kₜ(x,·)). Two preparation laws μₜ,νₜ obey

\[
\Delta\ell_n=\sum_{t=0}^{n-1}(\mu_t-\nu_t)g_t.
\]

If Σₜ‖μₜ−νₜ‖TV<∞, their log-breadth difference still converges because
gₜ≤log|X|. Time dependence therefore does not itself guarantee escape.
A finite irreducible periodic chain also shares an asymptotic entropy rate,
although the bounded offset can oscillate rather than converge.

Distinct closed classes can carry different entropy rates, but a cross-class
advantage is not an advantage against the preparation's own class-conditioned
stationary reference. Infinite state spaces, growing finite-size limits and
non-forgetting driven laws require their own analysis; none guarantees a
positive generative advantage merely by escaping this proposition.

## D. Quantum history functional

For exhaustive orthogonal projective alternatives at declared cuts,

\[
C_\alpha=P^{(n)}_{a_n}U_{n,n-1}\cdots
P^{(1)}_{a_1}U_{1,0},\qquad
D_{\alpha\beta}=\operatorname{Tr}(C_\alpha\rho_\Sigma C_\beta^\dagger).
\]

With a pure root and |vα〉=Cα|ψΣ〉, this convention gives Dαβ=〈vβ|vα〉. D is positive semidefinite. For this exhaustive sequential-projector construction, Tr D=1 and ΣαβDαβ=1. The two normalizations have different reasons: summed sequential measurement probabilities and coherent reconstruction respectively. Trace-one is not an axiom for every abstract decoherence functional.

For a coarse history A, C_A=Σα∈A Cα, hence

\[
D(A,A)=\sum_{\alpha,\beta\in A}D_{\alpha\beta}.
\]

Medium decoherence, Dαβ=0 for distinct members of the family, suffices for ordinary probability addition. It does not establish that this particular family is physically preferred. Mathematical orthogonality, an algebraic record certificate and actual formation of a durable record are different claims.

An eventual breadth must identify its physical comparison domain. Agreement with every analyst-selected classical partition and complete independence from that selection are incompatible in the idle-qubit example. The task is to justify the physical boundary, not conceal the contradiction with a new entropy.

For unit cells it reduces to perplexity. For uniform occupation relative to those cell volumes, it equals their total volume. A reference unit is needed if the mᵢ are dimensional. This formula does not determine the cells or their physical masses.

## F. Decision implementations and matching

Write J for a physical decision lineage and m for a justified matching rule. Schematically,

\[
\Omega^m_\pi=\Omega\big(\mathsf{CF}_m(F,J,\pi),\mathcal L\big),
\qquad
\mathrm{Choice}_m=\operatorname{Max}_{\succeq}
\{\Omega^m_\pi:\pi\in\Pi_m\}.
\]

Here CF is a construction to be supplied, not an oracle already defined by physics. Πₘ contains implementations admissible under the matching assumptions. The evaluation relation is explicit; in this programme it is the intended lushness ordering, provisionally partial where the comparison is unresolved. For finite nonempty sets and a strict partial preference, maximal elements exist; an infinite plan space needs additional existence conditions. A family of justified matching rules can yield different maximal sets, which must be reported rather than silently collapsed into one answer.

Classically, selecting histories by a plan gives P(Y|π occurs). An implemented alternative gives the law generated by its physical realization under the declared matching. Equality requires justification. Native branch support alone is neither an implementation certificate nor a proof of causal control. In continuous spaces, nonzero support of an event or neighborhood must not be confused with positive probability of every individual trajectory.

The valid deterministic claim is limited: for a forward-deterministic law and sufficient global states, F(s)=F′(s) implies F(t)=F′(t) for t≥s. It does not select counterfactual semantics, prohibit comparison across lawful preparations, or convert relative quantum branching into indeterministic global evolution.

## G. First access, occupancy and maintenance

For a declared classical target class V, let τᵥ=inf{t≥0:Xₜ∈V}, with τᵥ=∞ when the target is never reached. The first-access profile is Aₓ(t)=Pₓ(τᵥ≤t). Then

\[
\int_0^T A_x(t)\,dt
=\mathbb E_x[(T-\tau_V)_+].
\]

The identity follows by integrating the indicator of arrival and exchanging the nonnegative integral and expectation. An early visit followed immediately by loss still scores almost T. It therefore measures arrival timing, not maintained organization.

A distinct occupancy diagnostic is

\[
O_x(T)=\mathbb E_x\!\left[\int_0^T\mathbf1_V(X_t)\,dt\right]
=\int_0^T P_x(X_t\in V)\,dt.
\]

Maintenance can instead require remaining in V for a declared duration after arrival; regeneration can concern return after a specified departure or disturbance. These observables answer different questions, and a coarse target class may omit important organization in every case. They are forward-rooted diagnostics, not a time-averaged present or a definition of lushness. Quantum versions need a physical monitoring or history construction; repeated projections cannot be inserted as harmless observation.

Effective resistance and relaxation spectra provide related but narrower access information. The graph commute identity applies with specified conductances, clock convention and boundary states or contracted sets. Reducing resistance does not establish that all target-specific hitting times or free-energy costs decrease; discrete-time conductance changes also change clock normalization [R18]. In a reversible continuous-time chain with fixed stationary law and increased symmetric conductances, the ordered nonzero eigenvalues of −Q cannot decrease. That spectral comparison is not a pointwise ordering of every access profile.

Equal endpoint laws give equal endpoint-only readouts. Convergence of laws implies convergence of a readout only under suitable continuity and integrability conditions. Neither statement erases distinctions in complete developments or mandates a particular cumulative score.

For a controlled finite model, a viability set collects states from which an
available policy keeps declared requirements satisfied against the specified
disturbances. Existence of a separate successful action for each hidden state
does not supply an executable selector. Full-state feedback and partial
observation therefore require different state descriptions and proof obligations.
Robust joint recovery needs one policy for the joint requirements; separate or
pairwise guarantees alone do not establish it. These are scoped tools for
implementation and maintenance, not alternative definitions of lushness.
