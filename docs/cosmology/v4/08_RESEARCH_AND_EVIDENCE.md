# 08 — Evidence, limitations and the next programme

Separating the physical object from its proposed measurements has produced both positive results and useful failures. This chapter records their scope and identifies the next discriminating tests.

## The reusable present-rooted foundation

The [multiway carrier](../../research_notes/omega_v2/multiway_carrier_report_v0.md)
implements a common classical object with native rates, cached sufficient
residual states, queried development occurrences and explicit unexplored frontier
mass. Its chemistry integration reproduced the existing shared- and local-fuel
generators to floating-point tolerance. Distinct prefixes can share a residual
computation, and independent composition is checked against the full joint law.
Local fuel allocation cannot generally be replaced by its total: the resulting
projection fails the Markov test in the reported adapter.

This foundation supports the adopted present-only requirement. The
[signature prototype](../../research_notes/omega_v2/rooted_signature_report_v0.md)
also showed that an exact root can generate nontrivial ordered-development
readouts without a prior over past cuts. Its coordinate selection and truncation
make it an instrument, not the physical carrier or the selected breadth measure.
The reusable carrier separates these tasks. Automatic higher concurrency,
general realization equivalence and quantum multiway evolution remain extensions,
so its existence is not a claim that the universal object has been implemented.

## What the finite work supports

The classical chain rule identifies complete-history perplexity exactly. It is cumulative expected stochastic branching, with configuration entering through the sequence of conditional laws. This is established mathematics. Whether it is an adequate substrate proxy is a further hypothesis.

In the local lattice-gas adapter, preparations controlled when collision branching became accessible. Some comparisons exceeded a broad stationary reference, but invariant-sector matching changed the interpretation. The later audit identified additional alternating invariants on the tested even lattices. Comparisons spanning those sectors cannot establish a matched thermal advantage. Small periodic systems also have strong finite-size effects. The [breadth report](../../research_notes/omega_v2/lattice_gas_breadth_report_v0.md), [scaling report](../../research_notes/omega_v2/lattice_gas_scaling_report_v0.md) and [invariant audit](../../research_notes/omega_v2/fhp_invariant_audit_v0.md) preserve those qualifications.

The reactive adapter provided a narrower positive result. It used two voxels, four molecules and reversible channels A↔X and B+2X↔3X, adapted from published reactive-model chemistry [R9]. The system is materially closed with an implicit isothermal bath; it is not an isolated energy-conserving universe. Two preparations shared molecular totals, occupancy pattern and stationary probability but placed the enabling molecules differently.

At diffusion rate d=.1, autocatalytic rate k=1 and sampling interval Δt=.2, the catalytically ready preparation exceeded the separated preparation by 4.3368 bits of expected fresh history entropy over the next four physical time units, after four time units of development. Each window contains twenty sampled transitions. It exceeded the stationary reacting reference by 3.2871 bits and the matched-composition placement reference by 1.3739 bits. These are differences in expected logarithmic breadth under sampled dynamics, not direct arithmetic differences of L. The expectation evaluates fresh continuation from each reached state; it does not replace the root with time-averaged occupancy.

The result demonstrates configuration-dependent weighted breadth in that law. It is not a direct comparison with the earlier nonreacting FHP gas. Removing the catalytic channel changes the law and serves as a mechanism ablation, not a same-law equilibrium baseline. The [reactive report](../../research_notes/omega_v2/reactive_breadth_report_v0.md) records both comparisons.

The advantage also has boundaries. Transport and observation interval can reverse the ranking. In the finite runs, net X abundance did not establish sustained autocatalytic multiplication; forward and reverse events both occurred. No new transformation repertoire or open-ended ecology was demonstrated. The [boundary assessment](../../research_notes/omega_v2/reactive_boundary_and_compounding_2026-10-07.md) therefore motivates tests of retained enabling and compounding rather than a claim that generativity has already been isolated from activity.

## The mechanism evidence behind the synthesis

The breadth runs sit on an earlier chain of finite distinctions. The
[structural probe](../../research_notes/omega_v2/structural_probe_10min_report_v0.md)
provides matched-content witnesses with different bounded decoding and repair.
The [timing probe](../../research_notes/omega_v2/timing_volume_counterexample_report_v0.md)
provides identical event-count laws with different record-to-response coupling.
These examples establish that the physical organization omitted by a summary
can matter to what follows. They do not impose an organization bonus on breadth.

The [catalytic production-history probe](../../research_notes/omega_v2/catalytic_history_report_v0.md)
adds an explicit succession mechanism. A descendant bond can catalyze further
formation after the initiating bond has disappeared. Stronger catalysis also
accelerates reversal; the additional descendant continuation largely offsets
shorter original survival rather than yielding an overall persistence gain.
The history observer preserves the physical marginal. Its ancestry labels add
no rates, physical possibilities or capability unavailable from the present.

Together these witnesses support links in the proposed ladder: organization
changes use and recovery; production changes later enabling; and the reactive
adapter shows a change in weighted breadth. The next synthesis must connect
these effects in one resource-accounted model and identify which mechanism
explains a measured advantage. Independent finite witnesses are not yet a proof
that the whole ladder compounds in nature.

Computational universality and generative physical construction are different
properties. The earlier cellular-automaton pilot does not establish a preferred
ranking: its signed ensemble responses can cancel, and its finite-ring invariant
assumptions needed correction. The
[preserved methodological account](../v3.1-source/OMEGA_COSMOLOGY_V3_1_COMPLETE.md)
retains those limits. Future automaton tests must demonstrate enabling mechanisms
and native weighting rather than use a known universal rule as the required winner.

The archive also retains local-flow, finite-fuel, mobile-binding and record-access
studies, with their individual scopes collected in the
[earlier evidence ledger](../v3.2/14_RESEARCH_AND_CLAIMS.md). Their contemporary
extent proposals are historical; the current manuscript uses their mechanism
findings and counterexamples. Reproducing an old readout is distinct from adopting
it as the present measure.

## A supplied compounding toy

A subsequent external report supplies a constructive next example: durable tools
can enable new tools under one stipulated production law. It reports a crossover
against a noise-ahead control near step 3,145 and a later rate near 11 bits per
update against 7.3. Construction saturates at 1,000 types; the remaining rate gap
is a legacy of unequal retained repertoires. This separates generativity during
construction from persistent use afterward.

The report is not yet reproduced: its two named source files were not supplied.
Its log M calculation is exact for uniform event choices, but the controls are
also described as deterministic in state. Physically distinct developments must
be separated from duplicate move labels before identifying this with the chapter
03 calibration. The [assessment and audit plan](../../research_notes/omega_v2/compounding_toy_assessment_2026-10-10.md)
also calls for a durable non-reproductive construction control, explicit resource
and clock accounting, and a recurrent-class analysis. Maintenance does not evade
finite mixing when the full chain remains ergodic. Finite sweep crossovers are
reported evidence, not established asymptotic thresholds.

The finite quantum tests establish obstructions to several proposed readouts. They also preserve useful positive identities and distinctions.

These are finite checks of stipulated models. They do not establish emergent observers, autonomous energy accounting, a continuum field theory or a universal quantum extent.

## Three productive lines of work

The first is a **compatibility construction or obstruction** for quantum breadth. Fix the native physical process and compare equivalent descriptions, identity checkpoints, actual record extensions, inert ancillas, independent copies and erasure. Either exhibit a justified comparison surviving those tests or show precisely which proposed requirements conflict. The existing [compatibility audit](../../quantum/COMPATIBILITY_AUDIT.md) is a specification for that work, not its completed result.

The second is **mechanism identification in generative classical systems**, beginning with a noise/activity control. Use a lawful model in which products can enable subsequent transformations. Match resources, initial branching, observation resolution and a declared noise/activity budget as closely as the model permits; report residual mismatches. Compare later breadth and retained enabling, including whether the advantage persists after the initiating arrangement is removed and whether descendants enable further descendants.

Preregister two different strengths of claim. A finite test can establish
successive enabling, regeneration after initiator removal and excess breadth
over a declared horizon, even if the model eventually mixes. Distinguish these
mechanism observations from an unbounded or higher-rate asymptotic advantage,
which the finite-mixing proposition in chapter 09 rules out under its assumptions.
Report both cumulative excess logbreadth and fresh-window excess against the
appropriate stationary or time-dependent reference, alongside mixing or
metastability evidence.

Products may unlock configurations already included in the model's fixed state
space; this suffices for a finite compounding test. For the stronger asymptotic
claim, specify how the model escapes common finite mixing before running it.
An unbounded resource-accounted construction model is one route. Increasing
finite cutoffs can test scaling, but each cutoff and the order of horizon and
size limits must be declared. Driving must include its reservoirs or boundary
accounting and demonstrate that it actually evades the relevant forgetting
condition. Different closed classes need class-conditioned references.

Matching complete path entropy would force equal perplexity by definition. It cannot test for an additional property inside the same statistic. Match specified inputs or an early-time control instead, then let later development differ. Thermodynamic entropy production is a separate physical quantity and, when available, should be reported separately from Shannon path entropy. A failure to outperform the control limits the proposed generativity mechanism; systematic conflict with independently justified value comparisons challenges the ethical proxy.

The third is a **local field-theoretic adapter**. Begin with a regulated, explicit model and native interactions. Specify constraints, state, boundaries, local observables and resolution. Check how continuum or coarse-graining changes affect the claimed quantities. Circuit readouts provide calibration tests; they do not dictate the field's ontology. A QFT adapter should earn physical consistency before it is used to make cosmological or ethical claims.

Consciousness and vortical dynamics are downstream lines requiring their own tests. The functional coordination proposal can be connected to existing neuroscience now. A futuresfield singularity needs a defined field, dynamical variables and a meaning of singularity before numerical exploration would be informative.

## What would count as success

A counterfactual research line connects the physical programme to ODT. Its first tests should establish ordinary implemented control, contrast conditioning with an actuator under a common cause, and compare deterministic preparation variations without claiming multiple actual futures from one deterministic root. Later tests can vary policy persistence across a prediction and examine several admissible anchors. The latest-history-consistent-divergence rule is a hypothesis; a preferred Newcomb answer must not select it in advance.

Access probes should separate first arrival, transient occupancy, sustained maintenance and regeneration under the same law and budget. Control-capacity density remains a proposed target selector, not an established criterion of valuerhood. Noise and energy-capture advantages must be outcomes to test. These additions are a research specification only: no ODT or access probe was run for this revision.

A useful result could be a transferable physical representation, a valid partial comparison, a finite mechanism for compounding breadth, or an impossibility theorem exposing an incompatible demand. A unique universal scalar is not the only scientific success. Conversely, additional machinery that merely renames the same ambiguity is not progress.

The ethical research needs independent pressure too. Cases involving reliable achievement, preventable suffering, irreversible loss, cooperation and capture should be specified before selecting a successful proxy. Agreement with those cases would be evidence about the proxy's adequacy; disagreement must remain capable of changing the proposal. The objective is an account that explains more of the world, not a metric protected from unwanted answers.

## Adversarial substrate-proxy test

A proposed preregistered rejection criterion makes this challenge concrete.
Before calculating breadth, declare the physical frame, horizon range, resource
matching and independent grounds for judging loss: for example, increased
preventable suffering and reduced recovery or agency of the affected valuers,
with any compensating opportunities included. Compare a maintained arrangement
with a lawful noisy-failure variant. If the variant robustly increases the
candidate's breadth while worsening those independently justified consequences
throughout the declared comparison, reject an unrestricted rule that higher
breadth is ethically better. One sound counterexample defeats that universal
rule; repeated failures across representative cases would retire native breadth
as the programme's general default value proxy. Retain it as a physical measure.
This tests the operational candidate and its declared scope, not the premise that
value depends on valuer-supporting substrate. Any narrower proxy claim must
state its domain and face new tests, rather than selecting a favorable frame
after seeing the outcome. This criterion is proposed
for the next protocol; no new ethical-proxy experiment has been run.
