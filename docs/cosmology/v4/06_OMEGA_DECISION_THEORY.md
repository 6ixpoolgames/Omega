# 06 — Omega Decision Theory: physically situated choice

Omega Decision Theory (ODT) asks which physically realizable variations of an embedded decision process correspond to choosing differently, and how their complete consequences should be compared. Its ambition extends beyond an audit checklist. It seeks a decision theory grounded in the physical organization of the decision itself. The counterfactual construction needed to complete that theory remains open.

The futuresfield is prior to the decision problem. Organisms, controllers, institutions, plans and evaluators are structures within native development. An action menu describes a situated part of physical possibility; it does not define that possibility. Generic ODT studies implementation, counterfactual dependence and comparison. GE-ODT applies Gradient Ethics' conditional commitments to the resulting continuations. Neither an evaluation nor counterfactual semantics is supplied merely by naming the physical carrier.

## Decision lineage, information and plans

A **decision lineage** J is the physically persisting organization carrying the decision through the relevant interval: a motor-selection process, learned disposition, planning architecture or institutional rule. Persistence can include learning, turnover, repair and self-modification. The proposed boundary must be justified by the decision's physical organization rather than the verdict wanted.

Available information can include classical records, analogue variables and coherent quantum degrees of freedom. Recording requires evidence where a record is claimed. Control does not require an already classical record; possessing a record does not establish control over what it describes.

A **plan** is a physically implementable organization of response to available information. A classical policy can map information histories to actions. Coherent control instead requires quantum operations and memory and need not admit an equivalent classical policy. Plans include waiting, investigating, committing, delegating and modifying the decision architecture. Their consequences include these activities and their costs.

Three objects remain distinct:

1. **Native continuation:** development generated by the actual sufficient present and law.
2. **Implementable variations:** alternative operations or organizations the lineage can realize under specified physical conditions.
3. **Counterfactual matching:** the rule specifying which conditions vary and which remain matched when implementations are compared.

A deterministic exact root has one actual trajectory. Studying the same mechanism under different lawful inputs or preparations remains possible, but varies those conditions. It must not be described as multiple continuations of an unchanged sufficient root. Conversely, stochastic alternatives need not be controllable choices. An internal malfunction can be possible without being selectable by the lineage.

## Physical implementation requirements

An admissible implementation needs a witness: a model of how the proposed change occurs, retaining the native law, constraints, resources and consequential interactions. The lineage must participate in realizing the operation in the claimed way. External rewiring establishes another system's influence, not automatically the lineage's own control. Delegation can be its plan when arranging and using that delegation belongs to its decision process.

Locality constrains implementation; it does not justify instantaneously replacing a register while omitting the required apparatus and correlations. A costly sequence reaching a state is not evidence for a free present edit to that state. Equivalent descriptions must not add options. A coarse quotient must preserve the relevant implementation and consequence distinctions, not merely an endpoint law.

These requirements constrain implementable alternatives. They do not yet supply a universal criterion for endogenous control, lineage boundaries or minimal deformation. Those remain construction problems for finite models before becoming claims about agents generally.

## Counterfactual matching and anchors

A **matching rule** m identifies the physical variations instantiating compared plans and specifies the treatment of surrounding conditions, shared causes and records. Consequences must develop forward under the law, without downstream edits to obtain a preferred answer. Realization costs remain represented.

An **anchor** is a proposed section rooting the compared implementations. Present-edit matching asks for alternatives lawful from that section onward. History-consistent matching additionally requires an appropriate lawful common past. The first does not guarantee implementability; the second does not always select one section.

The proposed **latest history-consistent divergence** is worth testing. It seeks the organization actually carrying the alternative, avoiding both unnecessarily remote variation and an artificial final-act substitution. A policy formed before a prediction can have different dependence from a fresh decision afterward.

This is a candidate matching rule, not a theorem. Several incomparable sections may qualify; continuous settings may have no latest qualifying point. Remote divergence can carry unrelated differences, and matching them away may require an additional choice. These ambiguities must remain visible.

Under a forward-deterministic law, equal sufficient global states earlier imply equal global states later. Different later states therefore cannot arise from an identical earlier global state under that law. This does not establish that distinctive counterfactual reasoning requires indeterminism. Everettian universal evolution is itself unitary: relative/decoherent branching and alternative global-state evolutions are different levels of description [R7]. Quantum anchors require an explicit construction rather than an appeal to Everett branches.

Present-edit matching also does not by itself prove equivalence to causal decision theory. That limit requires a specified intervention model and treatment of dependencies. With a justified act-level causal intervention and expected utility, ODT recovers the corresponding act-based causal decision calculation. Other matching rules require separate assessment.

## Conditioning is not implementation

Restricting native histories to those in which a plan occurs generally gives a conditional distribution. It need not describe the effects of implementing the plan.

For example, let an unobserved physical condition Z be equally likely zero or one. Action C agrees with Z with probability .9, while outcome Y is determined by Z and unaffected by C. Then P(Y=1|C=1)=.9. A physically modeled actuator setting C=1 without altering Z leaves P(Y=1)=.5. Selecting histories by action has selected its common cause. Native weighting does not remove that confounding.

An implementation can legitimately change shared causes or earlier records where its realization requires that change. The task is to identify the dependence, not stipulate that every comparison holds all earlier circumstances fixed. Newcomb, lesion and correlated-agent cases test the matching construction; their answers must not be built into the lineage definition.

## Complete-continuation comparison

For each admissible matching rule m and plan π, let Ωᵐπ denote the resulting complete continuation object. An evaluation relation ≽ compares these objects. It can be partial, and a quantum continuation can retain coherence without becoming a classical branch distribution. Generic ODT does not identify ≽ with lushness or moral value.

Consequences include transient achievements, information, waiting, losses, recovery and effects on other systems. A shared endpoint does not make intervening developments equal. This does not prescribe summing an arbitrary score at every time step: the full object comes first, and a cumulative statistic needs its own justification. Finite horizons expose a comparison profile. An uninformative or divergent infinite limit does not settle it.

For finite plan sets, maximal options can be reported for each justified m. Changed recommendations across m are a finding; a stronger recommendation may survive an entire justified matching family. The evaluation still needs grounds. Physics constrains consequences; GE supplies the conditional ethical commitments of chapter 04.

Information needs no separate bonus. An observe-then-act plan contains its apparatus, conditional responses, delay and opportunity costs. Commitment matters through its effects, including others' responses. Reframing after successful preparation cannot remove its original probability and cost. Normalizing a local institutional frame likewise cannot erase affected people from the encompassing account.

## GE-ODT: breadth, access and maintenance

Value, value-capable substrate and lushness remain distinct. Lushness retains its meaning as weighted breadth. Access, persistence, resources and influence can explain or diagnose a comparison without becoming an unannounced replacement definition.

An access profile can describe reaching a declared physical target by a deadline, arrival times and resource costs at a specified reliability. Selecting targets by control capacity divided by free energy is one proposed experimental proxy. It is not an established definition of valuer prerequisites. Sweeping its threshold does not establish the appropriateness of its boundary, denominator or controllability assumptions.

First arrival differs from maintenance and regeneration. If τ is first arrival, integrating P(τ≤t) up to T measures expected time remaining after arrival, even if the target immediately disappears. It does not measure continued occupancy or retained enabling. Chapter 09 gives the identity and separate diagnostics. These are forward queries from one root, not time-averaged substitutes for the present.

Noise can obstruct access or enable exploration and escape. Routing an existing energy flow can help while also introducing dependencies and costs. Faster reversible relaxation does not prove that every target is reached sooner or with less free energy. Noise, capture, accelerated spending and conservation are cases to compare, not predetermined winners or losers.

## Bounded judgment: ODT0–ODT2

The earlier architecture remains the practical layer:

1. **ODT0 — License:** check whether implementation, matching and consequence models support the requirements justified in the situation.
2. **ODT1 — Compare:** retain supported comparisons and their dependence on scope, matching, horizon and unresolved effects.
3. **ODT2 — Arbitrate:** where action remains necessary despite incomparability, state the authority and rationale of the rule used.

Demonstrated violation differs from insufficient justification. Emergency compromise does not prove every requirement was met. Arbitration can use institutions, negotiated priorities, lotteries or declared scores without claiming a uniquely correct physical exchange rate. This layer does not settle every counterfactual or Pascal-style problem.

Consider repairing a shared water system. A fast proprietary controller, a slower distributed repair and further investigation produce different continuations of supply, health, knowledge and dependency. ODT represents how each plan is implemented and how environmental conditions are matched, compares supported consequences, and handles urgent unresolved tradeoffs. Neither plurality nor speed is an automatic bonus.

Stable preferences support a limited consistency result. If every voluntary revision strictly improves the evaluated complete plan, strict preference is transitive and irreflexive, and pure resource loss is strictly worse, a finite sequence cannot return to the original plan with only an additional loss. This is a conditional preference theorem, not a physical derivation of rationality. Plans can contain learning; changes in beliefs or evaluation between revisions require separate dynamic-consistency analysis.

## Research status

Next tests should distinguish physical mechanism from counterfactual convention: ordinary implemented control; deterministic preparation variation; a confounded action with and without an actuator; policy formation before and after prediction; and multiple candidate anchors. An access test should contrast first arrival, immediate loss, sustained maintenance and regeneration. No favourable Newcomb verdict or ethical ranking is a prerequisite for success.

An earlier finite decision prototype achieved 5/6 where direct optimization under the same guards achieved 35/36; a revised-plan stratum remained zero. The [original report](../../research_notes/omega_v2/lushness_decision_report_v0.md) remains evidence against that candidate, not validation of this rebuild. No new ODT probe has been run for this revision.

The central hypothesis is that physical organization and its lawful implementations constrain counterfactual dependence more tightly than an arbitrary choice of act or policy scope. Whether they determine it uniquely remains open. A justified construction or a precise obstruction would advance that question, while the bounded layer already helps keep decisions accountable to the continuations they affect.
