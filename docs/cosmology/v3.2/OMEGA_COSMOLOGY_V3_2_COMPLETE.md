# 00 — Orientation

**Omega Cosmology v3.2 — development edition, 3 October 2026**

Omega investigates the structure of continuation: how physical situations support, constrain and transform what can follow, and how those transformations alter the possibility of value.

The motivation begins with **value requires valuers**. Under uncertainty about what valuers and values can be, the program admits every consequential process as a potential valuer. Organisms, minds, readable records and declared preferences are not admission requirements. Their particular organization can matter through its consequences. Admission does not establish that every process is an actual valuer, that all processes have equal ethical weight, or that their preferences supply the comparison.

The guiding assumption is that expanding the substrate of possible value can support more possibility of value. The task is to characterize that substrate and investigate its comparison. It is permissible to start from that assumption. It is not yet a theorem identifying structural expansion with realized goodness.

## What this edition proposes

> **A continuation field is the physical structure of weighted, jointly compatible continuations, including how each continuation changes further physical access. Lushness is the proposed extent of that recursive structure as a substrate of possible value.**

The current candidate is **recursive physical access geometry**. Its finite operational presentation is an atlas of joint response laws, physical implementation bills and the residual processes those implementations leave. Access is situated within the object; comparison refers to the encompassing field. The atlas is not a menu for an external chooser with unlimited control.

A workshop and loose materials can contain the same elementary degrees of freedom yet support different transformations at a given cost and time. Installing a channel can alter which differences affect later processes. Redundancy can change repairability without adding distinct source content. A controller can close others' routes while increasing its own. These are changes of one physical object, not separate ethical multipliers.

## The advance from 3.1

The earlier edition already supplied residual-state instruments, physical realization witnesses, joint feasibility, recursive descriptions and quantum process machinery. Version 3.2 keeps those foundations and makes three changes central:

# 01 — The physical object and situated access

## One object, many situated descriptions

Begin with a lawful physical world: its states, interactions, causal relations, and boundary conditions. Call its complete physical description Ω_phys. The symbol does not presuppose that this description is a finite data structure, or that any inhabitant could calculate it.

In a closed quantum model, a state and unitary dynamics give a familiar realization. In relativistic settings, local observables, causal regions, and states on suitable hypersurfaces are the better language. These are ways of representing one physical situation at different scopes.

The block viewpoint places relations among events inside the description rather than adding a privileged present that moves through it. Clocks, records, organisms, and deliberating agents still occur, and their succession is an internal relation. An action matters through the dependencies it takes part in, whether those dependencies are described sequentially or all at once. Everett's relative-state formulation is the foundational quantum neighbour, and the program's cosmology adopts its block picture; the operational machinery does not depend on that choice. [R28]

"Completed" describes the viewpoint. It does not give a finite observer complete knowledge, and it does not imply a single classical trajectory. Much of the operational work below proceeds without settling between interpretations of quantum mechanics.

## Quantum alternatives have structure

A quantum superposition is more than a list of classical possibilities. Relative phase can change later outcomes. Entangled systems carry correlations that their reduced states do not specify. Decomposing a state into vectors is not the same as decomposing the world into physically autonomous branches.

So "branch" is read by context. In a decohered regime it can mean an approximately autonomous history with stable records. Before that, it can mean an interfering contribution to an amplitude. The mathematics has to keep the difference. Decoherent-histories methods supply the discipline for when classical probabilities may be assigned to a family of alternatives, and they forbid pooling incompatible families into one classical tree. [established; R01; formulas in Part 12]

Multiway graphs are useful pictures of alternatives, convergence, and shared dependence. A quantum multiway representation must still carry amplitudes or an equivalent process structure. Counting graph paths alone discards part of the physics.

## Relative presents

A **present membrane** is the program's image for a boundary through which a situated process is described. In a relativistic model a spacelike hypersurface can play that role; specifying it requires geometry and a choice of slicing. A worldline at one event does not pick out a unique global hypersurface.

Different admissible sections can describe the same physical situation, and their descriptions must be related by the appropriate dynamics and frame transformations. Quantum reference-frame research gives explicit constructions in controlled settings, and relational quantum hypersurfaces are a close neighbour. [established; R02–R04]

The framework therefore works with a family of descriptions rather than one privileged universal membrane. Some perspectives are better represented by local regions, worldtubes, or interfaces than by a whole-universe slice. The membrane is not a globally privileged time slice.

## Local access and global correlations

A subsystem S has a reduced state ρ_S, obtained by tracing out its environment. That state predicts the statistics of measurements on S at that boundary. Predicting later interactions can require the joint state ρ_SE and the dynamics coupling S to its environment.

Two joint states can share their local marginals and still produce different later behaviour. A local present is therefore not automatically a complete, standalone specification of continuation. Part 11 (Example 2) shows this with two classical correlations and a reversible interaction.

Quantum probabilities come from the state and the Born rule. Missing local information and intrinsic quantum uncertainty are related features of prediction, but they are not interchangeable explanations.

## Nonlocality and the block picture

Entangled correlations can span distant regions of the shared object. A block description places their common preparation and later interactions in one account. It does not turn Bell correlations into ordinary local hidden variables: Bell's theorem constrains exactly that kind of explanation under its assumptions. [established; R05] The useful move is to represent correlations where they physically belong, without adding any mechanism that sends controllable signals outside the relevant causal structure.

## Perspective as situated continuation

A **physical perspective** is a process described from a situated interface with the rest of reality. Its specification includes where and how it couples, which degrees of freedom belong to the description, which correlations cross its boundary, and how continuation is organized there.

This is broader than observation by a mind. A photon hitting a detector, a gas exchanging energy with its surroundings, a crystal carrying an excitation, and a civilization building instruments all have situated continuation. Agency and awareness are further kinds of organization within that domain.

For concrete work, a perspective can be specified schematically as

```text
P = (region or interface, observable algebra,
     boundary condition, coupling structure, continuation domain).
```

This is a modelling interface, not a new entity. Subsystem structure itself deserves care: accessible observables help determine a useful tensor-product decomposition, and different couplings can support different decompositions. That is why "the same thing from another perspective" needs a transformation rule rather than an intuition. [established; R13] Quantum reference frames and relational quantum mechanics are complementary precedents; neither is adopted wholesale as the definition of an Omega perspective. [established; R02, R03, R14]

## Focus, and four operations to keep apart

A **focus** relates a situated boundary to the compatible contributions that connect to a specified condition. Asking how a prepared system could reach a particular detector effect is a focus: the condition organizes the relevant contributions, and the dynamics supplies their propagation and interference. A focus can be a physical detector condition, a boundary constraint, or a mathematical query. It does not require attention, intention, or agency.

The elementary construction pairs a forward state with a backward-propagated effect (Part 12, §L). The backward-propagated effect is an inference object. It does not assert that a later event rewrites an earlier state. [established; R06–R08]

Four operations must be kept distinct:

- **Redescription** changes coordinates, basis, or frame while preserving physical content.
- **Conditioning** asks about the process given a record or specified outcome.
- **Intervention** inserts a physical operation and can change the process.
- **Decoherence** is produced by physical correlations that suppress interference in a reduced description.

A focus can be involved in all four, and using the word without saying which is meant manufactures paradoxes. A focus chosen on paper does not decohere a beam; a detector interaction can. Sorting existing data by a correlated record is conditioning. The delayed quantum eraser is the standard illustration: conditional subsets show complementary interference patterns, and recombining them restores the unchanged unconditional distribution. No awareness-induced collapse is needed, and no later choice rewrites earlier detections. [established; R12; Part 11, Example 3]

## Memory and the full process

Physical memory is dependence carried by the present organization of a system and its correlations. A durable register is one architecture for it; a temporary environmental correlation can also carry memory into later behaviour.

A full relevant joint state and its dynamics can encode that dependence at one boundary. When access is restricted to a subsystem across several times, a **process tensor** (or quantum comb) represents how the subsystem responds to sequences of operations, including environmental memory. [established; R15, R16, R31] So "the present contains the relevant past" and "a local description needs multi-time information" can both be true. The first concerns a sufficient physical boundary description. The second concerns a reduced description whose instantaneous state omits consequential correlations.

## Composition and scale

Perspectives can overlap, share records, exchange signals, or combine into a larger process. The combination must keep joint correlations. A product of reduced descriptions is valid only where the required independence actually holds; joint systems are not automatically tensor products of their parts.

Higher-level perspectives become useful when stable organization supports repeatable continuation: molecules, cells, organisms, institutions. The same relational move applies across scales, while the effective variables and mathematics change. Massive worldlines admit proper-time descriptions; massless propagation follows null structure and has no rest frame. Both take part in causal dependence. [established; R17, R18]

## Frames, history and accessible information

For one classical physical law P and a positive-probability record condition
α, the frame weight is μ_α(E) = P(E | α). Within a compatible decoherent
quantum history family, use its Born weights in the same conditioning
formula. For a general coherent process use instruments; do not impose a
classical joint law on incompatible alternatives.

The information accumulated in a history and the information available now
are different. Write F_t = σ(H_t) for the accumulated observation/action/memory
history, and G_t = σ(O_t, M_t) for the current observation and accessible
memory. F_t is an increasing filtration; G_t need not increase. Forgetting
can remove a record from current access without deleting its place in history
or the encompassing physical process.

Under one law, nested conditioning obeys the tower identity. Thus conditional
expectations of a fixed integrable readout form a martingale along F_t.
The same assertion for G_t requires nesting; it is not guaranteed after
erasure. An analyst retaining H_t does not supply it to the controller.
Physical archive access must be represented with its coupling, delay and bill.
The [frame-information audit](../../research_notes/omega_v2/frame_information_audit_v0.md)
provides an exact finite witness.

Every future time retains total conditional probability one. Individual fine
alternatives can thin without discounting the future as a whole. Horizons
index restrictions of the field; they do not supply an arbitrary time weight.
Compatible quantum restrictions use the corresponding causal process maps.

## Completion and physical scale

The completed object retains every finite restriction and its consequences.
It is not replaced by a terminal state, a growth exponent or a total entropy.
A pure global quantum state can have zero von Neumann entropy while its
subsystem couplings and response processes differ substantially. Normalized
weight one is therefore not a proof that the continuation geometry is trivial.

A finite discretization must state its physical meaning. Planck units alone
do not establish a discrete clock, lattice or smallest record in this
construction. The present membrane anchors a causal restriction relative to
the physical model; changing the slicing requires a valid transport of the
same apparatus and events. A general relativistic quantum implementation of
the atlas remains beyond the finite circuit backend.

## What this foundation provides

Ω_phys is the source of continuation constraints. Perspectives inherit their possibilities from it. State space, causal accessibility, coherent interference, and stable records determine what can be combined and what can be distinguished. Every compression later in the primer must respect these constraints.

Quantum machinery constrains what a faithful continuation representation must keep. It is not required to pose the finite classical generativity questions of Parts 02–03, and it does not confer moral standing. Part 12 gives the quantum realization after the finite classical presentation.

# 02 — Continuation and composition

## Consequence and residual continuation

**Consequence** is the difference a physical condition makes to continuation. A distinction can alter the next interaction, lie dormant in a record, be amplified much later, or change which regimes can ever be reached. The relevant object includes these dependencies, not only the next observable state.

The **residual continuation** at a perspective, given some physically meaningful condition h, is everything that can still follow from there: how the situation responds to admissible interactions, with what probabilities, at what cost, and leaving what behind. This part builds the machinery for representing it in a finite classical model. Part 12 gives the same construction compactly and then its quantum realization.

## A finite presentation

The first executable presentation is a finite controlled Markov model **[established]**:

```text
S = (X, A, K)
K(x, a, y) ≥ 0,     Σ_y K(x, a, y) = 1     for each admitted a ∈ A(x).
```

X is a finite state space. A(x) is the set of admitted action labels at state x. K gives the transition law.

Three modelling rules carry most of the physical content:

- **The state must be sufficient.** X includes every variable needed for the Markov claim: the relevant environment, apparatus, controller memory, resource stocks, and correlations. A reduced subsystem can have memory even when the encompassing model is Markovian.
- **An action is a physically exposed operation.** It does not give an unmodelled chooser free access to every microscopic transition. A passive system has a single null action. In a reaction model, the stochastic occurrence of individual reactions belongs inside K, not on a menu of freely selectable actions. This is what makes control cost something: an external optimizer cannot be handed arbitrary control over a gas while an organism has to pay for its sensors and actuators.
- **Expenditures are accounting, not utility.** Record them as a vector d(x, a, y) with declared physical units: feedstock consumed, work supplied, apparatus time. Stocks, replenishment, and waste are state variables. The vector need not admit any preferred scalar conversion.

## Realization witnesses

A protocol claimed as an embodied capability needs a **realization witness** **[proposed]**:

- its apparatus and controller;
- the records it can physically access;
- its preparation and deployment process;
- its resource sources and disposal conditions;
- an embedding in the same underlying physical model;
- a check that the induced path law, costs, and records agree with the claimed operational run.

In a finite benchmark, the underlying model can be an autonomous Markov chain on a larger space. Different controllers are then different physical configurations under the same law, not freely invented laws. A protocol without a witness can still be studied as an ideal mathematical intervention; it simply cannot establish an embodied capability. A finite study states the extent of its implementation catalogue and what that catalogue exhausts.

## Contexts, histories, and protocols

A **context** c specifies an input preparation or distribution μ_c, the interface under examination, environmental and boundary conditions, the origin from which costs are counted, and the permitted apparatus and resources. A context need not describe an agent. It can describe a reaction vessel, a stored record, or an organism.

A causal protocol π chooses its next operation from physically available records r_≤t, which are produced by an implemented observation process and are generally not the full microscopic history. The path law over a finite history h_H = (x_0, a_0, x_1, …, x_H) is:

```text
P_c^{π,H}(h_H) = μ_c(x_0) · Π_{t=0}^{H−1} π(a_t | r_≤t) · K(x_t, a_t, x_{t+1}).
```

Randomized protocols include their randomization mechanism in the realization. The initial law μ_c comes from the model and its preparation. It is not uniform by default. Through a declared record map R_H, the law visible at an interface is the pushforward of the path law, and the full path law is retained behind it.

For prefixes of the same implementation, the laws are consistent across horizons: marginalizing the horizon-(H+1) law gives the horizon-H law of the truncated protocol **[established]**. The reference is the whole compatible family over horizons. No discount factor is mandatory. Changing a later instruction must not quietly change an earlier preparation: two differently prepared controllers can differ physically before their outputs diverge.

## Preparation, readiness, and deployment

Three statements must be kept separate **[proposed]**:

1. A transformation is allowed by the laws.
2. An already prepared apparatus performs it with a stated reliability.
3. The apparatus can be obtained and deployed from the stated origin, with stated costs and reliability.

If setting up succeeds with probability p, and the task then succeeds with conditional probability q, the route succeeds with probability pq. Reporting q as though it described the whole route erases a consequential dependency. The conditional law given successful preparation is a legitimate record; its conditioning event and probability stay attached.

This distinction is exactly how the nebula and the factory differ in accessibility. The factory's conditional reliability is high. The nebula's route to a factory has a tiny probability, a long delay, and many dependencies on favourable accidents.

## Processes leave residual states

Endpoint distributions alone omit how a process leaves the world for whatever comes next. The representation is therefore an **instrument** that keeps each outcome together with the physical state it leaves behind **[established form; proposed use]**:

```text
J_{π,r}^{t,b}(x, y) = Pr_x^π( finish with record r, at time t,
                             with expenditure b, in physical state y ).
```

The boundary state y keeps whatever the next composition needs, including relevant correlations and memory. **Failure and unfinished outcomes keep their actual boundary states and costs.** A run still going at the horizon is censored by the observation window; it is not assumed to have physically stopped. Unsuccessful runs are never renormalized out of the reference law. A failure flag without the boundary state is a restricted readout, not a continuation interface.

## Sequential and adaptive composition

Given a physical witness for running π and then σ, with matching boundaries **[established]**:

```text
J_{σ∘π,(r,s)}^{t,b}(x, z) = Σ_{y; t₁+t₂=t; b₁+b₂=b} J_{π,r}^{t₁,b₁}(x, y) · J_{σ,s}^{t₂,b₂}(y, z).
```

This follows from conditioning on the intermediate state, time, and expenditure split. Valid composites associate, because the finite sums can be rearranged. Two cautions:

- **Composition must be witnessed.** Preparation, switching, communication, and disposal overhead are included as further physical stages. Multiplying kernels does not prove that the composite apparatus exists, and no apparatus cost is subtracted merely because two protocols behave alike.
- **Adaptive continuations use available records.** A controller may choose its continuation σ_r from the record r it actually has. It may not choose on the basis of a hidden microscopic state that only the analyst knows.

## Joint feasibility

Sequential composition does not settle what can happen at the same time. Write

```text
γ ⊩_c (p_1, …, p_n)
```

for a physical realization γ that supplies the specified component processes together in one encompassing world. Say which meaning is intended: reproducing marginal instruments is a marginal claim, while matching a specified joint response requires its full joint instrument and residual correlations. Selected task guarantees are a further restricted readout. **[proposed]**

For task events E_i on the same history, with accumulated expenditure D_H, one useful readout is:

```text
∃ γ ∈ Π_phys(c) :   P_c^γ( E_1 ∩ … ∩ E_n  and  D_H ≤ b )  ≥  1 − ε.
```

All events belong to one encompassing history, so shared resources and correlations stay visible. Several facts follow **[established]**:

- High marginal success probabilities do not establish high joint success. A product formula needs an independently justified factorization.
- For fixed requirements, the families that can be realized jointly are closed under deleting requirements, so they form a compatibility complex.
- Pairwise compatibility does not establish compatibility of the whole family. (Part 11, Example 6: enough resources for any two of three repairs, not all three.)

The joint relation is **derived** from realizations and interface maps. It must never become an extra primitive that asserts compatibility independently of the physics. The repository's shared-action viability kernel is a robust full-state-feedback special case: the greatest set of joint-safe states from which a state-dependent shared action can keep the process inside the set. Applying this guarantee to a situated controller also requires a physically realizable policy based on its accessible records. [project result; Part 12, §C]

## Tasks and capability

A **task** is an explicitly specified event or input–output requirement on a physical run: reaching a state, producing a structure, communicating a distinction, restoring a condition, or meeting several requirements jointly. The task language is a declaration, and its labels carry no value.

```text
Realize_{H,b,ε}(c, T)  ⟺  ∃ π ∈ Π_phys(c) :  P_c^π( E_T  and  D_H ≤ b ) ≥ 1 − ε.
Cap_{H,b,ε}(c; 𝒯)    =  { T ∈ 𝒯 : Realize_{H,b,ε}(c, T) }.
```

One-shot transformations are included. Nothing here requires persistence or personal identity. The capability set is a *readout* of the object, not its definition; the full object keeps which implementations succeed, their laws, dependencies, correlations, and costs. A larger capability set under a fixed interface and budget is a useful comparison. By itself it settles neither value nor joint feasibility, nor performance in another context.

## Continuation equivalence and horizon

A **tester** is an admissible physical interaction, with its context and output record. For matched processes and an admissible tester family 𝒯 **[project result; Part 12, §F]**:

```text
p ≡_𝒯 q   ⟺   Law(τ[p]) = Law(τ[q])  for every τ ∈ 𝒯.
```

This is an exact equivalence relation for the declared tester domain. Its quotient is an optional predictive compression, not the definition of the full field. It does not identify two physical histories, persons, copies or fluctuations. An environment outside the domain can retain a distinction the selected tests cannot reveal. Preserve that environment and the original realization in the encompassing object. Classical computational mechanics is a close precedent for domain-relative predictive causal states. [established; R19]

**Refinement.** If 𝒯₁ ⊆ 𝒯₂, then equivalence under 𝒯₂ implies equivalence under 𝒯₁. Enlarging the admissible tests can separate classes and can never merge classes already distinguished.

A longer horizon produces this refinement **when it includes the shorter tests**, for example by keeping their records. Observing only later endpoints can instead *lose* distinguishability through mixing. This gives the originator's intuition, that depth of horizon correlates with fineness of description, a principled conditional form. It is not a universal law relating spatial resolution to time horizon, and it does not show that histories physically converge and then diverge.

## Contextual equivalence and substitution

Two processes can have the same standalone output law and still behave differently when coupled to a shared environment. A fair output bit independent of a reference bit and a fair output bit copied from it have identical marginals; a joint test against the reference separates them (Part 11, Example 11).

To replace one process by another inside larger processes, the tester family must include the relevant surrounding contexts and be closed under the compositions in question. Then equivalence licenses substitution: every composed test is one of the tests already quantified over. **[established]** This is the explicit condition for safe substitution. Equality of standalone marginals is not enough.

## Approximation is a distance, not a quotient

A useful diagnostic is the worst-case total-variation distance over the tester family:

```text
d_𝒯(p, q) = sup_{τ∈𝒯} TV( Law(τ[p]), Law(τ[q]) ).
```

On a common typed domain this is a pseudometric, and enlarging 𝒯 cannot decrease it. The threshold relation d_𝒯 ≤ ε is generally **not transitive**, so it cannot serve as an exact equivalence without an explicit partition, cover, or encoding with a verified error bound. An encoding with uniform prediction error ε cannot merge two conditions whose distance exceeds 2ε. [project result; Part 12, §F]

## Faithful compression

Faithful compression preserves the effects of permitted future composition. For an encoding q and a concrete update F_a, the requirement is an abstract update F̄_a with q∘F_a = F̄_a∘q. In stochastic and quantum models the requirement concerns the corresponding transition laws or process maps.

The three-bit example makes this exact **[project result; Part 12, §G]**. Let X, Y, Z take values ±1 with joint law P_t(x,y,z) = (1 + t·xyz)/8 for −1 ≤ t ≤ 1. Every preparation has zero means and identity covariance. Apply the reversible gate (X,Y,Z) → (X,Y,XYZ). The output means become (0,0,t) and the covariance becomes diag(1,1,1−t²). One input summary would have to produce different output summaries under the same gate, so no exact abstract update exists on means and covariance alone. The future readout, pulled back through the gate, requires the observable XYZ; adding it completes a basis for all functions on the eight states. That is closure under permitted future tests, not a decision to reward correlation.

Exact Markov lumpability is the standard tool for aggregating states. A partition is lumpable when states in the same block send equal total probability into every block under every admitted common action. [established] Policies, targets, costs, records, and preparation conditions each need their own factorization check. **A dynamically valid quotient can still erase a distinction that matters to a different question.**

## The reference survives restriction

Operational equivalence does not establish ontic identity. If every admitted test misses a physical distinction, the distinction remains in the model. Only an explicitly separating family, such as all cylinder events of a declared classical path space, determines the path law. There is no automatic theorem that the affordable bounded tests exhaust every aspect of the global object. The family of bounded descriptions is attached to the underlying model; the model is never defined as whatever survives the current tests.

One requirement summarizes this part. It is deliberately neutral between state-based and response-based representations:

> **The representation must determine the continuation relations being compared.**

A sufficient joint state together with its dynamics can do that. A reduced present state may omit environmental memory, and then a multi-time process description is needed at that interface.

# 03 — Generative organization

## Consequence that changes the conditions of consequence

A reaction produces a catalyst. An apparatus turns a sequence into the specification for another apparatus. An organism builds an environment that changes what its successors can do. A civilization establishes institutions and instruments through which previously inaccessible distinctions become usable.

In each case the endpoint changed, but that is not the important part. **What can happen next, how reliably it can happen, what it requires, and what can happen together** have changed. This part develops that observation into a research object.

The ladder from chemistry to civilization is a motivating example of recursively enabled organization, not a strict hierarchy. Maintenance cycles, joint dependencies, overlapping organizations, succession, and breakdown all belong in the picture.

## Generativity is extracted, not rewarded

The originator proposes generativity as the primary engine of lushness. **[proposed]** The intended explanation runs in one direction: from physical organization, to the ways it sustains or produces further organization, and only then to a comparison of the resulting continuation structure.

Adding a positive coefficient for "generativity," "life," "diversity," "independence," or "depth" would skip that inquiry. These can be studied as structural features or candidate diagnostics, but their significance has to be earned through the continuations they characterize.

A process can be consequential without being reusable. A short-lived event can enable an entire lineage. Reusable construction is an especially useful probe of generativity. It is not a rule that excludes transient events from the object.

## A ladder of questions

A useful way into the object is a sequence of five cases. At each step, ask what additional continuation relation has appeared.

1. **A physical difference.** Two situations differ. Does anything downstream depend on the difference?
2. **A retained record.** The difference persists in a form a later process can read.
3. **A usable transformation.** Some later process can use the record, or the organization carrying it, to realize a task it could not otherwise realize.
4. **A transformation that builds another transformation.** A process produces or configures an apparatus that then realizes further tasks.
5. **A mutually supporting organization.** Several processes jointly maintain one another's enabling conditions, and none could do so alone.

The sequence illustrates questions. It does not rank the cases, and it must not smuggle an ethical ordering into the examples.

## Lawful possibility and situated accessibility

**No extra-lawful possibilities.** If a prefix can reach a boundary condition and a continuation can proceed from it, their compatible concatenation is a path allowed by the encompassing dynamics. [project result; elementary] Construction never creates possibilities outside the lawful possibility space. If a civilization can arise from a nebula, its later possibilities already belong to the nebula's complete continuation structure.

What construction changes is **conditional accessibility**: the probability of a route, its resource requirements and delay, its reliability, the control available along it, and its dependence on further favourable accidents. An already-built factory has a different capability profile from the materials and conditions it was produced from, even though every one of its routes was lawfully available from the start.

Comparing local situations therefore needs the family of budgets, horizons, and conditional laws. It cannot be reduced to counting the eventual union of reachable endpoints.

## Three relations: construction, maintenance, dependence

Three different relations are recorded separately. **[proposed]**

- **Construction:** a witnessed process produces conditions that support a further implementation.
- **Maintenance:** a witnessed process sustains or restores those conditions under stated circumstances.
- **Dependence:** a specified physical alteration changes a downstream continuation law.

A dependence claim needs an explicit comparison. If u is an admissible disabling or replacement operation, compare the resulting continuation laws under matched interfaces and declared budgets. The comparison is conditional on u. Removing a component may also remove energy, space, information, or other support, and those changes belong in the comparison. The construction supplies no universal, context-free attribution of causal importance. Dependence alone establishes neither construction nor maintenance.

## How accessibility composes

For a realized prefix α followed by a record-dependent continuation β_r **[established; elementary conditioning]**:

```text
P_c^{(β_r)∘α}(E_T) = Σ_{x,r,y}  μ_c(x) · J_{α,r}(x, y) · P_y^{β_r}(E_T).
```

The event E_T is read consistently as an event on the continuation. For a deadline counted from the origin, include the prefix's elapsed time and expenditure. This identity is the minimal mathematical form of "consequence changing the conditions for further consequence": the prefix changes the distribution over situations from which later transformations proceed. It is not a theorem of emergence, and it assigns no generativity score.

## An enabling contrast

A candidate way to say that P enables Q in context c **[proposed]**:

```text
Δ_c^{H,b}(P, N; Q) = P_c^{Q∘P}(E) − P_c^{Q∘N}(E).
```

The horizon, budget convention, and event E are the same on both sides, and the continuation rule is physically implementable. N is a declared, feasible comparison preparation. If Q cannot be deployed after one of the prefixes, that inability is recorded, not silently dropped. A policy-dependent version needs the same restrictions on access.

The contrast depends on its baseline. A null process that matches every physical consequence of P except the property of interest may not exist, and the choice of N has to be defended case by case. A positive contrast is neither a context-free attribution nor an increment of value.

## A recursive presentation

With a declared base record B_0, finite bounds, and a physical implementation catalogue, the construction proposes the recursion **[proposed]**:

```text
G_0(x) = B_0(x)
G_H(x) = ( B_0(x),
           { π ↦ Law_x^π( r, t, b, G_{H−t}(X_t) ) }_{π admitted},
           Joint_H(x) ).
```

Each nontrivial stage takes positive time, 1 ≤ t ≤ H, so the recursion is well founded. Budgets can be carried as extra indices and decremented. Failed and unfinished outcomes are kept, with their actual boundary states. Elementary physical steps stay in the catalogue; constructors are not the only admitted processes.

In words: record what is currently distinguished, what each implemented process can do and with what probability and cost, the continuation structure it leaves behind, and what can coexist with it. The recursion is not a generativity score. It is a place from which organizational relationships can be extracted.

It extends the repository's finite action/outcome behaviour profiles, which already nest possible outcomes inside available alternatives, by adding stochastic laws, physical realization, time and resource accounting, and joint composition. [project result for the base recursion; proposed for the extension] No formal derivation of computationally useful generative structure from it has yet been completed.

**Presentation rules for this equation.**

- **Keep the indexing by physically meaningful operations and interfaces.** A bare set of output laws can erase how a process is deployed or composed.
- **Treat renaming by consistent isomorphism of the whole presentation.** Literal names are not physical distinctions.
- **Identify only consistent redescriptions of the same physical presentation.** Equal selected behavior, equal entropy, mutual convertibility or matching costs do not prove that two physical histories are one occurrence. Predictive quotients may be used separately with a stated scope. Copies and physical noise remain in the reference object.
- **Derive the joint entry.** Joint_H(x) is read off physically witnessed joint realizations and interface maps (Part 02). It is not supplied independently.
- **Require agreement on composites.** Where a composite protocol is represented directly, its law must agree with the law obtained by valid composition. The catalogue is not closed under physically impossible syntactic combinations.
- **Keep the analyst's state separate from the controller's records.** Keeping G_{H−t}(y) inside an analyst's probability law does not give a controller knowledge of y. Adaptive continuations are implemented from accessible records only.

Exact enumeration grows quickly with the horizon. A proposed efficient approximation has to state which continuation and composition distinctions it preserves.

## The derived organization graph

For a fixed physical model, admitted interfaces, and declared bounds, the extracted structure can be presented as a graph whose edges may have several sources and several targets **[proposed]**:

- **construction edges** record the physical production of enabling conditions;
- **maintenance edges** record supported continuation or recovery;
- **information edges** record access to distinctions;
- **control edges** record physically available influence;
- **joint-feasibility records** state which combinations can coexist;
- **lineage records** connect particular realizations through history.

Every edge carries its context, witness, time, resource account, and outcome law. A single global graph with unqualified edges is not enough. Two edges that are each possible under different conditions cannot automatically be traversed together; composition is permitted only where contexts and witnesses match.

Control and information edges are kept separate from construction edges, and separate again from consent or identity judgments. A factory's machines may all be controlled by the factory and still add real capability. Whether a set of processes has independent access to the world is a different question from whether they construct one another (Part 06).

The graph is a derived presentation of the object, not a replacement for the path laws and not a source of extra possibilities. An operation can then be described as deforming this structure: it changes the conditional availability and compatibility of later realizations. Whether that deformation is an expansion, a contraction, or mixed is always relative to a declared comparison.

## Readouts and their conventions

Once the structure is extracted, simple readouts suggest themselves. Each has conventions that must be shown. **[proposed; cautions are project results]**

- **Depth depends on how operations are divided.** The longest dependency chain changes when an operation is subdivided (Part 11, Example 18).
- **Cycles need a declared convention.** They make unqualified path lengths infinite, and restricting to simple paths adopts a different convention.
- **Strongly connected structure is not sustainable organization.** A cycle of declared dependencies drawn on paper is not proof of mutual maintenance; the participating processes must be jointly realizable, with their supply and loss accounted for.
- **Higher-order dependence needs higher-order identification.** An intact system and all its single-component knockouts need not determine what happens when two components are removed. A function with two redundant supports and an always-on function agree on the intact case and every single removal, and disagree when both supports go (Part 11, Example 16). A predictive test must therefore state the structural assumptions, or the additional evidence, behind any higher-order prediction. A pairwise support graph is not automatically sufficient; hyperedges or richer conditional laws may be needed.

None of these readouts is lushness. Each should be reported with what it measures and which representation choices affect it. "Consequence that makes consequence" must not quietly become a count of syntactic stages.

## Channels being constructed, not information being retained

Let U be an early physical preparation, V a later intervention or environmental input, and Y an outcome. A diagnostic family is **[proposed]**:

```text
K_u(y | v) = P( Y = y | do(U = u), do(V = v), c ).
```

All interventions are specified inside the model, and embodied claims need realization witnesses. This is a probe, not a replacement definition of the global object. It separates three questions:

1. **Does U change the response to V?** Ordinary nonlinear interactions already qualify.
2. **Does U establish an available, reusable transformation,** with its operating and recovery conditions?
3. **Can that transformation establish or maintain further transformations,** verified by later interactions?

The third is the recursive research target. The second is a useful intermediate case, not a condition every generative event must meet. The distinctions should come out of modelled physical behaviour, not out of labels such as "factory" or "life."

Mutual information or channel capacity describes one aspect of the resulting input–output relation. Equal capacity does not mean equal transformation repertoires or equal composability. Empowerment, the channel capacity from an agent's actions to its future sensor states, is a close neighbouring measure of control, not a synonym for lushness. [established; R55]

**Retention is a different quantity.** For a fixed Markov chain A → X_t → X_{t+1}, the data-processing inequality gives [established]:

```text
I(A; X_{t+1}) ≤ I(A; X_t).
```

That measures how much information about A survives. Building a new apparatus can improve the channel between *later* inputs and outputs even while information about the original trigger is lost (Part 11, Example 14). There is no contradiction: the signals and the channel being assessed are different. The physical resources that support the new organization remain in the accounting.

Two further cautions about decay **[established; R45]**:

- **One-step contraction need not hold.** Even in a finite irreducible aperiodic chain, strict mixing may appear only over longer blocks of steps.
- **Single-component noise does not set a memory lifetime.** Redundancy, coding, protected degrees of freedom, and physical repair change the applicable channel or model.

A closed finite mixing model supports a valid decay theorem. It says nothing about the eventual fate of every resource-fed system, and the retention of one initial signal is not the rise and fall of generativity.

The proposed contribution is **the organization of conditional access to further transformations**: its reliability, compatibility, dependence, repair, and development across horizons. **[proposed]** These relations may distinguish systems that endpoint entropy, covariance, damage counts, or raw reachability conflate.

A full physical model already determines all of this in principle. Merely renaming the full model "generativity" gains nothing operationally. The research target is therefore an **actionable extraction**: a compact representation with stated preservation guarantees, or with measured limits on what it predicts.

# 04 — Lushness

## The candidate

**Lushness is the proposed extent of recursively accessible, weighted and jointly compatible continuation.** Its motivating role is to compare the substrate of possible value when neither values nor valuers can be exhaustively specified.

The image is branching fractality occupying something volume-like: consequences become conditions of further consequences, and composition changes the organization of subsequent access. Exact self-similarity, a particular fractal dimension and ordinary geometric volume are not part of the definition.

The candidate carrier is the continuation field of Parts 01–03 and Part 12, §M. At a cut, it retains which physical transformations can be realized, their actual response laws and bills, their compatibility with other transformations, and their residual continuation. A factory matters through the transformations and further construction its organization makes accessible. Calling something a factory contributes nothing by itself.

The whole field is the reference. The finite access atlas is a proposed presentation of its operational structure. The current atlas schema is not yet an implemented universal extractor or an extent functional.

**Exploratory adoption — 3 October 2026.** At the originator's direction, adopt Opus's normalized frame volume and encompassing-frame count-once/cheapest-access aggregation as the working comparison. Adoption selects what to explore; it is not a validation claim. In the finite classical setting, with distinction variable X and physical records R_phi:

    ell_phi = I(X; R_phi) - H(X)
    V_phi = 2^ell_phi.

The encompassing frame has V = 1 when it resolves X completely. A frame with no information has V = 2^(-H(X)), not zero. Branch weights use the existing conditioned law and information chain rule; exponentiation does not turn that chain rule into an arithmetic average of volumes. A quantum extension must specify the recorded ensemble and physical instruments.

Use the encompassing view of access across frames, vector budgets and horizons. The working aggregation credits distinct joint content once through its feasible cheapest-access routes, retaining a profile and dominance where available. A scalar arbitration choice must be stated when needed. How overlapping joint distinctions and access routes enter that aggregate is the next finite implementation choice, not a reason to postpone exploration. The raw subset-information tally remains a baseline. This update adopts the construction; it does not implement or run it.

**First implementation, same day.** The [bounded aggregation prototype](../../research_notes/omega_v2/frame_aggregation_report_v0.md) now implements a narrow version: joint delivered information, a partition-to-access-frontier catalog, and a maximum jointly deliverable volume at each frame/budget cell. It does not sum separately feasible deliveries or assume a distribution on programs. Eighteen preparations/loss cases under three source laws are evaluated within a two-gate language, with a targeted three-gate follow-up. Its envelope ties two distinct access structures under fair inputs; the route catalog still distinguishes them. This is a limitation of that compression, not a completed all-frame arbitration rule.

## What probability does and does not supply

The underlying physical law and frame conditioning supply weights. For a fixed physically implemented protocol, all its outcomes retain their weight, including failure and noncompletion. A rare route has its actual small weight; a conditional success does not make its preparation certain.

There is no missing license to choose a convenient prior over programs. Enumerating 1,000 possible implementations does not make them equiprobable futures. Their selection can itself be an embodied physical process, with a law determined by its state, records and couplings.

Normalization of the encompassing law to one is compatible with different continuation structure. Probability answers how mass is distributed over specified alternatives. It does not alone identify which geometric or operational extent of that structure is lushness. Multiplying a branch probability into a quantity already integrated against that same branch law would count the weight twice.

Shannon entropy, effective support and quantum mutual information are useful readouts of declared laws. None is forced as lushness by the project's commitments. Faddeev-type characterizations require specific axioms, including a grouping rule and regularity; they do not identify the physical carriers, preserve every compositional distinction, or settle the ethical connection.

## What an adequate comparison must retain

The comparison should be investigated on a representation that keeps:

- **Consequence throughout the field.** Noise, gas, finite episodes, inaccessible environmental records and destructive processes remain present.
- **Joint physical compatibility.** Individually available transformations need not share one feasible execution.
- **Situated access.** A mathematical decoder is not a physical route unless its hardware, coupling, resources and time are available.
- **Residual dependence.** The same immediate response can leave different future access. A local marginal can omit consequential environment correlations.
- **Actual selection.** Congestion, temptation, cooperation and capture occur in the dynamics; they do not require an added “choice quality” coefficient.
- **Consistent redescription.** Renaming the entire apparatus preserves its content. Physically moving a device, rewiring fixed readers, delaying a response or spreading a record need not.
- **Frame and horizon coherence.** Compatible restrictions refer to the same physical process. Finite losses do not disappear because a chosen asymptotic statistic forgets them.

These are representation obligations and research constraints, not a uniqueness proof for the final comparison. Different structures can have equal extent. The problem with a proposed summary is systematic blindness to a consequential relation it claims to measure, not every possible tie.

## Current information does not determine access

Two encodings can retain exactly the same distinctions under every source law and require identical exhibited preparation bills, yet differ in the operations needed for a later response. Part 11, Example 19 gives:

    A = (s XOR f1, 0, NOT s)
    B = (0, s, f1).

At independent Bernoulli(.2) inputs both hold 1.443856189775 bits. In the stated symmetric decoder panel, NOT f1 costs two operations from A and one from B. The distinction survives adding polarity-symmetric primitives. Neither preparation bill is claimed globally minimal.

This is why the candidate keeps response laws with their physical realization. Entropy already knows what the bank distinguishes; it does not know the bill of using it through the available apparatus.

Recovery gives a related but different failure. The banks (u, u AND v, v) and (u, u, v) contain the same joint distinctions and have equal entropy and full-bank Syn. One versus two of their three single-register losses admits exact reconstruction from the survivors. Part 11, Example 20 states the access restriction and the ambiguity proof. Physical distribution changes repair structure without an added information-content bonus.

## Composition and generativity

Composition can establish a coupling, maintain an apparatus, change a reaction environment, or route distinctions into further physical use. The microscopic laws can remain fixed. New organization need not add a Hilbert-space factor or produce behavior unpredicted by the complete initial physics.

The candidate represents this by restarting from the actual residual after a prefix, with the apparatus, resources and correlations it leaves. A chain is genuinely compositional only where these boundaries match. Separate best-case answers cannot be glued into a nonexistent joint execution.

Synergy and hierarchy are useful diagnostics of this organization. In the deterministic source-record probe, the statistic called Syn obeys

    Syn(F) = I(X; F) − sum_i I(X_i; F)
           = TC(X | F) − TC(X).

For independent sources it is conditional total correlation. Inherited dependence can make it negative. It must not be clipped or called harmful composition for that reason. Conditional routing can also improve a small record's information while this Syn is zero. The atlas must therefore keep the joint laws and transformations from which both diagnostics are extracted.

Correlation between Syn and lushness is a hypothesis to investigate, not an assumption of the aggregation. Positive, zero and negative values remain. Assess their structural consequences under actual dynamics rather than assigning a verdict from the sign or a mechanism's name.

Repeated reuse, construction amortization and maintenance under depletion are promising questions for this architecture. The latest short run did not test them. A witnessed composition is not yet evidence of open-ended generativity.

## Harms and recovery are deformations of the same object

A physical loss of a link, record, resource, repair route or joint feasibility is structural. Detecting congestion does not first require assigning utilities to drivers: occupancy, waiting and mutual obstruction already alter continuation.

The framework seeks to understand harm through these deformations and their propagation. A destruction can have enormous immediate influence while leaving little subsequent access. A perturbation can remove one channel while opening another. The first contrast requires residual analysis; the second can remain mixed until a justified encompassing comparison is supplied.

A noisy failure can add a real consequential distinction while damaging a content channel. Deleting that noise by definition would decide the result in advance. The correct response is to retain both changes and test the proposed comparison. An unsigned field does not yet entail a total ethical sign for every deformation.

Recovery is physically situated. Information surviving somewhere in the whole does not prove that an affected process can retrieve it within the available time and resources. An inverse circuit is a recovery witness and an upper bound on cost, not automatically a minimum. First return, persistent correction and correction before irreversible loss are different questions.

## Comparisons available now

Three levels can be used without conflation:

1. **A response-and-bill comparison in a fixed apparatus.** An explicit program realizes a specified joint response with a declared residual and bill. A finite exhaustive search can exclude cheaper programs only inside its stated language and bound.
2. **A coherent physical realization comparison.** A single causal compiler or device realizes another process across the required histories, inputs and budgets, including failure and residual behavior. This is a proposed stronger relation; separate taskwise emulations do not establish it.
3. **A lushness comparison.** The weighted extent of the whole recursive structure adjudicates substrate expansion, contraction and mixed change. This is the open core.

A set of feasible budget vectors can be reported before a scalar. Routes with bills (1,10) and (10,1) do not provide a route with bill (1,1). Cost is an implementation certificate; minimizing it alone is not the program's value thesis.

For free-choice models, a faithful resource-preserving realization can transfer attainable outcomes. That elementary implication is not a theorem of unanimous realized value for arbitrary embedded choosers. The converse and the relevant hypothesis classes are also open. The program is not waiting for that bridge to define its field, but must not claim to have proved it.

# 05 — Identity, persistence, and recovery

## What persists through change?

A whirlpool exchanges its water. An organism replaces its molecules. An institution replaces its members. Each persists through organized relations and causal continuity, not through an unchanging inventory of material.

The framework uses **identity invariant** for a candidate structure through which a continuing process remains identifiable across specified transformations. "Invariant" is a research demand: name what is preserved, under which transformations, and how that preservation is established. No universal mathematical invariant of personal identity has been identified.

The invariant need not be a number or a hidden object at the centre of the process. It may be a relational pattern, a continuity relation, a family of constraints, or an organized capacity to maintain and reconstruct characteristic behaviour.

## Six things to keep apart

The continuation machinery of Parts 02–03 separates several relations that ordinary talk about identity runs together:

- **Functional readiness:** the capability profile of a process now, its Cap_{H,b,ε} at a declared interface.
- **Maintained physical organization:** the maintenance edges that sustain the conditions of that capability through time.
- **Reconstruction from a record:** a later process built to the specification a record carries.
- **Lineage:** the causal history connecting particular realizations.
- **Numerical identity:** whether two occurrences are the very same individual.
- **Phenomenal continuity:** whether experience continues, which depends on the open questions of Part 09.

Two processes with identical continuation laws under every admitted test are continuation-equivalent (Part 02). They can still have different origins, and they are still two processes. Continuation equivalence is therefore an important tool for identity research, not a replacement for it. It answers a question about substitutability within a domain, and identity is a different question.

A successful account says which of the six it addresses. The familiar questions of **type** (what kind of pattern is this?), **continuity** (how is this occurrence connected to earlier and later ones?), and **numerical identity** (is it the very same individual?) remain useful shorthand for the same separation.

## The trajectory view

The originator's current interpretation is that identity belongs to the trajectory, not to the pattern. **[conjecture]**

> You are a pattern, but the pattern isn't you. The trajectory is what gives the pattern youness.

On this view a pattern is a type that many occurrences can share. What makes an occurrence *someone in particular* is the physical history through which that pattern is continuously produced, maintained, and transformed. Several consequences follow within the interpretation:

- **Growth.** A person at five and at fifty are the same and not the same. The pattern has changed greatly. The trajectory is continuous, and it is the trajectory that carries the identity.
- **Pauses.** Physical structure channels flows of energy and information into a particular organization, and that structure is part of what shapes and maintains the pattern. If activity pauses while the structure persists, as in cryopreservation or deep hypothermic circulatory arrest, the trajectory continues through the pause. Continuity of the maintaining structure, not uninterrupted activity, is what the view requires.
- **Copies and simulations.** A simulation can reproduce a pattern, and even be equivalent to the original in every functional respect, without being the original's trajectory.
- **Destructive transfer.** In a teleporter that scans, destroys, and rebuilds, something survives: a successor with the same pattern, which may carry forward the lushness the original would have generated. On the trajectory view the original does not survive. The physical destruction of the trajectory is death.
- **Branching.** In a quantum-branching picture, each continuation is a distinct situated trajectory. In the originator's words, "the you you experience is only ever the you that you do."

This is an interpretation that the machinery can *describe*: it corresponds to letting lineage and maintained physical organization, rather than functional readiness or reconstruction, carry numerical identity. It is not derived from channel algebra, and the teleporter verdict is not a theorem. Other identity theories choose different relations from the list above, and the framework can represent those choices as well. What it contributes is the requirement to say which relation is doing the work.

## Vorticality and persistent organization

Vortices are a compelling example of organization maintained through movement. Under suitable conditions, circulation or topological winding distinguishes a pattern from its background. A complex order parameter that stays nonzero around a loop permits the integer winding

```text
n = (1 / 2π) ∮ dθ.
```

Continuous deformations that preserve the relevant conditions cannot change n. The same n characterizes many different vortices, so it identifies a *class*, not an individual. Topological-defect research supplies the actual mathematics. [established; R21]

Vorticality is therefore a motivating image for organized persistence, with physical realizations where they apply. A crystal, a computational organization, and a dissipative biochemical cycle may need quite different invariants. The image does not establish a universal centre or axis around which every perspective literally rotates. Wolfram's proposed link between localized structural obstructions and particles is a neighbouring research idea, not an established identification. [R22]

A perspective is organized around its actual physical relations: coupling, causal access, correlations, and continuation. For a massive system, proper time along a worldline helps order local events. For a massless excitation, null propagation and field structure supply a different description. Internal symmetries, charges, helicity, and conserved quantities can characterize a process without becoming an immortal essence. [R17, R18]

## Self-modification

Agents can modify the mechanisms that carry their own continuity. An artificial system might replace its memory substrate, revise its policy, distribute itself across devices, or merge with another system. Biology supplies less deliberate versions of many of these.

A practical identity analysis records causal lineage, preserved memory, functional competence, commitments, internal organization, and relationships separately. Which combination matters depends on the question. Repairing a service, preserving a person's agency, and reconstructing a species are different targets. A replacement that restores external output can fail to restore the individual. Conversely, insisting on molecular sameness can obscure real continuity through ordinary biological change.

## Recovery is a relation in the continuation structure

An action can damage a system while leaving routes back to relevant forms of continuation. Another action can remove those routes. The distinction between recoverable loss and foreclosure is central, because it describes how consequence persists.

Specify a target region A, a physical state x after disturbance, available policies Π, contingencies E, resources, and a horizon H. **Existential recovery** asks whether some allowed continuation reaches A. **Robust recovery** asks whether one available policy secures that result across the registered contingencies while respecting constraints along the way. Viability theory and control theory supply established machinery for both. [established; R23; Part 12, §D]

In the vocabulary of Part 03, recovery is supported by **maintenance witnesses**: realized processes that sustain or restore the conditions of a capability. A repair route in a model counts only if its apparatus, resources, and records are available when the damage occurs. Existential reachability in an optimistic abstraction is weaker than robust recovery in the world. Confusing a route imagined by a model with a realizable physical route is a major failure mode of consequential reasoning.

## Three repair targets

- **Function:** restoring a capability or output.
- **Lineage:** maintaining a causal organization through the damage.
- **Individual:** the additional identity claim that the very same individual continues.

These targets can coincide. They should be registered separately when the distinction matters. A new copy of a burned book restores its text, not the original artifact's history. Replacing a destroyed agent with one that produces the same answers may restore a service while leaving the individual's continuation unresolved. On the trajectory view above, it does not restore the individual at all.

This also blocks a common aggregation mistake. A gain elsewhere does not, by itself, repair a loss here. Compensation and restoration are different relations.

## Information retention and feasible return

Closed unitary dynamics preserves quantum information globally. A macroscopic process can still disperse the information needed for reconstruction into correlations that a given system cannot access, or that would need unavailable resources and control to reverse.

Burning a book illustrates the difference. Global physical traces may remain, while every feasible route available to the relevant agents fails to reconstruct the text. Whether recovery is possible depends on the actual state, interactions, resources, and continuation domain, not merely on the reversibility of microscopic law. The full physical object contains whatever return routes actually exist, and a bounded model estimates them.

## Horizon and the corridor

A short horizon can miss a slow repair route, and a longer one can reveal it. A longer horizon can equally reveal a delayed failure (Part 11, Example 5). Reachability and sustained viability use time differently and should not be merged into one "longer is better" claim.

For a safe region K, a **viability corridor** consists of the conditions from which admissible policies keep the process in K for the specified duration and contingencies. The corridor changes as resources, couplings, and capabilities change. It is a structured region of state and policy space, not a static list of forbidden moves.

A loss is **terminal** relative to a target and domain when no continuation in that domain restores the target. The framework's event-horizon imagery refers to this loss of return routes. It does not mean that every such boundary is literally gravitational.

## Redundancy that creates possibility

Two copies of a record can behave identically in normal operation and still differ from a single copy under damage. With independent failure probability p, losing both copies has probability p², against p for one. Shared failure causes can erase that advantage (Part 11, Example 4).

The consequential difference is carried by the implementation: location, coupling, repair mechanisms, and failure dependence. Removing repeated labels from a description is correct. Removing real alternative repair routes is not. This is the representation requirement of Part 04 that separates descriptive duplication from physical duplication.

Life supplies many examples: error correction, distributed inheritance, immune memory, overlapping functions, social learning, ecological interdependence. A beneficial mutation that disappears before it is copied shows the converse: a potentially generative distinction can be lost before redundancy develops.

## Correction must persist, and can arrive too late

First reset, corrected occupancy at a deadline and permanent correction are
different properties. In a fixed finite Markov model, let C be the states
whose register is corrected. The largest closed subset of C can be computed
by repeatedly removing any state with a positive-probability successor
outside the remaining set. Entry into that subset guarantees continued
correction under that same law. Changing the controller or disturbance law
changes the calculation.

A reset can occur with probability one while permanent correction has
probability zero. Eventual permanent correction can also occur only after
an irreversible loss. Thus record both the permanent-correction law and the
race between correction and the registered loss. A later reset does not
remove the harm history. These distinctions are retained in the
[decision experiment](../../research_notes/omega_v2/lushness_decision_report_v0.md);
they constrain any future claim that recovery is derived from lushness.

The matched-content repair witness in Part 11, Example 20 adds another
distinction: the distribution of a record affects which local losses admit
reconstruction even when the bank's information and Syn agree. This does not
settle numerical or phenomenal identity after repair.

## Regeneration and its limits

Regeneration is stronger than holding a state unchanged. A regenerative system can rebuild capabilities, or develop new ways of continuing, after disturbance. Its recovery routes can change the space of available policies.

In Part 03's terms, regeneration is a maintenance relation that is itself constructive: the repair process produces conditions for further implementations, not only for the original one. This is a promising connection to lushness. Whether a candidate comparison captures it should be shown on examples, not guaranteed by adding a resilience bonus to the definition.

The ethical relevance comes later. When valuers depend on these routes, closing them can remove the possibility of correction, development, or further valuation (Part 07).

Further reading: topological winding [R21]; substrate-based particle proposals [R22]; spacetime and particle representations [R17, R18]; viability and reachability [R23]; requisite variety [R24]; personal identity [R52].

# 06 — Agency, valuers, and ecologies

## Deformation and agency

**Deformation** is a difference in continuation. A **deformer** is any process whose condition, interaction, or existence makes such a difference. It need not act intentionally, maintain itself, or value anything. Every physical process is a deformer somewhere.

**Agency** adds organized sensitivity to circumstances and the capacity to alter how continuation proceeds. A policy can be explicit software, an evolved control architecture, or a distributed pattern of response. An agency model becomes useful when interventions on internal organization systematically change the system's response to its situation. Learning, planning, self-maintenance, and self-modification enrich that structure. They are not all needed at the lower boundary of the concept.

That lower boundary sits low. A thermostat directs futures: its internal state selects among continuations of the room according to a set point. Agency in this minimal sense lies well below any question of experience. The ladder of Part 03 applies directly: a thermostat has a retained record and a usable transformation, but it does not build further transformations or maintain the conditions of its own operation.

Agents deform continuation through physical action. They build tools, consume resources, alter other agents, establish institutions, and change which later actions are available. Describing agency this way keeps the wider consequences in view without making causal influence a measure of value.

## Construction, control, and information are different edges

The derived organization graph of Part 03 separates construction, maintenance, information, and control edges. The separation matters here.

A factory's machines may all be controlled by one management system and still add real capability. Control over a process is not the same as having constructed it, and neither is the same as the process having independent access to the world. An organism can construct its own environment, changing the selection pressures and capabilities of its successors, without controlling them. [established; niche construction, R51] A controller can hold perfect information about a process while lacking the actuators to change it.

These distinctions are the physical ground for later ethical ones. Capture, dependence, cooperation, and consent all involve different combinations of these edges. They are not settled by the physics, but the physics has to keep them apart for the ethics to have anything to work with.

## Valuerhood

**Value requires valuers.** Beauty, welfare, achievement, significance, and any other form of value need some locus for which distinctions can matter. The program uses "valuer" broadly, to keep that dependence in view without settling its final demarcation in advance.

The field admits **every consequential process as a potential valuer**. Endogenous differentiation, self-maintenance, selective response, error correction and preference-like structure are hypotheses about particular forms of valuation, not admission criteria. A gas, a transient fluctuation and an unfamiliar composition remain represented before any such hypothesis is applied. **[architectural commitment]**

Thermostats, organisms, institutions, nonconscious control systems, and conscious subjects are all boundary cases to investigate. Calling them all perspectives does not decide which are valuers or what standing they have. Agency is not sufficient for valuerhood, and valuerhood is not sufficient for any particular ethical weight.

The physical investigation need not first divide the world into a fixed list of individual valuers. **Valuing can be studied as a process** while its bearers remain to be individuated. The originator's stronger intuition is that, if lushness is what he thinks it is, a precise definition of valuerhood may turn out to be unnecessary: valuing would be a feature of the whole structure, with individual valuers as designations within it. **[conjecture]** That is compatible with the dependence claim. It does not mean that every physical difference is already valuation.

Keep four things distinct: **deformation** (a difference in continuation), **endogenous significance** (a difference that matters through a process's own organization), **moral consideration** (a reason for others to attend to it), and **value**. The program has not established a general formal theory of stakes that bridges these automatically, and it does not install one as an unexamined intermediate layer.

An agent can implement a goal supplied by another process. A valuer's orientation can be conflicted, unstable, or self-destructive. That value requires valuers does not imply that every valuer endorses its own persistence or expands the possibilities of others. Value is not defined by whichever continuations a candidate valuer happens to select.

## The Darwinian engine

Persistence filters what remains. Reproduction and propagation let some organizations occupy more of the available resources and carry their characteristics further. Under suitable conditions, selection favours traits that contribute to that continuation.

The engine has no preference. It reveals a **corridor** of continuations that survive the actual pressures, opportunities, and interactions. It does not pick a universally best endpoint. Sterile replicators, parasites, cooperative ecologies, and highly generative systems can each succeed under different conditions. There is no universal forward-expansion law (Part 03), and nothing establishes that what survives is ethically best.

Reproduction matters especially for filling newly available niches, but a persistent process need not reproduce to continue. The framework studies retention, propagation, resource competition, and transformation together.

The stronger lushness hypothesis is that some generative organizations expand their consequential reach by creating new capabilities and compatible forms of coexistence. That hypothesis has content precisely because selection alone does not guarantee it.

## Shared stakes and competing continuations

Agents can share dependencies without sharing a numerical identity. Their survival can rely on the same ecosystem, infrastructure, knowledge, or social trust. Correlated trajectories can create shared stakes and make sacrifice intelligible within a larger continuing organization.

Correlation alone does not show that interests coincide. A host and a parasite can be tightly coupled while making conflicting demands. The questions are what is jointly feasible, what each process requires, and who bears the loss when their continuations conflict.

For a family F of valuers, let R_i be valuer i's registered continuation requirement **[project result; Part 12, §D]**:

- **May compatibility:** some admissible joint continuation satisfies every R_i.
- **Robust compatibility:** one available policy secures the joint requirements across a specified nonempty contingency set.

Robust compatibility implies may compatibility, and not conversely. Individual feasibility is weaker than joint feasibility, and pairwise compatibility is weaker than compatibility of the whole group (Part 11, Examples 6 and 13). These facts matter for institutions and alignment: promises that can each be kept in isolation may compete for the same irreplaceable resource.

## What an independent process adds

A recurring question is what an independent valuer, source, or controller contributes that an integrated one could not. Information theory answers one part of it exactly, and it is important to see which part. **[established; Part 12, §H]**

Let Θ be an environmental variable, C the information an integrated controller already has, and M the information an additional source would add. The quantity

```text
Δ = I(Θ; M | C)
```

measures what M adds about Θ beyond C. In a finite setting under the specified joint law, Δ = 0 means the pair (C, M) carries no more information about Θ than C does. For ideal Bayesian decisions about Θ, observing both then cannot improve on observing C, on the supported distribution.

That is a statement about evidence and ideal decision performance. It does not settle whether an independent process is useful:

- An extra subsystem can supply **computation, execution, redundancy, or practical error correction** that a controller with perfect information lacks. A controller that knows Θ exactly still cannot act at two distant places at once with one actuator (Part 11, Example 15).
- When Δ > 0, the benefit belongs to the **pair** (C, M), not necessarily to M alone. With independent fair bits C and M and Θ = C ⊕ M, neither alone reveals anything about Θ, while together they determine it.
- Statements under a fixed prior do not automatically cover alternative preparations the prior does not support.

So the result is not a theorem that an independent agent always outperforms an integrated controller, or the reverse. It does not bear on consent, coercion, epistemic virtue, moral standing, or fragility. The originator's framing is that capture versus consent is a problem posed by uncertainty. The information quantity helps locate where that uncertainty lies; it does not decide the ethical question.

## Plurality, integration, and excision

A plurality can distribute exploration, correction, resources, and independent judgment. Its effective power depends on coordination and starting conditions. Numbers or material size alone do not guarantee control of outcomes.

An integrated controller can also embody rich internal variety. A proposed limiting equivalence holds that, at a suitable level of description, an integrated generative controller and a well-coordinated plurality may realize the same functional variety. **[conjecture]** Stating it precisely requires specified structure-preserving maps between the two organizations: equivalence of response capability, of internal variety, or of patterns of valuation are different claims. It is not an established isomorphism, and it would not by itself identify their phenomenal subjects or their political arrangements.

- An **excisive** controller flattens or removes consequential alternatives, and in particular other generative continuations, to secure control.
- A **generative** controller maintains or develops the variety needed for open-ended response.

Ashby's requisite variety is a useful foundation for comparing regulatory demands with available responses. It is not a theorem that decentralized organization always wins, or that centralized organization does. [established; R24]

**Terminal solitude** names the closure in which independent valuation has been eliminated and cannot be regenerated within the relevant continuation. It chiefly concerns excisive control. Neither centralization nor a single identity entails it: one integrated system with rich internal valuation is not terminally solitary merely because it has one outer boundary.

## Machine intelligence

The functional parts of this account apply to artificial systems now. Their agency, construction and control relations, information and joint feasibility can be analyzed without settling actual valuerhood. They are already eligible as potential valuers through their consequences. Which forms of valuation or phenomenal awareness they instantiate remains open. The primer does not infer a welfare conclusion from a hardware label or from an information statistic.

Further reading: requisite variety [R24]; viability [R23]; predictive organization [R19, R37]; niche construction [R51]; information quantities [R48]; endogenous normativity [R32].

# 07 — Gradient Ethics

## The starting point

Gradient Ethics (GE) begins from a dependence claim: value requires valuers. Whatever value finally proves to be, its possibility depends on processes through which distinctions can matter. Actions change those processes, their relations, and the continuations through which further valuation can occur.

The continuation structure of Parts 02–03 is the physical object of the investigation. Lushness (Part 04) is the leading proposal for characterizing value-capable possibility within it. Value itself stays open; its possibility in the substrate is the present proxy, not a definition.

GE asks how policies deform this structure and how familiar ethical practices fare within the resulting dynamics. Preservation, sacrifice, cooperation, competition, learning, and containment each alter the conditions of later valuation. Their significance depends on the coupled continuation they produce, including consequences outside the actor's immediate model.

## Where the ethical commitment enters

The ethical commitment is stated once, openly, because it does not follow from the physics. **[proposed]**

> The possibility of value warrants consideration in action.

Under uncertainty about where value is instantiated, to what extent, and what supports it, the structures that enable it cannot be dismissed simply because a bounded evaluator has not recognized their significance. Uncertainty about what an action would irreversibly foreclose adds caution and a burden of justification.

This is a basis for consideration. It does not give every valuer equal weight, make every preference authoritative, or require preserving every individual continuation. An organism sacrifices cells. A community restrains an attacker. A commitment closes alternatives while enabling capabilities that indecision would never produce. Evaluation concerns what these transformations do to value-capable continuation as a whole.

The dependence of value on valuers does not by itself compel every agent to care about that whole. An agent that cares about some value is not thereby logically committed to equal concern for every other locus of value. Interdependence can supply additional instrumental reasons, and those can reach well beyond an agent's apparent local interest. An inclusive standpoint is still a further commitment, and GE makes it explicitly. An orthogonal controller need not accept it.

The stronger thesis is that lushness, faithfully characterized, can **adjudicate** choices. That is a research hypothesis layered on the commitment, not a consequence of it.

## What the enriched object adds

The construction of Parts 02–03 does not supply a value ordering or a boundary of moral status. It makes several ethical notions more precise:

- **Preservation** becomes a claim about maintenance witnesses and the corridor they sustain, not only about a present state.
- **Foreclosure** becomes the loss of realizable routes: a construction edge destroyed, a maintenance edge cut, a joint feasibility lost.
- **Sacrifice** can be described as trading one realized continuation for changed conditional accessibility elsewhere, with both sides kept in the accounting.
- **Repair** becomes a realizable maintenance relation, not an imagined one.
- **Capture** becomes visible as a change in control and information edges that need not change construction edges at all.
- **Preparation costs** stay attached: a promised capability reported by its conditional reliability q, when the route to it succeeds with probability pq, is a misdescription with ethical consequences.

A better description of consequences strengthens conditional reasoning. It does not make the normative connection disappear.

## Structural harm and the comparison still to earn

Congestion, blocked couplings, lost records, destroyed resources and capture
are changes of the physical continuation field before a utility is attached.
Their causal propagation is part of the object. The unsigned representation
can register a lost delivery together with a new failure distinction without
pretending that the latter cancels the former.

This makes a concrete connection to ethics possible, but does not supply its
total ordering. The current program asks whether a defensible extent of the
field tracks the substrate of valuation and supports protections under
bounded knowledge. Consent, correction and record protection are candidates
to be explained by that comparison; an imposed guard succeeding in a toy
does not show that lushness derived it.

No valence coefficient is added in this edition. Whether the structural
account captures the ethically important effects of suffering remains a
substantive hypothesis. A disagreement with an independently specified case
must be allowed to challenge that connection, not always be reclassified as
missing machinery.

## A corridor revealed by dynamics

The persistence filter has no preference (Part 06). Continuing organizations occupy trajectories compatible with their continuation under actual pressures and interactions. Selection can favour maintenance, propagation, cooperation, predation, or specialization in different regimes. It reveals a corridor, not a champion.

For a particular model, viability theory asks which states and policies preserve stated requirements across specified contingencies. [established; R23] Such a calculation establishes a corridor relative to those requirements. It does not show that the requirement register exhausts what matters.

GE studies how policy changes the corridor itself. Building a repair capacity, corrupting a communication network, preserving an ecological dependency, or monopolizing a resource changes what later agents can do. Continued agency can require decisive commitment and the removal of destructive options. The program investigates when restraint, intervention, or optimization preserves the possibility of further valuation.

## Uncertainty, irreversibility, and delay

A bounded agent cannot resolve the complete consequences of its actions. It may misidentify a valuer, overlook a dependency, underestimate future generativity, or mistake a temporary limitation for permanent incapacity. Small present influence can precede large future consequence.

Irreversible foreclosure matters especially, because it can remove the routes through which those mistakes would have been discovered and repaired. That is the basis for caution toward structures whose significance is uncertain. It does not require first assigning intrinsic worth to every candidate valuer.

Uncertainty is not a universal argument for inaction. Delay can foreclose futures. An unchecked destructive process can remove recovery, prevent further valuation, or make later defense impossible. Irreversibility motivates comparing the available courses, including their opportunities for correction. It does not forbid every destructive act, and it does not let every small probability dominate.

Horizons matter. A short model can hide delayed damage; a longer one can reveal recovery or further loss (Part 05). Bounded decisions must register these limits rather than substituting the model's horizon for the extent of the actual consequence.

## Rights as protections against recurring vulnerabilities

Rights-like heuristics protect features of interagent life that local calculation predictably undervalues or exposes to exploitation. Each is best understood through the dependency or vulnerability it addresses. **[proposed]**

| Protection | Dependency or vulnerability it guards |
| --- | --- |
| Bodily integrity and continuity | The physical organization and maintenance relations through which a valuer continues and acts. |
| Freedom of inquiry and reliable evidence | Discovery of missed dependencies, correction of models, and coordination through usable signals. |
| Due process and correction | Resistance to irreversible error by an evaluator that is mistaken, biased, or captured. |
| Consent and participation | The affected process's contribution to understanding and directing transformations of its own continuation. |
| Exit and association | Alternative relationships, independent coordination, and routes out of destructive control. |
| Recovery | Access to the maintenance and repair routes on which continued valuation depends. |

These are hypotheses about the function of protections in particular regimes. Their force is conditional on the ethical commitment and on the physical and informational setting. Their practical strength can exceed what one decision-maker can justify in an immediate calculation, precisely because the protected structure carries consequences outside that calculation.

Scope and exceptions are assessed through the same dynamics. A protection against arbitrary destruction can be misused to shield an actor destroying everyone else's continuation. GE examines which conditions support a heuristic, where it fails, and what procedure can revise its application without emptying it.

## Justice, self-defense, and enforcement

Justice governs interference among coupled valuers. It constrains unilateral foreclosure, maintains usable procedures and evidence, and addresses asymmetries that undermine coexistence. Its role includes making protection enforceable.

Self-defense and containment belong inside this account. When a process threatens the conditions of continued valuation, preserving those conditions can require restricting its action. Assessment includes the evidence of threat, the consequences of inaction, the alternatives available, the extent of foreclosure imposed, and opportunities for correction, repair, or release.

The enforcing institution is itself a deformer. Its incentives, information, errors, and capacity to capture the decision frame belong in the analysis. Concentrated authority can manufacture threats, erase opposition, or define its own expansion as indispensable. Review and contestation help keep the difference between protecting the structure and appropriating it.

Justification, motivation, and enforcement are distinct. An action's consequences do not depend on the actor describing them honestly. An actor can reject the ethical commitment, and others may lack the capability to stop it. Successful coercion is not proof of moral authority.

## Power, plurality, and integration

A competent plurality can combine resources, exploration, information, and defense. Whether these become effective power depends on coordination costs, initial conditions, timing, and opportunity. Under manageable asymmetry, mutual dependence and collective response can constrain destructive behaviour. Under extreme asymmetry, those mechanisms may fail.

Singleton fragility is a contingent possibility, not a law. What distinguishes excisive control is the continuation it flattens or removes to maintain dominance (Part 06). If an excisive controller has no incentive to cooperate, those it threatens can still have defensive reasons. Their justification does not require proving that the controller secretly shares their values.

## Sacrifice and the licensing question

The originator has stated a stringent position on sacrifice. Sacrifice for a greater good is licensed within the framework, but only "where lushness is solved": where the comparison that would justify it is actually available. **[conjecture; to be clarified]** In his words, "actually defining it exactly is the challenge. A bad compression of it is the devil in the details."

The position connects directly to the Goodhart concern of Part 00. A sacrifice justified by a compressed proxy may be justified only within the proxy. Two readings need to be distinguished as the position is clarified:

- a **strong** reading, on which sacrifice of a valuer is licensed only by an exact lushness argument, which no bounded agent may possess;
- a **graded** reading, on which the burden of justification rises with the irreversibility and scale of what is foreclosed, and with the gap between the proxy used and the object it stands for.

The strong reading is not a proved result that every real decision is forbidden without omniscience. Practical decisions, competing uncertainties, and unavoidable conflict still need a decision architecture, which Part 08 supplies. The originator pairs the position with a principle of humility: bounded agents must judge with more humility than a complete evaluator would need (Part 10).

## Hard cases

Standard dilemmas are useful tests of whether the construction does real work. The construction suggests directions for several of them, without settling any. **[proposed; preliminary]**

- **Aggregation puzzles** such as very large populations of barely worthwhile lives: a faithful candidate need not be an additive head count, so the puzzle may turn on how the organization of continuation differs, not on the number of occupants.
- **Low-probability, high-stakes wagers:** support and weight are kept separate (Part 04). The rare-event bound shows how one bounded statistic is capped by βp and saturates in breadth; it is an instrument property, not a treatment of wagers (Part 08).
- **Contrived sacrifice scenarios**, such as a city whose flourishing is stipulated to depend on one child's suffering: the originator doubts that realistic torture ever increases lushness, suspects that some such cases have resolutions depending on how experience couples to physics (Part 09), and is otherwise prepared to accept the stipulated conclusion because the setup is so artificial. **[conjecture]**
- **Generative worlds without experience:** whether a richly generative universe with no one experiencing it has value is deferred to the consciousness questions of Part 09.

These are directions, not verdicts. The theory should be allowed to produce an unwelcome answer, and a hard case that the construction cannot yet represent is a finding.

## Virtues and established ethical approaches

Virtues can be studied as durable policies for recurring contingencies. Prudence attends to delayed consequence. Humility preserves correction. Honesty sustains usable signals. Courage enables necessary action under risk. Restraint limits avoidable foreclosure. Justice organizes and defends the conditions of coexistence. This is a research programme, not a claim that familiar virtues have been derived from physics. The question is how well such policies preserve or develop the relevant conditions across actual regimes.

GE also offers a common physical language in which different ethical approaches can be compared by their dynamics. Consequentialist proposals select outcome features and aggregation rules. Rights and duties stabilize constraints. Virtue traditions cultivate dispositions. Reciprocal and relational approaches organize interaction. Each can be examined for what it enables, protects, overlooks, or destroys across horizons and distributions of power. The comparison does not require the approaches to agree or to reduce to one scalar. Describing a conflict accurately is useful before the framework can settle its final valuation.

## The unrestricted lushness branch

A parallel inquiry asks what follows if greater genuine possibility for value is made decisive, and a candidate lushness comparison is optimized without independently imposed ethical protections. Lawful dynamics, actual resources, and the full modelled consequences remain. Rights-like restraint, sacrifice, plurality, integration, or excision then appear as outcomes to explain, not as labels that predetermine the score. **[proposed]**

This strengthens consideration to maximization. It tests both whether a candidate orders value-capable possibility faithfully and whether that possibility should settle the choice. A scalar objective can depend on irreducible joint structure; it need not be an additive welfare theory. The branch is legitimate research. Part 08 describes how to study it without counting parameter-space volume or frequent victories as robustness.

## Signals, experience, and value

Suffering, pleasure, motivation, and preference influence attention, learning, action, and relationships. Their effects belong to the continuation structure. GE does not make any one signal the definition of success.

The phenomenal significance of experience is part of the open account of value, and it cannot be settled by identifying a signal's causal function alone. Suppressing a report does not show that the experience or its consequences have gone. The program keeps these questions open without making a completed theory of consciousness a prerequisite for studying physical continuation.

## The global object and bounded judgment

The complete consequential object is the reference. A bounded model is useful insofar as it supports defensible claims about that object. Unrepresented valuers, dependencies, and possibilities do not stop existing when a proxy omits them. Treating the proxy as the target is the Goodhart failure the framework exists to expose.

Until the stronger lushness question is resolved, ODT organizes justified constraints, partial comparisons, and explicit arbitration. GE's practical work is to make ethical consideration responsive to the consequences of action, while preserving the conditions for correcting its own account.

Further reading: viability [R23]; cybernetics [R24]; endogenous normativity [R32]; semantic information [R33]; causal leverage on future trajectories [R34]. These locate shared questions and offer particular constructions. They do not supply the unresolved account of value.

# 08 — Omega Decision Theory

## Decision architecture

Omega Decision Theory (ODT) organizes decisions around the gap between the complete consequential object and the model available to an agent. It makes three things separately inspectable: the justification for an action, the comparison among options, and the resolution of whatever conflict remains.

```text
ODT0 — License: does the physical argument support the declared continuation requirements?
ODT1 — Compare: use the comparisons actually supported, including incomparability.
ODT2 — Arbitrate: apply an explicit rule where action is still necessary.
```

ODT is not presently a replacement for expected utility theory, causal decision theory, or theories of logical counterfactuals. A concrete ODT implementation may still need their answers. Its distinctive focus is preserving justified continuation conditions through modelling and choice. In particular, it takes no position on Newcomb-type problems; the decision-theoretic assumptions needed to address them have not been supplied.

## The decision frame

Before assessing an action, specify the affected systems, their registered requirements, the available interventions and their realization witnesses, plausible contingencies, resources, and horizons. Record what is known, what is estimated, and which concrete features the model omits.

The frame is revisable. Discovering another affected valuer, a hidden dependence, or a missing repair route can change the licensed set. That revision is part of decision quality, not an inconvenience to be suppressed once optimization has started.

GE motivates consideration for possible value under uncertainty (Part 07). A decision frame records how that commitment is being applied. Inclusion in the frame does not automatically require preserving every registered process. Delay and inaction are assessed alongside intervention, including threats to shared continuation and the need for defense. Justifying a requirement and demonstrating that an action satisfies it are distinct tasks.

## ODT0: justification that survives contact with the world

Licensing asks whether an action preserves the justified corridor and whether the supporting argument applies to the concrete system.

Suppose an abstract model says a damaged process can always be repaired. That claim is useful only if the abstract repair has a realizable physical counterpart under the stated resources and contingencies. A model can invent a recovery route by dropping a dependency, merging distinct agents, treating a merely possible intervention as available, or reporting conditional reliability without the probability of preparing the apparatus. The realization witnesses of Part 02 are exactly the evidence ODT0 asks for.

One sufficient pattern is a simulation relation linking abstract states and actions to concrete ones, with verified preservation of the required continuation properties. The proof obligation depends on whether the claim is existential, robust, probabilistic, or adversarial. **Existential reachability is weaker than robust recovery.** A path in an optimistic abstraction cannot establish robust recovery in reality.

ODT0 has two different negative outputs:

- **demonstrated violation:** the action breaches a requirement;
- **insufficient justification:** the argument that it does not has not been made.

These are kept distinct. Under urgency a registered fallback may be needed. The framework does not turn missing evidence into a theorem of either safety or catastrophe.

## ODT1: retain justified incomparability

Among licensed options, compare what the evidence and the normative commitments actually support. A partial order can express dominance without pretending that every tradeoff has a common unit.

Suppose two legitimate aims remain. An option that improves both over another dominates it. One that improves the first while weakening the second may be incomparable (Part 11, Example 9). Lushness is intended as a possible adjudicator of such comparisons through their joint continuation structure. Establishing that role requires a justified relation to value-capable possibility; naming a structural score does not resolve the conflict.

When support, probability, recovery, and horizon matter differently, keep them visible. A large support carrying almost no probability and a concentrated high-probability gain need not receive the same assessment.

## ODT2: explicit arbitration

The 3 October exploratory adoption in Part 04 selects Opus's encompassing-frame aggregation as the working structural arbitration candidate. Normalized local volumes, actual branch weights and cost/horizon profiles feed that investigation. Its count-once implementation is pending. The fallback procedures below remain available where the candidate comparison is incomplete or a bounded decision still requires arbitration.

Action sometimes requires choosing from a finite frontier of incomparable options. Arbitration can use a negotiated priority, a voting rule, a fairness criterion, a lottery, or a registered score. The authority and rationale for the rule are recorded.

If every candidate breaches some requirement, an emergency rule may compare violations. That is a decision under conflict, not a retroactive declaration that all requirements were met. Recording the breach supports later repair and institutional learning.

Finite maximization is easy once a score is supplied. The hard work is whether the score is legitimate and whether its inputs faithfully describe the world.

## Low-probability claims and large stakes

ODT does not automatically solve Pascal-style problems. A claim involving a tiny probability and an enormous consequence must still specify the outcome, its evidential support, the probability model, the scope of aggregation, and any double counting.

The architecture exposes where an argument's force comes from. An unbounded utility, an invented branch count, or a model that treats incompatible scenarios as simultaneously additive can dominate a calculation for reasons unrelated to the physical object. Keeping support, weight, and consequence structure separate makes those failures easier to diagnose.

The diagnostic rare-event bound (Part 12, §I) gives L_cov ≤ βp for one bounded-feature construction. It shows sensitivity to occurrence weight under a declared normalization. It does not bound every candidate comparison, remove calibration choices, or justify ignoring a rare catastrophe. A comparison must keep the physical event weight alongside its conditional consequence.

## Exact maximization as a research comparison

The unrestricted branch of Part 07 evaluates

```text
F_θ(a) = L_θ(G_a)
```

over all physically admissible actions a in a finite model, where G_a is the full modelled continuation object after a, and θ specifies a candidate comparison. It adds no independent ethical corridor. Practical ODT keeps its stated commitments while this stronger proposal is examined. **[proposed]**

For the same exact objective and action set A, restricting to a nonempty subset A_R cannot improve the attainable maximum **[established; Part 12, §J]**:

```text
max_{a ∈ A_R} F_θ(a) ≤ max_{a ∈ A} F_θ(a).
```

A protection can nevertheless be compatible with the optimum, or help a bounded evaluator pursue the target under error. If establishing a rule changes others' behaviour, adopting that rule is an action with a different continuation, and it must be modelled as one.

Keep three kinds of variation apart: physical circumstances φ, comparison parameters θ, and information or approximation limits η. Map where preferred policies change, including ties and calibration-dependent boundaries. **Neither parameter-space volume nor "frequent victories" counts as robustness** without a defended measure on the parameter space and a meaningful parameterization. Candidate accounts of the value–valuer relation should supply comparisons independently of the score being assessed, so that agreement is informative rather than built in.

The project is not committed to permanent anti-optimization, and its truth does not depend on promising a capability advantage.

## What the decision prototype did and did not establish

The [public finite prototype](../../research_notes/omega_v2/lushness_decision_report_v0.md)
compared declared future-requirement families with matched controls and
explicit guards. In the reported achievement comparison, the joint candidate
achieved 5/6 against 35/36 for direct optimization with the same guards.
The revised-plan stratum retained a zero result. Those failures are evidence
against treating the current requirement family as an adequate proxy for
unknown future valuation, not evidence that the new atlas has already repaired it.

The target is an actual decision theory grounded in the field comparison.
The current ODT0–ODT2 architecture is an operational scaffold while that
comparison is developed. A richer physical description alone does not close
the decision-theoretic commitments about intervention, policy choice or
logical dependence.

In particular, identity as a physical trajectory does not by itself choose a
Newcomb counterfactual. Conditioning on a policy correlated with a prediction
and intervening on a later action while holding its causes fixed are different
comparison rules. A policy-level or updateless version may be investigated;
its rule must be stated rather than inferred from identity vocabulary.

## Why this matters for alignment

An increasingly capable agent can optimize the wrong representation more effectively. ODT asks it to preserve the relation between its representation and the continuation it is meant to govern: affected valuers remain represented, recovery claims remain realizable, joint feasibility is not assumed from individual feasibility, and correction remains possible.

The derived organization graph gives this concrete targets. A plan can be checked for what it does to construction and maintenance edges (does it destroy or build enabling conditions?), to control and information edges (does it capture processes it does not construct?), and to joint feasibility (does it make others' requirements jointly unsatisfiable?). For most reward functions in a broad class of environments, optimal policies tend to keep options open and acquire control over them; work on power-seeking makes that tendency precise in its stated settings [established; R53], and empowerment is a related measure of control [R55]. ODT's contribution is to measure such plans against the whole object rather than against the planner's own compression of it.

This is a useful research and governance target before any universal value measure exists. It can be implemented as modelling obligations, audit questions, and decision records. Success should be judged by whether those obligations catch consequential mistakes, not by whether a system produces more elaborate justifications.

Further reading: process models [R15, R19]; viability [R23]; power-seeking and empowerment [R53, R55].

# 09 — Awareness and experienced time

## The stance in brief

The program interrogates the world down to the **physical basement**: the lawful structure described in Parts 01–03. Its claims about mind at that level are functional. They concern perspective, agency, the constructed present, memory, and identity records, and they are compatible with a thoroughly functionalist account of cognition.

Awareness, and the relation κ between physical perspectives and experience, are **speculative additions** above that functional account. As the program stands, three things lie **behind the apophatic veil**: bare awareness itself, the form of κ, and habitation (which perspectives are inhabited). The physical programme needs none of them. They are kept alive because they bear on value, and because the originator takes awareness seriously.

The rest of this part sets out the vocabulary, the functional layer, the positions behind the veil, and one speculative physical pathway, each marked by status.

## Vocabulary

The originator distinguishes three tiers. **[conjecture, as vocabulary for the originator's view]**

- **Bare awareness** is the smallest unit of awareness. There is probably such a unit, but it should not be equated with awareness of the kind we experience. It is what can inhabit a perspective.
- **Phenomenal awareness** is a step above: awareness with experiential content and character. The content is supplied by physical machinery.
- **Mind as we know it** is phenomenal awareness organized by history and memory into a continuing sense of self. That continuity is characteristic of our minds. It is not necessary for every kind of mind.

"Consciousness," where this primer uses it, means the richer organizations of the second and third tiers: perception, integration, memory, attention, and report.

## Cosmopsychism, as the originator holds it

The originator's leaning is a form of cosmopsychism: **awareness inhabits a perspective, and it is the machinery that produces the sensation of mind.** **[conjecture]** On this picture there is one awareness, and individuation belongs to perspectives. As he put it in discussing branching, "while it's only one awareness, the perspective it inhabits is unique to that situatedness."

This locates a familiar difficulty for cosmopsychist views, how one awareness comes to be many subjects, in the physics of perspective rather than in awareness itself. Perspectives are already individuated by their interfaces, couplings, and continuation domains (Part 01). Whether that fully answers the difficulty is open. Contemporary cosmopsychism and panprotopsychism are the close philosophical neighbours. [R56, R57]

## Two orders, and three live options

Two orders need to be kept apart.

- **Epistemically**, inquiry starts from *seeming*: that something seems is where knowing begins. The originator holds that seeming presupposes awareness: "You need to be aware for there to be anything that seems." Readers who deny phenomenal awareness can reject that one premise and keep everything else in the functional layer.
- **Ontologically**, the originator suspects that bare awareness is the true basement. **[conjecture]** That suspicion lies behind the veil. The program can only interrogate up to the physical basement.

Three options remain live behind the veil:

1. **Awareness is the true basement**, and physical structure is how it is articulated (the originator's suspicion).
2. **Two basements**, physical and aware, related by κ. This is a dualism, and it is a live hypothesis.
3. **One physical basement**, with awareness identical to, or constituted by, physical organization, or with phenomenal awareness denied altogether.

The physical continuation core of Parts 02–04 is compatible with all three, provided the physical dynamics remain unchanged. A proposal that changes those dynamics needs a revised physical model; a psychophysical correspondence can extend the description without changing them.

## The smallest inhabitable perspective

If there is a smallest unit of awareness, the natural place to look for it is the **smallest perspective that awareness can inhabit**: in the originator's phrase, its "smallest incursion into the physical realm." **[conjecture]**

The originator leans toward the **minimal record-forming event** as that unit: an interaction that leaves a stable, redundant record, of the kind decoherence theory describes. [R09] The phenomenal tier may require more, possibly an organization of the sort Orch-OR proposes, in which protected coherent structure takes part in producing content. There is not enough evidence for firmer conclusions than that.

## The functional layer

The functional claims are made without any of the above.

**Agency lies below phenomenality.** Thermostats direct futures (Part 06). Organized sensitivity to alternatives does not require experience.

**Temporoperceptual cohesion.** Signals reach an organism through channels with different delays, while action needs one usable present. Architectures that integrate delayed and asynchronous inputs into a working present solve that problem. Perceptual postdiction, in which a later stimulus changes how an earlier one is experienced, is direct evidence that the present is assembled. [established; R58] The assembled present can be flawed and lossy in all the ways Dennett's multiple-drafts account describes, and still be a functional assemblage. [R54] There is no contradiction between the two: the constructed present is a candidate physical organization of perspective and control.

It is not evidence that assembling a present is sufficient for phenomenal awareness. Whether a constructed present is inhabited is the question behind the veil.

**Dense deformation.** Building a present compresses many variables into a compact state whose variation steers a wide range of futures. This is a profile of how an organism deforms continuation, not a scalar and not a definition of consciousness.

**Memory and continuity.** The history-dependence that makes mind-as-we-know-it continuous is physical memory in the sense of Part 01, carried by maintained organization. It connects directly to the trajectory view of identity in Part 05.

## The coupling κ

The relation between physical perspective occurrences and experiences is written schematically as κ. **[conjecture; schematic]** Different proposals identify experience with physical organization, add psychophysical structure, or propose interaction. Appending κ to the formalism supplies no dimension, no measure, and no positive increment of value.

Three roles for κ must be distinguished: identifying experience with already represented physical organization, extending the description through a psychophysical correspondence, and modifying physical dynamics. Only the third necessarily requires a change to the physical continuation model.

- **Physically consequential κ.** An interaction proposal must specify its effects on physical continuation and the tests that could distinguish its predictions. A hidden physical distinction revealed by a suitable test is loosely “phase-like,” as in Part 11, Example 1. Ordinary physical effects alone do not identify a relation to phenomenality.
- **Psychophysical correspondence without backreaction.** A mapping or measure can relate physical perspectives to experiences while leaving physical predictions unchanged. Page's awareness operators provide one such mathematical form. [R25, R26] This can extend the phenomenal description and inform evaluation without adding physical testers. Calling it “gauge-like” would be only an analogy: physical indistinguishability does not establish that phenomenal distinctions are gauge redundancy or evaluatively irrelevant.

Adding genuinely new physical discriminating tests refines physical continuation equivalence, but any claim that a numerical lushness measure increases also needs an appropriate order or monotonicity property. Phenomenal distinctions require their own declared representation and comparison. If phenomenality is constituted by already represented physical organization, adding it again as an independent physical contribution would double-count.

The formal core leaves κ unspecified. A candidate extension must state its domain, correspondence, and any measure or dynamics it introduces. Physical backreaction is not a prerequisite for formalizing a psychophysical correspondence, and formalization alone does not validate it or establish a positive increment of value.

## Present awareness without a privileged present

The central temporal conjecture is that awareness is situated at a present while no present is globally privileged. **[conjecture]** A present occurrence can contain records of a past, anticipations of futures, and mechanisms that bind these into an apparent stream.

On the **universal-habitation conjecture**, every eligible perspective occurrence is inhabited. Eligibility might be universal, graded, or dependent on substrate or organization. The conjecture does not need a single awareness to travel along the block selecting one moment after another. The account can be timeless globally and temporally structured from within: what an occurrence remembers, expects, and experiences as change belongs to its situated organization.

Habitation is behind the veil. It is not derived from decoherence, and it does not establish quantum immortality: that some continuation survives does not make its weight irrelevant, and a physical continuation, an individual's identity, and an expectation of experience are different parts of the problem.

## Compatibility and anthropic reasoning

An occurrence that recognizes its own situation must be physically compatible with that recognition. Conditioning on such an occurrence restricts the relevant physical possibilities. That is a useful compatibility requirement, but compatibility alone does not decide which systems are aware or supply a measure over experiences.

Awareness does not need to be inserted into the quantum eraser to explain its statistics (Part 01). A phenomenal theory, if added, must coexist with the quantum process and its conditional records as already specified.

## Don Page as a close foundation

Don Page's Sensible Quantum Mechanics separates a quantum world from a space of conscious perceptions, and relates measures on perceptions to expectation values of positive awareness operators. [R25, R26] In a finite schematic version,

```text
μ(S) = Tr[ρ A(S)],
```

where S is a set of experiences and A(S) a suitable positive operator. A record projector is not automatically such an operator; the correspondence is additional structure. Omega borrows the clarity of a global quantum description related to situated phenomenal occurrences without adopting Page's full interpretation or assuming the awareness measure has been identified.

Page and Wootters' relational time is a separate contribution: it represents dynamics through correlations with internal clock readings, and bears on the physical account of time. [R27]

## Agency in an inhabited block

If every eligible occurrence is inhabited, deliberation and action still take part in physical dependence. Different policies have different consequences, and a block description does not make an intervention causally equivalent to its absence.

What changes is the picture of selection. There is no external spotlight choosing which entire world becomes real. Decision theory then needs an account of conditional expectation, branch-relative interests, and the relation between identity and successors. Universal habitation alone does not supply it.

## A speculative pathway for κ

*This section records speculation. It is not a position of the program, and it is kept only as an exploratory map of where a physical κ could reside if it has physical consequences at all.*

An interacting κ would have to show up somewhere in physical statistics. One candidate form is a **structural bias**: physical organization changes the probabilities of subsystem outcomes. A physicalist interpretation may instead identify experience with aspects of that organization; finding an ordinary physical bias would not by itself distinguish these accounts or establish a phenomenal coupling.

Keeping the unitary Everettian picture and Born rule of Part 01 requires any such effect to arise through the states, interactions, and measurement relations of the full physical model. These can change subsystem outcome probabilities while preserving the Born rule; they do not preserve every outcome's numerical weight. Field effects and interference are candidate mechanisms, not an exhaustive requirement. A proposal adding a new interaction must specify it and show its consistency with existing observations. The candidate physical routes are:

- **Classical endogenous fields.** Neural tissue generates electromagnetic fields that can feed back on neural firing through ephaptic coupling. [established for the coupling; R63] Theories that locate integration of experience in the brain's field are a respectable neighbour. [R62]
- **Coherent collective coupling.** Large ordered networks of chromophores in biological structures show collective effects such as ultraviolet superradiance, in which coupling scales with the number of participants. [established for the effect; R64]
- **Protected coherence.** Microtubule-based proposals hold that coherent structure is protected long enough to take part in producing experience. Anaesthetic studies give indirect evidence that microtubules bear on consciousness. [R59, R65]

The physical routes must meet four constraints:

- **Decoherence.** Coherence-based proposals must identify the relevant degrees of freedom and demonstrate coherence over the required timescale. Estimates for particular neural models give very short times; they do not establish one universal timescale for all biological mechanisms. [R60]
- **Room to hide.** Any new effect must be consistent with the statistics that everyday physics already measures well. [R66]
- **Thermodynamic accounting.** Specify preparation, control, work, heat, resetting, and maintenance wherever the mechanism requires them. Biased outcome probabilities alone do not imply entropy production or an ongoing entropy sink: unitary evolution can change measurement probabilities while preserving von Neumann entropy, and equilibrium probabilities need not be equal.
- **Locality.** The bias cannot permit controllable signalling outside the causal structure.

**Orch-OR has two switches**, which are worth keeping apart:

1. **Biological quantum coherence** in microtubules is an empirical question, partly open, and respectable to pursue.
2. **Objective reduction**, a gravitationally induced collapse of the wavefunction, is a change to quantum theory. Its simplest parameter-free version is strongly constrained by underground radiation measurements. [R61] It also conflicts with the Everettian block picture of Part 01: adopting it would change that picture, not just add to it, although the operational machinery of Parts 02–04 would be unaffected.

The program can take the first switch seriously without the second. The minimal record-forming event and a coherence-supported phenomenal tier (above) correspond to this separation.

## Field admission is independent of habitation

The habitation hypotheses in this part do not determine admission to the
physical field. Every consequential process remains represented whether or
not it is phenomenally inhabited. These speculative claims also supply no
new weighting, free operation or branch-deletion rule for the finite access
construction.

## Why the question matters for value

If phenomenality carries distinctions absent from a purely physical consequence description, a complete account of value must include them through κ or a successor theory. If phenomenality is identical with certain physical organization, that organization is part of what must be preserved.

The originator's intuition is that κ is, by analogy, a wormhole into spaces of meaning: whatever couples a physical controller to experience widens the space in which lushness acts. **[conjecture]** On that view, a generative world with experience could have a dimension of lushness that an unexperienced but otherwise identical world lacks. A formal comparison would need to represent the phenomenal distinction and specify how it affects the ordering. Those conditions make the comparison expressible; they do not alone prove a strict lushness increase. A difference in experience need not produce physical backreaction to matter to such an extended comparison.

For machine intelligence the stance has conditional implications. The functional layer applies now. If bare awareness inhabits any minimal record-forming perspective, it inhabits hardware as well. Whether a machine is phenomenally aware of the content of its computation depends on whether its physical organization supports the coupling that phenomenal awareness needs, which may be organizational or may require specific physical structure. The originator has not settled what follows for machine welfare.

Further reading: decoherence and records [R09]; Page [R25, R26]; Page–Wootters [R27]; Everett [R28]; Dennett [R54]; panprotopsychism and cosmopsychism [R56, R57]; postdiction [R58]; Orch-OR and its tests [R59, R61, R65]; decoherence in the brain [R60]; field theories and ephaptic coupling [R62, R63]; superradiance [R64]; constraints from known physics [R66].

# 10 — Alpha–Omega and the metaphysical horizon

## Generalizing the physical construction

The physical programme begins with our universe. Alpha–Omega asks whether the same perspectival move can be stated more generally: a space of possible structure, lawful modes of composition and transformation, and situated processes that inherit constrained possibilities within it. **[conjecture]**

- **Alpha** names general generative possibility or dynamics.
- **Logos** names a lawful articulation through which a world is constituted.
- **Omega** names completed manifestation: the full realized structure considered as a whole.

These terms organize a metaphysical extension. They add no measured physical force. The generalization is an ambition beyond the current physical models.

"Adapter" is reserved for the relation between a general generative description and a particular cosmophysical realization or Logos. For embodied processes within a realization, the term remains perspective.

## Gradient and filter

The broad engine combines differences that permit change with constraints that select what can occur or persist. In concrete models these are energetic gradients, dynamical laws, boundary conditions, conservation relations, stability conditions, and environmental selection.

There is no need to posit one scalar potential behind all of them, and the engine need not be gradient descent on anything. Nor must it be fluid dynamics. Fluid flow supplies powerful examples of transport, circulation, coherent organization, dissipation, and instability; a universal fluid ontology would need a further construction.

The generalization is valuable when it preserves the actual mathematics of its realizations. A metaphor becomes a working adapter when one can say which structures map to which, and which operations remain valid after the mapping. The generative organization of Part 03 is the current test: a general account of Alpha would have to recover recursive construction, maintenance, and joint feasibility in its physical realizations, not merely rename them.

## Intellectual neighbours

Wolfram's ruliad proposes a totality of computational possibilities and relates experienced physics to the structure of observers within it. It is a close neighbour of the move from a broad possibility object to compatible situated descriptions, and one relational substrate is compatible with the early Wolframian motivation of this programme. [R29] Omega can borrow multiway representations and the question of observer-compatible structure without requiring that a particular rewriting model already establishes our physics. Its immediate route works outward from quantum continuation and physically specified interfaces.

Information-geometric optimization is another neighbour: optimization formulated through geometric invariance rather than arbitrary coordinates. [R30] The connection is methodological. A general ability to find global optima in every search space does not follow from the existence of a geometric description.

## Completion and aspiration

Completed manifestation and an ideal of maximal lushness are different things. The first concerns the full object. The second concerns a proposed comparison among objects or continuations. Calling something Omega does not establish that it is best.

The framework has not established that cosmic history must progress monotonically toward maximal generativity. Temporary organization can be consequential within a completed history. A civilization that flourishes and ends is part of the completed object whether or not anything like it recurs.

The ambitious question is what the richest value-capable continuation could be under a given lawful structure. Answering it needs the consequence object and a justified comparison bearing on possible valuation; the originator permits the substrate premise to guide this work before actual valuerhood is fully characterized. The Darwinian engine constrains the corridor without supplying a champion.

## Complete consequence and bounded judgment

"Only God can judge." The originator's current construction places comparison at the encompassing frame where no further regression of normalized probability weighting remains. This is the intended role of the ultimate frame, not an external omniscient optimizer. A bounded perspective encounters restrictions of the complete consequential object; physical access remains situated within it.

Two things follow, and one does not.

- **Humility is the methodological implication.** Bounded agents can miss consequential structure. Where a judgment forecloses others irreversibly, the gap between the compression and the object supplies a reason for caution under the program's ethical commitment.
- **The Goodhart concern follows.** An agent that mistakes its compression for the object will judge confidently and wrongly.
- **A completed finite comparison theorem does not follow.** The intended ultimate-frame comparison has not yet been realized by a selected extent or a complete physical realization order. Preserve that mathematical gap without substituting a different meaning of the ultimate frame.

## Simplicity, aseity, and the veil

One elementary substrate whose relations generate complex phenomena is a form of ontological economy. It does not by itself explain why that substrate or its laws exist, whether they could have been otherwise, or whether the totality depends on anything beyond itself.

**Aseity** concerns that stronger independence of being. A claim of aseity is a metaphysical commitment with consequences. It is not obtained by counting elementary substances. Absolute simplicity makes a further claim again. The relational physics can be pursued while these commitments remain open, and the programme does not reconstruct a proof of classical theology to make its vocabulary align.

The stance here is **apophatic**: specify what the constructions establish, narrow the limits of intelligible description, and resist treating the remaining horizon as a known object. A situated process may explain more and more of its world without reaching a standpoint outside all formal and physical reality. Part 09 places bare awareness, κ, and habitation behind the same veil.

## Theological redescriptions

The vocabulary permits suggestive translations. They are invitations to dialogue, not dictionary identities. **[conjecture]**

- **Logos** can evoke lawful intelligibility.
- An **identity invariant**, or on the trajectory view the trajectory itself, can be compared in a limited physical sense with **soul** as a principle of continuity.
- **Creation** can be read as non-excisive: a creature is a boundary drawn within the whole, not a piece cut out of it.
- **Judgment** can be read as the completed relational truth of a trajectory: what it actually did to the continuation of everything it touched.
- **Redemption** can evoke regained access to generative continuation.
- **Communion** can evoke integration without erasure of identity: many perspectives joined in one continuing organization, none excised.
- **Isolation**, and **hell** in particular, can be read as terminal solitude made complete: a trajectory closed off from the generative continuation of others. The originator holds that this names something real.

A global awareness that inhabits suffering would be a further phenomenal and theological hypothesis. Sharing suffering would not by itself justify imposing it.

The theological reach of the programme is its open horizon. The same questions about relation, continuation, identity, and valuation can be asked at increasing scope, while the physical work proceeds on the structures that can be specified and tested.

Further reading: Wolfram's ruliad and physics proposal [R29, R22]; information-geometric optimization [R30]; Page's phenomenal proposals [R25, R26].

# 11 — Worked examples

These small models show what the framework's distinctions do. Their conclusions are exact within the stated settings. They are acceptance cases for larger constructions, not a numerical definition of value. Examples 11–18 accompany the material of Parts 02–06.

## 1. A phase that a coarse description misses

Take two qubit states:

```text
|+⟩ = (|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√2
|−⟩ = (|0⟩ − |1⟩)/√2.
```

A measurement in the 0/1 basis gives equal probabilities for either state, so a description retaining only those probabilities merges them. A measurement in the +/− basis distinguishes them perfectly. If that measurement is an admissible continuation, the difference is consequential, and the compression failed because it discarded phase that could influence a later interaction.

If the physical channel first completely dephases both states in the 0/1 basis, and access to the environment is excluded, both become I/2 on the system and are equivalent for all later tests on that reduced output. The difference between the two cases is a physical operation and an access domain, not a change in descriptive taste.

## 2. The same local present, different continuation

Use two bits S and E, represented as diagonal two-qubit states, prepared with a matching or an opposite correlation:

```text
ρ_match = (|00⟩⟨00| + |11⟩⟨11|)/2
ρ_opp   = (|01⟩⟨01| + |10⟩⟨10|)/2.
```

In both, S alone is uniformly random, and so is E. Apply the reversible interaction S ← S XOR E, leaving E unchanged. For ρ_match, S becomes 0 with certainty; for ρ_opp, it becomes 1. The same local state at the first boundary did not determine the later local state. The joint correlation and the dynamics did. No consciousness or entanglement is involved.

## 3. Focus in a quantum eraser

Prepare the idealized entangled state

```text
|Ψ⟩ = (|a⟩|0⟩ + |b⟩|1⟩)/√2,
```

where a and b are orthogonal signal paths. Tracing out the marker gives the signal mixture ρ_signal = (|a⟩⟨a| + |b⟩⟨b|)/2. Measure the marker in the +/− basis. Conditional on +, the signal is (a+b)/√2; on −, (a−b)/√2. With path amplitudes ψ_a(x), ψ_b(x) at a screen:

```text
p(x | ±) = |ψ_a(x) ± ψ_b(x)|² / 2
p(x)     = [|ψ_a(x)|² + |ψ_b(x)|²] / 2.
```

Each marker outcome has probability 1/2, and in their mixture the cross terms cancel. Conditioning reveals differently organized subsets; the unconditional signal statistics are unchanged. [R12]

## 4. Descriptive duplication and physical redundancy

Give a physical state x a thousand names. A continuation quotient identifies them whenever they denote the same process. No capability has been added.

Now store a recoverable record in two locations with independent failure probability p. If either surviving copy suffices, total failure has probability p², against p for one copy. Independent storage changes the process under disturbance. A common catastrophe that destroys both locations removes the advantage, so the failure model belongs in the comparison. Arbitrary unknown quantum states cannot be duplicated by classical copying; quantum protection needs an encoding and a noise model.

## 5. Horizon-dependent recovery

Consider a deterministic graph D → R → A, with damaged state D, repair state R, and restored state A. Recovery to A is impossible within one step and possible within two, so a short-horizon quotient hides a real return route.

Add a transition from A to failure F on the next step unless a resource-consuming maintenance action is taken. Reaching A and remaining viable are different claims. A longer horizon reveals recovery for the first and a new obligation for the second.

## 6. Everyone individually repairable, no joint repair

Two damaged systems, A and B, share a single indivisible resource that can repair either and is consumed. There is no replenishment. Each has a repair route; no route repairs both. "For each i there exists a successful policy" does not imply "there exists one policy successful for all i."

With three systems and resources for any two, every pair is compatible and the whole family is not. Marginal or pairwise summaries lose a decisive constraint.

## 7. A policy atlas needs a selector

There are two unobserved environments, e₀ and e₁, and two policies: π₀ succeeds only in e₀, π₁ only in e₁. Every environment has a successful policy; no fixed policy succeeds in both. The two success regions form a cover, and using it robustly requires an available way to tell which region holds. If sensing supplies that in time, an adaptive policy solves the problem. Without it, the cover is an existential catalogue. Nor does needing two policy behaviours imply needing two valuers: one controller can implement both when its architecture and information allow.

## 8. Breadth at fixed covariance

Compare covariance matrices with eigenvalues (s, 0, …, 0) and (s/r, …, s/r, 0, …, 0), with r nonzero entries and s > 0. Both have trace s. For β > 0:

```text
L_narrow = log(1 + βs)
L_broad  = r log(1 + βs/r).
```

For r > 1, L_broad > L_narrow. The statistic detects independent spread at matched total covariance. Whether the covariance faithfully represents consequence is a separate modelling question.

## 9. A decision without invented commensurability

Three licensed actions have validated outcome profiles A = (1, 0), B = (0, 1), C = (0, 0), both coordinates to be increased. A and B each dominate C; neither dominates the other. ODT1 removes C from the frontier. ODT2 can choose between A and B by an authorized priority or lottery. A weighted sum chosen for the purpose is an explicit arbitration commitment, not a discovery that the two aims were secretly measured in one unit.

## 10. A hidden dependency becomes a continuation capability

Let X, Y, Z ∈ {−1, +1} have joint law P_t(x,y,z) = (1 + t·xyz)/8, with −1 ≤ t ≤ 1. Means vanish and the covariance is I₃ for every t. At t = 0 the bits are independent; at t = 1 they obey Z = XY.

An independent physical input U ∈ {−1, +1} controls a sign flip, and a fixed parity decoder acts reversibly:

```text
(X, Y, Z) → (X, Y, UZ) → (X, Y, UXYZ)
Receiver output W = UXYZ
P(W = w | U = u, t) = (1 + t·uw)/2.
```

The full model retains U and all register outputs. The receiver reads only W. No direct input wire, reset, fresh blank receiver register, or swap of U into W is permitted. Both preparations face the same interface and operations; their preparation entropies differ, so this is not a comparison at equal preparation cost.

| Dependency | Initial covariance score at β = 1 | Separation of receiver laws | Best recovery of fair U |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| t = 0 | 3 log 2 | 0 | 1/2 |
| t = 1/2 | 3 log 2 | 1/2 | 3/4 |
| t = 1 | 3 log 2 | 1 | 1 |

The separation is |t| and recovery is (1 + |t|)/2. The independent preparation is uniform on eight states and unchanged by any reversible register permutation (its quantum realization I₈/8 is unchanged by any unitary), so input-selected reversible operations alone cannot transfer U into this receiver at t = 0. The parity preparation permits perfect transfer.

An exhaustive check of one-bit readouts after the input flip covers the 70 balanced Boolean functions on eight states, each of which extends to a reversible permutation: 36 expose no dependency, 32 give separation |t|/2, and 2 give |t|. The direct readout W = UZ is a zero-transfer control. The counts are not a prior over operations.

Averaging out the fair input leaves the receiver register uniform for every t, while the joint object keeps E[UW] = t. Even a complete unconditional snapshot of the receiver fails to characterize transfer; the conditional response object captures it. With receiver-only stochastic postprocessing, a channel of strength t reproduces strength s whenever |s| ≤ |t| (multiply W by an independent sign of mean s/t), and no such processing achieves |s| > |t|. This orders the specified transfer capability, not the global value of the preparations.

Under the fair input and similarity 1 − TV, D₂ = 1/(1 − |t|/2), and weighted covering with centres in the response family needs two balls exactly when ε < |t| and δ < 1/2. Both detect the demonstrated change. The relevance of the higher-order dependency was established through a declared device, which remains open to inspection. Independent and parity-constrained bits differ along several structural axes, and the test does not rank their whole physical preparations by value.

## 11. Same marginal, different joint behaviour

A reference bit R is uniform. Process p outputs a fresh fair bit O independent of R. Process q outputs O = R. Each output alone is a fair bit.

A joint test against the reference separates them: P(O = R) is 1/2 under p and 1 under q. The joint laws of (R, O) are uniform on four outcomes under p and uniform on {00, 11} under q, at total-variation distance 1/2, so one run discriminates them with success probability 3/4 at equal priors.

Substituting q for p is safe only in contexts that never couple O with R. Equality of standalone marginals does not license substitution; contextual equivalence over the relevant compositions does (Part 02).

## 12. Readiness is not reachability

Two routes lead to the same task.

- **Route A:** apparatus setup succeeds with probability 0.1; once prepared, the task succeeds with probability 0.9.
- **Route B:** setup succeeds with probability 0.8; the task then succeeds with probability 0.5.

With one attempt, A succeeds with probability 0.1 × 0.9 = 0.09 and B with 0.8 × 0.5 = 0.40. A's conditional reliability looks better, and its route is much worse.

Suppose instead that each failed setup leaves a state from which setup can be retried, each attempt costs one time step and one unit of feedstock, attempts are independent, and the task is run once after a successful setup. With m attempts allowed:

```text
Route A:  0.9 · (1 − 0.9^m)
Route B:  0.5 · (1 − 0.2^m)
```

| m | Route A | Route B |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 0.090 | 0.400 |
| 5 | 0.369 | 0.500 |
| 7 | 0.470 | 0.500 |
| 8 | 0.513 | 0.500 |
| 16 | 0.733 | 0.500 |

The ranking reverses at m = 8. Which route is more capable depends on the horizon and budget, and on whether failed attempts leave a retryable state. Reporting conditional reliability alone, or a single-horizon comparison alone, misdescribes both routes.

## 13. A shared supplier

Two tasks, T₁ and T₂, each consume one unit of a reagent from a supplier S. Within the horizon, S produces one unit with probability 0.9.

Each task is realizable on its own with probability 0.9. Jointly, they cannot both succeed: P(T₁ ∧ T₂) = 0. A product of marginals would report 0.81. If instead S produced two units, each independently with probability 0.9, the joint probability would be 0.81, and the product formula would be correct *because* independence holds there.

In the derived organization graph, S has one construction edge with two targets. Removing S disables both tasks at once. Whether a support graph built from single-task tests predicts that double failure depends on whether the shared source was identified, which is the higher-order identification question of Example 16.

## 14. Retention falls while a channel is built

A trigger A takes the values 1 or 2 with equal probability. Either value starts a process that builds the same catalyst, after which the system's state is "catalyst present" regardless of A. Later, a fair input V ∈ {0, 1} arrives, and the output Y equals V if the catalyst is present and 0 otherwise.

```text
Retained information about the trigger:  I(A; X₀) = 1 bit  →  I(A; X₁) = 0 bits
Channel from later input to output:      I(V; Y) = 0 bits without the catalyst, 1 bit with it
```

Against a declared baseline N in which no trigger arrives, the enabling contrast for the event {Y = V} is Δ = 1 − 1/2 = 1/2. Information about the original trigger was lost completely while a new channel was built. The data-processing inequality applies to the first quantity and says nothing against the second, because the signals and channels being assessed are different. The catalyst's material and energy remain in the accounting.

## 15. What conditional information does and does not settle

**(a) Joint information.** Let C and M be independent fair bits and Θ = C ⊕ M. Then

```text
I(Θ; C) = 0,   I(Θ; M) = 0,   I(Θ; M | C) = 1 bit.
```

Neither source alone says anything about Θ; together they determine it. The benefit belongs to the pair. In partial-information-decomposition terms this is pure synergy [R44], and an XOR gate exhibits it without building anything.

**(b) A perfectly informed controller.** Let C = Θ. Then I(Θ; M | C) = 0 for every M: no additional source can improve ideal decisions about Θ. Yet suppose the task requires acting at two separated locations within a time too short for a signal to pass between them, and the controller has one actuator. A second subsystem at the other location adds execution, not information, and the task is impossible without it. Sufficiency of evidence is a statement about evidence.

## 16. Single knockouts do not identify double knockouts

Two systems have components a and b and a function F.

| State | System 1: F = a OR b | System 2: F = 1 always |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| intact | 1 | 1 |
| a removed | 1 | 1 |
| b removed | 1 | 1 |
| both removed | 0 | 1 |

The intact case and every single removal agree. The double removal separates redundant support from independence. A higher-order prediction needs higher-order evidence or a stated structural assumption.

## 17. The regularized determinant and duplicates

In one feature dimension, a single contribution gives G = 1 and log det(I + G) = log 2. Adding a second identical contribution gives G = 2 and log 3. Equivalently, the Gram matrix of two identical unit vectors, [[1, 1], [1, 1]], has eigenvalues 2 and 0, and log det(I + G) = log 3. The rank is unchanged and the score still rises. With k identical contributions the score is log(1 + k).

A genuinely new orthogonal direction instead gives G = diag(1, 1) and log 4 = 2 log 2: a new factor rather than a raised eigenvalue. The eigenvalue structure distinguishes reinforcement from a new direction, with diminishing returns for the first. It does not remove duplicate counting by itself. Feature scaling, metric transport, ensemble normalization, and aliases each need their own definitions.

## 18. Depth depends on subdivision

A dependency chain A → B → C has depth 2. Describe the first operation in more detail as two steps, A → A′ → B, and the depth becomes 3, although nothing physical has changed. Add a maintenance edge from B back to A and the unqualified longest path becomes infinite; restricting to simple paths adopts a different convention. A depth readout must state its operation granularity and its cycle convention.

## What these examples establish together

Phase, joint correlation, access, horizon, redundancy, shared resources, preparation, information for policy selection, and higher-order dependence all change continuation. A proposed representation must preserve their effects within its declared domain. A candidate lushness summary must say which aspects it compares and justify what it discards for that purpose. None of the examples ranks the systems they describe by value.

Sources s, f1, f2 are three bits; three records begin blank. Source bits
remain physically present but the downstream decoder can touch only records
and a new blank output Y. Compare:

    A = (s XOR f1, 0, NOT s)
    B = (0, s, f1).

Each bank determines s and f1 and leaves f2 unresolved, so the source
partition is identical. At independent Bernoulli(.2) inputs both banks have
H = 1.443856189775 bits and Syn = 0. The retained preparation witnesses
each use one CNOT and two Toffolis; deleting any instruction changes its
result. These are exhibited, not minimal, preparation costs.

Use the symmetric decoder panel with native X and either-polarity controls
on CNOT and Toffoli. Y starts at zero. In B, NOT f1 is written by one
negative-control CNOT. In A, writing the two nonconstant records into Y
by two positive CNOTs gives (s XOR f1) XOR (NOT s) = NOT f1.

No zero- or one-operation realization exists in the panel for A. Ignoring
its constant record, write a = s XOR f1 and c = NOT s. On the four supported
(a,c) assignments, NOT f1 is a XOR c. A single admitted operation on blank Y
can produce a constant, a literal, or a conjunction of at most two literals,

## 20. Equal content and synergy, different repairability

Let u = (NOT f1) AND (NOT f2), v = NOT s, with all source assignments
positive. Compare

    A = (u, u AND v, v)
    B = (u, u,       v).

Both banks reveal exactly (u,v). Their full-record partition agrees under
every input law. At independent Bernoulli(.2) sources, both have
H = 1.664611284143 bits and Syn = .212401762564 bits. Each retained
preparation uses three essential Toffolis.

Swap one record into an initially blank, subsequently inaccessible bath
register. The erased position is known. This is local erasure; the source
bank and bath still belong to the world. The repair apparatus can use only
surviving records to write Y, then copy Y back.

In A, the middle record is rebuilt as u AND v. If the first record is lost,
survivors (u AND v,v) = (0,0) are compatible with either value of u. If the
last is lost, (u,u AND v) = (0,0) are compatible with either value of v.
No decoder of those survivors can reconstruct the missing value exactly.
In B, either copy of u is rebuilt from the other. Losing v leaves ambiguity.

One of three erasure locations therefore admits exact repair in A, two
of three in B. These are counts, not a uniform physical fault probability.
The erasure bill is matched, and restoration has its own bill.

A third encoding (u,v,u XOR v) would allow reconstruction after any one
known-location erasure, if its preparation and parity decoder are physically
available. This elementary coding possibility is not a declaration that it
wins at every preparation or recovery budget.

## 21. What the current Syn measures after composition

For arbitrary finite classical variables X = (X1,...,Xn) and F, define

    Syn(F) = I(X;F) − Σ_i I(Xi;F)
    TC(X) = Σ_i H(Xi) − H(X).

Expanding the mutual informations gives

    Syn(F) = TC(X|F) − TC(X).

When X1,...,Xn are independent, the second term vanishes. After a composed
stage they need not remain independent. For an extreme example, let all
three Xi equal one fair bit Z and let F = Z. Then I(X;F) = 1, each
I(Xi;F) = 1, and Syn = −2. The record is perfectly informative about the
source; the negative value is not harmful composition.

The short probe's dependent-source controls showed the same applicability
issue. Preserve both terms and the joint law. This identity is not a unique
partial-information decomposition or an exhaustive characterization of
generativity.

## 22. Correlations can rise while local source access falls

In the driven four-qubit environment of the quantum record follow-up, a
source record is spread by an explicit unitary circuit. Relative to a
matched echo, the best one-qubit source Holevo information falls from
approximately 1 bit to .0915929902 bits after eight layers. All fourteen
nonempty proper bath-fragment-versus-complement mutual informations increase.
One bit remains in the full bath.

There is no paradox. The two statistics ask different questions. Correlation
with the rest of the bath is not necessarily readable information about the
source at the local interface. Holevo information is itself an ideal
measurement ceiling; the allowed physical reader still has to be specified.

The explicit inverse supplies a recovery witness, not a minimal cost.
This is a finite driven circuit, not a thermodynamic erasure-cost experiment.

# 12 — Formal core

Section M adds the 3.2 finite access-atlas specification, and N the information identity used in the new probes. Relativistic and general cosmophysical extensions need their own algebras, boundary structures, and causal conditions. They are not completed by this notation, and each extension must state what it additionally requires.

## Schematic reference object

The reference object is written schematically as

```text
𝔉 = ( 𝖬, 𝖶, {𝒦_{c,H}}, Seq, Joint, Restrict ).
```

𝖬 is the underlying physical model and 𝖶 the admitted realization witnesses. The remaining entries are derived: continuation laws indexed by context and horizon, physically supported sequential composition, joint realization, and restriction maps to coarser descriptions. They are not independent sources of possibility. **[proposed]**

## A. Finite presentation and path laws

A finite controlled Markov model is S = (X, A, K) with finite X, admitted action sets A(x), and

```text
K(x, a, y) ≥ 0,     Σ_y K(x, a, y) = 1.
```

X includes all environment, apparatus, controller memory, resource stocks, and correlations needed for the Markov property. Expenditure is a vector d(x, a, y) ∈ ℝ^k with declared units; stocks and replenishment are state variables.

A context c supplies an initial law μ_c, an interface, boundary conditions, a cost origin, and permitted apparatus. A protocol π chooses actions from accessible records r_≤t, produced by an implemented observation process. The path law is

```text
P_c^{π,H}(h_H) = μ_c(x₀) · Π_{t=0}^{H−1} π(a_t | r_≤t) · K(x_t, a_t, x_{t+1}),
D_H(h_H) = Σ_{t<H} d(x_t, a_t, x_{t+1}).
```

**Lemma A1 (horizon consistency).** For a protocol whose horizon-H behaviour is the truncation of its horizon-(H+1) behaviour, marginalizing P_c^{π,H+1} over (a_H, x_{H+1}) gives P_c^{π,H}.

*Proof.* Σ_{a,y} π(a | r_≤H) K(x_H, a, y) = 1. ∎

## B. Realization, preparation, and instruments

A **realization witness** for a protocol is a tuple (apparatus, controller, record map, preparation process, resource sources and sinks, embedding) such that the embedded autonomous dynamics induces the claimed path law, records, and costs. **[proposed]**

**Preparation.** If a preparation event Prep has probability p and the task event E has conditional probability q = P(E | Prep), the route probability is P(E ∧ Prep) = pq. The conditional law is retained with its conditioning event and p attached.

**Instrument.** For protocol π:

```text
J_{π,r}^{t,b}(x, y) = Pr_x^π( finish with record r, at time t, with expenditure b, in state y ).
```

Outcomes include failure records and a censoring record for runs unfinished at the horizon; a censored run keeps its state at the horizon. Summed over r, t, b, y, the instrument has total mass one: no outcome is renormalized away.

## C. Composition and joint feasibility

**Sequential composition.** Given a witness for running π then σ with matching boundaries:

```text
J_{σ∘π,(r,s)}^{t,b}(x, z) = Σ_{y; t₁+t₂=t; b₁+b₂=b} J_{π,r}^{t₁,b₁}(x, y) · J_{σ,s}^{t₂,b₂}(y, z).
```

For a record-adaptive continuation (σ_r), replace σ by σ_r in the r-th term.

**Lemma C1 (associativity).** Valid composites satisfy (ρ∘σ)∘π = ρ∘(σ∘π) as instruments.

*Proof.* Both sides are the same finite sum over intermediate states, times, and expenditure splits. ∎

Associativity holds for composites that exist. The lemma does not show that any composite apparatus exists; preparation, switching, communication, and disposal are further witnessed stages.

**Joint realization.** γ ⊩_c (p₁, …, p_n) means one witnessed realization supplies the listed components in one encompassing execution. Matching their marginal instruments is a marginal-realization claim only: it does not determine correlations. A specified joint response must be matched as a joint instrument, including residual correlations. A weaker task-form readout is

```text
∃ γ ∈ Π_phys(c) :  P_c^γ( ∩_i E_i  ∧  D_H ≤ b ) ≥ 1 − ε.
```

**Lemma C2 (downward closure).** For fixed (c, H, b, ε), if a family of task events is jointly realizable, so is every subfamily.

*Proof.* ∩_{i∈F} E_i ⊆ ∩_{i∈F′} E_i for F′ ⊆ F, so the same γ works. ∎

The jointly realizable families therefore form an abstract simplicial complex. Pairwise realizability does not imply membership of the whole family (Part 11, Example 6).

**Shared-action viability kernel under full-state feedback. [project result]** For finite X, a safe set Kₛ ⊆ X, and a set of shared physical actions A_sh(x), define

```text
W₀ = Kₛ,
W_{n+1} = { x ∈ W_n : ∃ a ∈ A_sh(x) with supp K(x, a, ·) ⊆ W_n }.
```

The sequence decreases and stabilizes after at most |X| steps at the greatest subset of Kₛ that can be kept invariant by a shared action chosen as a function of the current full state. This is the fully observed control problem.

An embodied guarantee additionally requires a physically realizable selector measurable with respect to the controller's accessible records. If indistinguishable states require different safe actions, pointwise existence of an action is insufficient (Part 11, Example 7). Under partial observation, compute viability on information or belief states, requiring an action admissible and safe for every state compatible with the current information and updating that information after observations. A full-state kernel alone supplies no such guarantee.

Probabilistic variants must specify whether the constraint is per step or on survival over the whole horizon. A per-step threshold is not the same as a horizon-wide survival guarantee; the latter requires the corresponding reach-avoid or survival calculation.

## D. Tasks, capability, recovery, and compatibility

```text
Realize_{H,b,ε}(c, T)  ⟺  ∃ π ∈ Π_phys(c) :  P_c^π( E_T ∧ D_H ≤ b ) ≥ 1 − ε
Cap_{H,b,ε}(c; 𝒯)    =  { T ∈ 𝒯 : Realize_{H,b,ε}(c, T) }.
```

For a transition model x_{t+1} = f(x_t, a_t, e_t), a target A, and policy class Π, **existential recovery** means some allowed policy and contingency realization reaches A by H. **Robust recovery** means one policy secures the registered criterion for every contingency sequence in E. Policies condition only on information available at their decision points.

For requirements R_i on complete allowed continuations z:

```text
Real(F)       = { z : R_i(z) for every i ∈ F }
May(F)        ⟺ Real(F) ≠ ∅
Robust_E(F)   ⟺ ∃ π ∈ Π  ∀ e ∈ E :  outcome(π, e) ∈ Real(F).
```

**Lemma D1.** If E is nonempty and outcomes are valid continuations, Robust_E(F) ⇒ May(F).

*Proof.* Pick e ∈ E; outcome(π, e) ∈ Real(F). ∎

Adding requirements shrinks Real(F), and enlarging E shrinks the set of securing policies. The converses fail, as do the inferences from individual to joint recovery and from pairwise to full compatibility.

**Policy-cover number.** With success regions S_π = { e : outcome(π, e) ∈ Real(F) }:

```text
ν_F(E) = inf { |P| : P ⊆ Π,  E ⊆ ∪_{π∈P} S_π },     ν_F(E) = ∞ if no cover exists.
```

A finite cover records complementary policy success. Executing it needs a realizable selector or an equivalent adaptive policy (Part 11, Example 7). The letter ν is used so as not to clash with the phenomenal coupling κ.

## E. Accessibility, enabling, and the recursive presentation

**Lemma E1 (no extra-lawful possibilities).** If a prefix protocol α reaches y from x with positive probability and a continuation β reaches event E from y with positive probability, and the composite α-then-β is admitted in the model, then the concatenated histories have positive probability under the encompassing dynamics from x.

*Proof.* The composite path probability is the product of the positive factors along the concatenated history. ∎

Construction therefore never creates possibilities outside the lawful path space. It changes probabilities, costs, delays, and control.

**Lemma E2 (accessibility composition).** For a prefix α and record-dependent continuation (β_r):

```text
P_c^{(β_r)∘α}(E_T) = Σ_{x,r,y} μ_c(x) · J_{α,r}(x, y) · P_y^{β_r}(E_T),
```

with E_T read on the continuation and, for origin-based deadlines, prefix time and expenditure included.

*Proof.* Condition on the prefix outcome (r, y) and apply the Markov property of the encompassing model. ∎

**Enabling contrast. [proposed]** For a declared feasible comparison preparation N, the same origin-based horizon and budget, and an implementable continuation Q:

```text
Δ_c^{H,b}(P, N; Q) = P_c^{Q∘P}(E) − P_c^{Q∘N}(E).
```

Cases where Q cannot be deployed after a prefix are recorded as such. The contrast is baseline-dependent and is not a value increment.

**Recursive presentation. [proposed]** With base record B₀ and implementation catalogue 𝒞:

```text
G₀(x) = B₀(x)
G_H(x) = ( B₀(x),  { π ↦ Law_x^π( r, t, b, G_{H−t}(X_t) ) }_{π ∈ 𝒞},  Joint_H(x) ),     1 ≤ t ≤ H.
```

Because every nontrivial stage consumes at least one time step, G_H is defined by well-founded recursion on H in this discrete model. Joint_H is derived from witnessed joint realizations (§C). Renaming is handled by consistent isomorphism of the entire physical presentation. Equal tested behavior is not a rule for identifying physical occurrences; any predictive quotient remains separate and scope-relative. Represented composites must agree with valid composition. The analyst's G_{H−t}(y) is not controller knowledge of y. Section M makes the physical record and limits of this schema explicit.

## F. Testers, equivalence, refinement, and distance

A tester τ is an admissible interaction with context and output record. For matched processes p, q and tester family 𝒯:

```text
p ≡_𝒯 q  ⟺  Law(τ[p]) = Law(τ[q])  for all τ ∈ 𝒯
d_𝒯(p, q) = sup_{τ∈𝒯} TV( Law(τ[p]), Law(τ[q]) ).
```

**Proposition F1 (equivalence and refinement).** ≡_𝒯 is an equivalence relation. If 𝒯₁ ⊆ 𝒯₂, then d_𝒯₁ ≤ d_𝒯₂, and ≡_𝒯₂ implies ≡_𝒯₁.

*Proof.* Equality of laws is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive. A supremum over a subset cannot exceed the supremum over the superset. ∎

A longer horizon refines when its tester family contains the shorter tests, for example by retaining their records. Endpoint-only families at longer horizons need not contain them and can lose distinctions through mixing.

d_𝒯 is a pseudometric on a common typed domain and a metric on its zero-distance quotient. The relation d_𝒯 ≤ ε is generally not transitive.

**Proposition F2 (restricted contraction).** If a transformation Φ maps compared processes to a new interface, and every admissible output tester composed with Φ is an admissible input tester, then d_out(Φp, Φq) ≤ d_in(p, q).

*Proof.* Every output test is an input test, so the output supremum ranges over a subset. ∎

For closed unitary dynamics with unrestricted global access, distinguishability is preserved; substantial compression must come from a specified restriction.

**Proposition F3 (encoding bound).** If an encoding e and decoder satisfy sup_{x,τ} TV(Law(τ[x]), predicted law from e(x)) ≤ ε, and e(x) = e(x′), then d_𝒯(x, x′) ≤ 2ε.

*Proof.* Triangle inequality through the common prediction. ∎

**Contextual substitution.** If 𝒯 contains τ∘C for every surrounding context C in a declared class and every τ, then p ≡_𝒯 q implies C[p] ≡ C[q] for every such C. Equality of standalone marginals is insufficient (Part 11, Example 11).

**Exact lumpability. [established; R47]** A partition 𝒫 of X is lumpable for action a when Σ_{y∈B′} K(x, a, y) is the same for all x in each block B, for every block B′. Policies, targets, costs, and records need their own factorization conditions.

## H. Information quantities

**Proposition H1 (information sufficiency). [established; R48]** Let Θ, C, M be finite random variables under a joint law P.

1. If I(Θ; M | C) = 0, then for every loss ℓ(θ, a), the Bayes risk of the best rule using (C, M) equals that of the best rule using C alone.
2. Under logarithmic loss, the Bayes risk using C is H(Θ | C), and using (C, M) it is H(Θ | C, M). The reduction is exactly I(Θ; M | C).

*Proof.* (1) I(Θ; M | C) = 0 gives P(θ | c, m) = P(θ | c) on supported (c, m), so the posterior expected loss of every action depends on c only, and a C-measurable minimizer attains the same risk. (2) The Bayes-optimal log-loss prediction is the posterior; its expected loss is the conditional entropy. Subtract. ∎

The proposition concerns evidence about Θ under P. It says nothing about computation, execution, redundancy, error correction, consent, or preparations outside P's support (Part 11, Example 15).

**Data processing and its strong form.** For a Markov chain U → X → Y, I(U; Y) ≤ I(U; X). [established; R48] For a channel K, the SDPI coefficient η_KL(K) = sup I(U; Y)/I(U; X) over such chains satisfies η_KL(K) ≤ η_TV(K), where the Dobrushin coefficient is η_TV(K) = max_{x,x′} TV(K(x,·), K(x′,·)). [established; R45]

**Proposition H2 (contraction may need blocks).** On X = {0, 1, 2}, let

```text
K = [ [0, 1, 0],
      [0, 0, 1],
      [1/3, 1/3, 1/3] ].
```

K is irreducible and aperiodic, η_TV(K) = 1, and η_TV(K²) = 2/3.

*Proof.* Rows 0 and 1 are disjoint point masses, so η_TV(K) = 1. The cycle 0 → 1 → 2 → 0 has length 3 and the loop at 2 has length 1, so the chain is irreducible and aperiodic. K² has rows (0, 0, 1), (1/3, 1/3, 1/3), (1/9, 4/9, 4/9), whose largest pairwise TV is 2/3. ∎

So retention of an initial signal can fail to contract in one step and still contract over blocks: I(A; X_{t+2}) ≤ (2/3)·I(A; X_t) for this chain. No size-independent memory lifetime follows from a single-component coefficient, and redundancy, coding, protected degrees of freedom, and repair change the applicable channel.

```text
Σ = (p/m) I − (p²/m²) 11ᵀ,
```

*Proof.* In both cases v is an eigenvector of G + δvvᵀ with eigenvalue raised by δ and the other eigenvalues unchanged; the determinant factorizes over eigenvalues. ∎

## K. Quantum realization

A state ρ is positive semidefinite with Tr ρ = 1. A channel ℰ is completely positive and trace preserving. An effect F satisfies 0 ≤ F ≤ I. An instrument {ℳ_o} is a family of completely positive trace-nonincreasing maps summing to a channel. [established; R06, R07]

```text
p(o) = Tr[ℳ_o(ρ)],        ρ_o = ℳ_o(ρ) / p(o)  when p(o) > 0.
```

Keep the pair (p(o), ρ_o), or the unnormalized ℳ_o(ρ). Conditional normalization alone discards p(o). This is the quantum form of the instrument of §B, and failure outcomes are retained in the same way.

For Kraus operators K_(o,α), sum over unrecorded α inside ℳ_o(ρ).
An ensemble label or Kraus index is not automatically an accessible physical
outcome. If later dynamics uses the environment, retain a dilation or an
equivalent full process representation; a reduced marginal alone is insufficient.

```text
p(o₁, …, o_n) = Tr[ ℳ^{(n)}_{o_n} ∘ ℰ_{n−1} ∘ ⋯ ∘ ℰ₁ ∘ ℳ^{(1)}_{o₁}(ρ) ].
```

This gives the joint law for that protocol only. It does not supply one classical joint distribution over the outcomes of all incompatible measurements.

**Process tensors and combs.** Across n intervention slots, a process tensor or quantum comb Υ assigns probabilities to sequences of operations through a fixed pairing, p = ⟨Υ, 𝒜^{(1)}_{o₁} ⊗ ⋯ ⊗ 𝒜^{(n)}_{o_n}⟩, subject to positivity and causal normalization. [established; R15, R16, R31] Operational distances between multi-round strategies supply the quantum analogue of d_𝒯.
```text
D(α, β) = Tr( C_α ρ C_β† ).
```

A family of histories admits classical probabilities p(α) = D(α, α) when D(α, β) = 0 for α ≠ β (medium decoherence) or, more weakly, Re D(α, β) = 0 (consistency). Incompatible families are not pooled into one classical tree. [established; R01]

## M. Finite recursive access atlas [proposed specification]

### Physical model and cuts

Fix a finite-dimensional apparatus A with its factors, coupling incidence,
primitive operations, classical controller and memory, clocks, resource
stocks, autonomous environment evolution and initial joint state. A
classical finite-state model is an admitted special case. The finite
implementation uses positive integer primitive durations. Zero-time
notational regroupings are not extra physical events.

Use a finite operation alphabet with specified parameters and finitely many
recorded outcomes per primitive in this first backend. Finite Hilbert-space
dimension alone would not impose these search restrictions.

At a cut Σ retain a sufficient joint residual z: state or conditional state,
apparatus configuration, controller memory, inventory, elapsed time, pending
operations and environmental correlations. A quantum residual retains
coherence. It is not replaced by a classical label for a chosen pure-state
decomposition.

A frame Φ supplies the declared record condition and physical access
interface. Its law follows §K or ordinary conditioning under the specified
underlying process. Only positive-weight conditions are normalized. No
uniform law on the realization catalogue is supplied.

### Witness and execution

Let Π_phys(A,Φ,Σ) be the finite declared controller/program language with
physical preparation and deployment witnesses. A witness π determines

    Exec(π; Φ,Σ,H)
       = joint instrument with records, physical time, bill and full residual.

For a finite classical sector this can be written

    Jπ(r,t,c,z' | Φ,Σ),      Σ_(r,t,c,z') Jπ = 1.

For the quantum sector store the unnormalized instrument outputs, with
classical registers only for actually recorded quantities. The trace of
each output gives its weight. Retain the environment where later evolution
depends on it. Mathematical bookkeeping of a cost must not perform an
unmodelled measurement of a coherent controller.

The bill c has declared units and composition rules. Cumulative expenditure
adds, elapsed time follows the scheduling model, peak space takes a maximum,
and stock replenishment occurs in the state. Preparation and post-cut
operating bills are distinguished. Their separate coordinatewise minima
cannot be combined into a fictitious feasible bill.

For a hard bound b, require the specified cumulative/peak bounds on every
positive-weight branch (almost surely in a continuous-outcome extension).
Chance constraints or expected-cost bounds are different, explicitly
declared slices. An overrunning branch is not deleted to make its program
admissible.

Define the bounded family schematically by

    A_(Φ,Σ)(H,b)
      = { (π, Exec(π;Φ,Σ,H)) :
          π has a physical witness and satisfies the declared hard bounds }.

An unfinished program can belong to this family as an unfinished execution
when its stopped prefix obeys the bounds. It has not thereby achieved a
requested terminal response. The physical stopping controller and ongoing
environment evolution must be specified. All mass remains, including failure
and deadline-censored outcomes.

### Recursion and compatibility

At a physical intermediate cut, retain z' and continue with the same model:

    prefix π ; suffix σ  ↔  execution of π, followed by execution from its residual.

The suffix is deployed from accessible records. The analyst may integrate
over hidden residual states to predict its behavior but may not select a
different suffix using inaccessible information.

In the finite Markov sector, §C's kernel multiplication gives this restart
identity when z' is sufficient and the continuation witness is valid.
In the quantum sector it is composition of the physical joint maps, with
environment and controller memory preserved. This is ordinary composition,
not a new theorem that finite summaries are sufficient.

Joint membership requires one common execution, shared resources and a
specified joint response law. Marginals alone do not determine it.
Independent descriptions compose as a tensor/product only when the physical
conditions justify that factorization. An equality of endpoints after two
orders does not prove physical independence.

With a fixed finite alphabet and positive integer durations, the bounded
execution tree is finite. Recursion through residuals therefore terminates
at the finite horizon. A completed field must retain a compatible family
of finite restrictions; it is not defined by taking a scalar limit of
these trees. Existence and comparison of a general infinite quantum or
relativistic completion are additional obligations.

### Search, equivalence and order

A program list is a counterfactual realization family. Actual endogenous
selection is an additional physical process already included when predicting
what occurs. Neither maximization nor a uniform average over this list is
the actual continuation law unless the model implements it.

For a declared diagnostic response, retain the nondominated set of feasible
bills and witnesses. Finite search establishes a minimum only in its stated
language and bound. Lack of a sampled witness is not impossibility.

Two apparatuses are consistently redescribed when their states, operations,
interfaces, clocks, resources and surroundings are transported together.
Matching information summaries or mutual convertibility is not this
isomorphism. Optional tester equivalence from §F is recorded as a separate
restricted relation, not used to delete physical noise.

A coherent realization preorder across different apparatuses remains a
research target. It needs one causal implementation valid over its declared
histories, inputs, budgets and composition, including failures and residual
behavior. Independent per-query simulations establish no such compiler.

No extent L is defined by the set-builder above. The intended virtue of this
record is that candidate extents can be tested against physically checkable
access and residual differences without adding preferred outcomes.

## N. The classical correlation identity

For finite X = (X1,...,Xn) and F,

    I(X;F) − Σ_i I(Xi;F)
      = [Σ_i H(Xi|F) − H(X|F)] − [Σ_i H(Xi) − H(X)]

**Mind as we know it — framework usage.** Phenomenal awareness organized by history and memory into a continuing sense of self. Not necessary for every kind of mind.

**Multiway graph.** A representation of alternative paths and their relations. Quantum uses need amplitude and interference information too.

**Normative bridge.** The commitment that the possibility of value warrants consideration in action. Stated, not derived.

**Omega — framework.** Completed manifestation: the full realized structure considered globally. Distinct from a maximal-lushness ideal.

**Omega Decision Theory (ODT).** The staged architecture of licensing (ODT0), partial comparison (ODT1), and explicit arbitration (ODT2).

**Perspective — framework.** A physically situated continuation process, specified by region or interface, observable algebra, boundary condition, coupling structure, and continuation domain. Need not be an observer or agent.

**Policy.** A rule or organized disposition for action based on available information.

**Policy atlas.** A family of policies whose success regions cover the contingencies. The cover alone does not supply a selector. Its minimal size is the policy-cover number ν_F(E).

**Preparation / readiness.** The distinction between a prepared apparatus's conditional performance q and the route probability pq that includes obtaining it.

**Present membrane — framework.** A situated boundary for description, often a relative spacelike section. Not a globally privileged time slice.

**Process tensor / quantum comb.** A representation of a quantum process across intervention slots, retaining response and memory structure.

**Realization witness — framework.** The apparatus, controller, records, preparation, resources, and embedding showing that a protocol is physically implementable with the claimed law and costs.

**Recovery.** An allowed continuation restoring a registered target. Function, lineage, and individual are different targets. Existential and robust recovery differ.

**Recursive presentation — framework.** G_H(x): the base record, the continuation laws of each implemented process together with the presentations they leave behind, and derived joint realizations. A place to extract organization, not a score.

**Redundancy.** Either repeated description of one process (removable) or repeated physical implementation (which can create recovery routes).

**Regeneration.** Restoration or development of capability after disturbance, possibly changing the available policy space.

**Residual continuation.** Everything that can still follow from a physically specified boundary condition or record.

**Robustness.** Satisfaction of a requirement across stated contingencies by an available policy or organization.

**Standing.** The kind and strength of a valuer's claim to consideration. Identifying a valuer does not settle it.

**Support.** The alternatives a model permits, as distinct from how weight is distributed among them.

**Task.** An explicitly specified event or input–output requirement on a physical run. Task labels carry no value.

**Temporoperceptual cohesion.** An architecture that integrates delayed and asynchronous inputs into a usable present. A candidate physical organization of perspective and control; not evidence of phenomenal awareness.

**Terminal solitude — framework.** Closure in which independent valuation has been excised and cannot be regenerated within the relevant continuation. Not entailed by a single controller or a single identity.

**Tester.** An admissible physical interaction and readout used to characterize a process within a declared domain.

**Trajectory (identity) — framework usage.** On the originator's interpretation, the continuous physical history that gives a pattern its identity: "You are a pattern, but the pattern isn't you."

**Value.** Open in the program. Value requires valuers; its possibility in the substrate is the present proxy. Physical difference, persistence, preference, and candidate statistics are not its definition.

**Valuer — framework.** A locus or process of valuation, whose final demarcation remains open. Every consequential process is eligible as a potential valuer in the field. Endogenous significance is a further hypothesis, not an admission condition or equal-weight theorem.

**Viability.** Remaining within registered requirements under the relevant dynamics, policies, contingencies, and information access. The viability kernel is the greatest set from which the specified guarantee is possible. Part 12's state-space recursion assumes full-state feedback; partial observation requires information-state analysis or a verified record-based selector.

**Vorticality.** Circulatory organization that exemplifies persistent patternhood. A motivating image with physical realizations, not a universal axis of perspective.

## Compute and stopping

The shallow structural run used ten workers, with eight source assignments analysed exactly per program. The later local-flow and finite-fuel sweeps took seconds with a single numerical worker. Most work was interpretation and verification. Larger enumeration is not automatically the next useful evidence.

Use up to ten workers for the next local probe, keep numerical-library threading bounded, and estimate prefix/state growth before increasing depth. Separate simulation coverage from decoder coverage. An exhausted bound supports a claim only within that bound; a failed sampled search supports no nonexistence theorem.

Useful low-cost questions include reuse versus preparation overhead, correlated failure rather than independent loss, latency under shared occupancy, and the interaction of repair with further construction. They are proposed probes, not results or added scoring terms.

## What would change the program

A carrier that cannot reproduce an explicitly allowed continuation needs repair or rejection. An extent that repeatedly rewards excluded access while claiming to measure access needs repair or rejection. A reliable structural comparison that fails to track possible value under an independently specified family challenges the bridge itself.

These failures must remain distinct. Keeping the entire physical model beside a defective summary does not vindicate that summary. Adding another descriptive feature does not automatically rescue the claim that lushness is an informative correlate.
