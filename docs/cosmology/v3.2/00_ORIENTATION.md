# 00 — Orientation

**Omega Cosmology v3.2 — development edition, 3 October 2026**

Omega investigates the structure of continuation: how physical situations support, constrain and transform what can follow, and how those transformations alter the possibility of value.

The motivation begins with **value requires valuers**. Under uncertainty about what valuers and values can be, the program admits every consequential process as a potential valuer. Organisms, minds, readable records and declared preferences are not admission requirements. Their particular organization can matter through its consequences. Admission does not establish that every process is an actual valuer, that all processes have equal ethical weight, or that their preferences supply the comparison.

The guiding assumption is that expanding the substrate of possible value can support more possibility of value. The task is to characterize that substrate and investigate its comparison. It is permissible to start from that assumption. It is not yet a theorem identifying structural expansion with realized goodness.

## What this edition proposes

> **A continuation field is the physical structure of weighted, jointly compatible continuations, including how each continuation changes further physical access. Lushness is the proposed extent of that recursive structure as a substrate of possible value.**

The current candidate is **recursive physical access geometry**. Its finite operational presentation is an atlas of joint response laws, physical implementation bills and the residual processes those implementations leave. Access is situated within the object; comparison refers to the encompassing field. The atlas is not a menu for an external chooser with unlimited control.

A workshop and loose materials can contain the same elementary degrees of freedom yet support different transformations at a given cost and time. Installing a channel can alter which differences affect later processes. Redundancy can change repairability without adding distinct source content. A controller can close others' routes while increasing its own. These are changes of one physical object, not separate ethical multipliers.

## The advance from 3.1

The earlier edition already supplied residual-state instruments, physical realization witnesses, joint feasibility, recursive descriptions and quantum process machinery. Version 3.2 keeps those foundations and makes three changes central:

1. **The field precedes valuer identification.** Every consequential difference stays, including noise. Observation-relative equivalence is a diagnostic compression, not deletion of physical futures.
2. **Access includes implementation and continuation.** A current information summary does not specify the bill for using its content or what remains after doing so. Witnesses now make those distinctions explicit.
3. **The candidate has concrete counterexamples to simpler readouts.** Equal event counts, equal current information and equal preparation bills can conceal different later access or recovery. These failures constrain the next candidate; they do not yet identify its extent.

The short October 3 run sampled 100,000 programs with ten workers. It supplied small witnesses rather than a universal ranking: equal-information encodings with different bounded decoding costs; equal-content organizations with different repairable losses; and a correction to the interpretation of the current synergy statistic for dependent inputs. Part 11 presents the witnesses; Part 14 states their evidence status.

## Five layers, kept connected

| Layer | Content | Status |

| Situated continuation | Frame-conditioned laws, time, accessible couplings and records | Established machinery with explicit model assumptions |

These layers do not require five unrelated scores. They separate what has been specified from what remains to be established.

## Completion and frames

The intended object is completed continuation, including its finite structure. Completion does not erase a delay, missed response or temporary loss. Horizons select restrictions for calculation; they are not an automatic discount or an instruction to compare only growth exponents.

Frames already supply situated normalization under the physical law. History and currently accessible records must be distinguished when information is lost. The ultimate frame names the intended encompassing comparison where no further regression of normalized weighting remains. It is not an ideal agent outside the dynamics. This intended role has not yet become a completeness theorem for the candidate access order.

Quantum mechanics supplies the primary physical language. Coherent alternatives are not pooled into one classical branching tree. Finite classical models remain useful sectors and approximations whose scope is stated.

## How to read and use this edition

Parts 01–03 define the physical and recursive construction. Part 04 states the candidate lushness question. Parts 05–08 connect identity, recovery, agency, ethics and decision. Parts 09–10 preserve the explicitly speculative discussion of awareness and Alpha–Omega. Parts 11–12 give worked examples and mathematics. Parts 13–16 supply terminology, evidence status, references and provenance.

“Established” marks borrowed mathematics under its assumptions. “Project result” marks a stated finite result or check, with evidence linked in Parts 14 and 16. “Proposed” marks definitions and architecture; “conjecture” marks unestablished explanatory claims. An implemented toy is not evidence for a cosmology merely because it uses the same vocabulary.

This is a development edition. It makes the candidate concrete enough to investigate and preserves what has already failed. It does not announce a completed lushness invariant, a proof that gas loses, or a derived ethical optimum.
