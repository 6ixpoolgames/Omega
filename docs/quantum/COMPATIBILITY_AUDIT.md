# Next task: continuation-breadth compatibility audit

Specification, 2026-10-09. This is the next task, not a completed new experiment.
Use the [formalism](FORMALISM.md) and [evidence ledger](CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE.md).

## Question

Can an effective breadth/profile/comparison respect physical-description
equivalence, classical complete-history calibration and composition without
first constructing a literal volume measure?

Do not solve this by choosing a new entropy and checking only its endpoints.
Audit the requirements and their domains before proposing another functional.

## Inputs and distinctions

Retain the native physical specification, full sufficient present and actual
time-ordered evolution. Treat analytical history families and physical record
queries as separate declared views. No hypothetical intervention menu or new
readers are inserted to create extra distinguishable alternatives.

Each comparison must declare which operation is being performed:

1. Same physics, same resolved question, equivalent mathematical representation.
2. Same physics, different physical question or resolution.
3. Different physics with matched comparison conditions.
4. Later horizon or a newly rooted residual future.

Only the first category demands equality solely by representation invariance.
The others require an explicit relation rather than an assumed equality.

## Requirements to check jointly

| ID | Requirement | Domain/qualification |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Coordinate covariance and dummy-checkpoint invariance | All physical inputs and the same question transformed together |
| R2 | Classical `exp H(p_complete histories)` | Physically justified classical resolution, not every arbitrary projective family |
| R3 | Multiplicativity for independent systems | Independent law, product root and matched frame; no inference from marginal scores for coupled systems |
| R4 | Present sufficiency and rerooting consistency | Full state/environment and remaining law; no time-averaged replacement |
| R5 | Correct extension through time | Classical chain rule where applicable; no assumed quantum horizon monotonicity |
| R6 | Lower one / classical-upper calibration | Fixed justified family, weights and units; global purity is not a branch count |
| R7 | Preserve physically different intervening dynamics | Carrier distinguishes idle from excursion-and-return; breadth is not required to differ merely because the carrier does |
| R8 | No representation multiplicity bonus | Inert blank degrees, duplicate labels and bookkeeping edges cannot manufacture occupied breadth |

A profile must specify how the requirements apply to individual coordinates and
restrictions. A partial ordering must identify its comparison relation and
incomparable cases; it must not be reported as a completed scalar. No new base
measure or reference trace is mandatory.

## Deliverables and stopping criteria

1. A precise equivalence and frame-comparison specification for the fixtures.
2. An implication/conflict table for R1-R8, distinguishing mathematical proofs
   from finite counterexamples and open physical-selection assumptions.
3. One of the following, explicitly scoped:
   - a construction satisfying the declared requirements on these fixtures;
   - a proof that a stated subset of requirements conflicts;
   - a concrete missing physical declaration needed to make the question defined.
4. Only where useful, one small reproducible numerical probe of a genuinely new
   prediction. Retain negative outcomes and keep raw output local.

The existing idle Z/X obstruction and echo counterexamples are evidence to reuse,
not new discoveries. A failed entropy is not a no-go theorem for every possible
comparison. A passing toy construction is not a universal quantum extent.
