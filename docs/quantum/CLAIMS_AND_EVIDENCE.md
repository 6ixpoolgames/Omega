# Quantum claims and evidence ledger

Updated 2026-10-09. This ledger preserves the meaning of the recent finite probes.
It does not merge their different readouts into one measure.

## Claim classes

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Standard mathematics | An identity/theorem under explicitly stated assumptions |
| Finite evidence | Exact finite-model calculations to floating-point tolerance |
| Adopted interpretation/calibration | The programme's object or intended comparison, not a discovered physical law |
| Open hypothesis | A proposed property or construction still requiring justification |

The native evolving-state object and block interpretation belong to the third
class. Quantum evolution and the classical entropy chain rule use standard
mathematics. The toy circuits supply finite evidence. Universal lushness,
automatic native-history selection and the adequacy of lushness as an ethical
proxy remain open. GE's stated bridge is value requiring valuers together with
conditional normativity; the physics tests do not establish its scope or application.

## Results that survive the candidate failures

**Coherent reconstruction.** Summing class amplitudes before final weighting
recovers the native final Born law in the tested circuits. A mathematical cut
does not cause physical collapse.

**Record extension.** Fresh balanced hops with distinct actual memory registers
give complete-history breadth 2,4,8. Record correlations and descendant/parent
refinement checks pass. The actual joint memory state's spectral entropy gives
effective rank two in these examples, so it is a different quantity. See the
[bounded report](../research_notes/omega_v2/bounded_continuation_report_v0.md).

**Reversal and leakage.** In the explicit eraser, conditional fringes cancel in
the unconditional signal. Marker-only reversal leaves information in a coupled
environment; full modeled reversal restores signal interference. Early/delayed
marker-basis changes have the same final state in this circuit. All global states
remain pure. No universal whole-history `1 -> 2 -> 1` law follows. See the
[eraser report](../research_notes/omega_v2/quantum_eraser_report_v0.md).

**Physical family selection is additional work.** A conserved interaction axis
can identify a useful record question in special cases. The same coupling with a
different environment state need not record it. Algebraic branch projections and
medium decoherence alone do not select a native classical family.

**Bounds are insufficient.** The Holevo candidate satisfies the desired lower
and classical-upper limits while giving one or two for an unchanged echo under
different history expansions. The latter expansion fails the proposed memory
record certificate. Passing an inequality is not enough to establish physical
meaning.

## Assumptions not to promote

- The quantum bounds are proposed for a fixed justified family, weights and
  calibration. They are not a universal ceiling across all quantum processes.
- Record-based lower bounds on a future full extent are hypotheses requiring
  compatible restriction, not theorems about an already defined lushness.
- The existing local registers, preparations and pulse schedules are stipulated
  toy physics. They are not a derivation of emergent observers, autonomous energy
  accounting, relativistic field dynamics or quantum gravity.
- Equal upper/lower calibrations do not uniquely determine an interpolation.
- Classical breadth advantages in other adapters do not establish a quantum gas
  victory, sustained generativity, or ethical value.
