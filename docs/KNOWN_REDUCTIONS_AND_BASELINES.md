# Known Reductions And Baselines

Status: public reduction and baseline map.

Purpose: prevent novelty inflation by recording what nearby mathematical and
empirical frameworks already cover, and what a stronger Omega / Layer A claim
would have to witness before being treated as more than a promising synthesis.

Claim boundary:

```text
This document does not establish novelty.
It does not validate Omega.
It does not define or detect value, valuers, agency, identity, selfhood,
compatibility, boundary, support, capture, or erasure.
```

## Core Object Under Pressure

The current lower object can be described as:

```text
Layer A:
  consequence-bearing distinctions under admissible continuation.

It asks which distinctions are preserved, erased, recoverable, transformed,
composed, or made non-neutral by continuation under declared observations,
comparisons, and recovery contracts.
```

This object is not licensed by vocabulary. It is licensed only when a concrete
witness separates it from simpler baselines under declared controls.

Until at least a small battery of such witnesses exists, Layer A should be
described as a promising synthesis and measurement discipline, not as a
demonstrated new science.

## Reduction Cases We Should Welcome

Some adapters may reduce cleanly to known frameworks. That is not a failure.
It is part of the reduction map.

Examples:

```text
Exact deterministic transition plus observation equivalence may reduce to
bisimulation.

A finite channel with fixed source/target observations and a declared decoder
may reduce to channel decoding or support recovery.

Viability with a single declared constraint may reduce to viability-kernel
membership.
```

The novelty burden lives where the current framework distinguishes a structure
that a baseline collapses, or where it forces a provenance, declaration, or
claim-boundary discipline that the baseline result by itself does not supply.

## Required Witness Classes

The next credibility target is a small suite of baseline-controlled witnesses.
Each witness should name the controlled baseline, output a retained fixture, and
state what it does and does not support.

### Same Reachability, Different Recoverability

Purpose: show that reachable support or frontier count is insufficient for
declared consequence-bearing recovery.

Minimal substrate: finite state/channel pair over the same carrier.

Baselines controlled: reachable support, reachable count, or frontier size.

Expected artifact: CSV/JSON fixture plus a test or smoke command.

Claim supported: reachability count alone does not determine declared
recoverability.

Claim not supported: value, Omega, agency, identity, or valuerhood.

### Same Entropy, Different Consequence-Bearing Structure

Purpose: show that distributional spread is insufficient for distinction
preservation.

Minimal substrate: paired finite distributions or channels with equal entropy.

Baselines controlled: entropy and carrier size.

Expected artifact: fixture with declared distinctions and pass/fail recovery
checks.

Claim supported: entropy alone does not determine which declared distinctions
are preserved or erased.

Claim not supported: that lower entropy or higher entropy is better.

### Same Mutual Information Or Capacity, Different Declared Recovery

Purpose: separate generic information transfer from declared registry recovery.

Minimal substrate: finite stochastic channels with matched information metric.

Baselines controlled: mutual information, capacity, or Bayes-best recovery.

Expected artifact: retained registry run with provenance and adversarial audit.

Claim supported: optimized information recovery does not automatically license
the declared instrument's recovery claim.

Claim not supported: semantic recovery or substrate-general recovery.

### Same Bisimulation Under One Panel, Different Consequence Profile Under Another

Purpose: show where the framework is panel-sensitive rather than a hidden
global identity claim.

Minimal substrate: finite transition system with two declared observation or
continuation panels.

Baselines controlled: bisimulation partition under the first panel.

Expected artifact: two panel specs and exact consequence-profile comparison.

Claim supported: panel declaration changes the admissible merge question.

Claim not supported: arbitrary post-hoc panel selection.

### Same Pairwise Compatibility, Different Joint Compatibility

Purpose: block the move from pairwise checks to class-level or family-level
claims.

Minimal substrate: finite triple with pairwise-compatible links and a joint
failure.

Baselines controlled: pairwise compatibility count.

Expected artifact: Lean theorem or finite fixture.

Claim supported: chain or pairwise evidence is not enough for joint
compatibility.

Claim not supported: any particular empirical class is meaningful.

### Same Frontier Morphology, Different Irreversible Loss

Purpose: keep Future Field Atlas morphology subordinate to consequence and
loss questions.

Minimal substrate: paired finite dynamics with matched reachable-frontier
summary statistics.

Baselines controlled: frontier size, rank, or morphology feature set.

Expected artifact: retained run comparing frontier features and irreversible
loss markers.

Claim supported: morphology alone is insufficient for the stronger trajectory
or loss claim.

Claim not supported: valuerhood, capture, support, or Omega validation.

### Same Compression Score, Different Merge Soundness

Purpose: prevent concise abstractions from becoming ontological objects by
default.

Minimal substrate: finite consequence system with two abstractions of matched
description length.

Baselines controlled: description length or compression score.

Expected artifact: exact profile plus soundness/completeness audit.

Claim supported: compression score alone does not imply merge soundness.

Claim not supported: that less compression or more compression is preferred.

## First Retained Witnesses

The first retained targets are:

```text
same reachability, different recoverability
same optimized recovery success, different declared recovery
same marginal Bayes-best success, different joint Bayes-best success
```

Retained notes:

```text
docs/research_notes/validation_results/baseline_witnesses/same_reachability_different_recovery_v0.md
docs/research_notes/validation_results/baseline_witnesses/same_optimized_success_different_declared_recovery_v0.md
docs/research_notes/validation_results/baseline_witnesses/same_marginal_success_different_joint_success_v0.md
```

Implemented reachability construction:

```text
Use a finite carrier and two finite channels or continuations.
Match reachable target support or frontier size.
Declare a distinction and a recovery contract before scoring.
Construct one channel that preserves the declared distinction under the
contract and one that erases it, while preserving the reachability baseline.
Emit a retained fixture and a testable pass/fail result.
```

The supported claim would be narrow:

```text
Reachability count is insufficient for declared consequence-bearing recovery.
```

It would not support:

```text
Omega validation;
value or valuer detection;
agency or identity detection;
substrate-general theory validation.
```

Implemented optimized-vs-declared construction:

```text
Use a finite carrier and two finite channels.
Match reachability and entropy controls.
Make both channels exactly recoverable by an optimized observation choice.
Declare one target observation before scoring.
Construct one channel where the declared observation recovers the distinction
and one where only a nondeclared optimized observation recovers it.
Emit a retained fixture and a testable pass/fail result.
```

The supported claim is narrow:

```text
Optimized recovery success is insufficient for declared theorem-transfer
readiness.
```

It would not support:

```text
semantic recovery;
Omega validation;
value or valuer detection;
agency or identity detection;
substrate-general theory validation.
```

Implemented marginal-vs-joint construction:

```text
Use a finite two-bit source carrier and two weighted channels.
Match per-source support count, per-source weight total, per-source entropy,
and Bayes-best recovery success for D_A and D_B.
Construct different Bayes-best recovery success for D_joint.
Emit a retained fixture and a testable pass/fail result.
```

The supported claim is narrow:

```text
Matched marginal diagnostic success does not determine joint recovery success.
```

It would not support:

```text
exact marginal preservation;
semantic recovery;
Omega validation;
value or valuer detection;
agency or identity detection;
substrate-general theory validation.
```
