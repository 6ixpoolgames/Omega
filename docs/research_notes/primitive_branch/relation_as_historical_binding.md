# Relation as Historical Binding

A primitive revision of Omega

## Purpose

This note clarifies the primitive layer beneath Omega.

Earlier Omega drafts often begin with the presence of valuers, agents, observers, or coherent systems. That was a reasonable starting point because the original project was aimed at the consequences of agency: viable trajectories, recoverability, pseudo-Omega traps, mutual corridors, and the structure of value-bearing futures.

The recent primitive work asks a lower question:

> What must be true before valuerhood is definable at all?

This note gives a compact answer while setting a boundary. Omega does not need to replace the Free Energy Principle or active inference as a theory of minimal self-maintaining agency. Omega only needs a primitive vocabulary clean enough to meet that floor. The project should not get trapped trying to rederive all of agency from cellular automata motifs or minimal worlds.

The descent stops at recoverable historical identity. Omega begins above it.

## Revised primitive triad

The current primitive stack is:

```text
distinction:
  differences exist

asymmetry:
  different paths lead to different futures

relation:
  futures remain conditioned by histories
```

This corrects an ambiguity in earlier formulations. Relation is not first inter-agent relation. It is not primarily social coupling, spatial adjacency, graph connectivity, edge memory, or neighbor dependence. Those are implementations or later-scale expressions.

At the primitive level:

> Relation is historical conditioning: what can happen next depends on the path by which the present was reached.

Correlation between past and future is not enough. Correlation can be accidental, passive, or epiphenomenal. Relation means historical dependence: prior path structure constrains the future possibility space.

When this historical conditioning coheres across time, it gives rise to identity. A system would otherwise be merely a sequence of states, not a continuing process.

## Inter-relation as recursive relation

Inter-relation is not a separate primitive.

Once histories exist, histories can interact. That interaction is itself path-dependent, and may become a higher-order history.

```text
H_A = path-dependent history of A
H_B = path-dependent history of B

interaction:
  H_A × H_B → H_AB

where H_AB is itself a path-dependent history
```

Thus relation scales recursively:

```text
state → history
history → interacting histories
interacting histories → higher-order history
higher-order history → larger-scale identity
```

This gives a principled bridge from primitive identity to ecology, agency, society, and Omega-compatible trajectories without adding new primitives.

## Historical identity

A sequence of states is not yet an identity.

```text
X0 → X1 → X2 → ... → Xt
```

It becomes a historical identity only when the states along the path are coherently bound as the continuation of the same process.

Informally:

```text
I(X0) ~ I(X1) ~ ... ~ I(Xt)
```

The equivalence cannot be cheap. If everything maps to the same identity, the identity relation explains nothing. If only exact state equality counts, the identity is too brittle to survive transformation.

The relevant object is therefore not a state, and not a raw trajectory, but an identity class of histories.

A historical identity is:

> a distinction-bearing path whose future remains conditioned by its history and whose continuation remains recognizable through transformation.

## Rejecting cheap persistence

The primitive floor is not mere persistence.

Many things persist in ways that are too cheap:

```text
stasis:
  persistence compresses to one state

clock:
  persistence compresses to phase modulo k

self-copy:
  persistence factors through isolated local recurrence

local phase cycle:
  persistence is carried by an internal symbol clock rather than history

symmetry fakeout:
  apparent asymmetry disappears under symmetrization

lock-in:
  persistence collapses to a terminal attractor

noise:
  persistence is only distributional churn, not identity
```

These are not useless phenomena. Clocks, cycles, and attractors can be components of living systems. The rejected object is clock-only identity: apparent persistence that factors entirely through a simple phase cycle with no recoverable historical depth.

The principled rejection criterion is:

> If apparent identity can be compressed to current state, phase, local clock, symmetry quotient, or attractor basin without losing predictive or recoverability structure, it is not historical identity in the relevant sense.

Equivalently:

```text
clock fakeout:
  I(H_t) = f(phase_t)

historical identity:
  I(H_t) depends on the path H_t
  and perturbation-continuation paths can preserve I without reducing to phase
```

The key test is not whether something repeats. The key test is whether the identity remains continuable under path-dependent perturbation.

## Recoverability as the floor

Persistence alone is too weak.

The actual floor for valuerhood is recoverable historical identity:

> identity-through-transformation that remains continuable under perturbation.

This is the point where the primitive descent meets the FEP / active inference floor.

A structure becomes relevant to agency when it maintains a boundary or identity under perturbing dynamics. It does not merely last. It resists dispersion, absorbs disturbance, and remains able to continue as itself in a nontrivial sense.

This is not yet Omega. It is the minimum condition under which Omega's older questions can start to apply.

## Where Omega meets FEP

Omega should not try to replace FEP as the theory of minimal self-maintaining agency.

The handoff is:

```text
primitive substrate:
  distinction, path consequence, historical conditioning

FEP / active inference floor:
  bounded self-maintaining identity under perturbation

Omega:
  viable, recoverable, value-bearing trajectory structure above that floor
```

FEP and related self-maintenance frameworks address the primitive question: how a bounded system maintains its identity under perturbation. Omega inherits that floor.

Omega then asks a different question:

> Among self-maintaining valuers, which histories remain viable, recoverable, compatible, and rich under uncertainty, cost, scale, and inter-history constraint?

## Valuerhood bridge

A minimal valuer can be defined without consciousness, explicit preferences, utility functions, or human-like goals.

Proposed definition:

> A valuer is a bounded, historically conditioned process whose interactions contribute to maintaining or expanding its own recoverable continuability.

This requires:

```text
boundary:
  enough distinction between process and environment for identity to be meaningful

historical identity:
  continuity through transformation

recoverability:
  perturbations do not immediately erase continuability

self-maintaining interaction:
  interaction with the environment contributes to continued identity

path consequence:
  different continuations change future viability
```

This definition is intentionally minimal. It identifies the point where persistence becomes proto-value: not because the system has preferences, but because continuability can be preserved or lost.

## Omega proper

Omega is not bare persistence.

Omega concerns the structure of value-bearing histories that remain recoverably continuable under physical, epistemic, resource, scale, and inter-history constraints.

The older Omega concepts live above the valuerhood bridge:

```text
recoverable reachability:
  futures remain accessible without irreversible collapse

distinguishable viable futures:
  future richness is structured, not just raw path count

bounded cost:
  continuability remains physically feasible

epistemic grounding:
  models remain connected to reality enough to guide continuation

scale consistency:
  abstraction preserves real viability and does not invent fake viability

inter-history compatibility:
  interacting valuers preserve or expand compatible corridors rather than destroying each other's continuability

pseudo-Omega attractors:
  locally persistent structures that preserve themselves by degrading broader value-bearing reachability

redemption / re-entry:
  damaged histories remain reconnectable to Omega-compatible trajectories before irreversible horizon crossing
```

Thus Omega begins where recoverable historical identities become value-bearing trajectories.

## Category-theoretic skeleton

The categorical framing is useful because it treats identity through transformation as primary.

A minimal skeleton:

```text
objects:
  states, configurations, identities, or identity classes

morphisms:
  transitions, transformations, histories

paths:
  composed morphisms

identity class:
  equivalence over histories, not exact state equality

historical conditioning:
  future morphisms available from an object depend on the path by which it was reached

recoverability:
  perturbation followed by continuation remains within the identity class

inter-relation:
  histories interact to produce higher-order histories

fakeout:
  apparent persistence factors through a cheaper subcategory
```

Examples of fakeout factorization:

```text
stasis:
  factors through identity morphism

clock:
  factors through finite cyclic phase

self-only:
  factors through isolated local dynamics

symmetry:
  factors through a symmetrized quotient

local phase memory:
  factors through a symbol clock

lock-in:
  collapses to terminal attractor

noise:
  lacks a stable identity functor
```

This gives a theoretical home to the empirical guardrails used in the minimal-world probes. Those guardrails are crude factorization tests. They ask whether apparent persistence survives after removing cheaper explanations.

## What minimal worlds can and cannot validate

Minimal cellular automata and related rule spaces can be useful floor-calibration tools.

They can test:

```text
persistence versus stasis
identity-through-transformation
path dependence
recoverability under perturbation
clock and local-phase fakeouts
symmetry and self-only fakeouts
```

They cannot validate Omega.

The q3/r1 experiments are therefore best interpreted as experiments on the primitive floor. They help clarify what recoverable historical identity is and how fakeouts appear. They should not be used as direct evidence that Omega-like structure has been found.

The G5 held-out result reinforces this boundary. The frozen detector found rare held-out positives and the explicit self/symmetric/output controls remained mostly clean, but the attempted fertile-band prediction failed. This means the minimal-world branch remains useful for floor calibration and detector anatomy, not Omega validation.

## Final thesis

Omega begins where recoverable historical identities become value-bearing trajectories.

The primitive descent clarifies what identity requires:

```text
distinction:
  differences exist

asymmetry:
  different paths lead to different futures

relation:
  futures remain conditioned by histories
```

FEP describes the self-maintaining floor: bounded identities that remain continuable under perturbation.

Omega studies what happens above that floor: which valuers, histories, and inter-history structures remain viable, recoverable, compatible, and rich under uncertainty.
