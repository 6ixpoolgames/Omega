## Flow

capacity 2. A declared external affinity A supplies signed work ±A per forward
or reverse extraction reaction. Rates acquire exp(±βA/2); bath heat is computed
from actual energy change minus this work. The affinity is zero with external
drive off, when all controller reactions remain enabled in equilibrium checks.
It is an explicit additional work boundary, not D's own handling bill or free
suppression of reverse events. These are driven-law contrasts, not equal-work
policy comparisons. Report boundary work and native source work separately.

Physical calibration precedes entropy. Measure expected gross F+R and net F−R
of extraction-pair transitions from the common root through T256. Use A=-3,0,3
and catalytic assistance in [0,2]. Find the common attainable gross interval
between the largest assistance-zero gross and smallest assistance-two gross.
If empty, stop. Select 25% and 75% of that interval. At each level fit assistance
for those three affinities, then fit a fourth point with the same gross and
zero net transfer, within the same amplitude/affinity bounds. A failed fit is
an unmatched design point, not an entropy/theory result.

2e-5 events at T256. An independently compiled event kernel must agree with the
linear calibration template within 1e-11. Calibration never queries entropy.

at net zero. Matching is not asserted at other horizons or for total world
activity, source throughput or fuel. Individual forward/reverse counts are not
