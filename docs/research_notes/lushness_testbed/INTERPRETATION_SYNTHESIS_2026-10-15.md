## Why rate matters in these comparisons

For a fixed finite irreducible generator, different initial preparations share
the same sampled entropy rate. Their differences can persist as offsets even
after both processes settle. The mortality fixtures give a concrete instance:
the body eventually ceases, the same environmental process remains, and the
access and register advantages are finite-life differences. Their common rate
does not make those finite differences irrelevant.

Different ongoing physical mechanisms or policies can induce different
generators and different rates. The renewal fixture makes this distinction
visible. IX and R0 receive the same offered boundary prescription in each
matched comparison, but their embodied information/action couplings differ.
They are mechanism contrasts, not a rate difference between two roots of one
fixed generator and not an exhaustive controller search. Occupancy, accepted
work and thermal routing may change; the background is not assumed to cancel.

Writing the late-horizon profile as

    ell(T) = h*T + b + o(1),

the difference is

    Delta ell(T) = Delta h*T + Delta b + o(1).

A positive Delta h distinguishes an ongoing contribution from a bounded
preparation head start. It indicates how an access investment can repay an
initial deficit. When Delta h is nonzero, the late-time estimate

    T_cross = -Delta b / Delta h

locates a candidate repayment scale. The full finite-history calculation
determines whether that repayment has occurred at a particular horizon. In
renewal, the progression from no IX wins atT64 to13 atT1024 is an actual
best-of-four finite-history result, not a rate-first ranking substituted for
lushness. The original T64 frontier predictions still fail.

Robust persistence determines whether the continuing return survives. A
high-rate channel that is easily destroyed may lose to robust preservation
over the complete continuation. Resource exhaustion, irreversible damage,
repair routes, substitutes and the physical opportunity window must therefore
be in the law. The renewal fixture supplies an indefinitely maintained source
and continuing bath reconstruction. It establishes an ongoing effect within
that regime; it has not established robustness against every relevant failure.

A temporal-resolution crossover is different: it varies cadence at a fixed
physical horizon. Its expected-jump and finite-term coordinates explain how
more finely resolving event timing can change the ordering. Neither that
finite term nor a limiting rate is a substitute for the physical Omega structure.

## Structural account of the access/renewal result

1. **What changed in Omega?** Paid sensing now influences costed movement,
   location-dependent capture and a reservoir coupled to renewable hardware.
2. **What causes the gain?** The informative writer accesses stocks differently
   from the stock-independent writer. Resource and event ledgers accompany
   the breadth comparison. Coordinate shares are explanatory and order-dependent,
   not unique causal entropies of individual reaction families.
3. **What supports persistence?** Hardware can be reconstructed through a paid
   reservoir reaction and through thermal assembly. Actuators and readers require
   hardware and available energy. There is no immortal controller.
4. **Does use maintain or deplete the channel?** Capture replenishes the bank;
   sensing, movement and upkeep spend it. The full ledger includes reverse
   construction and bath support. A gross hardware-return ratio above1 does not
   demonstrate that all running costs are funded by the access mechanism.
5. **Do descendants expand it?** Capacity2 permits an existing unit to assist
   construction of the second. Assistance raises both build and unbuild flux;
   retained two-unit occupancy falls slightly in all three tested contrasts.
6. **What remains without hardware?** Positive basal capture, precursor material,
   resource fluctuations and thermal reconstruction remain. Energy-zero is an
   unpowered channel, not restoration of a terminated individual identity.
7. **What does finite lushness report?** R0 wins at short horizons, while IX
   becomes the actual winner in more laws as the horizon extends.
8. **What does rate report?** A positive IX−R0 ongoing contribution at all57
   law/cadence comparisons, distinct from same-law preparation offsets.
9. **How resolution-stable is it?** No pooled IX−R0 sign flips occur across
   cadences0.125/0.25/0.5 at the four tested horizons. Hidden-register bounds are
   retained separately. This is not a complete state-grain or quantum audit.

## What remains open

The next physical distinction is genuine damage versus reversible thermal
turnover. The current wear parameter accelerates thermal assembly as well as
disassembly, so it does not isolate a harmful increase in breakdown. The
registered prediction about wear stays failed, with that structural limitation
explicitly identified.

The resource-use loop also needs a sharper energetic attribution: captured
resource energy, bath reconstruction and energy recovered by dismantling
hardware must remain separately legible. A sustained rate increment from
construction churn is real breadth under the standing randomness ruling,
but it does not establish greater retained generative capacity.

Open-ended compounding, an adequate disordered/thermal comparator, population
intertwining and cross-scale composition remain research targets. The local
mechanism evidence is already positive and adverse in informative ways. The
next modules should expose the missing relations without adding rewards for
the behavior the programme hopes to find.
