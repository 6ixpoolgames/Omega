# Next empirical proposal for v4.1: control over generative access

16 October 2026. Proposal for discussion. No new experiment or scientific run
is authorized by this document. It builds on the retained October 15–16
mechanism, structural-harm, spatial-control and exhaustive-policy results.

## Priority

Combine paid access and renewal with control over another party's access, then
compare the policies selected by self-subject and whole-field lushness. Retain
the oracle layer and finite-history objective. ML is a future search backend,
not the next scientific intervention.

The physical conjunction of successive enabling, persistence and positive
breadth has already been demonstrated in finite count chemistry. The largest
remaining empirical gap is the proposed ethical behaviour of the objective
when a policy can redirect another functioning, generative process. Existing
blocking comparisons show a resolved private/global conflict between particular
interventions. They do not establish that blocking is privately optimal over
productive alternatives. The recent 36-table search establishes adaptive policy
advantages, but omits spatial obstruction and leaves the private optimum open.

## Stage 0: resolve what the leading private choices actually are

Keep the existing world and numerical results. Concentrate additional query work
on the leading possible D maximizers, rather than extending every comparison.

Use deeper conditioning bounds where feasible. A complementary query can sample
physical histories from the known model and evaluate their exact marginal
observation likelihood by hidden-state filtering:

    H(Y_1:n | X_0=x) = E[-log2 P(Y_1:n | X_0=x)].

The likelihood computation uses the declared physical transition kernel. It is
not a learned density model or an intrinsic-exploration reward. Its expectation
is the intended subject-history entropy. Monte Carlo uncertainty and numerical
kernel error must remain separate from deterministic conditioning bounds.
Confidence intervals are not renamed certificates.

Conditioning on an observed D configuration restricts the filtering distribution
to the hidden states in that observation block. In the current adapter this can
be much smaller than the full physical state space. Profile that opportunity
before deciding how much tightening is affordable. Validate likelihoods and
entropy estimates against directly enumerable histories and against the retained
bounds. Stop when the leading policy differences resolve or report the remaining
outer set; a unique private optimum is not guaranteed.

Add whole-field regret ranges over D's possible maximizers, with numerical
allowances. If the whole-field optimum remains possible for D, the lower bound
on the loss from private optimization remains zero. Report active-idle and best
constant comparisons as well as no-command comparisons. Active idle is not an
activity-matched null, and its gain is not a causal allocation of entropy to the
selector.

## Stage 1: one integrated physical world

Start from the spatial access/control modules and the paid-selector architecture.
The required additions concern the causal consequences of access, not arbitrary
population size or more action labels.

- S has an energy store, apparatus, native maintenance and an independently
  present stochastic resource opportunity. Access to that opportunity can fund
  costed repair/construction of the access mechanism itself.
- D can invest in its own productive apparatus, fund assistance to S, transfer
  stored resources in either direction, and apply paid movement-only constraint.
- The constrained route has a real hazard and a real downstream opportunity.
  The same intervention can avert immediate loss and impede later access.
- S's output can physically supply an input used by D. Dependence is implemented
  through explicit material/energy channels, not a bonus for coexistence or a
  multiplier rewarding two functioning parties. Basal bypasses provide regimes
  with weak dependence and alternatives.
- Maintenance consumes resources; damage changes the repair/continuation graph.
  Include repairable impairment and a declared irreversible loss route. Do not
  substitute lower stock for lost capability.
- Where a function can be relocated, copied or replaced by D, that alternative
  must pay its construction, transport and delay costs. Do not establish a
  universal preference for plurality by making S indispensable by definition.

Keep S's native controller fixed in the initial assay. Bilateral strategic choice
and defence follow separately. These are functional controller models, not an
empirical identification of phenomenal consciousness.

Do not add all these state coordinates blindly. Specify the smallest integrated
adapter, count its full states and observation blocks, and profile complete
history and marginal queries before selecting a sweep. No silent inventory
reduction, omitted background or altered grain to force a computation to fit.

## Comparisons and operating range

Use a modest policy class that can express maintaining, assisting, exploiting
and restricting under different observed conditions. Enumerate the complete
class where feasible. A larger screened or locally optimized class must remain
labelled as such. Changes in sensing, memory and policy execution are physical
architecture changes; model knowledge available to the oracle is distinct from
information available to the embodied controller.

Begin by varying three mechanisms, separately before combining them:

1. How much productive access S's channel opens, relative to its construction
   and running costs.
2. How replaceable and recoverable that channel is, including damage to its
   repair capacity.
3. How much of D's later production actually depends on S's output, with bypass
   and substitution controls.

Physical source prescriptions and resource budgets are matched between policy
comparisons. Realized absorption, fuel use and event activity may differ and are
reported. Map material-return break-evens without assuming they determine
private entropy preferences. Selector-rate/cost sensitivities check that an
interesting ranking is not confined to one actuator setting.

At every horizon report whole-field maximizers, D/S possible maximizers, physical
action maps, unconditional access/function/recovery/loss profiles, owned-energy
accounts and actual transfer direction. Structural Omega queries explain the
comparison; none becomes an additional optimization reward.

Finite-history lushness decides each declared finite-window comparison.
Stationary rates, offsets, expected jumps and temporal coefficients explain
persistence and crossover behaviour. Use windows on both sides of observed
repayment/recovery times. Report temporal or adequate-state-grain dependence
instead of selecting a favourable resolution.

## What would change the manuscript

The primary question is not whether a protective action beats an idle action:

    What does private optimization choose over productive alternatives,
    and what does the whole field choose over those same implementations?

Possible findings include a private/whole disagreement, agreement on productive
exchange, conditional concentration, or continued unresolved private choices.
None is required by the design. Evaluate substantive theory claims against the
physical mechanism actually instantiated; a missed guessed winner is not a
violation of the principle.

This integrated assay would connect evidence that currently lives in separate
fixtures: paid access, recursive enabling, persistence, harm, resource control
and the subject distinction. It would support scoped statements about how
lushness arbitrates them, rather than another statement that organization can
sometimes generate breadth.

## Next large holes after this assay

1. **Plural ecology versus concentration:** build on the same modules, keeping
   resource budgets and functional repertoires comparable. Permit a concentrated
   system to reproduce multiple useful functions; make any advantage of
   distributed organization arise from physical interactions and costs.
2. **Uncertainty and correction:** replay well-understood oracle worlds with
   imperfect observation/model knowledge, paid inspection, reversible and
   irreversible commitments, and an information/correction channel. Compare
   against the oracle rather than attributing epistemic errors to the objective.
3. **Transfer beyond the toy family:** use an independently specified reaction
   network or a spatial substrate to test whether the enabling/control findings
   survive a different construction. A richer same-law disordered preparation
   remains part of this transfer question.

Quantum/QFT characterization, phenomenal valence and a universal
resolution-independent magnitude remain distinct theoretical questions. These
classical experiments can strengthen v4.1's mechanism and conditional ethical
claims without purporting to close those questions.

## Architecture for later ML

Keep the physical generator/sampler, controller interface, subject observation
maps, objective evaluator, optimizer and reporting modules separate. Exact
enumeration, heuristic search and a future learned policy can then use the same
physical actions and evaluator. Retain exact small cases as benchmarks.

A future ML backend must declare observations, physical memory and execution
costs, training/search budget and held-out physical regimes. Novelty bonuses,
action entropy, welfare terms or other surrogate rewards do not silently stand
in for lushness. Policy approximation, model uncertainty and objective behaviour
must remain separately diagnosable. ML is recorded as a future ambition; it is
not needed to execute the next proposed oracle assay.
