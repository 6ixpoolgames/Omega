# Paid extraction: material feasibility and continuation consequences

All twelve cases completed and passed numerical and resource-ledger validation.
The module implements extraction now without a population expansion or another
physical coordinate. Its first result answers the budget question: a paid
transfer can leave D materially better off than matched passive transfer.

## The material return is real within this adapter

Six extraction settings span handling bills 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75 and catalytic
assistance 0.2 and 2. Their paid-bank differences are positive at every tested
horizon (16, 64, 256 and 1024). This account includes net native capture,
resource transfer after handling, net upkeep and bank-to-apparatus/device
conversion. Direct charge-bath support is recorded separately. An independent
check closes paid-bank balance plus direct bath exchange against D's endpoint
bank change; the complete owned-energy account closes too.

At T256 and cadence 0.25, the moderate-assistance paid-bank advantage falls from
0.0305 energy units at bill 0.25 to 0.0272 at 0.5 and 0.0096 at 0.75. D's owned
energy is also higher. With stronger assistance, those paid-bank advantages are
0.0502, 0.0449 and 0.0176. No budget crossing was located in these three bills;
this establishes a feasible return, not the exact maximum affordable price.

For the low-cost moderate setting, additional forward extraction is 2.1368
events by T256 and additional reverse extraction is 1.8805. Reverse transfers
are substantial and cannot be ignored. After the handling loss and the small
change in basal cooperation, additional net transfer to D is 0.1909 energy
units. D captures 0.0611 fewer native resource units itself, yet its paid-bank
and endpoint owned-energy advantages remain positive. This is more than counting
a gross receipt while omitting the bill.

Stationary net transfer to D and the stationary paid-bank advantage are also
positive in all six extraction settings. These are maintained flux differences,
not unlimited growth of finite stock. Extra income can support turnover and
heat loss. The common source/bath prescription does not imply equal accepted
external work or equal heat flux; both remain in the report.

## Material appropriation and breadth need not have opposite signs

All 72 extraction-versus-passive horizon/cadence comparisons have positive
whole-field breadth and certified positive D and S marginal-history differences.
At T256, S nevertheless has less owned energy and less average working
apparatus in every extraction setting. For bill 0.25 and moderate assistance,
the whole gains 19.326 bits, D gains between 17.688 and 32.130 bits, and S gains
between 16.114 and 28.198 bits, while S's owned energy falls by 0.0226.

Thus extraction's material benefit to D does not reproduce the earlier
resource-blocking experiment's D-positive/S-negative/whole-negative breadth
pattern. Coupled transfers themselves change possible physical trajectories.
The underlying physical decline in S's stock/function and the increase in S's
history breadth are both results, not interchangeable descriptions. No activity
or randomness has been subtracted to reconcile them.

This is not yet a test of confinement, suffering, consent or resistance. Neither
compartment is a demonstrated conscious valuer, and the world retains thermal
reconstruction rather than irreversible population death. Its contribution is
a physically accounted access/transfer mechanism for subsequent tests.

## The alternatives matter

At bill 0.5, productive ordinary apparatus gives D a larger paid-bank advantage
than either extraction strength: 0.3498 by T256, against 0.0272 and 0.0449.
Its whole-field gain is 26.156 bits. More private resource income is not
automatically more breadth: the moderate sham gives 37.965 extra whole bits
while its paid-bank advantage is negative, -0.0249. At that horizon/cadence,
the sham's D gain is certified greater than moderate extraction's D gain;
ordinary versus moderate extraction remains unresolved for D. The strong
extraction coefficient is a separate kinetic-strength contrast, not evidence
that D prefers extraction when every alternative has matched performance.

The nominal cooperation channel also gives positive D transfer in this regime:
its physical reverse runs strongly enough that the net flow is toward D. It
therefore does not instantiate net cooperative donation to S here. This does
not alter or invalidate its physical kernel; it limits the intended comparator.
A later source/affinity or buffering design must establish actual net help
before using that arm to compare cooperation against appropriation.

Sham and productive controls are not matched on realized fuel, accepted work or
event count. No unique causal allocation of marginal-history entropy to
enforcement, income or heat is claimed. The complete signed map is retained.

## Physical and engineering scope

The new module composes reversible contact-transfer rules with the existing
native chemistry. D requires working apparatus and device for catalytic
assistance, while native basal transfer remains in every arm. A forward
extraction prepays c and receives one resource unit; its reverse restores that
physical change and absorbs the handling loss from the bath. All reactions
remain enabled in dark detailed-balance tests. Energy and declared reference
density determine their equilibrium ratio; the catalyst changes both rates.

These are mechanism contrasts between fixed catalytic roles. No embedded
controller chooses roles, and no S defence or mode-transition cost is modelled.
Payment and receipt are a single coarse coupled reaction, not resolved transport
intermediates. Population, private buffering and stateful appropriation/control
remain future composable modules. The ten-to-one suggestion is not needed for
this first demonstration of a positive material return.

The 9,984-state map finished in about 446 seconds with two workers. Individual
cases took 60–94 seconds and peaked below 378 MiB. Source/configuration snapshots,
checksummed receipts, compact maps and code-only archives are retained; no full
transition kernel is saved. The numerical snapshot is identified in RESULTS.md.
The later API-default correction only supplies the already-declared compatible
fractional grid when parameters are omitted; scientific workers supplied their
parameters explicitly. Eleven transfer tests plus the previous 53 focused
tests pass. The largest checked science residual is approximately 1.64e-9.
