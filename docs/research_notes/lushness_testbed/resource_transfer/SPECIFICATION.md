# Paid extraction module: first physical slice

## Terminology update — 16 October 2026

The reaction names describe forward stoichiometry, not measured net direction.
In the published regime the nominal cooperation pathway ran net toward D.
Call it a reversible donor pathway, not evidence of cooperative help. The sham
is **reversible bank/bath exchange**, distinct from friction's driven debit.
The adapter now accepts any positive bill that the bank can accommodate,
including bills of one or above. Original configurations and maps are unchanged.
The [funded-pump extension](../c5_upgrade/SPECIFICATION.md) resolves a separate
physical fuel store charged from D's bank; net direction remains measured.

15 October 2026. User authorized extraction now to address D's material budget.
The ten-to-one suggestion was a possible economy of scale, not a requirement to
add population. Population remains deferred.

Use the existing shared-resource, embodied two-compartment world. Add paired
contact reactions without adding state coordinates. A forward extraction
requires S's stored resource and D's prepaid handling fuel c: S loses one unit,
D pays c and receives one unit, and c is released to the bath. The actual reverse
transfers resource back and absorbs c from the bath. It has the same catalytic
coefficient and reference-density/energy factors. Reverse flux is retained.

Cooperation transfers one unit from D to S while D also pays c. A paired sham
exchanges c with the bath without transferring resource. Basal coefficients of
all three pathways are present in every arm. Working D apparatus and its device
boost the selected pathway only while the two compartments are colocated.
An ordinary arm uses the device material for native D capture instead.
Both source and bath remain common. All new reactions stay enabled in the dark
equilibrium check; there is no separate unpriced control affinity.

Transfer and handling form one coarse coupled reaction. The affordability gate
requires the bill before forward extraction, but payment and receipt are not
separate time-resolved intermediates. A future transport/actuation module can
resolve those intermediates and their failure opportunities explicitly.

The shared source maintains nonequilibrium. Changing symmetric coefficients
does not guarantee any direction of net transfer or profitability. This is
direct stored-resource extraction, not the earlier four-state packet proposal.
There is no private store added and no confinement or S defence mechanism.

## Readouts and interpretation

Complete finite sampled-history lushness is primary. Report actual D and S
marginal-history conditioning bounds, without ranking their midpoints. Rates,
expected physical jumps and temporal finite terms remain diagnostics.

Record gross extraction/cooperation, reverse transfers, gross handling bills,
reverse refunds, net transfer to each bank, native capture, construction,
upkeep, external work, heat, stocks and functioning. Per-family changes in each
subject's owned energy include bank, apparatus, device and trap potential.
Their integrated sum must match the subject's endpoint energy change. The full
first law must close independently. These counters add no physical state.

Distinguish a positive transfer margin from a positive complete matched D
resource balance and from a private lushness advantage. A finite bank's
stationary stock cannot increase forever: limiting net storage zero is not
evidence that productive income vanishes. The full family account and sustained
flows show what the income funds. Bath support and extra accepted external work
must remain explicit, rather than attributed to S's production.

No desired whole-field sign is imposed. A profitable transfer can reduce total
breadth, increase it or leave it unresolved. A private optimum is established
only within evaluated alternatives. The sham matches the catalytic parameter,
not realized fuel or activity; matched-flux calibration is a later comparison.
All randomness counts. No survival, domination or fairness rewards are added.
