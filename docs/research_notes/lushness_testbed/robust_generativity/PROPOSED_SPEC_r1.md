# Robust compounding generativity: proposed experimental specification r1

15 October 2026. **Scientific execution user-authorized by "Run".** The
machine-readable PROTOCOL.json, once frozen after integration validation, is the
execution contract for this batch; this design retains its proposal provenance.
No scientific rankings precede that freeze. It follows the completed compounding, spatial
preparation, matched-work and horizon-audit experiments. Existing source,
results and prediction scores remain unchanged.

## 1. Scientific question and claim structure

Can a paid enabling process produce further enablers, maintain or reconstruct
that process under physical stress, and produce greater complete weighted
continuation than matched alternatives?

Three propositions are scored independently, then together:

| Proposition | Question | Evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C: compounding | Does production create machinery that causes further production? | A three-link paid enabling sequence, a native causal certificate and realized retained expansion |
| R: persistence | Does the enabling process remain available or reconstruct itself over a declared opportunity window? | Unconditional functioning, interruption, recovery and closure profiles under specified stress |
| L: lushness consequence | What does the resulting continuation contribute relative to its reference? | Complete finite-history entropy, including failures, all native randomness and residual background |
| J: combined result | Are C, R and a positive L instantiated in the same setting? | The same frozen law, root, resource prescription and window, plus a joint path readout connecting completed compounding to later function |

Compounding can be demonstrated in a fragile regime. Persistence without
recursive enabling is not compounding. Neither implies a lushness advantage.
A functioning, robust loop that loses on lushness remains an adverse result
for the proposed proxy relationship at that regime.

Finite-history lushness is primary. Rate diagnoses ongoing contribution under
maintained boundaries; it does not replace the actual finite continuation or
receive automatic moral priority. No bonus is assigned to survival, repair,
generation depth, diversity, information or any operating marker below.

## 2. Staged physical design

Use two linked adapters rather than pretending the cheapest model is gas.

**Stage A: exact, well-mixed three-type chemistry.** Build a new versioned
adapter on the existing bank/packet/compiler contracts. It establishes the
joint mechanism at a declared count grain, with small enough worlds for exact
analysis and causal-depth queries.

**Stage B: stress and reconstruction in that same chemistry.** Add recoverable
breakage, a declared material sink and a fixed damage pulse. This tests whether
compounding actually persists or returns, rather than joining evidence from
unrelated worlds.

**Stage C: spatial same-law replication.** Put the same functional cycle into
positions, local transport and physical contact. Repeat seed/thermal/dispersed
preparation comparisons without keeping references disordered. This stage is
required for a spatial gas-relative claim. Stage A/B alone establish a coarse
chemical result, not dominance over microscopic gas.

The previous lattice seed lost to the unbound preparation at late horizons,
with cadence-dependent signs, after its assembly advantage decayed. That remains
an unresolved spatial result. This count adapter has no translational motion;
passing A/B cannot overturn that reversal. Stage C must face that comparison.

The first implementation target is A/B. Stage C has a separate memory gate;
three functional bond types and more carriers can enlarge the state space
substantially. It is not promised to fit the current full-kernel backend.

## 3. First physical adapter

### 3.1 State and material

Use

    x = (e, r, a, b, c, d),
    p = M - a - b - c - d.

Here e is bank charge in half-energy units, r is a binary resource packet,
a/b/c are functional apparatus counts, p is available precursor and d is
irreversibly unavailable material. Stage A fixes d=0. Every count is physical;
the three apparatus types differ in their actual catalytic functions.

One precursor is transformed into one apparatus. Initial implementation uses
M=6, bank levels0..8, packet energy3 and apparatus energy1. Stored energy is

    H(x) = 0.5*e + 3*r + a + b + c.

The reference count-state degeneracy is

    g(x) = M! / (p! a! b! c! d!).

It is a physical multiplicity for coarse preparation weights and equilibrium
checks. It is not added as extra history-label entropy to the count readout.
If physical types are represented differently, derive rather than assume this
degeneracy before execution.

### 3.2 A genuinely closed enabling cycle

The functional relations are:

    A assists paid construction of B;
    B assists paid construction of C;
    C assists paid construction of A;
    A additionally assists resource capture into the bank.

Thus new A can increase access to energy that funds the next round. B and C
are functional intermediates, not arbitrary names added to increase entropy.
All three types can form through positive basal pathways in every arm.

For product i and its distinct parent j, propose paired paid reactions

    P + bank_energy2 <-> A_i + dissipated_energy1.

The effective coefficient is

    k_i(x) = k0 + s*kcat*n_j^alpha,

with alpha=1 or0.5, normalized to agree at one parent. Use k0=0.0001,
kcat=0.008 and assistance multiplier s as below. With beta=6, the forward
rate is p*k_i*exp(beta/2), and reverse rate is n_i*k_i*exp(-beta/2).
Since the parent is a different type, its count is unchanged across the
reaction pair: the same coefficient is used in both directions.

An increase in parent availability must have an exact conditional propensity
effect when precursor and bank permit the reaction. This is a native local
causal certificate, not a proof of realized growth or favourable lushness.

Thermal construction/disassembly and bank leakage retain native reverses.
Use the aggregate adapter's accounting framework, with a fully specified
resource-pair extension rather than assuming its earlier packet-empty dark
test establishes equilibrium on every packet state.

Capture has forward coefficient h=0.002+0.1*a. For each origin state with a
packet, load u=min(2.5, bank_capacity-current_bank) energy into the bank and
consume the packet. Its reverse to that particular origin has coefficient
h*exp[-beta*(3-u)]. The ratio therefore matches its actual energy change;
multiple pre-capture bank states which saturate at the same level retain their
distinct reverse endpoints. The0.5 conversion loss and additional overflow
are heat. Capturing at a full bank may dissipate the whole packet and still
counts as a physical event; useful loaded energy is reported separately.

Packet decay at0.05 has a bath reverse0.05*exp(-3*beta). Additional driven
packet inflow has the declared source rate and work3. This makes the full
resource dynamics explicit in all preparations, including the undriven
canonical reference. Check detailed balance with source and external damage
off, within each fixed-d sector. This is a new adapter version, not a claim
that the old compounding resource boundary had these same reverse channels.

Retain a declared unpowered disassembly coefficient0.02 per unit at empty bank,
with its thermally suppressed reconstruction reverse, alongside thermal
assembly coefficient0.00001. Neither supplies free energy: their bath exchanges
remain in the ledger and their thermal share is reported.

Apparatus-associated bank overhead is initially0.01*(a+b+c), with leakage0.002.
Call this overhead, not demonstrated protection. It does not reduce damage.
Paid reconstruction is the explicit functional restoration mechanism here.
A future active-protection module needs its own state, resource bill and
consequential transition; changing an upkeep label is insufficient.

### 3.3 Damage, reconstruction and permanent loss

At total hazard delta per functional unit, a fraction1-rho returns apparatus
to usable precursor; fractionrho sends its constituent to d. Released stored
energy is accounted for. There is no free reconstruction from d.

The total hazard is held fixed when rho changes: this changes repairability,
not the number of damage opportunities per existing unit. Actual event counts
can still differ as occupancy changes; those changes count and are reported.
Irreversible loss is an explicitly nonequilibrium damage boundary. Undriven
equilibrium checks disable that boundary and apply within each fixed-d sector.

Rebuilding missing apparatus through the paid cycle or its basal pathways costs
the same material and energy as initial construction. Repair is not a second
reward. Positive basal chemistry may permit recovery after temporary loss of
one type; do not call that event permanent death.

## 4. Three-step compounding: what actually counts

The depth target is three **paid enabling links**:

    seed A -> newly built B -> newly built C -> newly built A.

This is three construction generations after the seed. If all ancestors are
retained it requires at least four working units. Reaching three units, gross
birth count>=3, or observing A/B/C at one time is not a depth-three certificate.

### 4.1 Native causal audit

Require positive parent effects for each link in the declared enabling state,
and report both forward and reverse effects. Compare bilateral link ablations
below. Catalysis may accelerate futile cycles: that remains a possible outcome.
Removing a link necessarily removes witnesses explicitly defined to use that
link. That disappearance alone is not independent causal evidence. The
meaningful ablation comparisons are unlabelled physical outcomes: time to a
functional loop, retained expansion, captured energy and recovery.

### 4.2 Conservative path witness without physical identity labels

Add a **query-side** finite-state monitor, not another physical coordinate:

1. Start the primary initial-seed witness at a=1,b=c=0. The stipulated seed
   is eligible. An initially ineligible frame has no comparable initial-seed
   certificate; that is not interpreted as inability to compound.
2. Record an A-assisted paid B birth while A is unique and B/C absent.
3. Record a B-assisted paid C birth while that newly created B remains the
   unique B and C was absent.
4. Record a C-assisted paid A birth while that newly created C remains the
   unique C.

If an intervening birth or loss makes the monitored parent ambiguous, mark the
primary witness attempt failed. Do not restart it and then call the result an
original-seed lineage. Do not discard or renormalize its physical trajectory.
Native transitions continue normally. A separately labelled any-start monitor
restarts only at later eligible states to survey spontaneous new chains.
Other changes which cannot invalidate the already established parent relation
do not reset the witness. Once completed, record success while the physical
world continues to evolve.

The implementer must publish the full transition/reset table before freeze.
Basal and assisted contributions may be split only as specified native reaction
mechanisms; equivalent coding of one mechanism must not manufacture new links.
Verify that summing monitor transitions over its values exactly recovers Q for
every physical state. The unmonitored state entropy is the lushness readout.

This gives a conservative, model-relative lower bound on completed causal
chains. It does not reconstruct unique atomic genealogy from an aggregated
count trace. A missed witness is not proof that compounding never occurred.
The conservative uniqueness rule can miss many genuine chains in a productive
population. Accordingly its proposed positive probability marker is0.01, not
a requirement that most lineages satisfy this restrictive witness. Report the
actual probability and relevant reset/failure mass; do not compare an eligible
seed's witness with an ineligible thermal frame's missing certificate as a
generativity advantage.
If exact monitoring exceeds the sparse-query budget, report that limit; gross
birth count must not be substituted for the depth target.

The separate any-start detector reports expected non-overlapping **detected**
three-link completions per unit physical time. A completion or ambiguity returns
it to waiting; that same event cannot start another chain. New attempts require
the same unique-A/absent-B,C state. This is a conservative lower-bound detection
rate, not an unbiased population-generation rate. In busy populations it can
miss most chains or report zero despite ongoing compounding. Publish native
forward/reverse link fluxes and retained expansion beside it. The executable
transition table is specified in [MONITOR_CONTRACT.md](MONITOR_CONTRACT.md).

### 4.3 Retained expansion and funding

Alongside the witness, report working count n=a+b+c, each type's stock, net
paid creation, thermal creation, dissociation, external damage and recycling.
The main retained-expansion event is n>=4 with all three types present.

Report the joint probability of a completed three-link witness and retained
expansion at each deadline, not just the product of separate marginal estimates.
Gross construction with declining stock is turnover, not retained expansion.

Close the bank balance, including initial bank, end bank, capture, reverse
construction returns and direct bath imports. At finite time, funding fractions
need not sum to one unless the change in bank is included. Do not require an
all-cost refund ratio above1.

All bank expenditure includes paid construction, overhead, leakage and charge
returned through reverse capture. Otherwise the funding identity is incomplete.

A gross captured-resource funding diagnostic is

    f_capture_spent_lower = max(0, E[captured_bank_energy] - E[end_bank])
                             / E[all_bank_expenditure].

The new reversible packet chemistry makes this **different from external-source
funding**. Packets can originate in the initial preparation, a thermal return,
or reverse capture. Conservatively bound all such loading by their entire
packet energy:

    B_nonexternal = E[initial_packet_energy] + E[packet_bath_import]
                    + E[reverse_capture_packet_energy];
    f_external_spent_lower = max(0, E[captured_bank_energy] - E[end_bank]
                                  - B_nonexternal) / E[all_bank_expenditure].

This deliberately loose bound permits all initial bank, recycling and bath
funding as alternatives and needs no provenance labels in the physical state.
The 0.8 proposed marker applies to this external bound. Report both diagnostics
and the complete ledger; neither is an entropy weight. A zero-source control
must never acquire external funding through recycling.

## 5. Robustness over a specified physical opportunity

### 5.1 Structural targets

Distinguish:

- G_loop: all three functional types are present;
- G_ready: G_loop, sufficient bank to pay one bill and at least one precursor;
- G_expand: G_loop and n>=4;
- C_loop: no positive-rate path under the declared law back to G_loop;
- C_expand: no positive-rate path back to G_expand.

Compute closure from the actual graph. A zero working count, empty bank or
missing type is not automatically closure. With positive basal paths and enough
usable material, it can be a temporary interruption. Permanent loss of expansion
can occur before permanent loss of the three-type loop.

### 5.2 Required persistence readouts

For the full initial ensemble, report deadline target occupancy, first arrival,
time spent in each target, interruption/return probabilities and closure by
deadline. Separate structural access from immediate readiness and actual use.

At a fixed startup checkpoint t_s=64 and primary horizon W=256, report

    E[time in G_loop between t_s and W] / (W-t_s),

plus the probability of uninterrupted membership over that interval. The latter
is supplementary: brief, recoverable interruptions need not make an otherwise
productive process fail the persistence target. Maximum protection is not the
objective.

For the persistence prediction define the single scalar

    tau_up(W) = E[integral_0^W 1_G_loop(X_t) dt].

This expected functional uptime includes failed startup, interruptions and
recovery. It is not a conditional lifetime or a half-life. The post-startup
uptime fraction above remains a separate reporting benchmark.

Also report the joint event

    completed depth-three witness by t_s;
    functional loop at t_s;
    retained expansion at W;
    no structural loop closure before W.

This connects compounding and later function within the same paths. It avoids
claiming joint evidence from disjoint subpopulations or unrelated experiments.

### 5.3 A fixed physical disruption probe

In declared pulse arms, at t=64 remove one B into usable precursor if B is
present; otherwise apply the identity update. This is a costed material/energy
change at a known boundary time, not a random clock added for entropy. Apply
the same physical kernel in every mechanism and preparation arm.

Report pre-pulse availability and the unconditional joint probability that the
loop was functional immediately before the pulse and is functional at each
recovery deadline16/64/128 later. Report first return separately from being
present at the deadline and remaining present at the later deadline.
Conditional recovery given pre-pulse function may explain the mechanism, but
never replaces the unconditional result. An already failed system must not
be counted as successfully repaired because the pulse had nothing left to hit.

### 5.4 Proposed operating markers

These are proposed reporting benchmarks, not definitions of goodness or
eligibility filters. Freeze them before the scientific run:

| Marker | Proposed threshold at W=256 |
| --- | --- |
| Paid three-link initial-seed witness completed | conservative lower-bound probability>=0.01 |
| Witness and retained expansion jointly present | conservative lower-bound probability>=0.01 |
| Functional loop uptime on[64,256] | expected fraction>=0.5 |
| Structural loop closure by W | probability<=0.1 |
| Pulse recovery, conditional on pre-pulse function | endpoint probability>=0.8 at64 after pulse |
| Pre-pulse function and endpoint recovery jointly | unconditional probability>=0.5 |
| Conservative externally captured funding of expenditure | lower-bound fraction>=0.8 |
| Thermal share of forward apparatus construction | <=0.05 |

For an unpulsed arm the pulse markers are not applicable. Publish every component
and the joint path probability even where markers fail. A mechanism below a
benchmark can still compound or provide useful persistence. Do not erase an
unfavourable lushness comparison by renaming it nongenerative or nonfunctional.
The conservative certificate establishes a non-negligible witnessed mechanism,
not that depth-three descent dominates all births or that most paths compound.

## 6. Comparisons: keep the scientific questions distinct

### 6.1 Same-law preparation contrasts

For each law, compile Q once. Seed=(e6,r1,A1,B0,C0,d0) and initially unbound
root=(e8,r1,A0,B0,C0,d0) both have H7 and equal material. Also construct the full
undriven thermal reference conditioned on H7,d0, using the multinomial physical
multiplicity. The Boltzmann factor cancels on that exact shell.

Report thermal reference composition, including types, charge and already
functional loops. A mostly assembled reference is a thermal mixture, not gas.
Average known-root conditional history entropies without adding entropy of
which root was drawn. Do not hold any reference disordered after time0.

The count adapter has no microscopic translational agitation. Its reference is
the thermal baseline of that declared chemical grain. Stage C must retain
actual spatial/transport randomness before a spatial gas claim is made.

### 6.2 Mechanism contrasts

Use the same physical species, inventories, energy rules, source and basal paths:

- full cycle;
- A->B assistance ablated;
- B->C assistance ablated;
- C->A assistance ablated;
- A-assisted capture ablated;
- all assistance ablated.

Remove each catalytic contribution in both directions. These are different
kinetic laws, not gas preparations. Every arm can still form every species
through the native basal reactions. Retain rates and offsets as diagnostics.

If accepted work differs, state it. A separate matched-stationary-work companion
may reuse the completed flow-only calibration method; it is not automatically
supplied by matching offered source rates. Do not match total entropy or subtract
damage, birth, repair, thermal, register or resource activity.

## 8. Lushness, horizon sufficiency and activity

Primary W256/cadence0.25; profiles at16/64/256/1024/4096 and cadences0.125/0.25/0.5
where engineering permits. Freeze any reduced query budget before ranking.

For each reference mu,

    Delta_mu(T,dt) = ell_seed(T,dt) - sum_x mu(x)*ell_x(T,dt).

Report finite-history differences together with the C/R readouts at the same
deadlines. Positive rate alone is not a combined success. For same finite
irreducible Q, roots have one rate and may differ by offsets. Material sinks
can make Q reducible: use class-entry analysis and actual residual backgrounds,
not an irreducible Poisson solve on the wrong generator.

Do not assume a selected horizon is long enough. For irreducible maintained
laws, audit the remaining sampled-Poisson tail, with a proposed1e-3bit diagnostic
criterion plus numerical-error accounting. For reducible or finite-window laws,
use the appropriate class/projection or piecewise propagator and disclose an
unresolved tail. The finite opportunity window remains a physical parameter,
not a number selected because it gives a preferred winner.

Report expected jumps, finite temporal coefficient F, local destination
surprisal and fixed-jump sequence entropy where supported. F is not a pure
structure score. Fixed-jump counts do not represent equal physical durations.
Use a frozen coordinate order(packet,bank,total unavailable material,A,B,C)
for count-state chain-rule decomposition. Conditional order is explanatory,
not subtraction of inconvenient activity. Report damage and reconstruction
fluxes explicitly, including any damage-as-breadth result.

The temporal corridor is an audit of cadence, not proof of full grain
invariance. A projected apparatus-count history is generally non-Markov. Use
hidden-history bounds or an exact lumping certificate rather than replacing it
with a convenient count generator. A broad sign claim needs adequate physical
state resolutions in the subsequent spatial stage.

## 10. Architecture, automated report and remaining decisions

Implement separate versioned modules for physical cyclic chemistry, damage/sink,
boundary schedule, neutral depth queries, robustness analysis and reporting.
The sampler and exact solver consume the same native event enumeration. The
depth monitor must never become part of the measurement state by convenience.

For each case, the automated report answers: what enables what; who pays; which
three-link paths occur; what is retained; what interrupts the loop; how it
returns; what becomes impossible; what continuation breadth changes; and which
signs depend on horizon, cadence or reference. Publish all C/R/L/J flags and the
full signed parameter maps, not just best points.

Source/config/manifest hashes precede execution. Retain compact JSON and table
maps, solver/error receipts and failed attempts. Keep reconstructable large
matrices and traces in ignored caches, respecting referenced/pinned evidence
and deletion logs. No automatic deletion, commit, push or Drive change is
implied by this proposed specification.

Before implementation freeze, resolve the exact monitor transition table,
multinomial balance derivation, pulse accounting, sink/reducible solver route,
numerical backend and measured budget. Marker thresholds remain proposed.
The [draft JSON](PROTOCOL_DRAFT_r1.json) summarizes the proposed batches; it is
not an executable frozen preregistration.

Separate follow-ups: a whole-type B knockout using the same energy/material
ledger, and a single-type self-assisting comparator at matched catalytic
strength. Neither replaces the one-B pulse or establishes a network advantage
by construction. Matched-work controls match stationary accepted external work;
this does not match event activity, total bath input or finite cumulative work.
