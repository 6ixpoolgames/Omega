# Lushness optimizer test bed: foundation contract

**Draft for design audit — 13 October 2026. No new world or experiments implemented.**

Initial implementation direction: [BioNetGen substrate specification](INITIAL_SPEC_r1.md),
[measurement contract](MEASUREMENT_r1.md), and
[experiment workflow](experiment-workflow.md). These are design drafts.
User clarification: specific lineage is deferred; population may be coarse.
Cross-scale agency, ecology and composition remain explicit expansion targets.

The governing foundation is v4 and the originator's latest clarifications.
This work continues in the original Omega repository on its existing active branch. This contract specifies the intended test-bed requirements;
it does not silently declare the old manuscript current or freeze a new law.

## Purpose and governing spine

Characterize a physical present and its native weighted continuation; search
implemented controllers that alter it; inspect the structural consequences;
test whether the resulting judgments support the conditions for possible value.
The ethical bridge is value requires valuers plus conditional normativity.
Alpha–Omega is the interpretive layer. A bounded optimizer and its interface
are experimental processes within the field, rather than its definition.

Classical weighted path breadth is the initial candidate readout. Structure
affects that readout through the lawful continuations it enables or obstructs.
No independent survival, cooperation, generativity or recovery bonus enters
the objective. Surveying structure is nevertheless mandatory: an equal score
can conceal different physical organizations and ethical consequences.

## 1. A sufficient, relational present

A present must retain every modeled variable needed to determine continuation:
physical components and supports; material and energy stores; actual bonds,
couplings and access paths; apparatus and internal records; controller state;
environmental boundary state and incident drive. Shared-resource constraints
and cross-component correlations belong to the joint state.

Relations must affect the native law. Structure cannot be a display-only graph
or an unverified label such as "cooperative", "damaged" or "generative".
Fixed topology is acceptable for the first fixture only when explicitly
declared; the contract allows components and connections to form and dissolve.
Common-pool mixing is a stated approximation, not spatial transport evidence.

Component names are coordinates. A passive renaming transports state, law,
couplings, controllers and observations together. Distinct physical occurrences
remain distinct even when their descriptions or residual laws are isomorphic.
Bookkeeping occurrence/lineage IDs cannot become extra probabilistic states.

The exact present is sufficient for future prediction. Original-root developments
remain available to the declared history readout. Specific historical lineage
tracking is deferred in the initial substrate; an optional survey can add it
later. Ancestry becomes a dynamical variable only where physical records or memory
require it.
Inactive, disassembled, replaced and reconstructed are distinct physical cases.

The implementation separates three records: (i) native physical state consumed
by the law, (ii) native physical development consumed by the breadth readout,
and (iii) query-side event/lineage provenance consumed by the survey. A survey
annotation cannot change transition weights or create a history alternative.
Re-rooting preserves an earlier query's archive while passing only the new
sufficient physical state into the next future calculation. Ordered enabling/
recovery witnesses cite their native intermediate states and resource residuals.

## 2. Native developments and composition

Every update declares its physical support, preconditions, resulting state,
native probability or rate, duration/schedule, resource exchanges and residual
conditions. Dependencies come from the actual law. Joint resource conflicts,
enabling, inhibition and boundary effects must be evaluated jointly.

Preserve physical intermediate states and distinct developments when they
matter to the modeled history. Aggregate outcomes that are only alternative
descriptions of the same physical outcome. Extra action labels, artificial
checkpoints and permutations of commuting bookkeeping operations cannot create
breadth. Declared physical timing must remain available.

The reusable law is defined at subsequent reachable presents, not just the root.
Composition passes actual remaining resources, apparatus, records, controller
state and correlations into later operations. Re-rooting changes the query;
it does not award ongoing credit to historical organization that has vanished.

## 3. Gradient and environmental boundaries

The first fixture has a declared usable-energy supply, finite internal stores,
capture pathways and dissipation/escape boundaries. Distinguish incident supply,
successful capture, internal storage and expenditure. Different preparations
are compared under matched law, matter inventory and incident drive.

Use physical units or explicitly stipulated coarse units. Fuel/energy accounting
alone does not establish a reservoir temperature, free-energy potential or
local detailed balance. A temperature/chemical-potential extension must supply
those ingredients. Optional changing drive must include its sufficient boundary
state; controllers receive only physically available information about it.

"Gradient" also has the programme's broader meaning of an oriented difference
that shapes continuation. A numerical lushness sensitivity or literal spatial
gradient requires its own declared function/geometry. It is not an extra force
added to make evolution increase the score.

## 4. The filter is a consequence of the law

Model loss, wear, failed maintenance, dissolution, reproduction, inheritance
where implemented, and the physical conditions for continued organization.
The resulting differential persistence is the filter. It may support generative
integration, sterile replication, domination, or collapse depending on the world.
It must not guarantee the programme's preferred outcome.

Tracking continued function, physical occurrence, copied organization and
lineage are separate surveys. Re-creating a function is not automatically
continuation of the same individual. An unbuilt precursor is not a living
agent. Numerical identity and phenomenality remain adopted/open interpretive
questions rather than facts manufactured by an ID field.

Before launch the fixture supplies operational lifecycle predicates: presence
of bodily/control apparatus, current energetic activity, availability of an
implemented feedback/function path, disassembly events and paid reconstruction.
Energy zero alone never implies death. Surveys report retained physical
organization and restored operation; causal lineage and pattern copying are
separate extension readouts, not requirements to identify individuals at launch.
"Same individual" and "death of a valuer" require a stated adopted account;
where it is absent they remain open instead of being inferred from a slot ID.
Unrecoverability is relative to declared lawful access/resources and deadline;
it is not automatically fundamental information destruction.

This filter is not a durable-only admission rule for the primary breadth
measure. Thermal and stochastic branching remain admitted at the declared
resolution. Any persistence-filtered readout is separately specified, with
its actual physical retention test and information losses disclosed.

## 5. Costs reside in implementation

Every operation has a cost declaration. It identifies material use, energy/work
transfers, dissipation, elapsed time, occupied apparatus, wear, and residual
conditions as applicable. Zero values are allowed when justified or explicitly
idealized; there is no universal positive energy toll per mathematical update.

| Operation | Minimum consequences that must be represented |
|---|---|
| Construction | Precursors, assembly resources/time, intermediate state, failures, residual apparatus |
| Maintenance/repair | Supplies, access route, operating time, restored function and retention after restoration |
| Movement/transport | Connectivity, traversal time, finite delivery capacity and applicable energy/risks |
| Sensing/communication | Physical observation/copy pathway, registers, delay, noise and applicable update/clearance costs |
| Control/computation | Available information, memory/implementation class, operation timing and declared hardware assumptions |
| Reproduction | Paid physical construction, transmitted organization, mutation if implemented, offspring residual state |
| Inactivity | Passing time, continuing exposure/metabolism/wear, and unavailable apparatus/opportunities |
| Interference/destruction | The actual operation, its implementation bill, resulting topology/resources/records and recovery routes |

Maintenance may be intermittent, failure-prone and repairable; it is not merely
a constant score deduction. Destroyed apparatus cannot return through a free
energy refill. Copying, clearing, restoration and replacement have their own
implementations. Energy/material must not appear through a reset or repeated
cycle. Future bill profiles remain joint witnesses, not independently minimized
columns assembled from incompatible procedures.

Opportunity cost comes from occupying time, resources or apparatus and thereby
changing other continuations. It is measured through those consequences, not
an analyst-chosen penalty subtracted from path entropy. Paying an operation's
physical debit and evaluating its altered future does not require an additional
negative reward for the same debit.

## 6. Control and information are physical restrictions

A controller receives a declared observation and retains only implemented
memory. Its proposals must be executable through existing physical couplings.
Conditional action distributions are controller laws, not an invented prior
over policies or possible purposes.

Independent local proposals are not conditioned on hidden joint legality.
Resource contention is resolved by the world with its actual timing, arbitration
and failure consequences. Coordination uses actual shared hardware or signals.
Unmodeled free sensing/computation is an explicit idealized boundary variant;
it cannot silently support claims about physically embedded execution.

The full-information optimizer may supply an analytical reference or bound.
Executing its discovered policy requires a declared implementation. Its design
search cost outside the simulated world and the policy's physical execution
cost inside the world are different quantities.

Every bound names its world law, initial frame, physical readout, horizon or
limiting comparison, controller/memory class, allowed operations, information
access and cost assumptions. A full-information optimum bounds an embedded
class only when that class is demonstrably included in the same modeled
feasible set and objective. If hardware/readouts/laws differ, report a benchmark
contrast rather than assert an upper bound. An optimum within a declared class
is not an optimum over every physically possible controller or ethical proof.

Policy randomization counts through its modeled physical source and outcomes.
Do not universally add entropy of abstract action labels. Where an action record
is physically distinguished, include it; where descriptions coincide, combine
their native weights. The execution/readout contract determines that distinction.

## 7. Survey structure without inserting rewards

The survey must support:

- Component/connection histories, turnover, births, disassembly and physical
  reconstruction, with distinct occurrences and response equivalence separated.
- Actual resource routes, bottlenecks, dependence, redundancy and joint effects.
- Enabling witnesses: construction followed by changed lawful transformation
  or access; later retained use and further construction reported separately.
- Loss profiles: changed future access, capabilities, records and control.
- Recovery profiles: first restoration, retained restoration, physical bills,
  residual deficits and remaining recovery routes.
- Effects on other processes, including delayed consequences and shifted costs.

Native event supports and resource provenance provide observations; they do
not by themselves establish counterfactual causation. An attribution query
specifies a physically valid alternative preparation/intervention, what is
matched, and the law used in each comparison. Matched throughput is an optional
efficiency diagnostic; primary incident-input matching preserves capture gains.

Survey predicates are declared observable structural questions. They neither
admit favored structures to the carrier nor determine a welfare/justice reward.
Functional prerequisites, adopted identity commitments, and ethical hypotheses
must be identified separately when interpreting a policy.

## 8. Measurement and comparison

The primary classical candidate is conditional entropy of the declared physical
future history, with effective breadth its exponential. Specify frame, state/
event grain, timing and horizon; retain complete native weights before readout.
Entropy of a preparation prior is not silently added. Outcome marks or controller
records enter only where supported by the physical model/readout.

Use v4's rate-first/offset-second comparison where its limits exist. Root-based
finite horizons and bounded planners remain visible, with no post-outcome horizon
selection. Different policies can induce different rates; roots in one fixed
irreducible law share a rate. Multichain, periodic and growing cases require
their actual structure. Undeclared discounting or welfare weighting is excluded.

Hold the measure separate from the survey, so failures can be attributed to
representation, dynamics, optimization, observation or proxy adequacy. Normative
agreement is not an implementation test; a score optimum is not ethical proof.

## 9. Extension points with real obligations

Provide separate world/law, controller/execution, measurement, survey, solver
and experiment interfaces. The first classical world must implement the
requirements above. Reusability is assessed by a second fixture, not empty
plugin names. Extend the same interfaces for:

- Spatial transport and boundaries; heterogeneous apparatus/controllers.
- Larger populations and growing assemblies, with paid material/energy supply.
- Additional construction stages, specialization and inherited control structure.
- More realistic sensing, computation, communication and environmental memory.
- Native clocks/causal partial orders and explicitly tested concurrency quotients.
- Quantum state/process and instrument adapters retaining interference rather
  than pretending that a positive classical transition matrix represents it.

Finite cutoff, sampling, compression and numerical budget belong to the
computation manifest, separately from physical boundaries. Lossless compression
requires equivalence of the law and relevant readouts. Exchangeable-count
lumping is not presumed valid for distinct local policies or memories.

## 10. Foundation gates before scientific launch

1. File a complete local MODEL_SPEC, state/update definitions, units,
   observation/control contracts and physical versus numerical boundary map.
2. Enumerate every operation and its cost/idealization declaration; check
   elementary resource closure and finite capacity constraints.
3. Exhibit actual dependence/enabling, loss and paid recovery within one law,
   including failures and residual conditions; these are structural checks.
4. Verify native probabilities, physical outcome aggregation, renaming transport,
   idle/checkpoint neutrality and absence of hidden observation/legality leakage.
5. Establish a same-law stochastic baseline and matched preparation/policy
   comparisons; noise is allowed to win. Keep every negative result.
6. State the optimizer's controller class and numerical certificate/limits;
   certify small instances before approximating larger ones.
7. Preregister scope, adverse findings, costs, horizons and caps before evaluating
   newly designed cases. Keep raw runs local; version source/specs/compact reports.

The first deliverable is a small world satisfying these gates and exposing
unexpected optimizer behavior. It need not claim a complete account of
consciousness, fundamental spacetime or quantum lushness to launch.
