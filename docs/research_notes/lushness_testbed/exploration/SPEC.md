14 October 2026. The physical question is whether connecting powered internal
variation to actual resource access changes complete future-history breadth,
food acquisition and lifetime. The oracle compares a declared finite family;
it is not an optimization over arbitrary controllers or an uncertain agent.

## Physics

State (e, location, signal, hazard, stock0, stock1). Energy retains the earlier
0.5 quantum and capacity4. There are three actual locations H/O0/O1, an internal
bit, stochastic hazard and two binary food-packet stocks: 408 physical states.

Outside foraging consumes the packet in the occupied patch. Every attempt pays
the same bill. Movement between O0/O1 costs 0.5 or1 energy. All native attempt
propensities, metabolism and bit bills remain explicit in the common physics.

Total energy includes store, activated register and packet energy. A refill
supplies external work equal to packet energy2; decay dissipates it. Foraging
transfers energy from a physical packet, dissipates its bill and any unretained
overflow; it does not import new work. The replenishment boundary stops accepting
packets at full stocks. Nominal offers are matched, accepted work may differ.
No equilibrium/detailed-balance or microscopic thermal-gas claim is made.

Native hazard and stock processes continue after death. Neither an inaccessible
resource nor a dead valuer freezes the environment. All movement/control stops
at e=0. This is a finite mortality world with no repair or reconstruction loop.
Its long-run rate describes the residual environment. It cannot establish
open-ended or sustained compounding.

## Five candidate mechanisms

- B: unvarying bit and basal movement.
- B_R: costed independent bit, basal movement; no response to bit.
- B_X: same bit and unit bills, movement rate v(1+k*s). The bit does not track
  which patch is productive. Both directions are native and possible.
- M_C: bit present, constant rate v(1+k*rho), rho=on/(on+off). This is a declared
  nominal-duty control, not fitted to realized movement or entropy. Death and
  charging thresholds can change the actual duty.
- S_A: bit present, stock-aware extra movement only when occupied patch is empty
  and alternative patch full, rate v(1+k). It pays the same movement bill. This
  is an idealized state-aware physical rule with no resolved sensor memory or
  separate sensing overhead. It is not an optimality bound or a demonstrated
  implementable agent. Full sensor embodiment is a future module.

Zero coupling must make B_X/M_C/S_A generators exactly equal B_R. No movement
names with identical outcomes add entropy. All modes use the compiler's native
events for sampling and exact analysis. Retain separate physiology, resource and
mobility modules for population/spatial/dependency extensions.
