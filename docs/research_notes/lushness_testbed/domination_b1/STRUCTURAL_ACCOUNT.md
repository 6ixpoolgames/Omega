Query-only analysis of the original physical operators. No scores or laws were changed. The four physical source files were matched byte-for-byte to the scientific snapshot. Occupation integrals were computed independently of the entropy solver.

## Count overlap is kept explicit

In this adapter, joint D/S events are the paid steering acts. Full event count equals D changes plus S changes, minus joint changes, plus packet-only changes. The identity and the independent capture-flow cross-check close within 10⁻⁶ events. These are physical count identities, not an additive entropy decomposition. No motion, thermal activity or chemical cycling is subtracted from lushness.
