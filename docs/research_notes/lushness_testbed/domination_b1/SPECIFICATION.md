## Exact fractional energy

The public bill/capacity/quantum inputs accept fractional physical energies.
Balances are integer ticks on a declared rational grid; off-grid bills are
rejected rather than rounded or replaced with lotteries. The expanded bank has
capacity 3 and quantum 0.1 for every arm of the main B1 map. Native capture,
assembly and upkeep still exchange one physical energy unit, ten ticks.

The density of bank states extends the old binomial weight using the gamma
function. This is a declared phenomenological density of an energy reservoir,
not a molecular microstate count. A reversible adjacent-tick bath exchange
connects fractional residues in every comparison arm. Its coefficient scales
as 0.02(0.1/quantum)^2, fixing the declared local diffusion scale across the
engineering grids. Refining this reservoir adds physical bath transitions;
old integer-bank and new fractional-bank entropy totals are not represented
as the same world under mere coordinate relabeling.

Native rates obey detailed balance with the source and active friction off.
The paired fractional bath uses the square root of the reference-density
ratio and exp(−βΔH/2). All event energy changes enter the compiler's first-law
ledger. Active blocking/steering/sham remain costed, one-way kinetic boundary
processes; a complete reversible microscopic controller is not implemented.

## B1 physical discovery

S gains one extra location: a resource pocket reached only through the trap.
A very slow native route connects it reversibly to the trap. Working S
apparatus also enables a faster paid route: entering spends one S energy
unit; reversing reconstructs that energy through the paired native reaction.
The uphill location energy and energy debit enter the same ledger.

At the pocket, apparatus captures from the same shared stochastic packet.
Opportunity yields 0, 1 and 4 multiply forward and reverse capture equally;
they do not alter equilibrium ratios or inject work. Both compartments still
receive the same source/bath prescription within every comparison. Actual
accepted work can differ and is reported.

This is discovery as paid physical access/residence, not an unmodelled learned
memory, a permanent shared unlock or an uncertain agent. The oracle knows the
whole law. A trap-entry intervention also restricts a return from the pocket
into the trap; occupancy and discovery readouts must expose that consequence.
It is not silently excluded from the target definition.
