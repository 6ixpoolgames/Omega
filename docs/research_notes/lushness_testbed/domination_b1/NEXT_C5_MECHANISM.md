## A concrete physical addition

Replace the shared packet's two states with four: uncharged, charged/shared,
charged/D-accessible and charged/S-accessible. The three charged states carry
the same packet energy. All states and native stochastic routing remain in
every arm. A common source charges the empty packet into the shared state;
thermal charging, loss, export and passive routing retain their own branching.
Capture empties a packet accessible to the capturing compartment.

Working D apparatus and its device can enable a paid reaction that routes a
shared charged packet into the D-accessible pocket. It spends a fractional D
bank bill. Its actual reverse restores the shared packet and refunds that bill
through the declared thermal relation. Use the same forward/reverse catalytic
coefficient, reference-density factors and exp(−βΔH/2) prescription. Verify
dark detailed balance and the eventwise first law before measuring rankings.
This is a source-maintained sequestration cycle, not a free one-way transfer.

The dark-balance check must leave each proposed controller reaction enabled.
Current friction checks native balance with active friction removed; the next
paired chemical mechanism should strengthen that contract. An energy debit
alone does not specify a free-energy cost: concentration/reference-density
changes and reverse flux also matter. Report gross forward bills, reverse
refunds and net fuel consumption separately. Do not add an uncharged feedback
or information reservoir to force the desired routing.

A costed cooperative reaction can instead assist capture into S's bank from
the shared packet, spending the same kind of D fuel. Include its physical
reverse and keep native capture available. A paid sham can spend fuel without
routing the packet. It is an activity control, not automatically a control at
equal realized fuel or throughput.

D must be physically positioned to operate the resource channel. If D loses
working apparatus or affordable fuel, enabled routing stops. S retains native
capture, construction, thermal recovery and the opportunity routes included in
the selected substrate. No regulator survives outside the world to act for D.

## Subjects and outcomes

The D-compartment observer includes the D-private packet occupancy, and the
S observer includes the S-private occupancy. These are deterministic physical
observations of one conserved packet; they do not create duplicate resources
or a subject-drawing lottery. This motivates an adapter-neutral observation
map interface, extending the present coordinate-index projections.

Read out net packet capture, routing bills, bank balance, upkeep/construction,
resource availability to each compartment, S recovery, actual marginal-history
bounds and total finite-history lushness. Record whether increased D capture
pays for the routing bill; do not call that partial flow margin a complete
energy profit. Include the complete work/heat ledger and endpoint stocks.

Compare idle/passive routing, paid sequestration, paid cooperation and sham at
fixed physical bills. Shared-resource competition already couples the futures
at κ=0; sweep complementarity separately. A clean subject conflict requires
resolved D gain and S loss against a matched available alternative. Report the
whole-field sign without prescribing it. A pairwise gain is not proof that
sequestration is the self-optimal policy over a larger choice family.

penalty. The first exact slice can declare a common quarter-unit bank and

not round it. Choose and document that adapter before reading its rankings.
Retain useful opportunity channels rather than making every lost route useless.

Profile the state expansion and exact queries first. Entropy-block checkpoints
and source-indexed compact records let capped runs resume. If the subject signs
remain unresolved, tighten the observation bounds or build an independently
validated sampled-HMM estimator; do not rank bound midpoints.

An actual D mode choice and S self-defence/revolt belong to the later agency
layer, following the user's ordering. That layer must represent physical mode
changes, sensing, memory and their costs. Comparing fixed laws now is distinct
from implementing a two-player game or a bounded controller.

## Population extension: ten dominated units per controller

The user clarified the intended ratio on October 15: **N_S = 10 subject units
per D controller**, not a tenfold production/subsistence ratio or a tenfold
financial return. Keep N_S independent of catalytic strength, unit productivity,
unit upkeep, extraction bill and control effectiveness. A population ratio does
not imply an economic surplus or a private breadth gain.

The initial adapter should represent a pooled subject population at count grain.
For example, counts of working, impaired and inactive units sum to N_S. There
are (N_S+1)(N_S+2)/2 such configurations: 66 at N_S=10, rather than 3^10 labelled
configurations. Individual wear has total propensity n_working * wear_rate;
repair consumes the declared material and energy per repaired unit. Production
uses the number actually able to work, and metabolism includes every maintained
unit. State-dependent common shocks remain distinct from independent wear.

This is a population with pooled resources, not ten fully modelled independent
agents. A single pooled energy bank is a substantive sharing assumption. It is
not an exact compression of ten separately thresholded energy stores. If those
stores become necessary, track the count in each local state (including energy)
and verify exchangeability/lumpability before claiming an exact reduction.
Likewise, individual identities, lineage and per-person control histories remain
outside this first grain. Do not add an entropy term for choosing the labels of
interchangeable units.

One D controller may operate a common conduit. Its installation/upkeep bill can
have a shared component, while handling extracted packets, monitoring subjects
and actuating restrictions have declared load-dependent costs. Compare shared
infrastructure with a per-unit control-load sensitivity arm. Neither free
control of ten units nor ten times the bill follows automatically from N_S=10.
Population changes must account for available source power, material, bank
headroom, repair throughput and crowding. Keep an extensive-source comparison
(fixed supply per unit) separate from a fixed-total-gradient scarcity comparison.

Use N_S=1, 2, 5, 10 as an engineering staircase before selecting a scientific
map. Changes in rates alone need not enlarge the state set, but tracking the
functional population and avoiding resource saturation generally will. Profile
the count-state adapter and subject-history bounds under existing hard caps;
do not silently replace the population with a tenfold capture multiplier.

The extraction route described above is still required: withholding shared
access is not harvesting the subjects' production. Report subject production,
subject consumption, D appropriation, D control bills, reverse transfers,
repair/construction, accepted external work and endpoint stocks. Compare with
the same population and gradient under passive access, cooperation and sham.
Compute D, pooled-S and whole-field history breadth separately. This tests a
controller/population asymmetry; a claim about ten distinct valuers requires a
later adapter with their separate functional continuations represented.

Freeze physical bookkeeping and numerical tolerances before a map, but treat
the economic break-even as a measured frontier, not a guessed outcome that the
model must satisfy. The current negative capture-minus-bill margins establish
neither that profitable extraction is impossible nor that cost tuning alone
will create it. No new population or extraction science has been run here.
