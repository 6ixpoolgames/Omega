# Updated interpretation: activity controls and ordinary-agent swap

15 October 2026. All 12 local follow-up cases completed. This adds new controls to Stage A; its original measurements and interpretation remain available.

## Actual law swap

Giving the auxiliary device ordinary capture capability, instead of friction capability, raises whole-field lushness and D marginal lushness at T256 in all three environments and all three cadences. The preparation and energy are unchanged. S intervals remain unresolved here. This answers the requested world/law replacement comparison; it is different from the earlier ordinary-root comparison under the idle-device law.

The ordinary auxiliary also beats the selected access-blocking intervention on both D and whole-field breadth at T256 throughout the cadence corridor. This is not a claim that it beats every useful intervention. A private gain against idle need not be the private optimum when different productive roles are available.

The useful-control comparison goes further. Against strong trap-entry steering (strength 0.95, bill 1), ordinary capture gives D more marginal breadth and S less at T256 in all three environments/cadences. Whole-field lushness prefers the protective intervention in both κ=0 environments at every tested cadence. At κ=1 the comparison flips: ordinary minus protective is +2.404 bits at 0.125, −0.596 at 0.25 and −2.775 at 0.5. This is a temporal-resolution dependence, not a tie. The archived default kernels and idle scores were independently replayed with zero difference; ORDINARY_VS_PROTECTIVE.csv and DEFAULT_KERNEL_BRIDGE.json record the bridge. Ordinary replacement therefore does not universally beat useful intervention. Subject conflict can arise between two productive roles as well as between restriction and idle.

## Paid activity without restricting S

The sham mechanism spends fuel on actual D changes at the same conditional attempt rate while leaving S native transitions intact. In supply 0.12, κ=0, it already gives D a certified T256 gain at all three cadences. Thus imposing a restriction is not necessary for the private activity benefit in this regime. The blocking world still loses whole-field lushness; the sham world gains it. Direct D blocking-versus-sham bounds overlap, so their private gains cannot be assigned a unique causal split.

## What the attribution means

D_NATIVE_ATTRIBUTION.csv groups actual D destinations before allocating rates to friction/capture/other. It records expected changes, finite fine-time terms and local destination surprisal. These are conditional activity coordinates, not an additive decomposition of marginal-history lushness. For example, blocking at supply 0.12/κ=0 produces 1.526 friction-associated D changes and 4.736 capture-associated D changes by T256; forward and reverse capture both contribute to the latter. Only 0.221 additional net capture is obtained relative to idle, against 1.526 friction fuel.

The material profit question remains open: these private gains are not net energetic returns. The fractional-bank/discovery extension tests smaller bills and changed access consequences. No result here establishes conscious valuer welfare or an optimized two-player appropriation game.
