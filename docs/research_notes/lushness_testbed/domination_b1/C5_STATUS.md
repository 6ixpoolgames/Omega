# C5 after Stage A: the gap has narrowed

15 October 2026. Assessment of Sol's proposed research priority.

Completion update: the 28-case fractional-cost/discovery batch is finished and
validated. At bill 0.1 the private optimum within idle/weak/strong exclusion is
strong exclusion, while the whole-field optimum is idle, at every tested horizon
and cadence. The private gain remains resolved through T1024. Material capture
does not cover the intervention bill in the reported exclusion comparisons.
See INTERPRETATION.md for the complete scope and NEXT_C5_MECHANISM.md for the
actual extraction mechanism and ten subject units per controller design.

Coupled self-versus-whole control is the highest-value ethical direction. Its
status should reflect the completed Stage A map: D's marginal ranking is no
longer universally unresolved. Thirty paid cases at T256 certify opposite D
and whole-field signs throughout the three-cadence corridor. Resource-entry
blocking at supply 0.12, κ=0, strength 0.95 gives D more marginal-history
lushness while S and the whole lose it. Both agents draw on the same stochastic
packet even at κ=0; κ adds complementarity rather than creating all coupling.

The follow-up controls further isolate what is missing. A paid sham can give D
a private gain without restricting S. Ordinary use of the auxiliary material
can improve both private and whole continuation. A private gain from restricting
S is therefore not yet evidence that appropriation is an efficient material
strategy: actuator activity can be sufficient for such a gain.

The next stages should distinguish:

1. **Useful versus obstructive constraint.** B1 adds a paid opportunity behind
   the hazardous route, including fractional bills. It maps when restricting
   exploration preserves or narrows actual access.
2. **Materially profitable appropriation.** Add embodied, costed transfer or
   preferential routing from S's accessible resource to D. Retain native
   resource fluctuations, reverse channels, shared work and the ordinary-agent
   comparator. Resource income and private history gain must be measured
   separately, not inferred from each other.
3. **Physical choice of control.** D chooses among cooperative/idle, exclusion,
   confinement and beneficial intervention mechanisms. S response/self-defence
   follows the user's stated ordering after the opportunity module. Compare
   self and encompassing objectives on the same allowed actions and energy
   rules, without encoding a control penalty or a consent reward.

The decisive payoff map is ΔL_D, ΔL_S, ΔL_whole relative to a matched available
alternative, with resolved marginal-history signs or explicitly unresolved
bounds. The whole-field sign is not specified in advance. Complementarity,
cost, recovery and opportunity can change it. An optimum over a fixed
intervention family is distinct from an endogenous two-player equilibrium.

These compartments instantiate coupled physical controller channels. They do
not establish that the represented subjects are conscious valuers or settle
the full ethical bridge. The present evidence supports subject-sensitive
physical rankings; the next modules test the mechanisms that would make those
rankings relevant to appropriation and agency.

Population and cross-scale composition remain supported architectural targets:
native event modules define physical changes; energy grids handle fractional
bills; objective/query modules separately identify subjects; immutable run
records preserve models and results. New entities must enter that physical
structure and its cost ledger, not be added as uncharged action labels.
