# Interpretation: fractional control, paid access and subject conflict

This experiment asks how physical constraint changes the continuations of a
controller D, a subject compartment S and their encompassing world. It resolves
some subject conflicts that the earlier integer-cost adapter could not resolve,
and separates physical access, harvested resources and history breadth.
All 28 cases completed and passed the recorded numerical validation. RESULTS.md
and the signed maps retain all numerical results: 288 intervention-versus-idle
comparisons and 144 complete family/horizon/cadence searches.

## Restriction can serve D while costing S and the whole

With a 0.1-unit intervention bill, strong resource-entry exclusion raises D's
actual marginal-history breadth and lowers both S's and whole-field breadth at
all four horizons and all three tested cadences. At T256 and cadence 0.25 the
whole loses 13.040 bits, D gains between 15.011 and 23.435 bits, and S loses
between 22.263 and 34.469 bits. The intervals are certified conditioning bounds,
not sampling error bars. The conflict remains resolved through T1024.

In the complete idle/weak/strong family at this bill, D's guaranteed choice is
strong exclusion while the whole-field choice is idle. This is stronger than
a pairwise example, but it remains a choice within that family. It does not
establish that restriction beats cooperation, productive ordinary apparatus or
harmless actuator activity when those choices are also available.

Across the complete map, all 72 trap-steering case/horizon comparisons are
whole-positive at all three clocks. Of the 24 resource-exclusion comparisons,
22 are whole-negative across clocks; the two short-horizon weak-exclusion
comparisons at bill 0.3 change sign with cadence. In particular, the whole-field
rejection is not a universal statement about every restriction at every horizon.

This is a private breadth advantage, not demonstrated profitable extraction.
At T256 the additional D capture is 0.0812 energy units and the intervention
bill is 0.3295. Their difference is negative. Even that flow difference is not
a complete energy-profit calculation; the full account includes upkeep,
construction, heat and endpoint stores. Current exclusion blocks S's route to
a shared resource. It does not transfer S's production into D's bank.

Both compartments already compete for the same physical packet at κ=0.
Consequently the result concerns coupled futures, rather than two independent
worlds. The subjects are functional apparatus/controller proxies at this grain;
the model does not establish their consciousness or instantiate ten individuals.

## The discovery intervention is not pure suppression of exploration

The new pocket is a paid physical route through the trap. Higher pocket yield
changes reversible capture kinetics there; it adds no free energy per packet.
The native basal route remains available in every arm. The oracle knows the
law, so "discovery" means reaching a physical opportunity, not learning an
unknown model.

Trap-entry steering blocks some arrivals but also intercepts the return from
the pocket to the trap and redirects attempted transitions into other native
movement and chemistry. The independent structural probe finds most acts
originate outside the pocket. Thus neither "only blocks discovery" nor "only
keeps S inside the pocket" accurately describes the mechanism. Raising yield
cannot change the first-arrival prefix because it changes rates only after
arrival. The structural account reports each effect separately.

At yield 4, cheap strong steering raises whole-history breadth by 32.408 bits
at T256 and cadence 0.25, despite lowering net S capture by 0.0926 energy units
and accepted source work by 0.1317. S makes more physical transitions. At T1024,
the same capture and work differences are positive. This separates a finite
continuation advantage from a claim of greater immediate harvest or efficiency.
Some positive finite-history comparisons have a negative limiting-rate
difference. Rates remain diagnostics; finite advantages are not automatically
permanent ones.

## Useful constraint and the available alternatives

The old-grid controls show that a paid sham can raise D's breadth without
restricting S. Repurposing the device material as ordinary capture apparatus
also raises D and whole-field breadth relative to idle and the selected access
blockade. These observations limit an interpretation based on domination being
uniquely attractive to D.

Ordinary productive apparatus does not universally beat beneficial control.
Against strong trap avoidance it raises D's breadth but lowers S's. The whole
prefers trap avoidance in both κ=0 environments at T256 across all tested
clocks; the κ=1 comparison changes sign with cadence. Separately, the existing
native conservative-field control gives a resolved D-negative, S-positive,
whole-positive comparison through T1024. Subject conflicts therefore occur in
both directions and are not confined to the active friction boundary.

These comparisons distinguish useful constraint from access suppression by
their physical consequences. No fairness, protection, consent or domination
term was added. Randomness and actuator activity remain part of the objective.
The D-coordinate event-family attribution is explanatory; it is not a unique
decomposition of marginal-history entropy and nothing is subtracted from scores.

## Scope and next physical addition

The fractional bank permits real fractional bills while using exact integer
ticks internally. The refined reservoir and its reversible bath are declared
physics, shared across matched arms. Active friction remains a coarse driven
control boundary. Native dark balance and first-law checks do not turn it into
a closed microscopic reversible controller. The next paired extraction
mechanism must check dark balance with its own forward and reverse reactions
enabled.

The user's ten-to-one proposal means ten subject units per controller. A pooled
count adapter can represent functional, impaired and inactive population without
ten labelled state spaces. It must scale production, subsistence, repair,
resource supply and control workload explicitly. Shared infrastructure may
produce an economy of scale, which is a question to measure. Pooled resources
are an assumption, not an exact replacement for ten separate thresholded banks.

NEXT_C5_MECHANISM.md specifies that population direction together with a paid
resource-routing/extraction path, cooperation and sham controls. The material
and private/whole break-even should be mapped rather than assumed from a chosen
cost. No population, appropriation or revolt result is claimed from this batch.

Source snapshots, configurations, receipts and compact signed maps preserve the
evidence. The exact sampled-process computations use a bounded Poisson-series
query, not a physical auxiliary clock. D/S projections are hidden-Markov and
unresolved signs remain unresolved. Three clocks are three tested resolutions,
not a proof over the continuous interval or over all physical grains.
