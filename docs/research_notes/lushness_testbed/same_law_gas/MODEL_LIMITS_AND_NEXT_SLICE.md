## Concrete limits in the implemented physics

- There is one carrier with charge capacity2 and one source packet. Capture
  requires the carrier at site0 in charge0. A partially charged carrier cannot
  capture another packet. This creates an actual transport/charge bottleneck.
- The law's `upkeep` channel is apparatus-associated charge overhead with a
  thermal reverse. It does not reduce an existing bond's damage hazard or make
  that bond more stable. Functional replacement occurs through paid joining
  after breakage. Do not call overhead alone a demonstrated maintenance benefit.
- Catalytic assemblies have positive stored energy. Thermal reverse and
  externally imposed damage remain accessible. Assistance speeds the paid
  reverse as well as forward construction.
- The6/7 geometry is dense and supports at most three pairs. Binding removes
  free monomer motion and halves cluster hopping. The thermal reference already
  has more bonds on average than the seed, so part of its disadvantage is reduced
  transient movement rather than delayed initial catalyst formation.
- Every law is finite and irreducible. Any preparation lead is eventually an
  offset. Persistent enabling, self-assembly delay and the resulting breadth
  remain distinct questions within that common future law.
