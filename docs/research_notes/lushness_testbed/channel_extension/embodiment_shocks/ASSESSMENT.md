## Interpretation and next capability

This upgrade answers the narrow objection: once switching needs functioning
apparatus, keeping it operational can win on native breadth. The absorbing tail
test also identifies a specific physical mechanism behind the prior ranking.
It does not establish that breadth produces ethical behavior generally.

The shock comparison has reached a current model limit: the best response is
constant servicing whenever any active apparatus exists, in all tested shock arms.
Its controller observes only local zero-versus-nonzero active stock. It cannot
distinguish a thin reserve from a robust one, allocate across compartments, or
respond to shock history. The next capability should be a versioned richer
observation/controller search, followed by a narrow shock-strength boundary test.
Do not immediately add another large parameter grid or infer criticality here.

Independent sensing/memory, spatial gas controls and expanding capacity remain
separate modules. The four-slot world cannot establish cross-scale or open-ended
compounding. The chemical boundary is maintained externally. All-trapped diagnostic
roots differ in energy from the E8 preparations; they do not isolate organization
at equal resource supply.
