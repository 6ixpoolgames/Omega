## What compounds

Its thermodynamic reverse is retained. The local certificate states the exact conditions; it does not assume unlimited growth. Deadline stock and stationary retention then test whether the resulting descendants persist.

At capacity8/cost2/packet3/damage0.005, the loop retains 7.9480 units, with at least two present 99.9941% of the time. Net paid creation is 0.042371units/time. Gross birth and reverse fluxes are also retained, so persistence is not inferred from birth count alone.

Gross hardware ROI can exceed1, but a full stationary bank budget cannot maintain a surplus. Bath support is small at the central capacity8 point and becomes more important at large inventory; no marker-failing point is excluded.

## Scope and next boundary

This is bounded recursively enabling apparatus growth in a declared resource-driven toy. Increasing the finite precursor inventory supplies room to grow; it does not prove unbounded growth, a rate that increases forever within one fixed finite world, or superiority over a microscopic gas. The basal mechanism preserves native packet and thermal dynamics but is a coupling ablation, not a full gas ensemble. Same-law seed and unorganized roots have equal limiting rates, with distinct finite profiles.

The source, precursor budget, native rate law and kinetic coefficient form the stated assumptions. The tests establish the consequences of those assumptions, not that every chemistry or our universe permits this regime. The next extension should test finite opportunity windows and maintained or expanding material access, with physical controller/valuer composition added as a separate layer.
