# Compounding boundary: resource-funded apparatus reproduction

15 October 2026. New module; earlier laws and results remain unchanged.

The target is repeated paid reinvestment: apparatus enables resource capture,
captured energy pays construction, and the constructed apparatus enables more
capture and construction. Test persistent expansion separately from reversible
turnover and from its lushness consequence. No success is stipulated.

## Physical law

State=(bank quanta, packet, working apparatus). The shared bank holds eight
half-energy quanta; the packet is binary; apparatus plus precursors equals M.
M=4/8/16/32 opens the capacity beyond the earlier two-unit fixture. These are
finite inventories, not an infinite or ever-expanding state space.

Packets arrive through one maintained source and decay through the same native
boundary in all mechanisms. Offered power=supply rate*packet energy. Actual
accepted work can change when capture changes packet occupancy. Basal capture
remains positive, with additional capture propensity proportional to apparatus.
Capture loses0.5energy, loads the remaining yield, and reports bank overflow.

Apparatus stores1energy. Construction spends1.5/2/2.5bank energy; its reverse
recycles1hardware energy and imports the dissipated remainder from the bath.
The construction coefficient is k0+k_cat*a in the forward direction and
k0+k_cat*(a-1) in the reverse, with precursor/apparatus count factors. Both
directions of each pair retain the same coefficient and local energy ratio.
Thermal hardware assembly is rare but positive and has its native reverse.
Hardware upkeep and bank leakage retain their thermal reverse channels, with
positive heat imports recorded. No one-way free thermal charging is introduced.

External damage converts apparatus to precursor and releases stored energy.
Changing its hazard does NOT alter thermal assembly, construction or source
parameters. Energy-zero also increases a fixed thermal disassembly/reassembly
pair; both directions satisfy the same local ratio. External damage is a
one-way driven perturbation, not an equilibrium reaction. With the source off,
no external damage and an empty packet, the whole native world satisfies
detailed balance against binomial material degeneracy and Boltzmann weights.

This is a coarse well-mixed apparatus/chemical model, not a microscopic gas,
individual identity, valuer population or an information-controller experiment.
The existing RuleModule/compiler/event-sampler contracts remain shared. Future
modules can add location, controllers, material inflow/sinks, worn capabilities,
population partitions and cross-scale couplers explicitly.

## Exact local feedback certificate

When a packet is present, another apparatus unit increases capture propensity
by h_cat. When the bank can pay the bill, the next forward construction rate is

    B(a)=(M-a)*(k0+k_cat*a)*exp(beta*(cost-hardware_energy)/2).

Therefore

    B(a+1)-B(a)=[k_cat*(M-2*a-1)-k0]*exp(beta*(cost-hardware_energy)/2).

This is positive below the stated crowding threshold, including multiple
successive descendant levels at M>=8. The reverse reaction is also accelerated.
This certificate proves local recursive enabling in the model, not realized
growth, persistence or an advantage on lushness. The experiment tests those
additional statements rather than inferring them from the formula.

## Funding is a balance, not a perpetual surplus

Report the exact bank identity at every horizon:

    Delta bank = captured + recycled hardware + bath bank imports
                 - construction debit - upkeep debit - leakage debit.

In stationarity these funding fractions sum to1. Captured energy divided by
all bank costs consequently has ceiling1, not a required crossing above1.
Gross hardware return can exceed1 when its denominator excludes other costs;
it is not sufficient evidence of autonomous growth. Report the source shares,
net bath exchange, gross/reverse construction, thermal reconstruction fraction,
retained apparatus and the full first-law balance. No provenance labels are
added as entropy-bearing physical registers.
