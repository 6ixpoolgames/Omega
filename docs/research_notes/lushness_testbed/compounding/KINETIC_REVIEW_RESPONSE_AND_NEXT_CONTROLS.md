# Kinetic plausibility: actual laws and next controls

15 October 2026. Response to Opus review, preserving the completed experiments.
The same-law lattice protocol was already frozen when these comments arrived;
it is not retuned from them. This note specifies subsequent controls, not results.

## What the actual compounding law assumes

The completed aggregate compounding adapter uses capture coefficient
0.002+0.1*a: a50-fold *increment* per apparatus, or51-fold total at one unit.
Paid construction uses0.0001+0.008*a: an80-fold increment, or81-fold total at
one unit, multiplied by precursor count and the common energy factor. Thus
the review's roughly3-fold construction number describes another fixture,
not this completed batch. Reverse construction uses the same coefficient at
the corresponding lower apparatus count. The protocol and exact parameters
are local under compounding; earlier renewal/exploration batches were pushed
at7251050, but the compounding packet has not yet been pushed.

Production is not globally exponential: finite precursor, finite charge bank,
source saturation, reversible construction, damage and depletion all matter.
The proportional catalytic coefficient supplies a locally self-reinforcing
reaction hazard. Realized persistent expansion was separately measured.

The new spatial adapter does not give every assembly global access. Catalysts
and a charged carrier must meet the actual geometric contact rules, and a
bound pair moves more slowly than its free constituents. Local assistance is
still additive in contacting spectators, so product inhibition remains an
appropriate next physical test.

## What the literature supports

Very large acceleration is possible for particular enzymes. The celebrated
10^17 enhancement concerns orotidine5'-phosphate decarboxylase; it does not
calibrate an arbitrary assembly's effective rate or abundance. Comparing this
single-reaction ratio with our apparatus increments cannot establish that our
kinetics are biologically conservative across all needed reactions.
[Radzicka and Wolfenden1995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7809611/).

Optimized cross-replicating RNA enzymes provide an experimental example of
exponential amplification with substrate supply and serial transfer. Earlier
versions were strongly inhibited by nonproductive complexes. This establishes
a mechanism's possibility under those conditions, rather than its inevitability
in generic prebiotic matter.
[Lincoln and Joyce2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2652413/).

A self-replicating peptide exhibited parabolic growth with an approximately
square-root dependence on initial template concentration. That supports a
sublinear effective-feedback control and an explicit product-binding mechanism.
It does not imply that all non-enzyme chemistry has the same growth exponent.
[Lee et al.1996](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8700225/).

RAF analysis identified a1335-reaction autocatalytic network in the compiled
prokaryotic anaerobic biochemical space. Network closure is relevant support
for studying cross-catalysis; it is not itself a demonstration of our paid
growth, path-entropy advantage, or ethics of plural valuers.
[Xavier et al.2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7126077/).

Maintenance expenditure varies with growth and energy availability. Our
declining capture-funding fraction with capacity is not by itself the same
biological relationship: its complement includes explicitly reported bath
imports and recycling. Upkeep, damage, input and bath funding must be separated.
[Kempes et al.2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28197128/).

## Next controls in implementation order

1. **Shape and strength together.** In a new versioned aggregate adapter, use
   k0+kcat*a^alpha with alpha0.5/0.75/1, all normalized at a=1. Apply exactly
   the same coefficient to both directions of each construction pair, using
   lower-counta in the reverse. Sweep assistance multiplier0/0.01/0.1/1 and
   retain the basal boundary. Preserve source, energy, precursor, capture,
   damage and maintenance. Freeze all points before ranking. This maps a
   finite grid boundary; it cannot assert an exact global critical strength.
   A square-root coefficient is a phenomenological availability control,
   not an explicit mechanistic product-inhibition model.
2. **Crowding upkeep.** Add a costed drain whose coefficient increases with
   density, such as a*[u0+u1*(a/M)^2], retaining the same native bath reverse
   energy ratio. Use a fixed u1 grid rather than selecting it for an expected
   winner. Report captured/recycled/bath funding separately and actual retained
   apparatus, not gross turnover as expansion.
3. **Explicit stuck copies.** Add bound catalyst/product complexes with native
   association and release, resources, energy and reverse channels. Only free
   catalysts participate in further construction. This tests the physical
   cause which a fractional exponent merely approximates.
4. **Two cross-catalytic types.** Shared precursor and gradient; A assists B
   construction and B assists A, with pairwise reverse consistency. Match
   total material and costs to a single-type comparator. Chemical diversity
   is a first composition test, not an instantiated plural-valuer or domination
   experiment. No diversity bonus or exclusivity of a favourable regime.

Every control reports finite-history lushness, retained growth, loop-minus-basal
rate and activity, physical funding, and temporal sign corridor. Loss of a
predicted advantage remains a result. Do not label a physically functioning
but unfavourable loop nongenerative after observing its breadth.
