## 1. The question

Does an organized preparation generate greater complete weighted continuation
than physically disordered preparations of the same constituents, under the
same law, bath, matter inventory, initial total energy and source prescription?

This is the **same-law preparation question** (proposed ledger C1a/A2). In a
finite irreducible law, these roots share one limiting rate; the lasting
log-breadth contrast is an offset. The finite-history profile remains primary,
with the offset and the reference's self-assembly time alongside it. The
thermal reference may catch up in organization while an accumulated history
advantage remains. A permanent organization-versus-gas rate advantage is not
an outcome this design can establish under those assumptions.

This conclusion depends on the finite fixed-law mixing assumptions. It is not
a theorem that an expanding or quantum universe has only preparation offsets.

The separate **mechanism/law question** (proposed C1b) asks what an enabling
relation changes in the ongoing process. The compounding batch bears on that
question under matched offered boundaries, but accepted work and activity differ.
A throughput-matched mechanism comparison is still pending; it is not silently
supplied by this same-Q preparation test.

The new compounding experiment establishes a paid recursive mechanism and
retained expansion. Its basal arm changes enabling couplings; it is not a gas
preparation under that same law. This experiment closes that particular gap.
The comparator can spontaneously assemble machinery, capture resources, grow
and disassemble. No arm is kept disordered by disabling a reaction or resetting it.

The first target is a finite, reactive lattice-gas adapter. All of its declared
diffusion and chemical randomness count. Success would establish a gas-relative
result at that adapter's grain, not dominance over every microscopic thermal
degree of freedom in actual matter.

## 2. One physical carrier and one generator

Replace the aggregate apparatus count with explicit locations and bonds. Use
a finite periodic one-dimensional lattice first, with a fixed source location.
A physical state contains:

- occupied sites for N identical monomer constituents;
- nearest-neighbor bonds, with each monomer having at most one bond;
- locations and charge levels of a fixed inventory of fuel carriers;
- a resource packet at the source;
- any physical charge, deformation or damage state later required by the law.

A bound pair is the first catalytic apparatus. It consists of the same two
monomers as an unbound pair. A dispersed configuration does not gain extra
particles or particle identities. Labels used to enumerate indistinguishable
monomers are bookkeeping and do not add history entropy.

Start with one fuel carrier whose internal charge takes levels0/1/2. Additional
carrier inventory is a later explicit physical expansion. Fuel has a location;
there is no globally accessible bank or free transport between reactions.
The source packet is physically present and stochastic in every preparation.

The initial Hamiltonian template is

    H(x) = epsilon_b * number_of_bonds
           + epsilon_f * total_carrier_charge
           + epsilon_R * source_packet.

Positive bond energy represents an activated, metastable catalytic assembly.
The first engineering calibration uses epsilon_b=epsilon_f=1,
epsilon_R=3: powered joining spends two charge units, stores one bond-energy
unit and dissipates one. These are toy energy units, not molecular measurements.
Alternative bond energetics require a separately declared physical law.

This carrier is richer than the count model: configurations with the same
apparatus count can have different capture, transport and construction access.
The global frame is its whole declared state. Local queries are derived views.

## 3. Native processes

| Module | Physical transition | Required safeguards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diffusion | Free monomers, bonded pairs and carriers move into admissible locations | Actual positions change; no solver-label entropy; both directions retained |
| Thermal association | Adjacent unbound monomers join or dissociate | Positive basal pathways and native reverse; same rules in every preparation |
| Resource boundary | A packet arrives at the source or leaves unused | Same source clock, packet energy and boundary prescription |
| Transduction | A nearby apparatus assists charging a carrier from the packet | Positive basal transduction; carrier must actually be present; no free transport |
| Powered construction | A local charged carrier funds joining an available pair | Charge and matter spent explicitly; thermodynamic reverse retained |
| Recursive assistance | An existing local bound pair assists joining another pair | Catalyst remains present; geometry/contact required; same pair coefficient forward and reverse |
| Maintenance | Apparatus and nearby fuel undergo a paid maintenance/relaxation channel | No upkeep-free controller; reverse bath contribution diagnosed |
| Damage | An assembly breaks to the same precursor matter | Separate external damage clock; thermal association rates held fixed |

A monomer hop must not break a bond silently. Bound pairs move through explicit
legal cluster translations or dissociate through their own reaction. Choose a
common mobility prescription before rankings; a proposed first rule gives a
two-monomer cluster half the monomer's attempt frequency. This is an explicit
constitutive assumption to audit, not a universal diffusion theorem.

For a native undriven reversible pair, use a symmetric barrier coefficient:

    q(x,y) = a(x,y) exp[-beta*(H(y)-H(x))/2],
    a(x,y) = a(y,x).

A spectator catalyst lowers that pair's barrier in both directions. Derive
reaction multiplicities once from actual physical matches; do not add a second
entropy for engine rule IDs. For externally driven boundaries, declare work and
the reverse or reservoir prescription explicitly. One-way damage is identified
as a nonequilibrium perturbation. The no-drive/no-external-damage control must
recover the declared equilibrium law and detailed balance.

Catalytic efficacy is stipulated chemistry. It may make access possible, but
the test must not stipulate a lushness gain: binding slows diffusion, consumes
fuel, occupies sites and competes with native thermal activity. Those costs and
possible losses remain in the actual trajectories.

## 4. Matching the preparations

Fix lattice size, constituent and carrier inventory, Hamiltonian, bath beta,
source location, boundary clocks, damage law, mobility law and measurement.
Compile one Q per parameter setting. All preparation arms reuse exactly that Q.

Construct an organized root by an outcome-independent physical recipe: one
bound pair near the source, nearby carrier access and adjacent free constituents
available for another paid assembly. Do not optimize its placement on lushness.
Retain every declared symmetry-related recipe and source-relative placement.

Equal energy is checked against H, not inferred from matching particle counts.
Energy invested in a bound assembly must be balanced by charge, packet or another
explicit physical store in reference states. A different partition of the same
energy is part of the preparation contrast; it must not be described as identical
available fuel. Report initial charge, bond energy and local resource access.

There are three reference questions, kept separate:

1. **Primary thermal reference.** The undriven equilibrium measure conditioned
   on the common matter inventory and total energy E. At a fully resolved
   constant-energy shell this weights states by their physical multiplicity.
   It includes naturally occurring assemblies and useful alignments. It is not
   artificially conditioned to lack machinery.
2. **Dispersed preparation reference.** The same equilibrium measure additionally
   conditioned on no initial bonds, where that equal-energy shell exists. It
   isolates a specifically dispersed start. Conditioning is used only to prepare
   the root; subsequent dynamics are unrestricted. If exact matching is impossible,
   declare that before execution rather than silently supply extra fuel.
3. **Arrangement-only reference.** Randomize placement while retaining the root's
   bond inventory and charge configuration. This distinguishes spatial access
   from already possessing a catalytic seed. It is not called an unbound gas.

During equilibrium calibration, report each reference's bond distribution,
cluster size, density and initial energy allocation. If the full thermal
reference is mostly assembled, call it a thermal mixture rather than a gas
phase. The initially unbound reference remains its own gas-like preparation
test. A dilute-gas claim requires an actual dilute geometry and appropriate
thermal degrees of freedom; it cannot be obtained by renaming a dense lattice.

### Temperature and exact-energy conditioning

Add bath beta to the corridor. For a fixed Hamiltonian,

    mu_beta(x | H(x)=E) proportional to g(x) exp(-beta*E),

so the common Boltzmann factor cancels. **The primary exact-energy reference
weights do not change with beta.** Bath temperature still changes transition
rates, future assembly and disassembly, and the time required to reach useful
configurations. The unconditioned canonical measure's bond occupancy can vary
with beta; it is a calibration readout, not a replacement reference with a
different initial energy budget.

Choose beta values from the undriven calibration before any lushness ranking:
record canonical bond occupancy, barrier scales and assembly times for a
fixed candidate temperature grid, then retain points representing distinct
kinetic regimes. Report the exact-energy shell composition separately. For
this positive-bond-energy adapter, heating can make activated assemblies less
suppressed; the direction would differ for negative binding energy. Do not
make a temperature-general claim independent of the stated Hamiltonian.

The driven source is switched on at the same present in every case. Preparation
costs and any external positioning are reported. The primary conditional-frame
comparison takes these already prepared presents as given; a later common-start
decision experiment would have to include the time/work of creating them.
No free external organizer continues acting after the initial cut.

## 5. Compare known frames, not entropy of drawing a frame

For the fully specified root x and cadence delta, define

    ell_x(T;delta) = H(X_delta,...,X_T | X_0=x).

For a reference measure mu, report

    Delta_mu(T;delta) = ell_organized(T;delta)
                       - sum_x mu(x)*ell_x(T;delta).

This is a weighted average of conditional-root comparisons. Do not add H(mu),
pretend the reference initially has an extra randomizer, or count uncertainty
about which microstate the evaluator was given. Also report the distribution
of individual-root differences: mean superiority is not superiority to every
reference preparation. The thermal reference's mixture weights are fixed from
the physical calibration, not selected to make organization win.

Finite-history lushness is primary. T16/64/256/1024 and cadences0.125/0.25/0.5
are proposed queries, pending feasibility. State resolution is site/bond/carrier
resolved. A count projection is a secondary observation, generally non-Markov;
use exact hidden-process calculations or certified bounds, not a new count CTMC
unless its lumping and measurement are independently justified.

## 6. Rate and the gas-to-organization trajectory

In a finite irreducible world, organized and thermal roots share one limiting
rate. Verify it. Their finite-history difference can converge to a nonzero
preparation offset; later mixing does not erase earlier weighted histories.
Do not demand a permanent rate advantage between roots of that fixed Q.

Verify common reachable classes before using that conclusion. In reducible
worlds, different class-entry weights can legitimately give different rates;
report the invariant sector instead of pooling inequivalent references.

A useful organized seed may accelerate access, while a disordered root eventually
builds the same machinery. The experiment therefore measures both trajectories:
assembly time, resource capture, reinvestment, retained apparatus, and the
accumulated lushness difference. A rate increase while the population develops
is a finite transient profile, not evidence of distinct limiting entropy rates.

Sustained mechanism comparisons from the earlier compounding batch remain valid
as their own comparisons. Physically maintaining a gas phase through constraints
or boundaries would be a further intervention, with its own costs and law; it
is not substituted into the present same-law test.

### Primary self-assembly profile

Define comparable machinery from the native geometric rules before ranking:
G1 contains a bound catalytic pair; G2 additionally has the local source/carrier
alignment needed for transduction; G3 contains another paid assembly, relevant
to the larger-inventory extension. Specify contact radius, charge and geometry
requirements in the frozen target table. A positive path to future machinery
is not itself current machinery.

For each reference and the organized root, report the first-passage distribution
to these targets, mass already in the target at time0, hitting probability by
each deadline, median/quantiles where reached, and mean hitting time where finite.
Report unreachable mass and infinite means explicitly. The full thermal
reference can contain machinery initially; never drop its time0 mass to make
the reference seem slow. Any conditional "starting without G" statistic is
reported beside its conditioning probability, not substituted for the full
reference.

First arrival is not persistence. Report target occupancy at the same deadlines,
subsequent residence/return statistics and retained assembly. A long assembly
delay plus a positive offset supports a preparation advantage in a declared
generative window. A delay alone does not imply greater lushness: the many
routes by which thermal matter assembles may themselves supply extra breadth.

## 7. Structural and energetic account

Each comparison answers the programme's nine structural questions. Specifically:

- which local catalytic/transport relations exist and which become accessible;
- whether a newly formed assembly enables another paid construction;
- how many assemblies persist, not merely how often bonds flicker;
- whether source capture, recycled assemblies or bath input fund the process;
- what depletes, repairs or substitutes for the enabling structure;
- how often the thermal reference self-organizes, and on what timescale;
- what remains when the source stops or a catalytic assembly breaks;
- what finite lushness, common rate and preparation offset report;
- which comparison signs survive the declared temporal and state resolutions.

Count all monomer/cluster/carrier motion, chemical reverses, thermal fluctuations,
damage and source activity. No Rule A, protection bonus, survival reward,
generativity term or background subtraction. A chain-rule breakdown explains
which coordinates carry a difference; it never deletes an inconvenient term.

### Activity and temporal decomposition

For every root/reference comparison and physical horizon, report expected
state-changing jump count N_bar and the finite temporal coefficient F, using
the same reference clock throughout:

    ell(T;delta) = N_bar(T) log2(tau0/delta) + F(T) + o(1).

For a reference mixture, average each root-conditioned N_bar and F with the
same reference weights. Report Delta_N and Delta_F with exact sampled-profile
verification; F includes rate/timing and destination terms, not a pure
organization score. Same Q does not mean equal transient activity.

Also report exact embedded-jump-chain history entropy at fixed jump counts
8/32/128 where feasible, and the expected local destination-surprisal integral
over each physical horizon. The latter is not the full entropy of a
random-length jump sequence. Event-count uncertainty and conditional random-length
mark/timing entropy require their own estimator or bounds; do not claim the
fixed-jump calculation supplies them. All decompositions explain the original
lushness comparison without subtracting activity or matching entropy by definition.

Close the full energy and material balances. Report offered versus accepted work,
thermal imports/exports, construction dissipation, carrier transport, maintenance
and damage. The stationary funding fractions balance; all-cost-coverage>1 is
not a success requirement. No persistent bath subsidy is hidden behind a gross ROI.

## 8. Evidence and outcomes

The constructive target is a declared regime where an organized root:

1. enables paid recursive assembly and retained expansion;
2. has positive complete-history Delta against the primary thermal reference;
3. retains that sign across adequate declared resolutions over a specified window.

All three are evaluated separately. Generativity without greater lushness is an
adverse result for the proposed proxy relationship at that regime, not grounds
to relabel the mechanism nongenerative. Greater lushness without retained
enabling structure is a breadth result whose cause may be transport or churn.
Failure against the dispersed reference, or only against the full thermal
reference, is retained with its correct comparator scope.

Report no crossing, one crossing or multiple crossings. Exact finite-history
checks certify any claimed temporal/horizon crossing within numerical bounds.
The proposed parameter corridor varies common mobility, catalytic barrier
reduction, cost/yield and isolated damage. Every mobility setting applies to
both preparations through the same law. Native rates are never slowed only
for the reference. Choose the numerical grid from energy/geometry and engineering
feasibility before comparing preparation rankings.

Source lifetime is a subsequent matched law family, not a post-hoc truncation
of unfavorable histories. It can test whether a seed repays its preparation
advantage before the physical opportunity closes.

### Competing signed expectations to freeze

File two directional targets rather than assuming organization must win:

- **Access-dominant target:** at a calibrated regime with productive local
  catalysis and slow thermal acquisition of the same arrangement, predict a
  positive organized-minus-thermal offset and a positive finite-history interval.
- **Assembly/diversity target:** at a calibrated regime with weak useful
  coupling and rapid common diffusion/assembly, predict a negative offset or
  negative finite-history interval because dispersed matter has more transient
  motion and assembly paths.

Before scientific execution, the final protocol must identify the exact
parameter points, reference, cadence corridor, horizon/window and tolerance for
each signed prediction. The calibration can use chemistry, geometry, equilibrium
occupancy and physical assembly times; it must not inspect lushness signs to
choose those points. If neither signed regime is physically instantiated, report
that design limit before the run. A positive, negative, tie or unresolved outcome
is then scored without changing its target. Both directional predictions may
succeed in different regimes, or either may fail.

## 9. Staged implementation and memory gates

1. **Engineering calibration:** two monomers on four sites, one carrier.
   Verify diffusion, joining, reverse paths, symmetry factors and equilibrium.
   This cannot demonstrate recursive construction of a second apparatus.
2. **First exact same-law comparison:** four monomers on six sites, one carrier.
   It can hold two dimers and test organization versus disordered continuation.
   It is a bounded second-assembly probe, not a long generation cascade.
3. **Required compounding extension:** six monomers with at least one vacancy
   and one carrier. It permits a seed, descendants and further descendants.
   Profile both seven- and eight-site geometry before promising an exact grid.
   Density is reported; a densely packed lattice is not described as dilute gas.
4. **Larger/dilute extension:** more sites, carriers and constituents once the
   exact or certified sampled method supports the declared grain.

Enumeration must preserve physical states and positive-rate paths. Quotient
only bookkeeping symmetries, never actual source-relative positions or bonds.
The existing exact compiler, SSA event object, sparse access surveys, blocked
entropy routines and frozen-run workflow are reusable. Bond geometry, cluster
transport and the energy-shell reference builder are the new modules.

First smoke:120seconds/512MiB, or an explicitly revised cap based on the profile.
Report state count, generator/kernel memory, reachable classes and elapsed time.
Science is a later gate. If exact calculations do not fit, stop before rankings
and propose a certified estimator or another physical design. Do not reduce the
physics silently. SSA alone can establish growth statistics, but cannot certify
a Shannon-history ranking without an entropy estimator and error bounds.

Freeze the law, root recipes, reference measures, numerical tolerances, grain
corridor, predictions and budget after engineering. Keep a complete parameter
map and compact machine-readable summaries; raw matrices and trajectories remain
local and reconstructable. No automated deletion of referenced/pinned evidence.
