## C1a: same-law preparation advantage

Question: does organized matter have a positive complete-history advantage over
the thermal/dispersed reference under one law and matched initial energy/matter?

Status: completed in the spatial report linked above, with conditional positive
thermal-mixture results and negative unbound offsets at the primary cadence.
Finite irreducible worlds have a common limiting rate. The primary profile,
preparation offset and reference self-assembly clock answer this question.
Thermal matter may recover comparable apparatus while the earlier accumulated
history difference persists. Long self-assembly time does not ensure a positive
offset: the assembly paths can themselves make the reference lusher.

Temperature varies the subsequent dynamics. Its Boltzmann factor cancels from
a reference conditioned on one exact energy shell under a fixed Hamiltonian.
Canonical equilibrium occupancy is a separate physical calibration.

## Throughput-matched companion: separate experiment

This cannot be obtained by renaming existing rows or conditioning trajectories
after the result. It needs a declared matched boundary prescription.

1. Fix inventory, bath, packet energy, damage, construction bills, chemical
   state space and measurement. Retain full-loop and basal mechanisms as the
   already declared kinetic contrasts. Neither is called a gas preparation.
2. Define throughput precisely. Proposed primary quantity is accepted external
   resource work per physical time, J_in = E_packet * stationary packet-inflow
   flux. Captured useful energy and material throughput are separate readouts.
   Offered work is not accepted work. Equal input does not mean equal capture.
3. Determine each mechanism's feasible J_in range by varying only its declared
   source rate over a common calibration range. Inspect physical flow, not
   lushness, while constructing the match. Saturation can prevent a match;
   increasing supply cannot force a slow basal receiver to accept arbitrary work.
4. Select targets in the intersection of the feasible ranges by a fixed rule,
   for example three interior quantiles of that shared interval. Freeze target
   values, matched source rates, flow tolerance and source brackets before any
   ranking at those rates. Calibration failures remain in the report.
5. Evaluate exact finite histories and rates at the matched settings. Report
   the changed offered-source rates explicitly: this is a mechanism/boundary
   contrast at equal accepted work, not the same generator or identical offered
   boundary. Include the earlier same-offered-source comparison alongside it.
6. Report retained apparatus, paid/thermal construction, funding, capture,
   dissipation, expected event count and jump-chain diagnostics. A rate advantage
   at equal accepted work strengthens an access/conversion interpretation.
   It does not automatically remove an activity contribution.

Matching total path entropy would force the scalar tie and is not a valid
control. Matching throughput does not match activity. A later activity-matched
physical control must have costed stochastic transitions and no enabling loop;
it requires its own model specification, not subtraction of observed noise.
