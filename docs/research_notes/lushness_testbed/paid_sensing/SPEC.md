## Extension and legacy correction

stock-aware motor rule had idealized free observation. It did not remove the bit
or give up its entropy. B_X only accelerated existing movement without detecting
opportunity. It is an uninformed-actuation control, not access creation or a
strong generativity mechanism. Existing frozen results remain unchanged.

Keep the exploration adapter's 408 states, stochastic stocks, external boundary,
quantized body energy, spontaneous bit relaxation and identical bit-to-motor rule
v(1+k*s). Replace independent bit activation with a paid sensor-read module.
Each read costs0.5 energy even when the output equals the current bit. A new
activation additionally transfers0.5 from the body to the register. Existing
upkeep0.02/time while active and relaxation remain identical. Failed writes at
insufficient reserve still pay the read bill but cannot create a powered bit.
Body death stops sensing/movement; the full stochastic environment continues.

The cue g is whether the occupied outside patch is empty and the other stocked.
I writes1 with probability0.9 when g=1 and0.1 when g=0. It is an instantaneous
noisy cue, not a guarantee of profitable movement or knowledge of replenishment
rates. R writes1 according to two fixed probabilities conditional on old bit,
never on stocks. Every reading has the same clock and unit bills. Both use
exactly the same mobility module; motor observes only the retained bit. All stock
dynamics remain physical in both arms, including when inaccessible.
