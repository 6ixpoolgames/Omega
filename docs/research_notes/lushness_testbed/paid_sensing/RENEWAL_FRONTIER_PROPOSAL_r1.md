# Paid renewal and access frontier

Proposal for review, 14 October 2026. Not frozen, implemented or run.

## Research question

Does physically useful access become preferable to uncoupled stochastic variation
when captured energy can maintain the machinery that enables further access?
Map where this occurs, where it fails, and how the boundary changes with renewal
burden. Do not assume there is one crossing or that every capable substrate wins.

The previous paid-sensing result established a positive information–actuation
interaction in all eight pooled settings. The uncoupled random mechanism still
won fourteen of sixteen individual primary cases. That is the starting point.
R0 is a costed, organized mechanism, not a thermal gas or free noise source.

## 1. Physical extension

Fork a new adapter; keep both previous frozen fixtures unchanged. Reuse their
compiler, sensor, mobility, occupation, entropy and workflow interfaces, and
the resource-feedback adapter's conserved precursor/apparatus-pool pattern.

Add an apparatus count a, initially capacity1, and precursors M−a. Apparatus
supports harvesting, sensing and actuation. It wears into precursors, and paid
construction converts precursors back into apparatus using stored energy.
Construction stores its declared apparatus energy and dissipates the remaining
bill; dismantling returns material and only the declared recoverable energy.
Every forward/reverse pair and any driven boundary must be explicitly declared.
Keep positive basal assembly/decay channels; removing a powered repair pathway
must not quietly remove native thermal possibilities.

Harvesting transfers energy from a real packet into the store, with a declared
conversion loss. Intact harvesting apparatus can be powered by the incoming
packet itself even when the internal store is empty. That is passive physical
transduction, not an immortal controller. Reads, commands and movement still
require apparatus plus the appropriate internal energy bill. Apparatus wears
while operating or waiting, including while control is unpowered.

When apparatus is absent, retain the native weak uncatalyzed conversion and
assembly channels. They have declared positive rates, energy costs and reverse
channels. They cannot provide a free reconstruction of the activated register,
memory, location or stored energy. Report how much recovery they supply.

The loop is therefore:

    physical stocks → captured work → paid apparatus construction/maintenance
                    → functioning access channel → subsequent captured work

Use a conservative fixed energy store and material inventory in the first slice.
No construction enlarges the declared state space during the run. Capacity2 is
a later, separately frozen bounded-expansion test. Neither establishes open-ended
growth or cross-scale agency.

## 2. Renewal is a new physical interpretation

In the previous fixture e=0 terminated the modeled valuer. Keep that result.
In the new adapter, e=0 means an unpowered channel whose remaining physical
apparatus and material can persist. Returning functionality is energetic
reactivation or reconstruction. This is not a retrospective resurrection of the
old valuer and makes no identity or phenomenal-consciousness claim.

There is no abstract survival clock or reset. Any return must be an actual native
transition consuming the declared resources. All register relaxation, damage,
resource fluctuations and reconstruction history remain in the complete carrier.

## 3. Comparisons

Retain four fixed mechanisms with the same native stock law, offered gradient,
unit bills, hardware inventory and information/motor implementation:

- R0: paid stock-independent writing, basal motor only.
- I0: paid informative writing, basal motor only.
- RX: paid stock-independent writing driving the motor.
- IX: paid informative writing driving the identical motor.

Freeze the mechanical renewal rules common to all four before outcomes. First
use a common automatic costed assembly rule, so differences reflect whether
access supplies the loop. Controller choices over maintenance are a subsequent
policy-family expansion, not an unannounced extra observation/action for IX.

A paid-repair-pair ablation keeps all material, resource and basal channels
present and removes only the specified additional energy-coupled repair pair.
It is a between-mechanism/law diagnostic. Do not conflate it with initial-state
advantages under one fixed mixing generator.

Within each physical law, add matched initial preparations with apparatus present
versus precursor/energy allocation instead. Match material and total initial
energy, using representable store levels rather than adding unmodeled energy.
Any preparation ensemble and its degeneracy weights must be declared. In a finite
ergodic law, these roots can differ only by finite history profiles/offsets while
sharing the limiting rate. They are coarse preparation controls, not a certified
microscopic thermal gas baseline.

## 4. Stakes and burden sweep

Primary physical axes:

1. Offered resource throughput: five prospectively declared levels spanning
   underpowered, marginal and ample supply. Initial engineering candidates are
   total packet-offer rates0.15,0.3,0.6,1.2,2.4 per native time unit. Both arms
   receive the same rate at each point; accepted work need not match.
2. Renewal burden: two declared apparatus wear rates at fixed construction bill,
   material inventory and assembly mechanism. Choose the numerical rates using
   energetic feasibility and relaxation profiling, not entropy rankings.

Keep packet energy, temporal units, sensor error, sensing cadence and motor unit
bill fixed initially. This avoids changing the number of energy levels while
calling it a stakes effect. Use the existing slow paid sensor and cheap movement
as declared starting controls, not a claim that those values are physiologically
universal. Retain both initial stock arrangements; declare the replenishment
contrast before running. A wider sensing/cost/contrast grid follows only if the
first frontier is interpretable.

At high supply, saturation may make information less useful; at low supply,
renewal may never repay its burden. Consequently the favorable region may be
bounded or disconnected. The protocol must allow no crossing or multiple ones.
Do not define the sweep as an exercise guaranteed to find a win.

## 5. Primary question and decomposition

For every declared initial frame and horizon/cadence, the decisive comparison is

    Delta = ell(IX) − ell(R0).

Report IX−RX as the narrower information-under-actuation comparison, and retain
all four absolute candidate scores and winners. A positive IX−RX alone does not
establish that actuation pays over remaining uncoupled.

The exact diagnostic identity is Delta=C+A+G, where C=ell(I0)−ell(R0),
A=ell(RX)−ell(R0), and G=(ell(IX)−ell(I0))−(ell(RX)−ell(R0)). All terms are bits of
complete continuation; G includes interactions and is not an independently
defined pure access bonus. Never add energy bills directly to entropy or replace
the objective by G. Every cost matters through its physical consequences.

Finite conditional sampled-history lushness remains primary. Proposed horizons
16/64/256/1024 and cadences0.125/0.25/0.5 are numerical queries, not an asserted
physical cutoff. Keep hidden-register non-Markov bounds and temporal N/F
diagnostics. Rate/offset are secondary tests of sustained process contribution,
with recurrent-class and mixing analysis. A larger limiting rate alone does not
establish robust generativity or ethical desirability.

## 6. Structural evidence required

Pair the scalar frontier with:

- captured work and all sensing, actuation, conversion and construction bills;
- apparatus occupation, wear, repairs and unpowered intervals;
- whether preexisting apparatus enables capture that actually pays for later
  apparatus, rather than being replaced mainly by a basal thermal path;
- restoration probability, deadline functionality and duration of restored function;
- persistence under the declared wear burden and any recurrent collapse classes;
- resource/bit/energy/motor coordinate contributions, with one frozen attribution
  order, and actual changes to background resource dynamics.

Register separately: sustained or repeated enabling-loop operation, superiority
on complete finite lushness, temporal-grain robustness, and physical functioning.
None substitutes for another. A noise-favored result remains a result.

## 7. Predictions and failure targets

Freeze after engineering feasibility, before rankings:

- Some intermediate drive settings support repeated paid renewal and positive
  IX−R0 at declared horizons, with signs stable across the three cadences.
- Enabling the costed loop shifts that favorable region relative to the powered
  repair-pair ablation. No specific unique or monotone boundary is assumed.
- Higher wear shrinks or relocates the favorable region; score any exceptions.

Failure targets include a physically well-supplied renewing channel still losing
to R0, positive breadth supplied mostly by futile switching or repair churn,
indistinguishable repaired functioning with different breadth rankings, and
resolution-sensitive frontier signs. Do not remove those events from entropy.

If the entire grid fails energetic feasibility, that is a substrate limitation,
not a test of whether a viable generative loop is favored. Specify the feasibility
criterion from energy/material accounting in advance. Do not reject an unfavorable
but functional regime afterward by renaming it nongenerative.

## 8. Engineering/workflow gate

Version apparatus, transduction and construction modules separately. Keep sensing,
control family, physical law and analysis independently versioned. Explicit fields
and module dependencies must support later population and interaction networks;
no lineage semantics or utility weights are introduced now.

First capacity1 capped smoke, exact-source snapshots, complete energy/material
checks and native-sampler/kernel agreement. Profile the enlarged state space
before freezing case counts or runtime caps. Start with512MiB and120s smoke;
science batch budget is chosen from that profile. Batch or change solver strategy
if necessary; never prune physical paths or shrink an already frozen law.

Keep machine-readable preregistration and full compact parameter maps, including
nulls and failed predictions. Retain source/configuration/hashes in the repository;
large reconstructible snapshots stay in ignored local cache under the existing
cleanup policy. No scheduled deletion, commit, push or publication is authorized
by this proposal alone.
