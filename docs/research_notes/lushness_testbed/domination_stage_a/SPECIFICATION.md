## Assessment of Opus r1

The supplied [original proposal](OPUS_PROPOSAL_r1.md) is retained unchanged as
design provenance. This assessment uses it and the implemented first map;
the r3 design notes it references were not supplied here.

The strength curves, confinement targets, random transition controls and separate
subject histories extend the initial map usefully. Control is judged through its
physical consequences. “Forced harvesting”, “forced labour” and “totalitarian”
name interventions to investigate, not verdicts. None is assumed beneficial or
harmful. Cost and device upkeep already impose opportunity costs, even though
the world lacks uncertain future opportunities and the subject's own choices.
Trap avoidance therefore has no guaranteed positive sign at every cost.

Four revisions make the proposal executable without changing its question:

1. **Fuel bills are 0, 1 and 2 quanta.** The existing bank stores at most two.
   Bills of 0.1 and 0.3 require additional physical energy states; rounding them
   or randomizing a unit bill would implement a different law. A bill of three
   is unaffordable here and would silently reproduce idle. Those refinements
   remain a subsequent reservoir module. Zero cost is a disclosed limit case;
   rejected null events do not produce entropy.
2. **Random controls are directed physical location edges.** Each ordinary role
   target has three of the twelve edges. Eight uniformly sampled, reproducible
   three-edge sets are reused across role comparisons. The all-mobility target
   has only one possible same-size set: itself. Random labels do not generate
   extra simulations or outcomes. Steering all mobility redirects into native
   local chemistry; it does not freeze every subject process.
3. **Preparation comparisons remain preparations.** The ordinary and unassembled
   roots have the same initial total energy and constituent pools. They can
   assemble under the idle law. Comparing them with an intervention is useful,
   but does not model the cost, delay or identity consequences of removing and
   replacing an agent. No excision license follows from this comparison.
4. **The energy-field check is a conservative mechanism contrast.** Its device
   adds `U*f*1[S.location=target]` to total energy. Every thermal reaction,
   including device formation and loss, uses that energy. The existing device
   upkeep remains; a static conservative field receives no invented continuous
   work bill. Consequently it cannot be honestly fuel-matched to rejection.
   Report it separately, from the ordinary root with the device initially
   absent, rather than selecting an arbitrary “matched” field cost.

## Subject bounds

The projected process remains hidden-Markov. For each cadence, construct the
exact joint distribution of `(Y1,Y2)` conditional on `X0`. The lower bound sums
`H(Yt | X[t-2],Y[t-1])` after the exact first term. The upper uses
`H(Yt | Y[t-2],Y[t-1])`, pooled over time by conditional-entropy concavity.
Intersect with the original full-state lower and one-observation upper bounds.
No midpoint ranks a subject. Validate against exact enumeration on small worlds.
This is bounded-depth tightening; deeper blocks require a separate resource
assessment and unresolved comparisons are retained.

## Physical analysis

Report full history scores, both subject intervals, history MI bounds, captures,
construction, apparatus, occupancy, fuel and suppression. Realized suppression
is query-side suppressed/redirected attempts divided by expected native subject
state-changing events under idle. This ratio is not a probability and may exceed
one; zero denominators are undefined. Paid blocking changes the dominator bank
while leaving the subject unchanged.

Capture ledgers count one energy quantum per capture; `net_capture_D` is forward
minus reverse capture, **before** friction and upkeep. The gain/cost summary uses
incremental capture minus friction minus **incremental** device upkeep versus
idle. It is a partial energy account, not the dominator's lushness or complete
income; full bank ledgers and energy residuals accompany it.

Report finite strength crossing brackets, cadence dependence, all certified
subject/global divergences and preparation comparisons. Fuel matching between
role and random curves is interpolation within observed support, not exact
work-controlled dynamics. Enumerate every branch if spending is nonmonotonic;
no extrapolation. Preserve the sampled data behind each interpolation.
Fixed-family optimizer choices keep the physical bill fixed and include the
same idle reference. Cost is a law/mechanism assumption, not a free action that
the controller may optimize away. Candidate counts disclose the smaller
six-target family in the cost arms; compare matched candidates across bills.

`N̄`, `F` and the asymptotic temporal crossover are diagnostics. Expected local
destination surprisal is not the entropy of an entire random-length jump history.
Do not rename it jump-chain entropy. The full latter readout remains outside
this batch's exact budget.
