# Next slice: internal variation coupled to exploration

Draft, 14 October 2026. Not frozen and not run. The temporal audit is complete.
Question: does connecting powered internal variation to physical resource access
change its breadth contribution and its functional consequences? A gain is not
automatically welfare or generativity; a loss is an informative result.

## Native extensions

Add separate resource-patch and costed mobility modules to the signal adapter.
Keep energy, metabolism, injury, charging/upkeep, hazard and continuing background
after death. Use actual locations H, O0, O1, with initially binary packet stocks
at O0/O1. Both stocks replenish and decay stochastically in every arm, even when
unaccessed. Foraging consumes a packet, transfers declared energy to the valuer,
and pays an attempt bill. Movement changes location and pays an energy bill.
Do not count physically identical action labels as alternatives.

Retain the current energy scale and root inventory. Profile state count and
solver memory before scientific freeze. Use a capped smoke. If needed, change
solver or batch execution rather than pruning rare paths. Future stock levels,
population, spatial organization and dependency modules extend these contracts.

## Controls

B is an unvarying register; B_R is independent costed flicker; B_X reads that
same register to modulate real movement between patches. B_X at zero coupling
must reproduce B_R's generator exactly. Both movement directions remain possible.
All arms retain basal movement, the same unit bills and the same external supply.
Hold bit rates fixed, and report downstream occupancy/energy differences rather
than assuming matching survives the intervention.

Declare coupling direction and strength before outcomes. The minimal B_X biases
switching without knowing which patch is productive. Stock-aware response is a
separate controller. Include a constant-mobility control to distinguish coupling
from simply moving more, and a stock-aware oracle benchmark for accessible
capacity. Never make useful movement certain or award an exploration bonus.

Compare homogeneous/heterogeneous patch supply with matched total offered
resource and work. Accepted work may differ through access; report it explicitly.
This is not equal-absorbed-work conversion efficiency unless separately matched.

## Readouts and freeze

Primary: complete finite conditional sampled-history lushness, including all
represented environment and register dynamics. Secondary: signal-hidden
non-Markov bounds, temporal N/F coordinates and exact crossover brackets, food
capture, survival, location/stock access, movement flux and energy cost. Reuse
the existing frozen workflow and common exact/sampled transition kernel.
No entropy subtraction, Rule A, survival reward or semantic bonus.

Freeze a small grid of movement cost, coupling strength and patch contrast,
horizons/cadences and hit criteria after engineering calibration. Pair scalar
differences with the native dependency/resource account. B_X may spend energy,
narrow alternatives or access useful capacity; distinguish those mechanisms.

The existing mortality law still absorbs into valuer death. Inflow alone does
not remove eventual extinction in a finite world. Same-law limiting rates may
therefore be the same background rate, leaving finite offsets. A sustained
process extension needs separately specified, costed native repair or
reconstruction. Reconstruction does not establish preservation of identity.
This binary-patch test cannot establish open-ended compounding.

## Acceptance checks

- Zero coupling reproduces B_R exactly.
- Identical resource/hazard/supply modules and unit bills in every control.
- Movement obeys the energy ledger and ceases without living actuation.
- Stocks remain physical and stochastic after death or when inaccessible.
- Existing signal/temporal tests pass; source-identical capped smoke precedes science.
