## Common law and genuine persistence

Distinguish a fixed induced generator with different roots from an encompassing
physical controlled law with different policies. Finite mixing fixed-generator
root differences can be bounded offsets; policy changes can induce distinct rates.
Neither fact alone establishes compounding generativity.

For a declared adequate model with existing limits, write
ell_pi(T)=h_pi*T+c_pi+o(1). A positive difference of h is a sustained breadth gain
IN THAT MODEL. A high rate conditional on the machinery still working is not its
unconditional rate. Evaluate complete trajectories through breakdown, trapping,
repair, loss of control and all residual thermal states. Do not stop at failure,
condition on survival, or delete the tail.

A metastable high-rate phase that eventually enters a common closed class has no
asymptotic rate advantage; its benefit is in the offset/profile. A robust lower
operating rate can win by lasting longer, but this is a testable outcome, not a
new protection bonus. Some fragile policies may still win after full accounting.

## Operational persistence gate

Before optimization, declare disturbance laws and uncertainty ranges using physical
parameters and feasibility, not winners. Include recovery costs and common drive
boundaries. Evaluate candidate policies under each law without reoptimizing them;
also report separately the optimum available if the law were known.

Report both when applicable; the first is diagnostic, the second a physical scope
for the primary continuation comparison:

1. **Sustained regime:** certify the relevant communicating classes, unconditional
   rates and numerical errors. Call a pairwise advantage robust only if its lower
   error bound is positive throughout the frozen disturbance/parameter set and the
   adequate resolution corridor. A finite grid supports only the tested points;
   interval-wide claims require bounds or other certification. State modeled drive
   duration. No finite experiment certifies persistence in our universe forever.
2. **Finite opportunity:** include resource depletion or a physical shutdown process
   in the law and evaluate complete continuation to the declared opportunity window.
   A fixed window is a model assumption; an endogenous/random stopping condition
   must be evaluated jointly with the histories. Do not average conditional entropy
   at each stopping time and discard information about stopping itself.

Whether or not persistence is established, report finite-horizon comparisons and
crossings as primary. Disagreement across horizons/uncertainty
scenarios remains visible. Do not invent a scalar risk penalty or secretly switch
to worst-case optimization: the robustness gate is a claim criterion. Any decision
rule under unresolved uncertainty requires its own declared frame and protocol.

The secondary rate-first comparator gives any positive permanent rate difference
priority over finite offsets. Robustness does not mathematically remove that
lexical consequence. Report offset losses and crossover horizons, including large
sacrifices; do not claim robust persistence alone resolves the ethical challenge.

## Thermal matching and mechanism attribution

Use matched material, drive, boundary prescription and native kinetics. Same law
does not imply equal thermal path entropy: organization changes occupancies and
accessible reactions. If B is a background HISTORY and G the remainder,
H(B,G)=H(B)+H(G|B). Cancellation requires identical marginal B-history laws, not
just a common temperature. Independence is sufficient but unnecessary.

Retain all randomness in the primary objective. Do not subtract a background or
declare a churn-driven gain invalid as breadth. Instead distinguish:
* breadth gain;
* enabling/generative mechanism supported by access and causal intervention tests;
* ethical proxy success or adverse behavior.

Use physical mechanism ablations and fixed-policy replay to attribute gains. These
are separate law contrasts, not same-law preparation comparisons. Event ledgers can
describe activity, but they are not automatically additive entropy contributions
for sampled state histories. Any chain-rule decomposition declares variables and
ordering; no unobserved event labels may inflate the primary grain.

## Resolution corridor

Cadence and state grain are separate axes. Freeze temporal sampling and physically
defined state projections before rankings. Include the primary full declared state,
pure relabeling checks, and justified coarse projections. A projection is adequate
for a claim only if it retains the distinctions needed for the specified access,
damage and intervention queries, exactly or within a prespecified error. This is
claim-relative adequacy, not a proven universal grain rule.

Bookkeeping equivalences require exact invariance. Physically different observable
grains can legitimately differ. Do not manufacture missing molecular identities to
run a 'fine' arm; they need an adapter with a validated projection. Quotienting a
gate while it affects future access requires memory or hidden-Markov treatment.

Report Delta ell(T,grain,cadence) primarily and Delta h(grain,cadence) secondarily,
with uncertainty, ties and sign changes. Strict positive
sign across the frozen adequate corridor is a robust gain at that horizon or rate,
respectively. A sign reversal
limits the claim's resolution scope; it does not prove the physics has failed.
