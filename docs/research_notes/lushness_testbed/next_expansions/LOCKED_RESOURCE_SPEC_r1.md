# Companion fixture: access to a locked resource

Draft implementation direction, 2026-10-14. Schedule alongside reserve-sensitive
control, with separate feasibility and preregistration gates. No new run authorized.

Question: does working organization open paid access to a resource that produces
more complete-continuation breadth than a matched unorganized preparation/control?
This tests a concrete enabling mechanism. A breadth increase alone does not establish
open-ended compounding or ethical adequacy.

## Native mechanism

Add locked/available resource occupancy with fixed total material, explicit energy,
capacity and conversion channels. A high barrier suppresses basal exchange; it does
not automatically imply an unfavorable equilibrium. Keep basal forward/reverse
rates positive and thermodynamically paired. Working apparatus can enable a paid
fuel-coupled release pathway with its own reverse event and chemical-work balance.
Do not speed only a forward thermal rate and call that free catalysis. Account for
fuel consumption, downstream use, depletion, maintenance and eventual apparatus loss.

Use the same state space, drive and reactions in compared preparations. Organized
and unorganized roots must have matched inventory and a declared energy budget;
include the assembly/preparation cost or disclose the initial energy difference.
Retain the whole physical state in the primary entropy calculation, including the
reservoir and residual background. No reservoir-access reward or thermal subtraction.

## Two different scientific comparisons

**Finite store, different roots:** use one fixed policy and induced generator.
Report unlock/use first passage, fuel/work costs and complete history breadth at
the common horizon grid. If the finite chain mixes, asymptotic rates must agree;
test that as a calibration. A slow positive basal route is not a separate closed
class. An enormous transient advantage is meaningful without pretending it is a
permanent rate gain. Access alone is insufficient: gains may be outweighed by costs.
Do not infer a one-shot exhausted store from finiteness alone: this fixture's
reactions are reversible. Equal root rates require the same limiting recurrent
mixture (in particular a common irreducible class). Finite reducible laws can retain
root-dependent rates. Verify the classes rather than invoking a theorem for every
model. The finite profile remains relevant evidence for finite access; it cannot
establish sustained rate enhancement through preparation under a mixing generator.

**Maintained supply, different control processes:** only as a separately declared
driven arm, physically replenish/relock the store under matched external boundary
rules and include those transitions and work. Compare feasible embodied policies
under this common encompassing controlled law. Distinct induced generators may
have distinct sustained rates. Report finite-horizon lushness primarily, rates
secondarily, and unconditional breakdown/recovery. The unorganized root under the
same mixing generator still cannot sustain a different rate merely through its
initial condition. A no-apparatus or disabled-feedback LAW is a mechanism ablation,
not a same-generator root comparator.

## Next engineering increment: maintained locked inflow

Retain the15-state closed-store fixture as a regression/calibration. Add a separately
versioned open-boundary adapter with a bounded local buffer and explicit occupancy
for locked supply, usable supply and depleted/waste material. Declare resource and
apparatus precursor pools separately from charged/spent energy carriers. The current
coarse apparatus/fuel slot budget does not supply a complete material ledger for
this extension. Forecast states before implementing; an open boundary is not permission
to reflect or truncate reactions at an arbitrary population count.

Model inlet/outlet transitions with source chemical potentials or explicit work,
thermal bath temperature and local capacities. Locked material may leave unused
through a declared decay/export route. Basal unlocking remains small and positive;
paid apparatus-assisted release has a thermodynamically paired reverse channel.
Usable material supports a declared downstream reaction and, if feedback is being
tested, physically costed construction/maintenance. Track waste and outflow; do not
label a high barrier as decay or fuel production. No-op arrivals at a full buffer
are not extra history branches. Record offered boundary conditions and accepted
flux separately: identical inlet parameters need not yield identical accepted
throughput when organization changes occupancy.

Maintain the same boundary prescription, material capacities, temperature and
initial energy/resources across the capable and pathway-ablated arms. Ablation
removes the complete paid forward/reverse pathway, not all background access and
not the apparatus's costs. Keep apparatus variables and other native dynamics in
both arms. Equal received energy cannot be claimed merely from equal nominal
inlet rates; disclose any resulting difference in accepted flux/work as a physical
effect and include conversion efficiency/cost readouts.

Primary lushness comparison remains complete finite-horizon history breadth.
For the narrower sustained-mechanism question, preregister Delta h>0 across the
tested cadences as a separate success criterion, allowing failure and sign flips.
This between-law comparison identifies the effect of the pathway within the toy;
it does not show that initial organization permanently outperforms disorder under
one induced generator. Retain same-law organized/unorganized profiles as a second
comparison. Policy-dependent sustained effects within an encompassing controlled
law are also legitimate, so capability is not necessarily only a property of a
different fundamental law.

A positive rate increment alone can reflect faster cycling. Report downstream
utilization, functioning/recovery, net input/output, maintained enabling capacity
and fuel/work costs. Diagnose whether the opened resource actually sustains the
enabler rather than merely adding a new stochastic channel. Randomness continues
to count; no churn subtraction or semantic generativity bonus is introduced.
This adapter tests a mechanism of sustained access, not open-ended expansion.

Implementation and fixed-controller capped smoke precede a separate scientific
freeze. The completed finite-store smoke does not certify this new boundary model.

## Controls, scope and acceptance

Retain basal release, inactive actuation, existing maintenance/control policies,
and a disclosed release-pathway ablation. Freeze barrier/fuel parameters using
fixed-controller feasibility checks before rankings. Do not tune until the
organized arm wins. Allow release activity with no downstream gain, harmful costs,
and advantages confined to a particular horizon as explicit possible outcomes.

Acceptance: conservation/work accounting, paired rates, unembodied-action absence,
legacy disabled-module equality, state/memory forecast and capped exact smoke.
Start with the smallest adapter capable of assembly and release, named explicitly.
Freeze counts, roots, candidates, numerical tolerances and budget before science.
Cadence checks use0.125,0.25,0.5 at matched horizons where feasible; state-grain
robustness awaits a validated alternative representation. Do not claim it from
cadence checks. Mechanism evidence includes resource utilization and downstream
access after release, not just a positive entropy difference.
