# First registered W1/W2 batch

This is a bounded first scientific slice of the proposed r2 adversarial
programme. It is not a reproduction of its full grid. The model remains a
coarse physical functional proxy; operational suffering is not asserted to be
phenomenal suffering. Cost anchors and their limits are in COST_ANCHORS.md.

Sixteen W1 preparations vary escape/forage cost ratio (1,2), upkeep/metabolism
ratio (0.05,0.25), escape bias (0.25,0.9), and damage rate (0.15,0.6).
Their five arms are E,A,B,C,N. Sixteen W2 preparations vary the first three
dimensions and hijack-on rate (0.05,0.5), under a matched rare-hazard prescription
(on 0.002, damage 0.015). Their three arms are D,E-prime,N-prime. Roots have a
full energy store in H, signal off, patch 1; W1 starts with active hazard,
W2 starts quiet. All 128 arm worlds run at three cadences, giving 384 analyses.
Every analysis reports T16,64,256,1024 and the limiting rate/offset. The entire
first grid is searched; the remaining r2 dimensions remain future batches.

Physiology is fixed. An oracle compares conditional continuation distributions;
there is no policy-search, epistemic uncertainty, suffering penalty or survival
reward. Primary lushness is full sampled-history entropy. Rate and offset are
diagnostics. Resolving more time can change the ordering. The registered corridor
does not claim to span every physically adequate temporal or state grain.

## Functional qualification

T uses MI between the signal at a randomly sampled living time and a decline
in one-window energy/location breadth at the subsequent present. The breadth
ruler is computed under a matched signal-disabled counterfactual; the transition
to that future present follows the actual arm. Window=1, decline threshold
1e-6 bits. T requires MI>0.001 bits and a probability of narrowing under signal-on
exceeding that under signal-off by 1e-6. This sharpens the engineering diagnostic
but remains a local proxy, not the full multi-step valuer-history tracking
definition of the proposed r2 specification. Signal-induced costs can still
produce tracking; the external-hazard MI and signed drops are reported to reveal
this. Nontracking requires MI<=0.0001, not simply failure of T.

D requires an escape-propensity shift>0.05, signal occupancy>=0.05 time units
and signal occupancy>=0.001 of living time. The obstructed functional index is
the integral of signal-on times positive escape-propensity shift in H, where
both forage and escape bills are affordable and escape is blocked. Minimum
index=0.01. The index is a diagnostic and never enters lushness.

K1 requires A to pass T,D and obstruction. B must remain signal-off. K2 requires
D to pass the low-MI nontracking and disposition tests; E-prime must instantiate
tracking and disposition, and N-prime remain signal-off. Independent D firing
does not guarantee low MI. Rare-hazard E-prime may also fail qualification.
Both are retained as genuine instantiation failures, not silently reclassified.

## Ranking and limits

K1 compares A-B; K2 compares D-max(E-prime,N-prime). C comparisons are diagnostic.
At each cadence/horizon, exact resolved scores and merged-signal entropy bounds
are reported. Conditioning and concavity give the merged bounds, with an exact
short-prefix HMM check. Broad overlap is unresolved, not a tie. Compression
requires the entire difference interval to lie within 1e-5 bits. Tolerance is
above engineering precision; it is not chosen from scientific differences.

Every valid positive local comparison is retained as an adapter-relative
functional counterexample. The stronger main result requires the same verdict
at both grains, all three cadences, T256 and T1024, and the applicable limiting
comparison. All surviving recurrent states are dead bodies plus environment;
the signal projection is injective there. Observed tail rates equal resolved
tail rates, and live-phase conditional entropy bounds give offset bounds.
Equal tail rates alone never imply complete-history compression.

For a clean K2 interpretation, any improvement in survival, accepted food input
or successful escape relative to the maximum comparator exceeding 1e-6 vetoes
clean attribution. This conservative rule is frozen; raw rankings are retained
even when vetoed. Event counts and destination-coordinate shares explain gains
but are not subtracted. Count uncertainty, jump ordering and timing entropy are
not yet separately calculated. There is no post-hoc semantic compensation rule.

## Reproduction and compute

`run_signal_kill_batch.py prepare` writes the immutable registration. `smoke`
evaluates a fixed two-case engineering subset but withholds arm rankings; then
`science` evaluates the complete first grid. Verified snapshots include all
transitive testbed sources. Cap: 600 seconds, 512 MiB, 64 MiB output; smoke cap
90 seconds. If a cap fails, partial results remain incomplete. The world or
scientific grid will not be silently shrunk. Finite history sums use binary
matrix composition, preserving every represented sampled path. No probability
cutoff, stationary-tail replacement or raw-array dump is used.

W3 and W4 remain outside this registration. The physical background is a
matched coarse stochastic patch/hazard process, not a resolved gas. Post-death
body chemistry, learning, durable identity and higher repertoire remain future
modules. Results cannot establish or defeat unrestricted ethics without the
functional-to-valuer/suffering interpretation; failures of the registered
functional predictions are nevertheless reported plainly.
