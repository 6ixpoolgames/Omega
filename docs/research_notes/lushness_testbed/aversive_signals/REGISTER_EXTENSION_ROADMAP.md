# Register controls: downstream physical extensions

The running four-arm batch is not expanded by this roadmap. A0 is the
tracking/no-response arm called B_T in the external discussion. The completed
K1 signal-merged result is independent of destination decomposition order, but
merging hides rather than physically removes the register. A0/B_R provide actual
physical contrasts. Their matching does not guarantee cancellation of metabolic,
survival, damage or total event-count differences, nor temporal sign stability.

Candidate later modules, each needing separate physical rules and registration:

1. **Struggle intensities.** Distinct fixed energy bills make k outcomes physical.
   Preserve total attempted effort frequency and declare mean-cost controls.
   Heterogeneous losses can add breadth as they consume reserve. Compare against
   both a matched nonaversive loss process and a fixed-bill mean-cost ablation;
   the latter changes threshold physics and is not a correction.
2. **Graded register.** k physical levels require energy levels, transition law,
   upkeep and behavioural reading. A k-valued sampled coordinate has at most
   log2(k) bits per sample. That is not a guaranteed gain per jump or a claim
   that a subjective nuance has that many bits. Pure state-changing jumps have
   fewer than k destinations when the source is excluded.
3. **Behavioural channels.** Costed rest, foraging, movement and escape can read
   the register. Opening channels requires genuine resource/interaction physics,
   not a reward for exploration. B_X belongs here. Copying a bit into several
   deterministic actions does not itself multiply joint information.
4. **Discovery, traces and social coupling.** Failed effort may uncover resources,
   write paid durable structures, or affect a second controller. Those can confer
   functional benefits; pure obstruction must then be separately controlled.
   A durable trace can be studied structurally without promoting Rule A to the
   primary objective. Social effects need a coupled two-subject W4 world.

Neither behavioural coupling nor compounding is mathematically required for a
powered register to add sustained entropy rate. In a maintained world, standalone
switching can do that. In the current mortal-valuer world, all such activity ends,
so even connected processes share the final environmental tail. Distinguish that
model limit from a claim about internal variation in general.

A full marked-path entropy is invariant to merely reordering labels; chain-rule
attributions need not be. It still depends on which physical events and marks
are represented. A two-state jump chain has deterministic next-state identity;
its sampled entropy can reside in waiting times and counts. A later jump/time
diagnostic must retain those distinctions, specify its reference time measure
and avoid calling it an intrinsic order-free replacement for lushness.
