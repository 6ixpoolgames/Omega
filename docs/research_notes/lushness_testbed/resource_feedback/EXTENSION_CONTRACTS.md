# Population and composition: extension contracts

The present engineering supports modular native rules, named subsystem products,
explicit cross-subsystem events, parameterized population/material counts and one
compiled transition law for exact propagation and event sampling. The new feedback
world has apparatus/precursor and charged/spent pools; construction changes the
active population at a carrier cost. Capacity is a physical inventory constraint,
not permission to truncate an oversized numerical state space.

Executable checks cover capacities one and two, heterogeneous subsystem products,
paired native energy/degeneracy accounting, independent entropy additivity and
joint carrier exchange with declared support. Independent composition follows
from the product law, without a composition bonus. Coupled events must declare
rates, supports, resource updates, reverses and work before their consequences
are scored. Interaction-energy models require a joint physical adapter that
updates local rate laws consistently, rather than simply appending an energy term.

Population expansion remains bounded by the fixed precursor pool. Open-ended
growth requires explicit material inflow and a changing or unbounded inventory;
reaching a two-unit cap does not establish open-ended compounding. Coarse population
counts do not assume lineage or valuerhood. Individual distinctions can be added
later if the native dynamics make them physically consequential.

Cross-scale emergence requires more than subsystem products. Future components
need declared containment/incidence, reciprocal micro/macro influences and physical
resource exchange. Higher-level descriptions should initially be derived views of
the native whole, not another independent stochastic process double-counting the
same events. Apparatus/agent/community names are not extent weights.

A strong-lumping check now rejects a proposed macrostate map when constituent
states have unequal outgoing macro laws. Passing certifies macro transition
closure, not preservation of fine-history lushness. Every coarse readout must
retain its projection and account for discarded distinctions. Two levels must not
both score the same physical branching independently.

Scalability roadmap:

1. Separate native state validation/event law from optional finite enumeration.
   Exact kernels still require quadratic storage; sampling must use the same law.
2. Expand precursor/resource buffers and subsystem incidence before adding lineage.
   Use count compression only under exchangeability and a verified law/readout contract.
3. Add joint enabling, shared-resource competition and substitution as native coupling
   modules; compare their effects with the independent-product control.
4. Add controller observation/memory/action costs at their actual physical supports.
5. Add explicit micro/macro maps and feedback; identify predictive sufficiency and
   hidden state before claiming a Markov macro law or cross-scale agency.

Current scope is finite classical kinetics with a shared time parameter. This is
not a relativistic/QFT realization. The contracts are adapter boundaries; a later
causal/quantum adapter must supply its own compatible propagation and readouts.
The programme remains frame/field first, with actual full-continuation lushness
primary and structural analysis explaining what generates it.
