# Resource-use feedback: next physical module (draft, not frozen)

The current maintained-resource law cannot power its enabler from resource use:
USABLE has energy 0 and WASTE energy 0.25, whereas a charged carrier costs 2.
Adding free recharge to that event would violate the intended energy accounting.
The next model needs a matched, explicitly energetic resource prescription.

One finite candidate retains the existing resource/apparatus/carrier coordinates:
EMPTY=0, LOCKED=5, USABLE=4, WASTE=0; functioning assembly=2; charged carrier=2.
These are proposed energy units at beta=1, not fitted empirical chemistry.

| Native route | Energy change | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| LOCKED -> USABLE | -1 | Basal thermal release, retained in every arm |
| LOCKED + charged -> USABLE + spent, assembly present | -3 | Existing costed access form |
| USABLE -> WASTE | -4 | Uncoupled background processing, retained in every arm |
| USABLE + spent -> WASTE + charged, assembly present | -2 | Proposed resource-use feedback |
| Precursor + charged -> functioning assembly + spent | 0 | Costed assembly/repair form |

Each internal route has its native reverse with paired rate ratio exp(-deltaE).
Assembly presence catalyses both directions without changing their equilibrium
ratio. The loop restores its carrier after paid access; net LOCKED -> WASTE
releases 5 units. Assembly repair is a competing use of that carrier and must
retain its reverse. This supplies a physically accounted mechanism for recursive
access and upkeep, not an extra score for the cycle.

Both loop-capable and paired-feedback-ablated arms must retain basal release,
uncoupled processing, thermal carrier excitation/relaxation and material inventory.
External charged-carrier drive should be disabled in both matched arms so that
it does not independently fund the claimed upkeep loop; weak native thermal
excitation remains available. Resource inflow/outflow supplies the declared
boundary work. Accepted energy input can differ with occupancy and must be
reported. Equal absorbed-work conversion would need a separate boundary test.

Ablate both directions of the feedback route only. Also compare a matched
non-catalytic route if separating loop organization from adding another stochastic
reaction channel. Freeze kinetic constants, controls, preparation, physical grain,
energy ledger and the contrast before any ranking. Do not inherit the old
resource energies and silently attach a charge increment.

Read full finite-history lushness alongside access/feedback/repair cycle flux,
functional availability, functional recovery, accepted work, exported heat and
hardware turnover. Test whether downstream use sustains its own enabler rather
than merely generating extra activity. A one-assembly finite model establishes
bounded self-maintenance at most; open-ended compounding needs a separate
material-growth extension. Damage conclusions from the current batch must remain
separate from this future energy/coupling prescription.
