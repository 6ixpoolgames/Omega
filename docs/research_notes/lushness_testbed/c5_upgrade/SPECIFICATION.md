# C5 physical upgrade

Implementation and capped experiment specification, 16 October 2026.
Finite sampled-history lushness is primary. D/S marginal histories are bounded
as hidden-Markov observations; no chain-rule share or midpoint substitutes for them.
No commit or publication is part of execution.

## Physical modules

1. **Funded pump.** A binary fuel store of energy 0.5 is reversibly charged
   from D's bank. Contact transport consumes that store; its actual reverse
   restores it. A common ordinary apparatus role powers native resource capture.
   Passive, extraction-assisted and donation-assisted modes share states, sources,
   thermal routes and charging physics. Both directions are accelerated by the
   catalyst. There is no external pump-affinity work boundary. Net charging cost,
   transport and owned-energy changes decide whether the intended object exists.
2. **Independent opportunity.** A second packet fluctuates in every arm and
   receives its own common maintained inflow. In the pocket S can spend banked
   energy to construct an access device. The device permits capture after S
   leaves; device upkeep draws energy. Thermal formation/dissolution and reverse
   refunds remain physical and are measured separately from paid construction.
   Concretely, upkeep here is reversible bank/bath exchange enabled by the device,
   with its signed net overhead reported. Successful payment does not gate device
   persistence. Capture itself converts a two-energy packet into one stored unit;
   the remaining energy goes to the bath. This is a passive access structure,
   not a separate actively maintained machine with a paid-repair failure state.
   This is a coarse binary-device adapter, not a claim of open-ended discovery.
   A conservative trap-region field is swept against the same no-field world;
   source intensity is varied without suppressing the background in any arm.
3. **Permanent material loss.** An explicit sink prevents reconstruction of the
   sole S apparatus unit. Environment, movement and bank fluctuations continue.
   Loss is an explicitly irreversible driven boundary, disabled in the equilibrium
   check. The analysis permits transients leading to one closed residual class.

Modules can coexist; extensions expose their actual physical coordinates to
subject observers, energy validation and history queries. Query counters do not
create histories. Symmetric fractional banks are supported. The extra device
is a coarse state with reversible thermal assembly, not an atomistic mass model.

## Matched-preparation and active sensitivity follow-ups

`run_c5_followups.py supplied-pump` repeats the three pump mechanisms with D's
bank initially at two and S's at zero. It tests the suggested resource-rich donor
preparation, without changing the law or adding entropy for selecting a root.
Each contrast has the same exact initial state; it is separate from the balanced
root comparison. An initial donation advantage need not persist at long horizons.

`run_c5_followups.py opportunity-active` compares idle, movement-only steering
and blocking with the independent source at 0.12. All three use the friction
hardware role. The primary conservative-field family used the ordinary hardware
role; therefore compare each family with its own idle reference, not directly as
equal-capture replacements of one mechanism by another.

The historical crossover probe retains the original chemistry-inclusive law
explicitly. It reproduces the original T256 value, solves for rate and offset,
and queries finite sampled histories near the implied horizon estimate. It
does not turn that historical steering case back into mobility evidence.

## Execution

`scripts/run_c5_upgrade.py` supplies pump, opportunity, loss, audit-controls and
cost-boundary batches. Every batch first receives a smaller engineering smoke.
Science uses the declared inventory, three cadences 0.125/0.25/0.5 and horizons
16/64/256/1024. No shrinking an inventory after inspecting rankings.
Two concurrent workers maximum, each with two internal entropy threads,
900-second timeout, 3 GiB process-tree memory and 32 MiB output cap. Frozen
source and config hashes accompany compact results; full kernels are discarded.
An engineering error stops the batch. A physical contrast that fails to instantiate
donation/profit/construction remains a result, not a reason to force parameters.

## Readouts

Whole-field finite profiles; certified D/S difference intervals; full limiting
rates and stationary marginal-rate bounds; expected physical jumps and fine-time
finite term; native D activity attribution; source work and heat; paid/reverse
transport; fuel-store charging/refunds; device creation/dissolution; access
capture and upkeep; owned-energy closure; apparatus function and sink arrival.
These distinguish breadth due to exchange activity from material return.
Cross-arm work and activity are not assumed matched merely because boundaries
or capacities match. Cadence stability is reported as a temporal audit, not full
state-grain invariance. No jump-chain-history entropy is claimed from local
destination-surprisal rates.
