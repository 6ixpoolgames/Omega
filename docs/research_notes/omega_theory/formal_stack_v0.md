# Omega Formal Stack v0

Status: working formalization note  
Date: 2026-05-16

This note freezes the current working stack after the primitive, FEP, valuerhood,
and viable-trajectory-space notes. It is not a final theory statement and is not
a validation result. Its purpose is to keep the project from drifting between
levels while the next empirical tests are designed.

## Core Correction

The earlier executable probes were useful, but most of them operated below
Omega proper. They tested primitive structure, toy-substrate persistence,
coarse-graining admissibility, and fakeout anatomy. They do not validate Omega
as a theory of value-bearing trajectory space.

Current working position:

```text
minimal rule-space and cellular probes calibrate the primitive floor;
Omega validation begins only once minimal valuers exist.
```

## Dependency Stack

```text
distinction
  -> asymmetry
  -> relation / causal continuity
  -> identity
  -> recoverability
  -> valuerhood
  -> viability
  -> Omega-compatible viability
  -> lushness of value-bearing trajectory space
```

### Distinction

A difference can exist.

### Asymmetry

Different paths can produce different consequences.

### Relation

Relation is causal continuity through transformation. It is more primitive than
identity and should not be reduced to adjacency, graph connectivity, social
relation, or static dependence.

### Identity

Identity is organized causal continuity through change.

### Recoverability

Recoverability is perturbation-continuability, not exact restoration. A system
is recoverable when perturbations do not destroy the relevant organized
continuity over the horizon being tested.

### Valuerhood

A valuer is a bounded historical identity for which different continuations
asymmetrically affect recoverable continuability.

This does not require consciousness, preference, reward, moral patienthood, or
explicit utility. It is a structural bridge between self-maintaining identity
and value-bearing trajectory space.

### Viability

Viability is constraint-satisfying continuability over a horizon, indexed to an
identity, lineage, or field. Viability is a gate, not the prize.

### Omega-Compatible Viability

Omega-compatible viability is value-bearing viability that does not
irreversibly degrade the broader nested conditions under which value-bearing
histories can continue.

Compatibility is not purity. It allows bounded perturbation, churn, exploration,
repair, failure, competition, and re-entry when regenerative filtering and slack
preserve broader recoverable continuability.

### Lushness

Lushness is horizon-dependent richness of distinguishable Omega-compatible
value-bearing futures. It is not raw entropy. It should include richness,
depth, diversity, composability, and mutual corridor preservation under
constraints.

## Empirical Consequence

The next serious validation target should not be a bare cellular automaton or a
field with no self-maintaining entities. The next target should be a minimal
valuer world.

Candidate world families:

- homeostatic agents;
- active-inference-lite agents;
- autopoietic toy systems;
- reachability or empowerment agents with explicit maintenance constraints;
- evolutionary artificial-life worlds;
- multi-agent viability worlds with corridor and collapse dynamics.

## First Measurable Proxies

### Minimal Valuer Detector

Required readouts:

- boundary persistence under turnover;
- organized causal continuity through perturbation;
- recovery after nonfatal shock;
- path consequence for future continuability;
- action or interaction channels that modulate future continuability.

Required fakeout controls:

- stasis;
- clock or phase cycle;
- random label continuity;
- lock-in;
- pure survival with no recoverability;
- high reachability with no self-maintenance;
- externally maintained persistence.

### Omega-Level Predictors

Given detected valuers, candidate predictors are:

- recoverable reachability;
- distinguishable viable futures;
- bounded cost;
- epistemic grounding;
- scale consistency;
- inter-history compatibility;
- mutual corridor preservation;
- regenerative filtering under slack;
- pseudo-Omega trap detection;
- event-horizon and re-entry structure.

### Baselines

Compare against:

- raw survival;
- raw reachability;
- empowerment;
- local viability;
- reward maximization;
- short-horizon persistence;
- entropy of future labels.

## Interpretation of Prior Probe Branches

### COM Fiber Transport

COM-like multi-step viable propagation through certified fibers remains the
strongest toy-substrate candidate object. It is a useful mathematical witness
for viable propagation and coarse-graining discipline, but it is not yet a
valuer-level Omega validation.

### Trajectory-Space Branch

The trajectory-space probes were useful as quotient-light diagnostics, but the
simple trajectory-geometry and invariant-stack versions failed their control
guardrails. Their main value is negative constraint and failure-mode anatomy.

### DAR / DAX Primitive Branch

The distinction/asymmetry/relation branch now belongs primarily to primitive
floor calibration. DAX-G3/G4 found motif ecology; DAX-G5 failed held-out
prediction. This supports the current boundary: primitive probes can discover
interesting causal-history motifs, but they should not be treated as Omega
proper unless valuerhood and recoverability are explicitly present.

## Current Roadmap

1. Keep this stack as the working level map.
2. Build a minimal valuer-world benchmark with perturbation recovery and action
   channels.
3. Freeze a valuer detector before large runs.
4. Test Omega-level predictors against survival, reward, reachability,
   empowerment, and local-viability baselines.
5. Treat CA/rule-space probes as fakeout controls and primitive calibration
   unless they are extended with genuine self-maintaining valuer structure.
