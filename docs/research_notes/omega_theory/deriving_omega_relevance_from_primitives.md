# Deriving Omega Relevance from the Primitive Triad

A derivation-centered formal note

## Purpose

This note gives the current Omega framework a derivation spine.

Omega relevance is not added as an external value criterion. It is derived by iterating the primitive triad — distinction, asymmetry, and relation — through time, perturbation, and scale.

The aim is to derive, rather than merely list, the central objects of the theory:

```text
history
identity
continuability
recoverability
valuerhood
viability
lineage continuity
inter-history composition
Omega-compatible trajectory space
fakeout factorization
```

The formal objects below are not independent definitions. They are generated by asking what follows when distinction, asymmetric path consequence, and causal continuity are allowed to compound across histories, disturbances, and nested scales.

This is still a preliminary formal skeleton. It is not yet a theorem system. But it should function as the bridge between the glossary, the theory notes, and future empirical validation.

## Primitive triad

The current primitive stack is:

```text
distinction:
  differences exist

asymmetry:
  different paths produce different consequences

relation:
  causal continuity links differences across transformation
```

These are pre-agentic structural conditions.

They are not moral. They are not preferences. They are not utility terms. They do not assume consciousness, agency, value, optimization, or goals.

They only assert that:

```text
there can be differences;
paths can matter;
and transformations can be causally continuous.
```

The rest of the stack is derived by iterating these conditions.

## Derivation principle

The methodological rule is:

> Every higher term should be derivable as a consequence of the primitive triad under iteration across time, perturbation, or scale.

The three derivation axes are:

```text
time:
  primitives iterate into histories

perturbation:
  histories are tested under disruption

scale:
  histories compose into higher-order histories
```

A term that cannot be connected to distinction, asymmetry, and relation should not be treated as foundational. It may still be useful, but it is derived, operational, or heuristic.

The goal is to prevent Omega from becoming a loose collection of preferred concepts. Omega relevance should appear as the consequence of the primitive structure becoming deep enough to support value-bearing trajectory space.

## From relation to history

Relation is causal continuity.

When causal continuity is iterated across transformation, we get histories.

A raw sequence is:

```text
X0, X1, X2, ..., Xt
```

A history is stronger:

```text
X0 -> X1 -> X2 -> ... -> Xt
```

where the arrows represent causally continuous transformations, not merely temporal adjacency or labeling.

A history is therefore:

> a causally continuous sequence of transformations whose path can matter.

The primitives contribute as follows:

```text
distinction:
  there are differentiable states, events, configurations, or patterns

relation:
  transformations link these differences by causal continuity

asymmetry:
  different paths through these transformations can have different consequences
```

A history is the first derived object because it is the minimal structure that can carry path consequence.

## From history to identity

Identity is not primitive sameness.

Identity is derived from organized causal continuity.

> Identity is organized causal continuity through change.

The derivation is:

```text
distinction:
  there is a pattern or difference that can be preserved, disrupted, or transformed

relation:
  the pattern is causally continuous through transformation

asymmetry:
  preserving, degrading, or disrupting the pattern changes future possibilities
```

A vortex is an identity in this sense. Its water molecules churn, but its causal organization remains coherently continuous. An organism is an identity in this sense. Its constituents turn over, but metabolism, boundary, repair, and organization continue. The Ship of Theseus is an identity question because it asks whether organized causal continuity survives material replacement.

Identity is not:

```text
same material constituents
same exact state
same label
same phase
same location
arbitrary equivalence
```

An identity class is:

> an equivalence over histories that preserves continuation-relevant causal organization.

This is the first place where arbitrary definition becomes dangerous. We can always define an equivalence relation. That does not make it a valid identity.

## Identity validity and anti-arbitrariness

No identity relation is valid merely because it can be defined.

A valid identity class must be:

```text
coarse enough:
  identity survives transformation, repair, and constituent churn

fine enough:
  continuation-relevant causal organization is not erased
```

Invalid identity classes include:

```text
overbroad identity:
  everything maps to the same identity

underbroad identity:
  only exact state equality counts

cheap identity:
  identity compresses to phase, attractor, distribution, or label
```

The criterion is not subjective familiarity. The criterion is whether the identity class preserves the causal organization required for continuability.

A valid identity class must support distinctions like:

```text
continued vs collapsed
repaired vs unrepaired
transformed vs erased
viable vs terminal
recoverable vs unrecoverable
```

If it cannot support such distinctions, it is not identity in the Omega-relevant sense.

## From identity to continuability

Once identity exists, the next derived question is whether it can continue.

Continuability is identity-indexed future availability.

> Continuability is the availability of admissible future paths that preserve identity-, lineage-, or field-relevant causal organization.

A useful notation is:

```text
Cont(I, H_t, C, T)
```

where:

```text
I:
  identity class

H_t:
  history up to time t

C:
  constraint set

T:
  horizon
```

Continuability does not mean:

```text
a next state exists
some material process continues
a score remains positive
a label can still be applied
```

Continuability means that admissible future histories remain available while preserving the relevant causal organization.

This is why continuability must be indexed to identity, constraints, horizon, and scale. Without indexing, viability claims become vague survival claims.

## From asymmetry to recoverability

Asymmetry means different paths produce different consequences.

Once identity has possible future paths, perturbations matter because they can change which paths remain available.

A perturbation can be written informally as:

```text
p: H_t -> H'_t
```

Recoverability is perturbation-continuability:

> After disruption, there remains a path back into, or still within, a continuable identity class.

Informally:

```text
there exists a continuation R
such that H'_t ⋆ R remains within or returns to a continuable identity class
```

Recoverability is not:

```text
exact reset
return to prior state
return to prior trajectory
survival of anything whatsoever
```

Recoverability is return to continuable identity class, not return to exact state.

This is derived rather than optional. If identity survives only one exact path, it is brittle. If every perturbation destroys its continuation, it cannot support valuerhood. If perturbation merely shifts phase in a clock, recovery may be real at the clock level but insufficient for higher claims.

## From recoverable identity to valuerhood

Valuerhood is derived, not assumed.

A valuer is:

> a bounded historical identity for which different continuations asymmetrically affect recoverable continuability.

The derivation is:

```text
identity:
  something persists as organized causal continuity

recoverability:
  its continuation can survive perturbation

asymmetry:
  different continuations can preserve, degrade, restore, or destroy it

distinction / boundary:
  there is a bounded locus for which these differences are meaningful
```

This is where proto-value enters.

Proto-value is not preference. It is not moral status. It is not utility. It is not reward.

Proto-value is:

> the emergence of a locus for which continuation, degradation, recovery, and loss are dynamically non-equivalent.

A valuer does not need consciousness or human-like goals. But it must be more than passive persistence. It must be a bounded identity whose future continuability can be affected by different paths.

This gives the bridge to FEP and self-maintenance. The primitive stack does not replace FEP. It explains why FEP-like self-maintaining identity is the natural floor beneath Omega.

## From valuerhood to viability

Viability is not generic survival.

Viability is:

> constraint-satisfying continuability of an identity, lineage, or field over a horizon.

This follows from the previous steps. Once a valuer exists, the question becomes whether its continuability can be maintained under constraints.

Viability should be indexed:

```text
Viability(I, C, T, scale)
```

where:

```text
I:
  identity, lineage, or field

C:
  relevant constraints

T:
  horizon

scale:
  level of organization under consideration
```

Useful forms:

```text
direct viability:
  the identity itself remains continuable

recoverable viability:
  perturbation does not erase access to the identity's continuable class

lineage continuity:
  successor histories preserve enough causal organization or value-bearing capacity

field viability:
  the broader ecology or trajectory field remains capable of generating, sustaining,
  repairing, and composing value-bearing histories
```

Lineage continuity is the formal term for legacy-like viability. It matters because many real systems persist through reproduction, succession, replacement, memory, inheritance, and field-level regeneration rather than literal individual continuation.

Churn is turnover inside a larger continuity. Churn may be regenerative or destructive:

```text
regenerative churn:
  lower-order turnover supports higher-order continuability

destructive churn:
  turnover erases the causal organization required for continuability
```

This is essential for biology, ecology, multicellularity, institutions, civilizations, and any higher-order agency composed from lower-order valuers.

## From relation recursion to inter-history composition

Relation recurses.

Histories interact, and their interaction itself becomes a causally continuous history.

```text
H_A × H_B -> H_AB
```

where `H_AB` is not merely an event. It is a higher-order interaction history that can reshape the future continuability of A, B, and the larger field.

Derived terms:

```text
inter-relation:
  causal continuity among interacting histories

higher-order history:
  interaction history formed by lower-order histories

nested identity:
  higher-order organized causal continuity
```

This is how the theory scales:

```text
identity
  -> valuer
  -> inter-valuer history
  -> institution / ecology / civilization
  -> trajectory field
```

The important point is that higher-order histories are not merely aggregates. They become causal organizations in their own right, and they feed back into the continuability of the histories that compose them.

## From nested histories to Omega relevance

Omega relevance is derived from nested valuer viability.

A structure is Omega-relevant insofar as it affects nested recoverable value-bearing trajectory space.

The derivation is:

```text
valuerhood:
  value-bearing loci exist

viability:
  their continuability is constraint-indexed

inter-relation:
  valuers compose into higher-order histories

scale:
  local continuability can preserve, degrade, repair, or destroy broader field continuability
```

Therefore the Omega-relevant question is:

> Does this structure preserve, degrade, expand, repair, or collapse nested recoverable value-bearing trajectory space?

Omega relevance is not imposed from outside the primitives. It appears when primitive-derived identities become valuers and valuers compose into nested fields.

## Omega compatibility

Value-bearing and Omega-compatible are related but not identical.

Value-bearing is local:

> continuation, degradation, recovery, and loss are dynamically non-equivalent for a valuer.

Omega-compatible is field-indexed:

> a value-bearing history remains compatible with the nested recoverable continuability of broader value-bearing trajectory space.

A locally value-bearing history can be Omega-incompatible.

Examples include:

```text
trap
lock-in
runaway exploitation
pseudo-Omega attractor
field-destroying optimizer
metaparasitic institution
```

A history is Omega-compatible only if its continuation does not irreversibly degrade the nested conditions under which value-bearing histories can continue.

## Lushness as derived value capacity

Lushness is also derived.

Lushness is:

> the horizon-dependent richness of distinguishable Omega-compatible value-bearing futures.

The derivation is:

```text
distinction:
  futures can differ

valuerhood:
  futures can be value-bearing

viability:
  futures must remain continuable

inter-relation:
  futures must remain mutually compatible across nested histories

scale:
  richness must not erase lower- or higher-order continuability
```

Lushness is not:

```text
raw path count
population maximization
entropy maximization
activity maximization
one agent's empowerment
one structure's persistence
```

Lushness is value capacity under compatibility constraints. It names the richness, diversity, depth, recoverability, and composability of value-bearing futures.

## Fakeouts as failed derivations

A fakeout is apparent satisfaction of a higher derived level by bypassing required lower derivation steps.

Use:

```text
fakeout_for(claim_level)
```

A fakeout is not necessarily false or worthless. It may be a valid lower-order component. It becomes a fakeout when it is used as evidence for a higher claim it does not actually support.

Examples:

```text
stasis:
  preserves state but bypasses transformation

clock:
  preserves recurrence but bypasses historical identity

noise:
  has change but lacks organized causal continuity

lock-in:
  preserves persistence but collapses continuability

self-only recurrence:
  has recurrence but lacks sufficient relational organization

symmetry quotient:
  erases asymmetry / path consequence

parasitic persistence:
  has local viability but degrades nested field viability
```

This is why fakeouts must be level-indexed.

A clock is not fake as a clock. It may be a valid timing component inside a living system. It is a fakeout if treated as evidence for recoverable historical identity.

Stasis is not fake as memory or scaffold. It is a fakeout if treated as agency.

Local phase is not fake as proto-memory. It is a fakeout if treated as full historical identity.

## Cheap factorization

Cheap factorization is the formal mechanism of many fakeouts.

Template:

```text
H_t -> Q(H_t) -> apparent success
```

where `Q` discards primitive-derived structure required for the claimed level.

Definition:

> Cheap factorization is lower-complexity explanatory compression that preserves apparent success while removing the causal organization required for the claimed level.

Examples:

```text
fixed-state quotient:
  stasis; identity compresses to one state

phase quotient:
  clock; identity compresses to phase modulo k

stationary distribution quotient:
  noise; persistence compresses to distribution

terminal attractor quotient:
  lock-in; viability compresses to basin membership

symmetry quotient:
  path consequence is erased

self-only quotient:
  relational organization collapses to isolated recurrence
```

Cheap means formally compressive, not aesthetically simple.

A claim is stronger when it cannot be explained by a quotient that bypasses the derivation.

## Regenerative filtering under slack

Regenerative filtering is derived from recoverability plus nested viability.

Slack is:

> surplus recoverable capacity that permits perturbation, exploration, churn, repair, and re-entry without collapse.

Filtering is:

> dynamics that prevent destructive modes from becoming organizing attractors.

A parasitic mode is:

> an exploited slack niche that extracts from a host or field without contributing to regenerative continuability.

Parasitism is not Omega-positive. A system may remain Omega-compatible despite bounded parasitic load only insofar as regenerative filtering prevents that load from degrading nested recoverable continuability.

Pseudo-Omega transition occurs when extraction captures or disables filtering, repair, epistemic, or coordination systems and becomes field-structuring.

This gives the principle:

```text
Omega compatibility is not purity.
It is regenerative filtering under slack.
```

This follows from the derivation: once nested value-bearing fields exist, preserving them requires the capacity to absorb disturbance without allowing destructive modes to become organizing attractors.

## Scale consistency

Scale consistency is derived from inter-history composition.

Because histories compose into higher-order histories, viability must be scale-consistent.

Definition:

> Scale consistency means viability claims survive admissible abstraction: abstraction must neither invent viability nor hide collapse.

Two directions:

```text
upward consistency:
  lower-order viability composes into higher-order continuability

downward consistency:
  higher-order continuability does not depend on erasing or hiding lower-order collapse
```

Failure cases:

```text
institution persists by crushing its members
civilization persists by destroying future valuer diversity
model says viable because it erased relevant identities
local optimizer survives by degrading the field
```

Scale consistency prevents pseudo-Omega structures from laundering local persistence into global value.

## Empirical guardrails as derivation tests

Empirical controls are useful when they test whether a claimed structure actually requires the derived stack.

Examples from minimal-world probes:

```text
center-only control:
  did relation / causal organization matter?

symbol-phase-only control:
  did identity collapse to clock or phase?

symmetrized control:
  did asymmetry / path consequence matter?

output-distribution matched control:
  did apparent persistence collapse to distribution?

frozen / chaotic controls:
  stasis and noise boundary tests

perturbation tests:
  recoverability tests

scale / lineage tests:
  viability beyond individual persistence
```

This reframes guardrails as derivation tests rather than ad hoc statistical hurdles.

Minimal cellular automata and rule-space probes test the primitive floor only. They may reveal histories, persistence, causal continuity, fakeouts, and recoverability-like behavior. They cannot validate Omega directly because Omega begins above valuerhood and nested value-bearing trajectory space.

## Open formal problems

This note defines the derivation spine, not a complete formal theory.

Open problems include:

```text
identity validity:
  how much causal organization must identity preserve?

admissible perturbations:
  which perturbation classes matter for recoverability?

cheapness ordering:
  how do we rank or compare explanatory factorizations?

lineage continuity:
  how much causal organization must successor histories preserve?

field viability:
  how do we formalize a field's capacity to generate, sustain, repair, and compose valuers?

lushness:
  how do we measure richness without collapsing into raw path count?

scale consistency:
  which abstractions preserve nested viability?

regenerative filtering:
  how do we quantify slack and filtering capacity?
```

These are not defects in the derivation. They are the next formal tasks.

## Final thesis

The derivation chain is:

```text
distinction
  -> continuation-relevant difference

asymmetry
  -> path consequence

relation
  -> causal continuity

causal continuity over time
  -> history

organized causal continuity
  -> identity

identity under future paths
  -> continuability

continuability under perturbation
  -> recoverability

bounded recoverable identity under asymmetric continuations
  -> valuerhood

constraint-satisfying continuability
  -> viability

interacting valuers
  -> nested value-bearing trajectory space

nested recoverable value-bearing trajectory space
  -> Omega relevance
```

Omega relevance is not imposed from outside the primitives.

It is what appears when distinction, asymmetry, and relation are iterated through time, perturbation, and scale until value-bearing trajectory space becomes possible.
