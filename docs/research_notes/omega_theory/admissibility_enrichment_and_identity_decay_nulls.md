# Admissibility Enrichment and Identity-Decay Nulls

Status: working formalism / theory-arm draft  
Date: 2026-06-02  
Claim boundary: formal orientation note, not empirical validation and not theorem closure

## 0. Purpose

This note clarifies how **Omega Formal Core v0.2: Future-Distinction Dynamics** and the proto-valuer compatibility-completions note should be unified.

The unification is not a forgetful map and not a projection downward. The project should not say that richer structure is recovered by forgetting. That does not match the ontology or the 3P discipline.

The unification is an **admissibility enrichment ladder**:

```text
base future-distinction dynamics:
  relation, distinction, asymmetry, dynamics

v0.2 admissibility:
  recoverable, non-erasing, compatible future-distinction propagation

proto-valuer admissibility:
  an admissible process-bundle designation maintains distinctions through churn,
  remains recoverable under perturbation, outperforms a matched identity-decay
  null, and self-conditions toward continued distinction-maintaining capacity

Omega-completion admissibility:
  families of proto-valuer-bearing dynamics compose without systematic
  irreversible destruction of one another's distinction-content
```

The universe is not forgetful. Our designations become more constrained, more predictive, and more revelatory as they earn additional structure.

## 1. 3P discipline

This layer should follow the project mantra:

```text
principled;
parsimonious;
predictive / revelatory.
```

### Principled

Do not introduce agency, self, valuerhood, identity, boundary, utility, or cosmic dissolution as primitives.

Every higher-order designation must be earned from future-field structure, recoverability, perturbation behavior, and comparison against declared controls.

### Parsimonious

Do not add a new metaphysical force when a declared null comparison is enough.

Dissolution is not a fundamental object. It is the statistically expected loss of recoverable distinction-content when a maintaining pattern is unsupported, passivized, ablated, randomized, or otherwise deprived of the activity that sustains its vortex-like identity.

### Predictive / revelatory

The layer must imply concrete tests:

```text
What process bundle is being designated?
What distinction-content does it maintain?
What perturbation class is used?
What matched null is used?
What maintenance gap is measured?
What activity channel distinguishes self-conditioning from passive persistence?
What compatibility audit blocks overpromotion?
```

## 2. Admissibility predicates, not forgetful maps

Let `D` denote a future-distinction dynamics over a relational substrate with declared observables and perturbations.

Instead of defining maps that discard structure, use nested admissibility predicates over the same unfolding future field:

```text
A0(D):
  D is future-distinction dynamics under relation, distinction, asymmetry,
  and admissible observables.

A1(D, P, N_P):
  P is a proto-valuer-bearing admissible designation within D, evaluated against
  a matched identity-decay null N_P.

A2(D, {P_i}):
  {P_i} is an admissible compatibility completion within D.
```

The same world is being examined. The later predicates do not forget earlier structure. They add stricter admissibility conditions.

## 3. Identity-decay nulls

Do not put a dissolution gradient into the substrate tuple as a primitive.

Use a declared matched reference:

```text
N_P = N(P; D, Q_adm, Pi, H)
```

where `N_P` is the **identity-decay null** for process bundle `P` under dynamics `D`, admissible observables `Q_adm`, perturbation class `Pi`, and horizon `H`.

`N_P` may be constructed by:

```text
passivizing P's activity channel;
ablating P's maintaining transitions;
randomizing P-conditioned asymmetries while preserving low-level marginals;
using matched unsupported evolution;
using matched passive baselines;
using controlled nulls that preserve substrate size, degree, frontier scale, or
other declared nuisance variables.
```

The null is valid only if it is declared, reproducible, and control-audited.

## 4. Anti-dissolution as a maintenance gap

Let `C(P, H)` measure recoverable distinction-maintenance of process bundle `P` over horizon `H` under admissible observables and perturbations.

The anti-dissolution test should be written as a gap against the identity-decay null:

```text
C_K^Pi(P, H) - C_{N_P}^Pi(P, H) >= eta
```

where:

```text
K:
  actual or activity-present dynamics

N_P:
  matched identity-decay null for P

Pi:
  declared perturbation class

eta:
  declared maintenance-gap threshold
```

This preserves the gradient/filter intuition without making dissolution ontological.

## 5. Vortex identity

Identity is not primitive. It is the recoverability of a dynamic pattern through churn.

A vortex does not persist by keeping the same matter. It persists by maintaining a reconstructible pattern through ongoing turnover.

Likewise, a proto-valuer is not a static thing. It is a candidate process bundle whose distinction-content remains recoverable under perturbation and whose activity helps maintain the conditions for that recoverability.

Thus:

```text
identity:
  not primitive

recoverability:
  evidential identity of distinction-patterns

identity-decay:
  statistical loss of recoverability when maintaining dynamics are absent

anti-dissolution:
  measured maintenance gap over the matched identity-decay null
```

## 6. Process-bundle notation

To prevent `P` from doing too much work, later notes should distinguish:

```text
tau:
  realized trajectory or trajectory segment

P:
  admissible designation / pattern schema over trajectories

[P]:
  fiber, class, or ensemble of trajectories matching designation P

a_P:
  declared activity channel associated with P

K^{act(P)}:
  transition law with P-associated activity present

K^{pass(P)} or N_P:
  matched passive / ablated / non-self-conditioning identity-decay reference

D_t(P; tau):
  observable distinction-content of designation P along trajectory tau at time t
```

A process bundle does not magically condition the transition law. It conditions the transition law only through a declared activity channel, intervention channel, or measurable pattern-conditioned transition difference.

## 7. Pre-proto, proto, and valuer ladder

To avoid circularity, split the ladder:

```text
pre-proto-valuer:
  an admissible process bundle that maintains nontrivial distinction-content,
  propagates a sufficient subset recoverably under perturbation, and outperforms
  a matched identity-decay null.

proto-valuer:
  a pre-proto-valuer whose declared activity channel increases the probability
  of future pre-proto-valuerhood relative to a matched passive/null baseline.

valuer:
  a proto-valuer whose induced asymmetry-preferences survive compatibility
  audits.
```

Self-conditioning should point first to future **pre-proto-valuerhood**, not recursively to proto-valuerhood, unless a later note explicitly defines a recursive stable proto-valuer fixed point.

Schematic self-conditioning test:

```text
Pr(P_{t+H} satisfies pre-proto-valuer criteria | K^{act(P)})
>
Pr(P_{t+H} satisfies pre-proto-valuer criteria | K^{pass(P)}) + gamma
```

## 8. Omega completions

Compatibility is not monotone in general. A process bundle may be compatible with `B` and compatible with `C`, but not compatible with `B` and `C` jointly.

Therefore Omega should not be assumed to be a single greatest fixed point.

The safer target is:

```text
OmegaFamily(D) = Max { Y : Y is an admissible compatibility completion of
                       proto-valuer-bearing dynamics within D }
```

In finite toy systems, `Y` may be represented as a subset of trajectory or process-bundle identifiers.

In richer systems, completions may need to be weighted, branching, or measure-valued. Subset maximality is a finite-support special case, not the final ontology.

## 9. Empirical implications

Current Future Field Atlas results do not detect proto-valuers, valuers, or Omega.

They motivate future tests:

```text
recoverability metrics;
identity-decay nulls;
activity-present vs passive matched baselines;
anti-dissolution maintenance gaps;
self-conditioning audits;
compatibility-completion diagnostics;
observable-extension passes.
```

The recent coupled morphology and shared-capacity results sharpen one point:

```text
marginal continuation is not compatibility.
```

A coupled future field may preserve or lose A/B marginals while changing joint geometry. Compatibility must be joint-field-sensitive and cannot be inferred from marginal persistence alone.

Compact formulation:

```text
Proto-valuerhood is an admissibility enrichment over future-distinction dynamics, not a primitive ontology and not a forgetful projection. It is earned when a process-bundle designation maintains recoverable distinction-content through churn better than a matched identity-decay null and self-conditions toward continued maintenance capacity.

Omega is the family of maximal admissible compatibility completions in which such proto-valuer-bearing dynamics propagate without systematic irreversible destruction of one another's distinction-content.
```
