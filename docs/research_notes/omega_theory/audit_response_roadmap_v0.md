# Audit Response Roadmap v0

Status: near-term roadmap
Scope: response to external-audit themes around scale, legibility, provenance, and uptake
Claim boundary: roadmap only; not evidence of completion

## Diagnosis

The strongest fair audit says:

```text
the project has a serious finite formal spine;
the main weaknesses are scale, adapter provenance, external legibility, and
the gap from abstraction integrity to full value/alignment claims.
```

The next batch should therefore focus on:

```text
making the spine legible;
showing how adapter facts are justified;
providing worked examples;
making contributions easier.
```

Not on immediately adding more high-level ontology.

## Current Rebase

The current contribution should be stated as:

```text
Layer A is a continuation-map integrity discipline.
```

It studies when:

```text
summaries fail to determine targets;
quotients merge forbidden pairs;
presentations fabricate reachability or viability;
abstractions hide loss;
recurrent support carries or loses a distinction.
```

Gradient Ethics and Omega require this layer, but are not proved by it.

## What Not To Do Next

Do not immediately jump to:

```text
terminal coalgebra;
valuerhood;
agency detection;
black-hole/vortex metaphysics;
full Laws of Form import;
Omega-terminal claims.
```

Those can remain long-run motivation. The audit pressure is currently on
legibility, provenance, and external stress testing.

## Success Criteria

