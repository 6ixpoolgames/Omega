# Future-Field Reorientation and Declaration Culling v0

Status: theory reorientation / declaration-debt cleanup
Scope: future-field relativity language, lushness/colonization status, scalarization, marginal/joint tiers, and branch control
Claim boundary: documentation only; not a new theorem, not value, not moral standing, not patienthood, not agency, not identity, not quantum mechanics, not Omega validation

## Purpose

Recent theory work made the future-field language more fertile but also more
dangerous. Several ideas were starting to enter the ledger as if they were
foundational premises. This note records the correction:

```text
derive it, demote it, or delete it.
```

Declarations should be scarce, load-bearing, and visible. A declaration is not
a way to keep a promising branch moving; it is a liability with a standing
bounty on it.

## Main Reorientation

The live stack should not promote a scalar field measure, population optimum,
or "field theory of value substrate" into the foundation.

The current proven and retained surface is more conservative:

```text
certified presentations;
robust corridors;
sound learning/update;
bounded recovery and nonrecoverable contraction;
declared-profile loss dominance;
cross-scale colonization-axis separation as a finite candidate coordinate.
```

The new theory should be read as a map of theorem targets and quarantined
generators around that surface, not as a replacement foundation.

## Deleted or Demoted Premises

### Field Measure

Delete `weight = volume x lushness` as a foundational premise.

The order-level theory does not generate utility-monster swamping by itself.
Declared nonrecoverable-loss profiles compare by a partial order. Cross-valuer
aggregation enters only when a common order, scalarization, or priority rule is
registered.

So the anti-monster lesson is:

```text
aggregation monsters are artifacts of scalarization;
the order-level floor is monster-free until a cross-valuer scalar/order is
registered.
```

Multiplicative high-dimensional field measures may remain useful as optional
triage instruments. They are not foundational.

### Fraction of Field Foreclosed

Demote fraction-of-field language to optional scalarization engineering.

The measure-free target is stronger:

```text
termination-supremum:
  per valuer, terminating the live field contracts the top of that valuer's
  declared fact order, hence is maximal relative to every monotone valuation
  over that order.
```

This is a theorem target over the existing nonrecoverable-loss machinery, not
a new axiom.

### Richer-Is-Better

Replace the broad slogan with a scoped theorem target.

Per valuer:

```text
expanding declared value-capable facts is weakly better relative to every
monotone valuation that treats those facts as goods.
```

That is the mirror of nonrecoverable-loss dominance and should be formalized as
`ExpansionProfile` / `ExpansionDominates`.

Across valuers:

```text
comparison remains declaration debt until a common order, standing relation,
or population priority is registered.
```

Directive force:

```text
there is no expansion gate.
```

The floor may refuse certified nonrecoverable contraction. It does not command
maximal expansion. Refusals compose; pursuits Goodhart.

### Vacancy

Split vacancy into two claims.

Weak vacancy:

```text
full saturation may contract existing valuers' open relational futures.
```

This may be recoverable as a corollary of recovery-aware protection once the
relevant relational facts are declared.

Strong vacancy:

```text
fallow volume is protected for merely possible future valuers.
```

This remains possible-people standing and is not derived. Do not wire it into a
gate without a separate standing/register argument.

### Field Theory of Value Substrate

Quarantine the phrase as an interpretation until the following survive:

```text
1. colonization/curl signal beyond one finite retained pair;
2. a joint-tier ensemble-span witness;
3. a no-static-configuration probe or counterexample.
```

## Promoted Distinctions

### Marginal and Joint Tiers

The marginal/joint split is not a ledger premise. It is a witness-backed
structural distinction.

Marginal coordinates describe one field:

```text
volume;
colonization/lushness candidates;
per-valuer correction and recovery capacity;
per-field expansion and loss profiles.
```

Joint coordinates describe an ensemble:

```text
relational composability;
ensemble diversity / span;
joint recovery and compatibility;
coupling structure not recoverable from a census of marginals.
```

Existing marginal-vs-joint witnesses already show that joint structure does
not generally factor through a list of individual coordinates. The next
instrument should test this deliberately through ensemble span and
census-blind witnesses.

### Future-Field Relativity

Future-field relativity is a useful interpretation layer if kept disciplined:

```text
there is no detached God's-eye valuer frame;
third-person objectivity is invariant or covariant structure across situated
valuer frames.
```

Repo-native theorem targets:

```text
reachability-indexed responsibility:
  normative pressure has traction only where control reach over still-
  foreclosable facts is nonzero.

priority relativity:
  some priority orderings may be frame-relative rather than missing data,
  especially for facts outside each other's causal reach.

harm covariance:
  frame-relative contraction profiles may transform through certified lenses;
  invariant claims are those surviving the transformation orbit.
```

Do not treat relativity language as a proof of value, standing, or a complete
field theory.

### Large Deformers

Large-deformer language is a useful branch-control handle:

```text
a large deformer is a system whose presence changes the reachable future field
for other systems.
```

This is not necessarily agency or valuerhood. It should be developed through
collapse audits, horizon/capture witnesses, and recovery/continuation effects.

### Quantum

Quantum language stays later and operational:

```text
quantum-compatible by interface;
quantum-like only when effective diagnostics force it;
literally quantum only when finite-dimensional operational witnesses require
noncommuting/contextual structure.
```

Do not promote quantum metaphysics. If a future biological or artificial system
is quantum-relevant, the load-bearing claim must be:

```text
destroying or classicalizing the quantum structure nonrecoverably contracts
registered perception, correction, relation, memory, or value-contact capacity.
```

Quantum relevance changes the preservation adapter, not moral standing by
itself. The same rule applies to biological and artificial systems.

## Non-Omniscient Licensing Principle

The strongest new theorem path is the non-omniscient licensing principle:

```text
if an agent lacks a certified total compensation relation over open-ended
future fields, nonrecoverable contraction cannot be licensed by appeal to
uncertified compensation.
```

Build path:

```text
1. CompensationClaim as a registered relation between contraction and expansion
   profiles.
2. Extend the recovery-aware gate so nonrecoverable contraction is licensable
   only with certified compensation.
3. Prove a stale-certificate theorem for self-extending registers:
   a static compensation certificate cannot cover facts that a live register
   soundly adds later.
```

This keeps gate asymmetry from resting on taste. The premise is finite
epistemic position: the system is not certified omniscient over the future
value-contact field.
