# ODT Adjacency and Delta v0

Status: positioning note / adjacency map
Scope: Omega Decision Theory v0-v2 against nearby formal traditions
Claim boundary: not a completed decision theory, not final value, not standing,
not agency, not identity, not stochastic Blackwell closure, not Omega validation

## Purpose

The ODT stack is now large enough that its novelty should be stated as a
delta, not as a free-floating invention.

The current compression is:

```text
ODT0 licenses through certified maps and corridor preservation.
ODT1 compares licensed outcome surfaces by value-parametric dominance.
ODT2 arbitrates only through registered least-violation data.
```

The project-specific contribution is not "decision theory from scratch." It
is the integration of:

```text
presentation certification;
fixed-point corridor preservation;
proof-carrying refusal;
value-parametric partial comparison;
explicit authority holes.
```

## Adjacent Work

### Guaranteed Safe AI

Guaranteed Safe AI frames safety around a world model, a safety specification,
and a verifier that can provide an auditable certificate. ODT is compatible
with that shape, but it focuses on a narrower consumer layer:

```text
if a decision justification uses a presentation,
that presentation must be certified for the consequence facts being used.
```

ODT0's distinctive pressure is therefore not only "verify the plan against a
model." It is also:

```text
verify that the map used to justify the plan is not fabricating or hiding the
relevant continuation fact.
```

Reference: [Towards Guaranteed Safe AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06624).

### Supervisory Control / Safety Filters

Supervisory control and shielding work restrict behavior so a plant or policy
does not violate a specification. ODT0's Gate G2 is adjacent: a licensed action
must keep every concrete successor inside the declared corridor.

The delta is the front half of the license:

```text
G1: certify the representational route.
G2: preserve the corridor.
G3: certify decision-process identifications.
```

The robust corridor and ambiguity-family containment theorems make G2
mathematically non-arbitrary. The map-certification gates make ODT stricter
than a plain safety filter.

### Blackwell Comparison

ODT1 does not replace Blackwell comparison. It consumes information-order
facts when they are available.

Current landed surface:

```text
deterministic factorization iff universal policy simulation;
finite rational garbling -> randomized policy simulation with preserved
state/action mass.
```

The Markov-categorical Blackwell literature gives a principled external
direction for the future stochastic converse. The current repo deliberately
keeps only the easy finite-rational forward bridge.

Reference: [Representable Markov Categories and Comparison of Statistical
Experiments in Categorical Probability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.07416).

### Modal Logic and Process Semantics

The process-coherence branch is adjacent to modal transition-system semantics:
facts about reachability, viability, path lifting, and observed languages are
properties of transition systems and their presentations.

The useful external lesson is:

```text
certified facts should eventually be organized by a small modal/fixed-point
language rather than by ad hoc fact lists.
```

The current repository has not yet implemented a modal mu-calculus spine.
Closure v2 is a pilot toward richer fact languages, not that spine.

Reference: [An expressive completeness theorem for coalgebraic modal
mu-calculi](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.08637).

## Current Delta

ODT's current specific posture is:

```text
1. decision frames are certified objects, not invisible background;
2. incomplete certification returns refusal or underdetermination, not scalar
   forced choice;
3. value comparison is parametric in a declared preorder/valuation discipline;
4. arbitration is procedural and registered, not a theory of standing;
5. ambiguity-family persistence is confined by a shared-action robust corridor.
```

## Nonclaims

This note does not claim:

```text
ODT is complete;
ODT chooses final value;
the registered preorder or violation score is correct;
full stochastic Blackwell theory has been proved;
supervisory control has been subsumed;
Guaranteed Safe AI has been solved;
agency, identity, valuerhood, moral standing, or Omega has been validated.
```

## Public Compression

ODT is the decision consumer of the map-integrity stack: first certify the map,
then preserve the corridor, then compare licensed surfaces, and only then
arbitrate through explicitly registered authority.
