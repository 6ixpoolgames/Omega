# Effective Layers: Realization, Forgetting, and Emergence v0

Status: reorientation note / anti-smuggling map
Scope: Alpha-Omega layer architecture, borrowed-framework translation, and near-term theorem obligations
Claim boundary: not a theorem, not empirical validation, not value, agency, valuerhood, identity, or Omega validation

## Purpose

This note records a project reorientation.

The project should no longer be read as trying to derive value, agency,
lineage, power, or ethics directly from bare Alpha primitives. The stronger and
more honest target is a staged effective-theory program:

```text
Alpha constrains the basement structure.
Continuation, agency, valuerhood, and Gradient Ethics are higher effective
layers realized by Alpha-compatible substrates.
```

The central question becomes:

```text
Do stable higher-level realization layers exist, and do their certificates
forget down to lower-level traces without allowing lower-level abstractions to
fabricate higher-level facts?
```

This preserves the strong ambition of deriving value from Alpha in the long-run
effective-theory sense, while rejecting the shortcut:

```text
relation + distinction + asymmetry -> agency or value.
```

That shortcut is false. A rock, a passive attractor, or a driven chemical cycle
can instantiate lower relational structure without thereby realizing agentic
power, valuerhood, or protected value-bearing continuation.

## The Tower

The active conceptual tower is:

```text
Valuer
  forgets to Agent

Agent
  forgets to Continuation

Continuation
  forgets to Alpha
```

The forgetting direction is comparatively safe:

```text
agentic power
  -> maintained controlled continuation
  -> consequence-bearing future separation
  -> relation, distinction, and asymmetry traces.
```

The lifting direction is not canonical:

```text
Alpha trace
  -/-> unique continuation realization
  -/-> unique agent realization
  -/-> valuerhood
  -/-> ethics.
```

This noncanonical lifting is not a defect. It is the same kind of
underdetermination that appears between microphysics and higher effective
sciences. The higher variables may be realized by lower structure, but they are
not uniquely named by the lower vocabulary alone.

## Layer Roles

### Alpha

Alpha contains the primitive-facing grammar:

```text
relation;
distinction;
asymmetry;
primitive apartness;
primitive path structure;
primitive noncollapse.
```

Alpha can support lower traces needed by higher structures. It does not by
itself choose observations, dynamics, interventions, agents, valuers, or
ethical targets.

### Continuation

Continuation adds effective semantics over time or transformation:

```text
transition or succession structure;
contexts and observations;
consequence profiles;
recovery profiles;
viability and reachability;
perturbation families;
presentation and abstraction discipline.
```

This is the current strongest formal layer. Most of the Lean theorem spine and
finite adapter work belongs here.

### Agency

Agency adds controlled and maintained alternatives:

```text
endogenous selection;
policy or intervention surfaces;
feedback;
constitutive maintenance;
lineage or process coherence;
counterfactual dependence of continuation on the process' own selection.
```

Agency is not recurrence alone, control capacity alone, or high leverage alone.
Those are precursors. A plausible agency realization needs at least controlled
optionality plus some maintenance or feedback condition.

### Valuer

Valuerhood adds standing or value-capability:

```text
protected continuation claims;
value-capable lineage;
comparative significance;
protected plurality;
joint compatibility across protected continuations.
```

The current project does not derive valuerhood. It can define formal
constraints that would apply once valuer-relevant continuation requirements are
certified.

### Gradient Ethics

Gradient Ethics lives above the valuer/continuation interface. It studies
constraints over transformations of value-capable continuation:

```text
irreversible contraction;
plurality loss;
autonomy loss;
incompatible concentration of power;
loss of future valuer capacity;
unsound abstractions that hide or fabricate continuation.
```

The intended formal bridge is conditional:

```text
given a certified continuation requirement Phi,
robust viability and abstraction-integrity theorems constrain policies and
presentations that claim to preserve Phi.
```

## Source-Of-Structure Statuses

The older supplied/derived split is not fine-grained enough. Use these labels.

```text
Primitive:
  part of the Alpha presentation.

Freely generated:
  canonical weak construction from lower structure, such as relation-generated
  paths.

Realization-supplied:
  interpreted or extracted from a particular substrate, such as a transition
  relation, observation map, target, policy class, or ambiguity set.

Certified:
  a realization-supplied object that satisfies explicit bridge obligations,
  such as recovery, soundness, reflection, path lifting, or provenance checks.

Empirically inferred:
  supported by data, measurement, or model fitting only to a stated confidence
  or approximation level.

Normatively supplied:
  standing, value relevance, or moral significance not yet derived internally.
```

The rule is:

```text
Do not treat realization-supplied objects as certified.
Do not treat certified continuation facts as valuerhood.
Do not treat valuer standing as derived unless the relevant layer has earned it.
```

## Forgetting Versus Lifting

The project should prove both directions where possible, but it should not
confuse them.

### Downward necessity

Higher-layer structure should leave lower-layer traces.

Example target:

```text
agentic power
  -> endogenous selection changes future continuation profiles
  -> some consequence-bearing asymmetry is exposed
  -> some Alpha relation/distinction/asymmetry trace exists.
```

This is a downward necessity theorem.

### Upward underdetermination

Lower-layer traces do not uniquely determine higher-layer structure.

Example target:

```text
the same Alpha frame admits inequivalent continuation or agency realizations.
```

This is an underdetermination theorem.

Together they give the right relation:

```text
agentic power -> Alpha/consequence trace
Alpha/consequence trace -/-> agentic power
```

## Optionality By Layer

Optionality is a major bridge, but it is not value by itself.

```text
Alpha:
  no optionality yet, only primitive differences and relations.

Continuation:
  raw, viable, robust, and recoverable optionality.

Agency:
  controlled and maintained optionality.

Valuer / Gradient Ethics:
  protected, jointly compatible optionality of value-capable continuations.
```

Use a tiered vocabulary:

```text
raw optionality:
  number or richness of reachable futures.

viable optionality:
  reachable futures that stay inside a constraint.

robust optionality:
  viable futures across an ambiguity set.

controlled optionality:
  futures selectable by actions or policies.

maintained optionality:
  controlled optionality exercised while preserving constitutive capacities.

protected optionality:
  optionality of protected lineages or value-capable continuations.

joint protected optionality:
  compatible optionality across multiple protected continuations.
```

This prevents the false compression:

```text
more options = more value.
```

Noise, domination, chaos, and power-seeking can all increase crude optionality
while damaging protected continuation.

## Borrowed Frameworks As Effective-Theory Compressors

The project should borrow heavily from established work, but it should not
import whole ontologies wholesale. Each borrowed framework should be treated as
an effective-theory compressor:

```text
it supplies mature objects, theorems, failure modes, and vocabulary for one
layer or bridge obligation.
```

It does not automatically certify the full Alpha-Omega tower.

### Translation Contract Template

For each borrowed framework, record:

```text
Framework:

Borrowed object:

Layer served:

Accepted local assumptions:

Rejected or quarantined assumptions:

Conflict with Alpha-Omega:

AO resolution or deferral:
```

## Adapters As Realizations

The word "adapter" currently covers three jobs. They should be separated.

```text
Substrate compiler:
  raw system -> formal representation.

Semantic realization:
  formal representation -> ContinuationRealization / AgentRealization /
  ValuerRealization candidate.

Theorem instantiation:
  certified realization -> generic theorem result.
```

For synthetic systems, the realization may be constructively exact. For
software systems, it may be extracted from code or declared interfaces. For
physical systems, it is empirical and uncertainty-bearing.

This separation prevents the adapter layer from silently smuggling targets,
dynamics, policies, or boundaries into the theorem layer.

## Phi Reinterpreted

`Phi` should not be the next Alpha-derived object.

It is a higher-layer continuation requirement or typed specification. A future
version may include:

```text
B:
  local constitutive capacities.

C:
  consequence or recovery profile.

L:
  lineage relation.

T:
  transport across presentations.

P:
  protected lineage family.

J:
  joint compatibility condition.

M:
  maintenance mode.
```

The robust kernel consumes only the state-local projection:

```text
phi_local(x) = local capacities + local consequence/recovery condition.
```

Lineage, transport, protected plurality, and maintenance are separate
obligations. They should not be buried inside one Boolean predicate and then
treated as if identity had been solved.

## Agency-Layer Candidate: Operational Causal Diamond

The causal-diamond / controllable-observable loop belongs in the agency layer.
It should not be treated as Alpha or value.

Candidate workflow:

```text
spectral or deformation detector
  -> candidate mode
  -> controllable-observable loop test
  -> live feedback versus matched replay
  -> reflexive maintenance of the observation-selection-action channel
  -> presentation robustness check.
```

This keeps spectral methods in their proper role:

```text
detector, not definition.
```

A plausible agency-candidate test is:

```text
Does a live controllable-observable loop preserve a continuation profile that
matched open-loop replay cannot, while preserving the future availability of
that loop?
```

This is an agency-realization criterion, not a valuer criterion.

## Gradient Ethics As Conditional Constraint Theory

Gradient Ethics should be framed as a constraint theory over certified
continuation requirements:

```text
If Phi is independently certified as necessary for the relevant continuation,
then robust viability and irreversibility theorems constrain policies that
claim to preserve Phi.
```

The formal result is:

```text
certified Phi + controlled dynamics + ambiguity set
  -> robust kernel K_Phi
  -> persistence containment / exit exclusion.
```

The content obligations remain:

```text
Why this Phi?
What makes it valuer-relevant?
Does it survive admissible presentation transport?
Does it preserve protected plurality rather than a destructive macro summary?
```

The theorem layer can isolate those obligations. It does not erase them.

## Strong Success Condition

The strong Alpha-Omega success condition is:

```text
There exists a stable tower of realizations from Alpha to valuer-level
continuation such that:

  higher-layer facts forget to lower-layer traces;

  lower-layer abstractions cannot fabricate higher-layer certificates;

  value-relevant lineages and plural compatibilities are preserved under
  admissible transport;

  irreversible contractions of that structure are formally excluded by the
  resulting constraint theory.
```

If this succeeds, "value from Alpha" is fair in the same sense that
thermodynamics derives from microphysics: not because temperature is visible in
one particle, but because stable effective structure emerges and connects back
down.

## Near-Term Theorem Obligations

### 1. Underdetermination witness

Goal:

```text
same Alpha frame;
two inequivalent continuation or agency realizations.
```

Meaning:

```text
Alpha alone does not determine higher-layer semantics.
```

### 2. Downward necessity theorem

Goal:

```text
agentic power witness
  -> consequence-bearing lower trace
  -> Alpha relation/distinction/asymmetry trace.
```

Meaning:

```text
higher-layer power forgets to Alpha-compatible structure.
```

### 3. Coarse/fine nonreflection theorem

Goal:

```text
fine target preservation/recovery -> coarse target preservation/recovery
coarse target preservation/recovery -/-> fine target preservation/recovery
```

Meaning:

```text
macro continuity does not certify protected fine continuation.
```

This is the first formal anti-erasure theorem for protected plurality.

### 4. Robust-kernel reflection theorem

Goal:

```text
abstract robust viability reflects to exact robust viability only under
requirement reflection, action realizability, and successor soundness.
```

Nearby counterexample:

```text
phantom robust viability.
```

Meaning:

```text
an abstract continuation certificate is not trustworthy without sound
realization.
```

## Immediate Implementation Recommendation

Use this ordering:

```text
1. Document effective layers and translation contracts.
2. Audit active definitions by layer.
3. Prove underdetermination and downward necessity.
4. Prove coarse/fine nonreflection.
5. Then return to robust kernels, lineage, and agency pilots.
```

Do not resume vortical, lushness, valuer, or Omega terminology until the
effective-layer map is stable.

Alpha-Omega should be treated as a staged effective-theory program with
