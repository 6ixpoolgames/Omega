# Substrate Classes and Large Deformers v0

Status: branch-control note / substrate taxonomy
Scope: continuation orientation, classical/quantum-like/quantum adapter classes, biological substrate warnings, large deformer language, and memetic/technological deformer boundaries
Claim boundary: documentation only; not a quantum theory, not biological privilege, not agency, not personhood, not moral standing, not value, not consciousness, not Omega validation

## Purpose

The future-field culling pass keeps quantum, relativity, and large-deformer
language from becoming foundations. This note records the remaining positive
taxonomy so the project can discuss substrate differences without smuggling
standing, agency, or metaphysics.

The central rule:

```text
substrate class changes the preservation adapter;
it does not by itself change moral class.
```

## Continuation Orientation

Omega does not require metaphysical open futurism.

The continuation layer requires oriented downstream dependence:

```text
some distinctions, states, facts, processes, or registers can affect what
follows, what remains recoverable, and what future updates can still certify.
```

That reading is compatible with several metaphysical packages:

```text
ontic openness;
block-compatible causal orientation;
Everettian branch structure;
proof/dependence structure;
embedded-agent update structure.
```

The project should therefore avoid treating "future field" as a commitment to
one theory of time. The formal burden is weaker and cleaner:

```text
preserve oriented downstream dependence and recoverability.
```

## Substrate Classes

The following classes are adapter categories, not status categories.

### Classical

Classical substrates are modeled with Boolean or finite predicates over states,
ordinary transition relations, and recovery facts that can be represented
inside classical presentations.

Typical interface:

```text
state       : finite or structured state;
action      : deterministic, possibilistic, or stochastic transition;
fact        : predicate or registered finite fact;
repair      : transition/action/recovery procedure;
contraction : loss of recoverable predicate, distinction, or register fact.
```

### Quantum-Like

Quantum-like substrates show contextual or observer-bounded behavior at the
effective layer, but may still be classically mimicked by a larger adapter.

Use this class for:

```text
contextual presentation dependence;
noncommuting-looking effective facts;
observer-bound loop closure;
coarse-grain effects that behave quantum-like without forcing Hilbert-space
ontology.
```

Quantum-like is a diagnostic class, not a metaphysical conclusion.

### Quantum

Quantum substrates require an operational interface with noncommuting effects,
channels, and contexts that cannot be replaced by a sound classical adapter for
the declared recovery problem.

Possible interface:

```text
state       : density/operator state or abstract operational state;
action      : channel;
fact        : effect or context-indexed proposition;
repair      : allowed channel or context-preserving procedure;
contraction : loss of recoverable effect, context, distinguishability, or
              correlation.
```

The future quantum branch should begin with operational recovery frames, not
consciousness or valuerhood.

## Quantum Relevance

Quantum relevance changes preservation requirements, not moral standing.

The relevant claim shape is:

```text
if a system's perception, correction, relation, memory, or value-contact
capacity depends on quantum-contextual structure, then interventions that
classicalize, measure, copy, simulate, compress, or decohere that structure
may nonrecoverably contract registered capacity.
```

That rule applies symmetrically to biological and artificial systems.

Do not claim:

```text
quantum = higher status;
biology = privileged substrate;
quantum agency = consciousness;
quantum effects = agency relevance.
```

## Quantum Agency Witness Criteria

A future quantum-agency claim would need at least:

```text
1. relevant quantum degrees of freedom at the right scale and duration;
2. causal load-bearing role in perception, update, action, memory, correction,
   relation, or value-contact capacity;
3. active regulation, protection, amplification, or routing by the system;
4. a strictness witness where classical adapters with matched resources fail;
5. nonrecoverable contraction of a declared register capacity when the quantum
   structure is destroyed or classicalized;
6. reflection across the quantum presentation into the actual substrate.
```

This would show quantum substrate relevance. It would not by itself show
valuerhood, standing, consciousness, or moral superiority.

## Large Deformers

A large deformer is any system whose state, policy, infrastructure, or
continued presence changes the reachable future field of other systems.

Examples may include:

```text
multicellular organisms;
civilizations;
institutions;
technologies;
platforms;
memetic ecologies;
large optimizers;
infrastructure regimes.
```

Large deformer does not imply:

```text
agent;
valuer;
person;
moral patient;
legitimate controller;
singleton.
```

The right audit question is:

```text
which continuation, recovery, register, observation, or relation profiles does
this deformer make easier, harder, recoverable, or unrecoverable for others?
```

## Technology and Memetic Deformers

Technologies and memetic systems can be large deformers without being valuers.

They can reshape:

```text
attention and observation channels;
memetic fitness landscapes;
coordination surfaces;
available repair paths;
register update rules;
which facts remain visible enough to correct.
```

Memetic agency, when discussed, should mean loop-closure and deformer
structure, not personhood.

Claim-safe phrasing:

```text
the meme/platform/technology deforms the future field;
it is not thereby a valuer or moral patient.
```

## Public Compression

Omega is substrate-agnostic, not substrate-blind. Classical, quantum-like,
quantum, biological, technological, and memetic systems may require different
preservation adapters. That changes what must be preserved and how recovery is
certified; it does not by itself assign agency, value, personhood, or moral
standing.
