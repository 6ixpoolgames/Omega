The block-Everett picture treats this development as a whole, with a present section locating the continuation under assessment. This is a motivating interpretation, not an additional dynamical field or a demonstrated account of quantum gravity.

Summing all class operators gives the native propagator. For coarse events, sum class operators before taking their quantum weight. Equivalently,

\[
D(A,B)=\sum_{\alpha\in A,\beta\in B}D_{\alpha\beta},

The interference terms are part of the quantum description from the outset. Intermediate projectors do not represent inserted physical measurements unless the apparatus interaction is actually included. \[

The common ruler means a common physical prescription across compared roots. It does not mean invariant counting mass across horizons. ## Analytical screening: two precise obstructions

### A state-only, fully unitary-invariant scalar cannot distinguish pure-state shapes

If E depends only on rho and obeys E(W rho W-dagger)=E(rho) for every unitary W, it is constant over pure states of a fixed Hilbert-space dimension: any normalized vector can be unitarily mapped to any other.

This does not prove quantum extent impossible. It proves that this narrow input/invariance choice cannot capture distinctions such as physical spatial spread. A spatial measure retains the position structure; our target must likewise retain relevant native relations and dynamics. Passive coordinate covariance transforms these structures along with the state. It must not be confused with invariance under physically rearranging the state while holding those structures fixed.

### Decoherence alone cannot select an intrinsic perplexity

Consider a closed qubit, rho=|0><0|, H=0, and one future cut. No apparatus is inserted.

For Z projectors, branch vectors are |0> and 0, so

\[
D_Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,0),\qquad \exp H(\operatorname{diag}D_Z)=1.
\]

For X projectors, branch vectors are |+>/sqrt(2) and |->/sqrt(2), so

\[
D_X=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/2),\qquad \exp H(\operatorname{diag}D_X)=2.
\]

Both families are decoherent, and both recombine to the same unchanged state. Therefore these two demands cannot both hold:

1. One intrinsic number is unchanged by selecting either family to describe this same native development.
2. That number equals ordinary perplexity for every decoherent family.

This is a direct algebraic result, not a simulation or a claim that X and Z are the same physical observable. They are distinct questions. It excludes promoting the answer to every such question into one family-independent number. An actual X or Z reader would be additional physical dynamics and a different comparison.

A defensible classical-limit condition must specify the physical classical frame/resolution in which it applies. A profile can retain both questions without declaring either the unique extent. A proposed common reference measure must explain this example rather than silently changing cell weights until the numbers agree.

## Conditions for the next candidate

Representation covariance must preserve a jointly transformed state, law, local structure and reference prescription. Adding identity checkpoints or merely subdividing a propagator must not create new physical evolution. Genuine extra physical interactions can change the result.

Independent composition should have a declared product rule; classical perplexity is multiplicative. Coupled systems must use their full joint law. Local marginals do not determine joint extent: correlated and independent fair bits share marginals.

An inert pure ancilla has no independent evolution and should not automatically double occupied continuation extent, even if ambient Hilbert volume doubles. Genuine new dynamics or correlations are separate cases.

Physical-time horizons and complete future development remain explicit. Same endpoint is not the same native development. A loop in quantum evolution versus an idle state can differ as developments even if their endpoints agree; no larger-lushness verdict follows solely from that difference.

No requirement forces organized systems, decoherence, noise suppression or persistent records to increase the extent. Those are empirical/mechanistic questions once a candidate is defined. Gas remains an admissible winner.
