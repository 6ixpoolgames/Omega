## 1. Intended meaning

Omega is the complete physical development of the universe, understood in the
quantum version as the native evolution of its sufficient quantum state and
relational/local structure. The futuresfield introduces no additional physical
substance. A block-Everett interpretation motivates regarding the whole
development together; the finite mathematics does not establish that cosmology.

Lushness names the effective weighted extent of future development from a
present frame. It may require a profile across regions, durations and physical
resolutions. It is not redefined as record count, organization, complexity, or
the list of all properties of the carrier. Generativity is a possible physical
cause of increased lushness: configurations change subsequent access and native
weights through the law. No extra generativity reward is introduced.

Alpha's distinction, relation and orientation remain structural motivations.
They do not determine clock rates, a probability rule, QFT, or an extent measure
by themselves. Wolfram-style rewriting can be an adapter only when its physical
weights, timing and equivalences are supplied; it is not an assumed derivation
of the field theory here.

## 2. Physical object, present and frame

Declare a physical theory or regulated adapter P containing local degrees of
freedom/observable algebras, spatial and causal incidence, constraints and gauge
identifications, and native dynamics. In a finite quantum adapter:

    P = (H_space, {A(R)}, constraints, H(t)).
    i hbar dU(t,s)/dt = H(t) U(t,s).
    rho_t = U(t,0) rho_Sigma U(t,0)^dagger.

The sufficient present rho_Sigma includes environmental state, entanglement,
records and phase information relevant to continuation. The native law and any
declared external schedule are also inputs. A subsystem's reduced state alone
need not be sufficient. Apparatus, controllers, reservoirs and work sources are
part of the physical accounting whenever modeled; an imposed pulse schedule
must not be described as autonomous closed dynamics.

Use

    Omega_(Sigma,T) = [P, rho_Sigma, U(t,s) for 0<=s<=t<=T]_physical-equivalence.

This retains native evolution throughout the interval, not just its endpoint.
The equivalence class is a requirement to specify allowed description changes,
not a solved universal quotient.

The familiar wavefunctional Psi_Sigma[phi] is one possible representation where
available. The algebraic description uses a normalized positive state functional
omega_Sigma on the physical observables; it is more general than assuming every
local QFT state has a finite density matrix.

A bounded frame restricts this same physical object to declared regions,
resolutions and durations. These are situated descriptions of Omega, not a menu
of hypothetical actions for a preselected chooser. Local continuation requires
the relevant environment and boundary correlations; isolated local marginals
cannot be glued into a unique global state without their joint correlations.

## 3. QFT supplies physical structure, not a solved volume

QFT is the preferred physical direction for the quantum adapter: local field
degrees of freedom, couplings, symmetries and causal constraints precede the
measurement. In relativistic formulations a complete suitable spacelike section
plays the role of a present. A finite lattice regulator supplies an approximation,
not automatic Lorentz covariance or a proof of refoliation invariance.

Algebraic QFT relates physical regions to observable algebras. Typical continuum
local algebras are type III, so the ordinary finite matrix trace used in our toy
reference formulas is unavailable there. Sharp-region entanglement also has
ultraviolet difficulties. A finite-regulator trace or extent must therefore be
examined as resolution changes, not promoted unchanged into the continuum.
See [Witten](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.04993).

Real-time Schwinger-Keldysh methods retain paired amplitude/conjugate histories
and interactions. They are appropriate dynamics machinery; the formal field
integration symbol is not already a positive continuation-volume measure.
See [Kamenev and Levchenko](https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.3586).

Process tensors and quantum combs can encode multitime processes and memory as
derived descriptions. Their open operation slots are not themselves physical
branches. Use actual native operations and identity contractions where no action
occurs; do not add a prior over imaginary interventions. Choi rank, tensor bond
dimension and normalized process-state entropy are not adopted as lushness.
See [Pollock et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00589).

These are complementary representations of the dynamics, not three competing
cosmologies or three established extent formulas.

## 4. Distinguish descriptions, classical development cells and coherent events

For a declared history resolution alpha in a finite adapter,

    C_alpha = P_n U_n ... P_1 U_1,
    D(alpha,beta) = Tr(C_alpha rho_Sigma C_beta^dagger).

The full complex D retains interference. For a coarse event A, first sum its
class operators, or equivalently sum every D(alpha,beta) within A. Summing only
diagonal entries generally inserts an incoherent treatment not warranted by the
native process.

Three distinctions must remain explicit:

1. A representation refinement inserts coordinates or computational cuts.
2. A physical resolution chooses which existing distinctions are being described.
3. A physical interaction changes the native development.

The first must not change intrinsic extent. The second can change a declared
frame-relative readout and must not masquerade as the first. The third can alter
extent because the physics changed. An X versus Z question is not merely a passive
coordinate relabeling with all physical structures transformed together.

An identity checkpoint or a different numerical subdivision of the same evolution
creates no physical alternative. An actual excursion followed by reversal remains
a time-extended development, even if its total propagator equals the idle endpoint
map. It does not receive a positive volume bonus merely for having occurred.

Do not erase past distinctions from a complete classical future history when
endpoints reconverge. Re-rooting at the later sufficient present is a new residual
query. Quantum recoherence requires coherent composition and cannot be modeled
by permanently retaining incompatible earlier classical branch probabilities.

## 5. Established classical calibration and extension rule

At a fixed physically declared classical resolution, let H_n contain the lawful
complete developments through cut n, with native normalized weights p_n(h).
Take the explicit unit-cell convention

    C_n = C^(H_n),          tau_n(a) = sum_h a(h),
    L_cl(n) = exp H(p_n).

An inaccessible cell has zero weight; normalization does not make occupied
extent one. A terminal outcome retains its weight and can continue as an
absorbing deterministic symbol.

For a next development e,

    p_(n+1)(h,e) = p_n(h) q(e|h),        sum_e q(e|h)=1,
    log L_cl(n+1)-log L_cl(n) = sum_h p_n(h) H(q(.|h)).

If h has k(h) distinct extensions, prefix inclusion j satisfies

    tau_(n+1)(j a) = sum_h k(h) a(h),

whereas the probability state satisfies omega_(n+1)(j a)=omega_n(a).
Reference extent extends while probability marginalizes. Fair binary extension
gives L_cl(n)=2^n; a deterministic checkpoint adds zero branching entropy.

This is a mathematically established effective-history breadth once the cells
are specified, and our chosen classical calibration for lushness. Its normative
adequacy and sufficiency as substrate extent remain hypotheses. Gas, reversible
noise and repeated stochastic activity may win. A deterministic development has
breadth one without earning a compensatory organization term.

Original-root cumulative classical breadth cannot decrease under extension.
Damage can reduce subsequent growth relative to a matched preparation; residual
extent after re-rooting can differ. These are different comparisons.

## 6. Physically supported classical boundary inside quantum evolution

In the exact pure-state toy regime, a record family R_h can certify histories by

    R_h psi_T = C_h psi_Sigma,

with orthogonal exhaustive R_h. The abstract commutative record algebra assigns
one reference unit per history distinction, not rank(R_h) units per microscopic
Hilbert subspace. It then carries p_h=||C_h psi_Sigma||^2 and exp H(p).

For stable extension, later descendant records refine the transported parent
record projections. This is a concrete quantum realization of the classical
prefix inclusion and permits continued growth without transporting a fixed
initial reference mass.

Our 1,2,3-record examples give 2,4,8 and pass these algebraic checks. Their joint
memory density matrices still have entropy log 2: state entropy and complete
history breadth answer different questions, even in this simple calibration.

The certificate alone does not select physical records. Arbitrary abstract
branch projectors can pass it. The finite example's actual local coupling
schedule supplies provenance; a general rule would need dynamics, locality,
environmental state and a stability/resolution profile. Strong decoherence and
redundant-record histories are relevant established approaches, not unique
selection theorems for Omega:
[Gell-Mann and Hartle](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9509054),
[Riedel, Zurek and Zwolak](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.0331).

Records certify a classical restriction of Omega. They do not exhaust Omega,
and an unrecorded coherent region is not assigned extent one by default.

## 7. The proposed bounds: preserve their domain

With one unit assigned to a single effective continuation, propose a minimum
L>=1 for a normalized nonempty finite-resolution continuation query. This is a
calibration condition, not a lower bound on every possible continuum volume.
Global quantum purity does not imply L=1.

For one fixed physically justified family alpha, native self-weights p_alpha
and common unit-cell ruler, investigate

    1 <= L_Q[alpha] <= exp H(p_alpha),

with equality at the upper boundary when that family becomes classical and
perfectly distinguishable. This is a candidate compatibility requirement.
The upper comparison holds its family and weights fixed. A physical decohering
interaction can also change later dynamics and weights; no universal monotonicity
or fixed ceiling follows for that changed process or a longer horizon.

It is inconsistent to demand equality to every incompatible decoherent family
while demanding one family-independent number. The idle |0> Z/X example already
gives 1 versus 2. A future intrinsic measure would need a physically justified
selection/equivalence prescription, or explicitly remain a profile of frames.

The proposed lower bound from a certified record restriction and the proposed
upper bound from a full specified alternative family concern different objects.
For a compatible nested classical description they can coexist:

    exp H(record) <= L_full <= exp H(full alternatives).

Do not combine bounds across incompatible families. The classical left inequality
follows from deterministic coarse-graining; its extension to a universal quantum
extent is still a requirement, not a proved result.

The latest Holevo candidate exp[S(sum p rho_h)-sum p S(rho_h)] realizes the numerical
bounds for declared ensembles, products and mixed-state controls. It still changes
under an invented midpoint history split of the same physical echo. It remains a
conditional-state distinguishability readout, not the definition of lushness.

## 8. Reference geometry and comparison

A comparison needs a common physical prescription for its calibration. Different
roots change state, accessibility and occupation; they must not silently change
the unit of extent. The ruler may depend on theory, constraints, region, horizon
and physical resolution. No canonical general Lambda or tau is supplied yet,
and the effective-breadth pivot no longer requires one: a common functional or
comparison rule may provide the needed calibration without a base volume measure.

Sol's finite-algebra trace sum_a Tr_a is a useful convention after an algebra is
specified. It cannot select the algebra, supply chronology, or become a continuum
QFT trace automatically. M_d and its diagonal C^d both have reference trace(I)=d;
decoherence is not automatically a growth of this reference dimension.

Coarse probability laws must agree under restriction where a common classical
law exists; coherent descriptions must agree under amplitude-level composition.
Scalar extents need not remain equal under coarse-graining or horizon extension.
Independent physical systems calibrate multiplicatively; coupled systems require
their actual joint law, not products of marginal extents.

Thermal/gas comparisons use the same law, conserved sector, resources, boundary
conditions, duration, frame and ruler. QFT vacuum and thermal gas are physically
different references; the earlier vacuum-energy language was an analogy. A
positive matched excess log L_A-log L_ref establishes an excess for that readout
and regime. It does not establish generativity as its cause or moral value as its
consequence. Value remains something instantiated within frames; lushness is the
proposed substrate proxy.

## 10. One restrained next programme

This was the sequence proposed at consolidation. The subsequent
[pivot](effective_breadth_pivot_2026-10-09.md) inserts a requirements-compatibility
audit on existing fixtures before step 3 and makes a base measure optional.

1. Formalize the existing quantum adapter as a local time-ordered process with
   exact native composition. Separate dummy cuts, physical frame projections,
   and real interactions in its interface. Reuse current fixtures.
2. Attach the verified record and prefix-refinement certificates as classical
   boundary tests. Test idle, echo, retained record and full reversal before any
   scalar promotion. Preserve coherent development where certificates fail.
3. Add one regulated local field adapter: a small coupled-oscillator/scalar-field
   lattice, initially quadratic, with any recording/environment degrees of
   freedom included as physical local couplings. Declare spatial spacing,
   occupation cutoff, time units, boundaries, initial state and any external work.
   This is a substrate upgrade, not a new measure or full continuum QFT claim.
4. Test invariance under coordinate descriptions and numerical time refinement
   separately from dependence on physical resolution. Check oscillator truncation
   before interpreting dynamics. Study nonlinear interactions only after these
   elementary checks; quadratic physics is a carrier calibration, not a test of
   rich generativity.
5. Admit a new extent candidate only with explicit calibration, description
   equivalences, horizon-extension rule and classical limit. Test the existing
   adversarial suite before larger field or gas runs.

No new simulation is warranted by the consolidation alone. It supplies a common
specification and identifies the next mathematical deliverable. Lushness remains
the intended extent; the quantum quantity has not been defined by fiat.
