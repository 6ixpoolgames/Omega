# Assessment of Opus's “Towards v3.2” notes

3 October 2026. Read after the originator requested continuation of the 3.2
edition and pointed to the new Drive assessment. The six read documents are
retained as [text extractions with source links](../../references/opus/towards-v3.2/manifest.json).
They are attributed reports and proposals, not automatically new instructions
or independently verified results.

## Useful contributions

The assessment identifies the right central gap: organized physical
possibility is not yet an established measure of possible value. It preserves
the idea that familiar protections should eventually be investigated as
consequences of a comparison rather than inserted as favorable coefficients.
Its warning about a changing readout is useful if applied as versioned
exploration, not a prohibition on correcting a failed candidate.

The most concrete new reported lead is the fixed-apparatus ethics sweep:
126,709 outcomes, five source-bias settings, with a whole-frame tally favoring
complementary records rather than the deciding bank's own optimum. If
reproducible, this would demonstrate a divergence between two specified
information objectives and characterize where it occurs. The simulator,
enumeration, score implementation and raw outputs were not supplied in the
Drive folder read here. It remains reported evidence, not a verified
derivation of non-excision, plurality or ethical authority.

The proposed time-extended and variable-population follow-ups are sensible.
They expose assumptions that the static tally holds fixed. The parity repair
example is also sound at the level of exact information: (u,v,u XOR v) permits
reconstruction after any one known-location erasure, given an available
decoder and positive support. Preparation, latency, fault law and repair
resources still determine its practical comparison.

## Corrections that matter to the edition

| Statement in the notes | Assessment |
|---|---|
| The comparison engine has converged | Premature. The subset tally is acknowledged to fail under changing population, its count-once replacement is not implemented, and the physical extent is unspecified. |
| The fixed number of registers prevents the tally flaw from biting | It removes that particular population-size confound. It does not establish a canonical measure on subsets or validate the tally as physical access. |
| Counting each distinction once follows from weights entering once | Two different issues. Normalizing event probabilities once does not uniquely determine how overlapping frames, tasks or physical channels should be aggregated. |
| Copies add nothing under the proposed replacement | True for distinct joint source content, false for access in general. New spatial readers, simultaneous delivery and recovery routes can be physically new without a new source distinction. |
| Time, energy and distance reduce to unweighted graph steps | Not established. A chosen finite gate count is a useful declared bill; topology alone does not determine physical duration, energy or metric distance. |
| Coding theorems derive the copying/composition result | Useful analogy, not an equivalence. These are bounded one-shot circuit and erasure examples. Source/channel coding theorems have explicit asymptotic, channel, error and coding assumptions. |
| Composition strictly improves whenever there are two distinctions | False generally. If a feasible copy bank already retains all source information, no deterministic record exceeds H(X). A language containing copying weakly includes its performance; strict improvement needs an actual constraint and witness. |
| Full-information optima “were simply copies of everything” | Full input partitions also admit invertible recodings. Their implementation and later access can differ. Partition classification is not physical identity. |
| Negative Syn means a general redundancy/synergy balance | The checked identity is TC(X|F)−TC(X). A particular PID interpretation would require further definitions; the sign alone is not a universal decomposition. |
| Quantum mutual information double-counts entanglement | I(A:B)=2H(A) for a pure entangled bipartite state is correct quantum correlation. It is the wrong readout for some classical-record questions, not an arithmetic duplication. Holevo information is an upper bound on accessible classical information for a specified ensemble. |
| Quantum mechanics is unnecessary for weights after branching | Classical bookkeeping works within a suitable recorded/decoherent family. Born weights, interference/recoherence and the choice of compatible family remain physical quantum questions. |
| A branch exists exactly while a record survives | A useful description of the stated eraser setup, too general as a universal criterion. The compatible history family and remaining correlations must be specified. |
| Identity as trajectory solves Newcomb | Identity does not choose between conditioning on a correlated policy and intervening on a later action. An updateless/policy comparison is an additional decision rule, not entailed by determinism or identity. |
| Bounded agents can never license any sacrifice | Does not follow from incomplete knowledge alone. That requires an additional exact-knowledge licensing rule; applying it generally can also block rescue or triage. The notes' practical/theological verdict is not a finite result. |
| Boundedness proves the richest proxy robustly forecloses fewer values | Faithful containment can transfer attainable responses. A larger scalar or different distribution does not establish containment or unanimous realized value. Plenitude may remain a motivating assumption without this extra theorem. |
| Surviving generativity generically defeats gas | Unsupported by supplied evidence; descendant-weight readouts and the previously audited thermal-tail mismatch remain. An inequality between upper bounds cannot by itself order the true quantities. |
| Capacity bounds solve fanaticism | A bound can constrain a fully specified utility/readout and region. It does not supply the unknown lushness measure, a bound on an infinite temporal aggregate, or a general decision theorem. |

## Exact tally problem

For N registers containing independent fair bits, the sum of their subset
entropies is N·2^(N−1): each bit appears in half of the subsets. For N copies
of one fair bit, it is 2^N−1. Thus ten independent records give 5,120 and
thirteen copies give 8,191. The arithmetic checks.

This does not declare physical copying worthless. It shows that the
particular aggregate weights the number of subset descriptions heavily.
Whether the resulting preference is warranted cannot be settled by calling
the sum the ultimate frame.

The suggested repair — credit a distinction only through its globally
cheapest frame — has a different potential blindness. If one local reader
already holds s at minimum cost, installing a distant copy can leave that
global minimum unchanged while enabling a second simultaneous response or
survival of the first reader's loss. The literal minimum-only rule misses
those changes unless its response coordinates explicitly retain destinations,
joint execution and residual recovery. That would bring it toward the access
atlas, but it is additional specification, not supplied by “count once.”

Also, frame size and a vector bill need not possess a common minimizing
frame. Keep feasible frontiers. The identity of a “distinction” must be
defined without privileging an arbitrary encoding or admitting an unlimited
set of logically overlapping functions as independent content.

## Verification and attribution

The notes attribute the 100,000-program sampled run and its 1,498/29,166
repair comparison to Astra. Those exact artifacts in this workspace were
produced in the present Codex workflow. An additional assistant recounting
them is not another independent replication.

Codex reconstructed the earlier G1/G2/G3 endpoint counts and selected synergy
examples; that does not verify all of the later claimed atlas statistics,
prefix/restart checks, scratch-register experiment or eleven-qubit run.
The reported 97% comparison rate also describes a different or unspecified
population from the local sampled report; the percentages should not be pooled.

The Drive notes add no code or raw outputs for those newer claims. The
edition can retain their exact provenance and use them to propose tests,
but cannot certify them as implemented and checked here.

## Disposition for v3.2

Retain the ethics sweep, count-once aggregation and policy-level decision
reading as attributed proposals or reported results, alongside this audit.
Add the population tally calculation and the access caveat to the candidate
discussion. Keep the source/actual-law distinction, physical bills and full
residual in the main architecture. Do not promote a new lushness formula,
Newcomb solution, universal gas ranking or derived prohibition on sacrifice.

The assessment is valuable as a research diagnosis. Its confidence in the
ethical conclusions exceeds the currently supplied evidence. The proposed
program remains worth pursuing without claiming that those gaps are closed.
