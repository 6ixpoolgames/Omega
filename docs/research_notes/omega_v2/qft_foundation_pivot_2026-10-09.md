# QFT foundation pivot

Decision date: 2026-10-09. Restored during the quantum documentation cleanup
from the adopted project brief. No new field simulation or continuum result.

Native local quantum field dynamics is the preferred physical foundation for
the next adapter. Omega denotes the physical development itself, with a
sufficient present and its law. Characterizing that possibility structure is
worthwhile independently of a scalar lushness measure or ethical application.

Finite circuits remain exact calibration fixtures. Observable nets, histories,
process tensors and Schwinger-Keldysh methods are representation tools; they
are not competing cosmologies or already established measures of lushness.
Hypothetical intervention slots must not become additional native possibilities
merely because a representation permits queries about them.

The next physical adapter may be a small regulated scalar/oscillator lattice,
with locality, environment, regulator and externally supplied work declared.
No specific Hamiltonian is adopted here. Such an adapter would not establish
Lorentz covariance, a continuum limit, quantum gravity or block-Everett ontology.
Typical continuum local QFT algebras do not supply the finite trace used in toy
proposals. A thermal gas reference is distinct from the QFT vacuum.

The [compatibility audit](../../quantum/COMPATIBILITY_AUDIT.md) comes first.
Its purpose is to determine which missing physical structure a larger adapter
must test. This pivot does not reinstate a mandatory base measure: see the
[effective-breadth pivot](effective_breadth_pivot_2026-10-09.md).
Use the [quantum platform](../../quantum/README.md) for the working formalism
and the [earlier consolidation](field_first_consolidation_v1.md) for provenance.
