# Continuation extent: proposal review and recommended repair

2026-10-05. Requested synthesis and log publication. This note recommends the next implementation; it does not report a new run or adopt a lushness measure.

## Recommendation

Continue with a finite, physically located history-covering profile. Repair the geometry and the treatment of frames before another chemistry sweep. Retain intrinsic geometric volume as a restricted comparison where the required smoothness and dimension actually exist.

The primary target remains the extent of weighted, relational physical continuation. The existing frame construction supplies conditional probabilities. Do not replace that target with event activity, recovery alone, an external chooser's menu, or a collection of unrelated performance statistics.

There are two separate repair jobs:

1. Mathematical well-definition: current stochastic jump histories do not automatically form the Riemannian manifold in Sol's latest formula.
2. Structural adequacy: a valid volume of distinguishable whole histories can still forget physical routing, composition, and internal structure.

Fixing the determinant alone will not fix the second problem. The recommendation below advances the second with a computation already within reach, while keeping the first honest.

## Review of the proposal sequence

| Proposal | What to retain | What prevents adoption as lushness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hill/Renyi numbers of consequential history classes | Native weights, complete histories, resolution profile | Residual annotations do nothing when the formula reads only the unchanged probability list. |
| Union of accessible cones; similarity or magnitude | Overlap, reconvergence, access geometry | Bare support loses catalytic kinetics; pairwise overlaps lose higher intersections; access difficulty and distinction distance have different meanings. |
| State-time-resource tube volume | Physical timing, constrained configurations, explicit resource state | Probability-weighted one-time occupancy reduces to T for equal cells. It discards temporal coupling. |
| Weighted history coverings | Executable extent sensitive to temporal dependence and probability concentration | Whole-state mismatch forgets physical subsystem relations; sample-selected centers can grossly misestimate population covers. |
| Local stochastic field and distinguishability geometry | Site/link representation, local and joint physical records, consistent restrictions | Physics does not uniquely supply a metric just because it is called distinguishability; one-site marginal distances can miss joint structure. A notation change does not improve the physical generator. |
| Induced Riemannian history volume | Genuine geometry-dependent measure on suitable smooth history families | Rank, multiplicity, jump discontinuities, mixed dimensions and probability atoms need explicit treatment. Cumulative history volume does not contract upon reconvergence. |

The supplied proposals therefore contribute compatible ingredients, but none of the supplied scalar formulas is ready to serve as the general extent.

## Why prioritize the geometry already implicated by the data

The completed local covering probe used two bits S,D with the same independent-fair preparation. In one process D relaxes toward S; in the other it relaxes toward zero, at matched error-clock rates. Whole-state covering tied in all 36 compared coordinates. Physically located destination-frame covering differed in 11 of 36. The full-state bijection (S,D) -> (S,D xor S) preserves the old mismatch metric while changing the physical destination map.

This locates a concrete loss: the whole-state metric allows more symmetries than the full physical apparatus does. It does not show that the complete frame profile failed. We should use the information already retained before changing the physics or seeking a more elaborate volume formula.

These are finite observed-law results, not a classical-chemistry gas result or a proof that every rewiring must change a scalar extent. Published log reports describe the completed local runs; reproduction code and raw archives are outside this documentation-only checkpoint.

## Concrete candidate v1

### 1. One law and its physical observation maps

Use one small existing finite generator Q and its existing frame-conditioned history law P. Retain the physical site/record incidence, resource constraints, local and joint observation maps, and their restriction relations. A history remains a whole physically compatible development.

For the first implementation, use two or three registers and a finite set of observation times. Enumerate the exact joint observed-history law from the same generator. This is a finite restriction of a continuous process, not a claim to resolve all continuous-time histories. Finer temporal restrictions come from the same process, not newly fitted independent generators.

Take every nonempty register subset in this tiny model as an observation region. For larger lattice chemistry, regions can later be indexed by physical locality and scale. The record available to the conditioning frame and the physical observations used by the encompassing description must remain distinct when they differ.

### 2. Preserve local and joint discrepancies instead of choosing fitted coefficients

For each region A, use an explicitly declared record map O_A. At sampled times t_j, with bin durations Delta t_j, define

    d_A(gamma, eta) = sum_j Delta t_j * 1[O_A(gamma(t_j)) != O_A(eta(t_j))].

This is a pseudometric on full histories: a physical difference outside A can be invisible in that coordinate while remaining present elsewhere. Time is measured in physical units. The initial instrument uses exact native discrete record equality, not a claim of fundamental quantum resolution.

Keep the vector of these distances. Do not sum site, bond, fuel and record differences with invented conversion coefficients. Joint-region records are essential; this is not a set of one-site marginal distributions.

For a vector of tolerances epsilon_A, a common history neighborhood is

    B_epsilon(eta) = {gamma: d_A(gamma,eta) <= epsilon_A for every retained A}.

This intersection is evaluated on whole histories. A representative must itself be a physically admissible joint observed history; independently selected local representatives cannot be glued into an impossible development.

The tolerances are declared resolution coordinates of the profile, not moral weights. Their grid is an exploration choice and can be expanded transparently. A single preferred set of tolerances is not being adopted as an ethical arbitration rule.

### 3. Compute one family of joint extents

    N(epsilon, alpha; Phi, Sigma, T)
      = minimum number of admissible common history neighborhoods
        whose union has native conditional probability at least alpha.

This is one systematic extent family on one law, not a sum of separately optimized frame counts. Overlapping frames and overlapping balls are handled by the probability of the actual union, so mass is not counted once per reader. Physical copies remain distinct parts of the apparatus; only duplicate descriptions are harmless.

No trajectory is deleted from the underlying law. Alpha controls the mass to be represented at a coordinate. Omitted mass remains visible, and alpha=1 is computable for the finite observed-law version. An infinite continuous support can require infinitely many balls and must not be silently truncated or renormalized.

This construction already includes the old per-frame profiles: set all other tolerances to their maximal value. Jointly constrained coordinates can be added without averaging or voting across frames. The full law, observation maps and restriction maps remain available for diagnosing collisions in the compressed extent.

Consistent relabeling must transform the generator, physical incidence and all record maps together. An arbitrary bijection of global state symbols alone is not sufficient to establish physical equivalence. No biological or identity labels enter the extent.

### 4. Preserve both cumulative and residual comparisons

The cumulative profile starts at a fixed original cut and keeps consequential prefixes. The residual profile starts at a later cut and uses the same process conditioned on records actually held there. Where a frame does not determine the complete physical state, propagate the conditional mixture over states; do not hand it a hidden microstate.

A repair can restore a residual channel while the cumulative record still contains the damage. A common suffix does not remove earlier history. These are restrictions/conditionings of the same continuation object, not separate ethical objectives to combine with a multiplier.

## What this repair does and does not establish

It fixes a specific inappropriate invariance of the old whole-state geometry and preserves joint compatibility in the proposed finite computation. It does not prove that larger N is greater lushness, or that the profile is a complete invariant of continuation structure.

A deeper limitation remains: every singleton whole-history space has cover number one, regardless of the internal organization of its sole history. No choice of distances between that singleton's points can change this. Likewise, reliable construction can concentrate path probability and lower a diversity readout. Those are structural limitations of a pure between-history extent, not grounds to delete failures or add construction rewards.

The full carrier therefore retains the internal physical relations and residual continuation maps. The next probe asks how much of that structure the repaired profile actually captures. If a consequential distinction repeatedly lives only in retained annotations while the proposed extent cannot use it, the extent remains a distinguishability diagnostic. We would then need a construction that measures the internal relational developments too. That would be a substantive new definition, not a missing factor that can be supplied by calling a determinant physical.

This is a computable next candidate for exploration, not a promise that all known limitations have been solved. It also does not require a universal winner or a categorical defeat of thermal continuation.

## How to repair the smooth-volume branch

Where there really is a smooth, finite-dimensional family, use intrinsic image dimension and the area formula to handle rank and parameter multiplicity. Preserve distinct histories even when their endpoints agree. Keep dimensional strata separate until a physical scale justifies combining them.

For Gamma_xi(t)=C exp(A t) xi, the induced history metric is the finite-horizon observability Gramian. A small contraction/expansion example can calibrate this volume against finite coverings. It is an analytic comparison case, not a reason to rewrite the chemistry as a smooth system.

For CTMC paths, do not smooth jump times merely to make the determinant finite. The one-jump family already has a valid nonsmooth metric geometry and exact covering behavior. Finite-resolution covers remain meaningful without an additive-volume limit. Native probabilities remain defined even where they lack a density relative to a proposed smooth measure.

If an intrinsic reference measure is later justified, the generalized effective-volume formula using p=dP/dmu is a legitimate additional readout. Its scale dependence and mixed-dimensional treatment remain explicit. It does not automatically repair an unsuitable underlying geometry.

## Bounded next implementation

1. Reuse the exact finite history laws and admissible centers; implement simultaneous frame neighborhoods and preserve the observation/restriction maps.
2. Rerun the matched coupling contrast and a consistent whole-apparatus relabeling. The latter checks representation hygiene; the former evaluates the repaired geometry.
3. Reuse temporal persistence, catalytic speed with unchanged support, reliable construction, independent noise and reconvergence. Add one small shared-resource/joint-enabling contrast only if the existing controls do not exercise joint neighborhoods.
4. Include the singleton/reliability limitations in the report. Classify unchanged coordinates as a real tie, known compression, unresolved approximation, or failure of the intended measurement claim; do not prescribe all rankings in advance.
5. Only after this small comparison, apply the same definition to selected existing chemistry trajectories. Sample coverage must be assessed out of sample; empirical codebook fits are not exact population extents.

The next deliverable should be an extent-profile table with failure explanations and replayable inputs. No large simulation sweep, QFT port, new fuel transport or outcome-fitted metric is needed for this step.
