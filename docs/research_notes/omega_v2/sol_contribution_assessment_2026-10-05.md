# Assessment of Sol's contribution/baseline comments

2026-10-05. User-supplied comments, assessed during the authorized corridor run.
This is an assessment, not wholesale adoption or authorization for new ecology
physics. Direct user commitments and the existing frame construction govern.

## Productive clarifications

- A contribution can occur before a structure dissolves. Persistence changes
  its extent, likelihood and renewal; permanent survival is not admission.
- Failure of event, information-share or correlation measures does not disprove
  the intended physical continuation extent. Access cost is a candidate part
  of its geometry, not a replacement for the full field.
- Mechanisms should be judged structurally. Familiarity, biological labels,
  numbers of copies and diversity do not carry automatic bonuses.
- The substrate premise remains a defeasible response to uncertainty about
  value. Better evidence should matter; refusing revision to protect a proxy
  would conflict with the programme's intent.
- Interacting ecologies are a hypothesis worth examining, not an established
  winner over replication. New jointly enabled transformations would be a
  substantive feature; mere multiplicity would not demonstrate it.

## Claims that require correction or qualification

1. **Thermal is a reference, not a structural null.** The current equilibrium
   has native reactions, fluctuations and substantial bonded assemblies.
   Calling it zero must not remove those histories. A comparison relative to
   a stated thermal preparation is legitimate; subtracting a universal
   background, or treating every difference as a surplus above a physical
   zero, has not been defined. Temperature, confinement, conserved quantities
   and the model's interactions shape that reference.
2. **All consequential structure is represented, including below-baseline
   cases.** Admission cannot require an observed substrate expansion. Such a
   rule would again discard unknown or currently adverse consequential forms.
   Representation and preference must stay distinct.
3. **A favorable coordinate identifies that coordinate's advantage.** A
   finite super-baseline episode in a defined readout can be a real witness.
   It is not automatically an increase in the yet-unselected lushness extent,
   nor does it require other coordinates to improve before it is informative.
4. **The old bounded-growth result remains qualified.** The earlier audit
   found changed transition laws between GAS and NUC, omitted reverse paths
   and an unproved common thermal tail. It did not verify a clean general
   gas-versus-lushness comparison. "Gas eventually wins" is not established
   by eventual equilibration either: equal endpoints do not erase different
   histories, and their comparison still requires a specified functional.
5. **Updating is a design commitment, not proved corrigibility.** Sol's
   expected-value-density language is a future interpretation, not an adopted
   formula or permission to add valuation multipliers to current probes.
   Learning must be accommodated within the existing frame/evidence machinery.
6. **The suffering discussion is a possible bridge hypothesis.** An
   evolutionary account or a structural correlation would not by itself
   settle the moral status of experience or justify ignoring cases where the
   proxy and experience disagree. No medical or evolutionary result is
   established by our simulations.
7. **Monoculture dominance must mean physical continuation dominance.** A
   catalogue of transformations, or one system's ability to emulate outputs,
   does not establish preservation of situated access, joint feasibility,
   timing, histories and actual probability weights. Even established
   structural dominance would enter the stipulated substrate argument;
   it would not independently prove permission for a real-world takeover.
8. **The proposed ecology ladder is not yet an experiment specification.**
   We should obtain replication, specialization and complementary access from
   common justified physical rules, rather than build success into five named
   model classes. Match resource/preparation accounts where possible and
   retain mismatches. Current chemistry has no demonstrated hereditary recipe
   or distinct specialized functional types.

## Relation to the new observations

The [corridor panel](lattice_corridor_report_v0.md) finds increased finite
catalytic product reuse in fueled preparations, with an intermediate-strength
peak across sampled densities. Some products assist construction then disappear.
This supports the transient-contribution distinction, while the assembled
equilibrium directly cautions against gas-as-structural-zero language.

The [template-failure panel](lattice_template_damage_report_v0.md) shows nearly
recovered endpoint formation rates with fewer accumulated constructions after
damage. This is a concrete history-versus-endpoint distinction. It identifies
a dependency without making fragility a reason to exclude the contribution.

These results advance the physical mechanism investigation. They do not select
a lushness scalar, prove an ecology ranking, or validate the normative bridge.
