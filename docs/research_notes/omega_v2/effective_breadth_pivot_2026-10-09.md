# Effective breadth pivot

Decision date: 2026-10-09. Restored during the quantum documentation cleanup
from the adopted project brief. Documentation of direction, not a new result.

Lushness asks how much physically weighted continuation a present admits.
It need not first be constructed as a literal geometric volume. A functional,
profile or partial comparison on native development is a legitimate target.
Classical complete-history perplexity is an effective number, not an additive
set measure. A reference measure, trace or manifold remains an optional route.

Removing that prerequisite does not remove physical calibration: equivalent
descriptions must agree; frames, resource constraints and weights must be
declared; physical composition must use the joint native law. No extra reward
is assigned to organization, generativity, records or value. Gas may win.

Preserve the sufficient present, full coherent development and existing frame
construction. Original-root classical histories retain their prefixes after
reconvergence; rerooting asks a new residual question. Quantum lower-one and
classical-upper requirements are conditional on the same justified family,
weights and calibration. Global purity is not extent one, and failure of a
record certificate is not absence of possibility.
