# Alpha-Omega Fundamental Theory Ledger v0

Status:

```text
working theory ledger / post-freeze reconstruction reference
```

This note records the current fundamental architecture, the reasons for its
choices, the live alternatives, and the claims that remain open. It is not a
proof and does not replace experiment protocols or retained reports.

## Project Intention

The project seeks a minimal and substrate-general account of:

```text
how distinguishable relational structure supports continuation;
how projected histories acquire directional structure;
how persistent processes become sites of valuer-relative consequence;
how several such continuations compose or obstruct one another;
and what additional normative commitment is needed to govern action.
```

The intended full sequence is:

```text
Alpha:
  value-neutral continuation structure

Omega:
  the complete compatibility geometry of valuer-bearing continuations

Gradient Ethics:
  the explicit normative interpretation applied after the Humean gap
```

Alpha and Omega are not independent foundations. They are the same
continuation fabric read from different ends: Alpha from the construction of
possible continuation, Omega from the global compatibility of the valuers
that arise within it.

## Primitive And Constructed Layers

### Structural substrate

The mathematical substrate is an ordinary relational structure:

```text
carrier:
  token positions with equality

relations:
  finite incidence among those positions
```

Minimal distinction requires no bespoke operation. Within a carrier:

```text
x != y
```

is token distinction. It means that alternatives exist, not that a process can
choose between them.

Token labels carry no theoretical significance. Structure is compared up to
isomorphism. This prevents temporary implementation names from becoming
primitive identity.

The project must retain four different levels:

```text
token distinction:
  inequality inside one configuration

structural distinction:
  separation under automorphism or isomorphism

presentation distinction:
  separation through a declared map

behavioral distinction:
  separation by continuation or observation
```

Only the first belongs at the substrate floor.

### Relation

The core should be parameterized by a finite relational signature. The first
executable adapter may use one binary relation, giving a directed graph.

This choice is deliberately ordinary. It allows graphs, hypergraphs, typed
structures, and physical adapters to meet a common interface without claiming
that one graph encoding is ontologically final.

### Rewrite completion

A local relational rewrite may be represented algebraically by:

```text
L <- K -> R
```

where the interface `K` records what one rewrite transports. There is no
primitive global identity across sections. Longer trajectories arise from
composed transport.

The abstract rulial move is:

```text
include every admitted finite local rule up to structural equivalence;
include every finite composition;
select no particular law in the abstract completion.
```

This is a mathematical completion construction, not a public claim that every
rule is physically instantiated.

The established general architecture has two levels:

```text
semantic interface:
  transition system or coalgebra supplied to Alpha

abstract generator:
  algebraic relational rewriting producing such a system
```

Actual-universe adapters may supply empirically supported dynamics directly.
They need not reconstruct those dynamics from the all-rules envelope.

### Universal operation analogy

NAND is functionally complete for Boolean functions. Small universal
combinator or rewrite bases similarly simulate general computation.

The project may use a small universal rewrite basis as an implementation
theorem. It must not identify computational universality with preservation of:

```text
locality;
branch multiplicity;
path length;
causal independence;
or physical interpretation.
```

The semantic definition therefore remains relational rewrite completion.

## Laws And The Rulial Sieve

A law sector is generated by a fixed rule family:

```text
Sector(R) = every history generated by R.
```

Lawfulness does not imply persistence. A law may:

```text
terminate;
disintegrate every input;
cycle;
branch;
preserve patterns;
or construct new patterns.
```

The phrase "rulial Darwinism" is retained as project shorthand. It means a
sieve or redundancy-selection picture, closer to the intended use of
"Darwinism" in Quantum Darwinism than to literal biological evolution.

The formal theory currently commits only to:

```text
the complete candidate envelope;
finite sector profiles;
and later tests identifying what each sector supports.
```

It does not yet derive a measure over rule space or claim that most rules
produce coherent observers.

For actual universes, an adapter supplies the investigated law sector. For the
abstract layer, all bounded sectors remain available. A future theory may
explain why observer-bearing sectors are selected, but current Alpha theorems
must not depend on that explanation.

## Projection, Orientation, And Order

A projection is a declared query, not a globally privileged observer:

```text
p : configurations or events -> ordered index
```

The abstract adapter may retain every bounded structural projection up to
isomorphism. An actual-universe adapter supplies a physically justified family,
such as:

```text
causal time functions;
spatial coordinates;
scale or renormalization depth;
or refinement order.
```

An observer selects one member of that family. Every conclusion remains
indexed by the projection used.

Relation plus projection induces a preorder:

```text
x <=_p y
iff
y is reachable from x through projection-nondecreasing transitions.
```

Mutual reachability forms equivalence classes. Their quotient is partially
ordered. Sections are level fibers, antichains, or frontiers of this projected
order.

No admissibility predicate is expected to identify one true projection.
Instead, findings are classified as:

```text
universal across the declared projection family;
existential;
projection-dependent;
or null.
```

An existential result under a hand-selected projection is weak evidence. A
result that survives a declared projection orbit is stronger.

## Directional Grain

The term "axis" has often served as informal intuition. The more precise
structure is:

```text
projection:
  the sectioning query

directional grain:
  an observed difference between its two directions

polarity or arrow:
  the sign or organization of a non-null grain
```

The abstract comparison may inspect:

```text
ancestor and descendant growth;
forward and backward residuals;
source and sink boundaries;
path-reversal support or weight;
contraction and expansion profiles.
```

Possible outcomes include:

```text
null;
one-sided;
cyclic;
Janus;
local;
or mixed.
```

The projection does not guarantee an arrow. It exposes a comparison. If the
comparison is null, Alpha reports null.

A Big-Bang-like boundary may appear as a low-complexity source stratum of a
complete projected sector. A simulation initialized at one seed has instead
declared an experimental boundary. These must not be conflated.

The familiar intuition:

```text
t+1 > t0 ~= t-1
```

is not a universal statement about raw reachable-state count. Existing v2
controls already exhibit expansion, contraction, equivalence, and mixed
transitions.

Its defensible observer-relative version concerns conditional uncertainty:

```text
future histories conditional on the present record
may remain less resolved than
past histories conditional on that record and a boundary condition.
```

The acquisition of a new record selects one residual cell. This is the
"zipper" intuition. It remains an empirical or adapter-relative asymmetry, not
a substrate axiom.

## Coherence Profile, Not Coherence Primitive

There is no single established predicate capturing every use of "coherence."
The project must keep separate:

```text
well-formed composition;
causal consistency of independent rewrites;
confluence;
termination;
recurrence;
bounded extendability;
predictive organization;
and structural stability.
```

A lawful system may be confluent and terminating. It may lawfully
disintegrate. A stochastic or branching system may be nonconfluent without
being malformed.

The current foundation therefore returns a sector profile. Any later sieve
must declare which coordinates it consumes.

## Histories And Coarse-Graining

History identity is stratified:

```text
exact event history;
structural history up to relabeling;
history up to commuting independent events;
presentation-relative history;
projection-relative history.
```

Coarse-graining can merge histories. It cannot retroactively make them
identical at the exact layer.

Every branch count or growth result must name its quotient. This is required
to prevent duplicate presentations and commuting event orders from inflating
continuation.

## Alpha

For a supplied law sector `R` and projection `p`, Alpha is the value-neutral
continuation object containing:

```text
sections;
projected histories;
reachability;
residual continuation structure;
directional profile;
and any discovered source or sink strata.
```

Capital Alpha may be read as the indexed family across sectors and
projections.

Alpha does not yet contain:

```text
value;
standing;
a moral objective;
or a preferred compatible realization.
```

Its role is to expose the geometry in which loss, reconstruction, and
alternative continuation can later become consequential.

## Pattern, Vorticality, And Process

Persistence is not required to establish the floor.

The first post-floor question is whether histories contain patterns that can
be reidentified under transport or predictive equivalence.

Vorticality is a stronger downstream candidate:

```text
constituents may turn over;
causal organization reconstructs the pattern;
records and effects remain attributable across the trajectory.
```

The trajectory, not a static token set, is the candidate bearer of identity.
Vorticality remains a bridge, not a proof of:

```text
observerhood;
agency;
consciousness;
valuerhood;
or standing.
```

## Soft Pan-Valuerhood

The project should not begin with a binary valuer threshold.

The soft pan-valuer maneuver is:

```text
admit every trackable projection-relative pattern into a candidate envelope;
assign no automatic moral standing;
derive stronger grades from endogenous consequence;
and test conclusions across candidate assignments and thresholds.
```

Potential grading evidence includes:

```text
endogenous records;
self-maintenance;
policy sensitivity;
causal reach;
reconstruction;
and deformation of reachable continuation.
```

This means that nothing is silently excluded before testing. It does not mean
that every bare token has equal moral importance.

## Value Requires Valuers

Value is not a property of the bare relational substrate.

A candidate becomes substantively valuer-like when distinctions among its
reachable continuations affect its own organization, policy, or
reconstruction. Its dynamics then supply a continuation ordering or gradient:

```text
C1 >_v C2
```

This need not be scalar. Value exists here as value for a valuer.

The first value-bearing substrate is a contiguous process trajectory. The
framework has not proved that persistence is lexically supreme, only that
without a continuing bearer there is no process-relative value to attribute.

## Capital Omega

For candidate family `F`, let:

```text
Real(F) = the complete fiber of histories jointly realizing F.
```

Then:

```text
MayCompatible(F):
  Real(F) is nonempty.

RobustlyCompatible(F):
  one policy secures a realizing history
  across every environment in the declared scope.
```

Capital Omega is the full decorated compatibility structure:

```text
every compatible face;
every realization fiber;
May and Robust kept separate;
higher-order obstructions;
incomparable maximal faces;
and transports across projections and presentations.
```

Omega is not one selected maximal coalition. A valuer outside one compatible
realization remains a valuer; its continuation is incompatible with that
realization, not erased from the underlying theory.

Existing Omega v2 machinery has already retained finite May and Robust fibers,
higher-order obstruction, and generated-controller separation after dynamics
are supplied. The new floor is intended to become their eventual upstream
foundation, not to rewrite those results.

## Generative Omega And Lushness

Generative Omega asks whether compatible realization remains open to:

```text
new valuer-bearing processes;
new correction registers;
new value distinctions;
and new mutually compatible continuation dimensions.
```

Lushness is not raw branch count. Accounting must distinguish:

```text
aggregation;
genuine emergence;
capture;
and phantom emergence.
```

Duplicate presentations must combine idempotently rather than count as new
continuation.

The theory has not proved that more reachable futures are always better.
Lushness remains a conditional structural and later ethical pressure where
nonduplicate compatible value-bearing continuation is genuinely available.

## The Humean Gap

Before the normative bridge, the framework may determine:

```text
what each candidate values;
which candidates are affected;
which continuations are jointly realizable;
which losses are recoverable;
which conclusions depend on projection;
which compensation claims are incomplete;
and where genuine obstruction exists.
```

Those facts do not by themselves entail that an actor ought to respect them.

The intended residual commitment is approximately:

```text
endogenous continuation gradients of affected valuers constitute
prima facie reasons for action;
and those reasons may not be erased by a merely flattering presentation or
projection.
```

This premise must be signed for rather than presented as a theorem of
dynamics.

## Gradient Ethics

Gradient Ethics begins after the Humean bridge.

It interprets Omega's valuer-relative continuation geometry through a
licensing discipline:

```text
use sound presentations;
include affected valuer-relative gradients;
preserve robust compatible corridors;
refuse fabricated or stale compensation;
do not presume irreversible contraction is replaced;
return obstruction when no joint continuation exists;
and choose only within the surviving action correspondence.
```

It is not a scalar maximization principle. Omega narrows the feasible and
justifiable action surface; Gradient Ethics supplies the explicitly normative
reason to treat that narrowing as action-guiding.
