# Contribution-first probe roadmap

2026-10-05. Logged at the originator's request; the next exploratory run is authorized.

The current threshold is credible favorable regimes, ideally a broad corridor,
not categorical dominance over gas. Fragility shapes the probability, duration
and cost of contribution; permanent persistence is not an admission requirement.
Failures, thermal noise and unsuccessful starts remain in the physical law.
Finite contributions and consequences through successors remain relevant after
the original structure disappears. None of these commitments selects a lushness
functional or licenses treating a favorable diagnostic as that functional.

1. Add the actual equilibrium preparation of the existing reversible 2D model.
   Inspect its assemblies rather than assume equilibrium means dispersed gas.
   Compare it with the same equilibrium configurations refueled, and dispersed
   fueled matter. Fuel addition and initial organization answer different
   questions and have different preparation accounts.
2. Read construction over history: which products actually assist later
   construction, what persists, what is rebuilt, and the accompanying resource
   use. Keep full trajectories and timing. Endpoint survival, event totals,
   depth and original identity alone are insufficient.
3. Explore neighboring density, bond-strength and catalytic-barrier settings.
   Report effects, uncertainty and crossings without a vote over diagnostics.
   Mobility and fuel-availability brackets follow informative physical gaps.
4. Run the working-template failure panel from the existing 60 eligible
   source contexts alongside the baseline study. Weight failures by native
   rates within the class and retain that class's rarity in the full sample.
   This explains dependencies; fragility is not a veto on the contribution.
5. Introduce spatial fuel, rotations or other mechanisms only when a concrete
   observation calls for the extra resolution, with a physical rationale.

Version choices and outcomes for exploration. No preregistration approval gate,
new favored valuer class, reward multiplier or required winner is introduced.

## First execution

Completed the [baseline/corridor panel](lattice_corridor_report_v0.md) and
[working-template failure companion](lattice_template_damage_report_v0.md),
with the chemical generator unchanged. 15,456 full physical trajectories and
2,131,055 events; roughly 65 seconds combined simulation/sampling time with
ten workers. Full histories, approximate-equilibrium sampler traces and all
comparison coordinates are retained. Fourteen focused checks passed.

The first corridor is for finite catalytic construction followed by subsequent
use. Intermediate bond strength has the largest observed reuse probability
among the sampled strengths for refueled equilibrium at every density, at both
nonzero catalytic settings. Some contributing products later disappear.
Native template failure changes accumulated history even when final activity
nearly recovers. Equilibrium itself is assembled, so this is not yet a
comparison against a dilute gas. Preparation costs and sampling uncertainty
remain explicit; no lushness scalar was adopted.

## Priority clarification: possibility extent

The originator subsequently reaffirmed that quantifying or profiling lushness,
including the intended possibility volume, is the main deliverable. Mechanism
probes support that goal; they cannot substitute for it indefinitely.

The next recommended milestone is therefore an explicit finite extent rule on
the existing continuation object and computed profiles under the existing
frame/horizon construction. Specify its physical carrier, what contributes an
element of extent, and how joint composition and residual continuation enter.
Keep the actual conditioned probability weights, all noise and failed paths.
No extra prior, preferred mechanism bonus or vote over diagnostics is implied.

Apply the resulting candidate to existing preparations and template-failure
contrasts before expanding the chemistry. State precisely what the rule loses
and whether observed disagreements expose the carrier, the extent rule or its
application. A bundle of unrelated bond, reuse and recovery counts is not by
itself a profile of possibility extent. The assessment has not selected a new
functional or claimed one is already adequate.
