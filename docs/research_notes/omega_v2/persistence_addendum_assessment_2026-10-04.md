# Direction from the persistence addendum

Read 2026-10-04: [GE and Lushness Addendum — Persistence, Value Substrate, and
Recent Model Updates](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Zj3FrzoPlOQz7lBtCQIphZPRJETOujAdJbQyFNtlYns/edit).
The accompanying Drive model-choice ledger reproduces our comparison map;
it is provenance, not independent evidence for the new substrate.

The useful advance is Section 3.1's emphasis on **dependencies of newly opened
continuation**. Construction can make further processes accessible while
concentrating access through a vulnerable component. The scientific question
is then how the residual law changes when that component changes, and whether
other paths or reconstruction preserve access. This fits the field-first
program and gives the next simulation a physical question without choosing a
valuer or inventing an ethical reward.

Persistence understood as continuing consequential capacity, rather than
identity survival or static longevity, is compatible with the brief. It should
guide which dynamics we examine. It does not yet identify a persistence
functional or make persistence alone the sought extent. The addendum is right
to retain crossing profiles and to treat the normative bridge as conditional.

## Corrections needed before treating the prose as a contract

1. **New access need not add a dependency to existing access.** An optional
   parallel route can improve both reach and resilience. The two sources of
   vulnerability described in Section 3.1 are possibilities, not a theorem that
   attack surface doubles or that greater capability is always more fragile.
2. **Do not select 'value-capable' components in advance.** Read this as the
   program's motivating interpretation of consequential structure, not a gate
   admitting familiar valuers, correction systems or constructors. Otherwise
   the proposed chain from field to substrate discards the unknown cases the
   field was meant to retain.
3. **The sterile-extension test is too strong as written.** A duplicate
   description of one physical variable adds nothing. A physically additional
   component is a different case: it may hold its own records, consume resources
   or become coupled later. Preserve its consequences and show the conditions
   under which a particular projection is insensitive to it. Do not adopt a
   blanket quotient or zero weight for something called irrelevant.
4. **Detailed balance eliminates net probability currents, not events or
   consequence.** Equilibrium histories can still carry reactions, fluctuations,
   coupling and responses. Read the addendum's flow summary in that restricted
   sense; it does not warrant calling equilibrium a dead history.
5. **Recovery and strict irreversibility differ.** In the current finite
   reversible substrate, a bond break does not permanently remove the reverse
   mechanism. Fuel exhaustion is not absorbing. Measure recovery probability,
   delay and physical resource use over frames/horizons; do not manufacture
   permanent harm by silently deleting reverse reactions. Permanent loss would
   need additional physical assumptions, such as escape beyond the represented
   accessible region, with the wider account stated.

## What the new 2D pilot contributes

The [pilot](lattice_chemistry_report_v0.md) has reversible construction and
disassembly, local template dependence, crowding, loops and mobile components.
It is now large enough for alternate local configurations and several
assemblies to coexist. The run does not yet measure their full residual access.
Its bond counts and catalytic ancestry are diagnostic summaries.

Useful observations include construction without a prebuilt seed in 88 of 96
catalysis-enabled dispersed trajectories, and at least two successive
catalytic production steps in 87 of 192 catalysis-enabled trajectories.
However, 500 of 1,335 catalytic formations reconnect a previously present
particle pair, and 68 return to a pair in the catalyst's ancestry. These
distinguish construction, repair and recycling; they do not assign value to
any of them. Large stable aggregates can also be kinetically restrictive.

## Recommended next question

From sampled cuts, examine **native local bond failures** and their actual
downstream effects, including later reconstruction. Compare residual physical
laws over distance, delay, conformation, bonding and fuel, with routes and
joint dependencies retained. Ask whether apparently similar present access
is supported by one fragile template or by several recoverable routes.

Use the same model first. Diagnose passive aggregates, active templates and
recycling from their behavior. Do not encode these as privileged species or
declare that redundancy must win. Present readouts are insufficient for a
whole-field ranking, but sufficient to begin identifying what the dynamics
make accessible and what particular failures change. This is a proposed next
probe, not a result already obtained in the pilot.

Follow-up: the [native-failure probe](lattice_damage_report_v0.md) has now run
on all 384 pilot cuts, with the chemistry unchanged. It separates first repair,
deadline bond occupancy and alternate connection, and retains spatial effects.
The broad sample selected only five currently working-template failures;
the catalytic-dependency claim therefore still needs a targeted conditional
sample. This updates the proposed-next-probe status without upgrading the
philosophical claims above.
